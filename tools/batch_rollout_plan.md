## Implementation Details

### Code Prefix System

#### Korean Prefixes (KR-)

- `KR-ECON-001`: Economic transformation figures
- `KR-CHAE-001`: Business leadership figures
- `KR-CHAE-002`: Corporate innovation figures
- `KR-CONF-001`: Political reform figures
- `KR-CULT-001`: Cultural export figures
- `KR-TECH-001`: Technology and IT figures

#### Brazilian Prefixes (BR-)

- `BR-NATI-001`: Monarchy and constitutional development
- `BR-MODE-001`: Independence and nation-building
- `BR-INDU-001`: Industrial development and urbanization
- `BR-AGRI-001`: Agricultural innovation and tropical development
- `BR-RESO-001`: Resource development and energy security
- `BR-SOCI-001`: Social development and welfare

#### Other International Prefixes

- `EU-*`: European figures (Germany, France, etc.)
- `UK-*`: United Kingdom figures
- `US-*`: United States figures
- `RU-*`: Russian figures
- `JP-*`: Japanese figures

### Quality Control Standards

#### Bilingual Requirements

- All JSON files must have complete bilingual (Chinese/English) fields
- English as primary language, Chinese as secondary
- Consistent terminology across languages

#### Historical Accuracy

- All figures must have verifiable historical sources
- Wikipedia QIDs for cross-referencing
- Accurate historical periods and dates

#### Mode Coverage

- Minimum 1 zero-coverage mode per figure
- Cross-disciplinary thinking modes preferred
- Diversity across nationalities and civilizations

#### Data Quality

- Complete metadata (era, domains, gender, ethnicity)
- Clear civilization sphere identification
- Historical domain categorization

## Risk Management

### Implementation Risks

1. **Historical Verification**: Ensure all sources are reliable and accurate
2. **Mode Mapping**: Accurate thinking mode assignment for each figure
3. **Cross-Cultural Bias**: Avoid Western-centric perspectives
4. **Technical Consistency**: Maintain uniform data structure across all entries

### Mitigation Strategies

1. **Multi-Source Verification**: Cross-reference historical information
2. **Expert Review**: Academic and historical expert consultation
3. **Cultural Advisory Board**: Diverse cultural perspectives review
4. **Continuous Testing**: Regular quality assurance and validation
