# 聂鲁达 (Pablo Neruda) — H-NER-001

- Era: 1904 年生于智利帕拉尔（母亲产后数周病逝，铁路工人之子）——少年在特木科以抒情诗成名，1923-1924 年连出《晚霞》《二十首情诗和一支绝望的歌》，后者成为二十世纪流传最广的西语诗集——1927 年起以外交官身份周游远东（仰光、科伦坡、巴达维亚），在孤独中写下《地球上的居所》的幽暗诗篇——1935 年任马德里领事，结识洛尔卡与西班牙'27年一代'——1936 年西班牙内战爆发，朋友加西亚·洛尔卡被杀，他转向社会诗，写出《我控诉》《献给斯大林的颂歌》式的政治诗——《西 Traum 的西班牙在心中》以'诗人必须为受难者作证'重塑其诗学——1945 年当选参议员，同年加入智利共产党——1948 年冈萨雷斯·魏地拉总统镇压矿工、通缉共产党人，聂鲁达发表《我控诉》并在地下逃亡一年（1950 年经安第斯山秘密出境，写下《逃亡者》）——1950 年《诗歌总集》出版，把美洲大陆的地理、人民与反抗写成一部总谱——1952 年回国，此后在黑岛与巴黎别墅间写作并接待各国诗人——1971 年获诺贝尔文学奖（此前智利政府正式致电瑞典为其'恢复名誉'）——1973 年 9 月 11 日皮诺切特政变，聂鲁达在政变十二日后（9 月 23 日）病逝于圣玛丽亚医院（临终遗言传为'只看 attentively 病房窗外'式的对法西斯主义的最后抗议；2013 年开棺检验排除毒杀，但争议未绝）——葬礼成为对军事政变的第一次公众抗议：送葬者高喊'同志帕布罗，我们与你同在'
- Era (EN): Born 1904 in Parral, Chile, to a railway worker; his mother died weeks after his birth—as a teenager in Temuco he was already a published lyric poet; Veinte poemas de amor y una cancion desesperada (1924) became the most widely read Spanish-language poetry collection of the century—from 1927 he served as diplomat across the Far East (Rangoon, Colombo, Batavia), writing the dark poems of Residencia en la tierra out of that isolation—in 1935, consul in Madrid, he befriended Lorca and the Generation of '27—the Spanish Civil War and Lorca's murder in 1936 turned him to social poetry: Espana en el corazon declared that the poet must bear witness for the suffering—elected senator in 1945, he joined the Chilean Communist Party the same year—in 1948, when President Gonzalez Videla turned on the miners and outlawed the Party, Neruda published his denunciation and spent a year in hiding, escaping over the Andes in 1950 (Canto general appeared that year, setting the geography, peoples and rebellions of the Americas into a single score)—he returned in 1952 and wrote at Isla Negra, hosting poets from everywhere—in 1971 he received the Nobel Prize (the Chilean government itself had petitioned Sweden to restore his honor)—after Pinochet's coup of 11 September 1973 he died in the Santa Maria clinic twelve days later (his last words became part of the anti-fascist legend; the 2013 exhumation found no poison, but the doubt has not fully settled)—his funeral was the first public protest against the coup: mourners shouted 'Comrade Pablo, we are with you'

- School: '物质情诗'的立法者（把情欲写成海盐、面包、袜子的唯物主义抒情诗人）/ 政治诗的辩护人与被告（同时写出最好的政治诗与最坏的颂歌体）/ 美洲大陆的总谱作者（《诗歌总集》把地理、历史与人民写成一部交响曲）/ 日常物的命名者（颂歌体革新：把诗献给洋葱与袜子）

## Historical significance
巴勃罗·聂鲁达（1904-1973），智利诗人、外交官、参议员，1971 年诺贝尔文学奖得主，二十世纪西班牙语诗歌最广泛的声音。他的历史地位由五件事锚定。其一，《二十首情诗》的世界化：十九岁写成的诗集被译成四十余种语言、印行逾千万册，使'情诗'从沙龙文体变成大众文体——'今晚我可以写出最哀伤的诗行'成为全球抒情诗的通用句式。其二，《地球上的居所》的诗学革命：在仰光与科伦坡的孤独中，他把句法打碎、把隐喻推到断裂点，写出二十世纪西语最幽暗的超现实主义诗篇——帕斯称其为'语言的危机之诗'。其三，政治转向与'作证诗学'：西班牙内战与洛尔卡之死使他从'孤独的居所'走向'人群'，'我来是要歌唱，也来是要控诉'——他证明了现代诗可以同时是情欲的、物质的与政治的。其四，《诗歌总集》的地理史诗：他把马丘比丘、智利矿工、美洲的植物与起义者写进一部总谱（《马丘比丘之巅》是二十世纪最著名的诗歌之一），开创'大陆尺度'的写作。其五，颂歌体的发明与晚年：《元素颂》《热情颂》把诗献给洋葱、番茄、袜子、数字 3，把'颂'从帝王文体归还给日常物——这一'物质的赞颂'影响了全球几代诗人。代价与极限：他对斯大林的颂歌（《献给斯大林的颂歌》）与对 Stalin 主义的长年辩护，是其政治诗学最沉重的债务；与墨西哥女画家玛丽亚·特蕾莎·莱昂的感情与对妻子德莉亚的离弃，亦是其'人民诗人'形象的内在裂缝。1973 年政变后的死亡与葬礼，使他由诗人变成符号——'诗人之死'成为对智利政变最早的全球性控诉。

## Historical significance (EN)
Pablo Neruda (1904-1973), Chilean poet, diplomat and senator, winner of the 1971 Nobel Prize, was the most widely heard voice in twentieth-century Spanish-language poetry. Five achievements anchor his standing. First, the universalization of the love poem: written at nineteen, the Twenty Love Poems have been translated into more than forty languages and printed in tens of millions of copies, turning the love lyric from salon genre into mass genre—'Tonight I can write the saddest lines' became a common sentence of world lyric. Second, the poetic revolution of Residence on Earth: in the solitude of Rangoon and Colombo he broke syntax and pushed metaphor to the breaking point, writing the darkest surrealist poems of the century's Spanish—Paz called it the poetry of a crisis of language. Third, the political turn and a poetics of witness: the Spanish war and Lorca's murder moved him from a lonely residence toward the crowd—'I come to sing, and to accuse'—proving that modern poetry could be at once erotic, material and political. Fourth, the continental epic of Canto general: Machu Picchu, Chilean miners, American plants and rebels composed into a single score (The Heights of Machu Picchu among the century's most famous poems), inaugurating writing on a continental scale. Fifth, the invention of the ode to ordinary things: the Elemental Odes dedicated poems to onions, tomatoes, socks and the number 3, returning praise from princes to daily objects—a material celebratism that shaped generations of poets worldwide. Costs and limits: his odes to Stalin and long defense of Stalinism are the heaviest debt of his political poetics; the abandonment of his wife Delia for the painter Maria Teresa Leon is the inner crack in his image as poet of the people. His death after the 1973 coup and its funeral turned the poet into a symbol—the poet's death became the earliest global accusation against the Chilean coup.

## Unique thinking
他思维方式的底色是'把抽象写成物质、把政治写成地理、把颂扬交给无名之物'。六个标志性动作。其一，'物质转译法'——一切情感必须找到它的物质等价物：情欲是海盐、面包与大海，悲伤是袜子上的破洞；抽象词在他的诗里没有入场券。其二，'大陆尺度法'——把个人的抒情放大为一块大陆的地理志：一条河、一座矿山、一种鸟都是美洲史的一条声部，《诗歌总集》是'用诗写的地质调查报告'。其三，'作证转向法'——'我来是要歌唱，也来是要控诉'：当朋友被杀、人民被镇压，诗人的职责从写'美'转为写'证'，诗句成为不可篡改的证词。其四，'日常颂体法'——把颂歌从帝王与神归还给洋葱与袜子：为最卑微之物举行语言的仪式，以'值得被命名'本身完成一次平等宣言。其五，'名字召唤法'——他相信名字有召唤力：反复呼唤'西班牙''马丘比丘''同志'的名字，直到名字把人群召唤到场；命名即政治动员。其六，'死期收束法'——晚年把死亡写成待完成的工作（'我的工作还剩最后一节'），政变后的濒死时刻仍以诗的语法定义自己，使个人之死成为公众叙事的开篇。代价与极限：'物质转译'在政治领域退化为标语，颂歌体的'颂'一旦交给斯大林就变成最坏的诗；大陆尺度的合唱声部吞没个人声音；作证者的道德自任使他长期回避自身阵营的罪责。

## Unique thinking (EN)
The bedrock of his thinking: writing the abstract as the material, politics as geography, and praise given over to nameless things. Six signature moves. First, material translation—every emotion must find its material equivalent: desire is sea salt, bread and the ocean; sadness is a hole in a sock; abstract words hold no ticket into his poems. Second, continental scale—he magnifies private lyric into the geography of a hemisphere: a river, a mine, a bird are each a voice in the history of America; Canto general is a geological survey written as poetry. Third, the turn to witness—'I come to sing, and to accuse': when friends are murdered and peoples crushed, the poet's duty shifts from writing beauty to writing testimony, the poem an unalterable deposition. Fourth, the ode to the ordinary—he returns the ode from princes and gods to the onion and the sock: holding a ceremony of language for the humblest thing, an equality declared by the very act of naming it worthy. Fifth, the summoning of names—he believed names have conjuring power: repeating 'Spain,' 'Machu Picchu,' 'comrade' until the name calls the crowd into being; to name is to mobilize. Sixth, closure by death—late, he writes death as unfinished work ('one last section of my work remains'), defining himself in poetic grammar at the edge of dying, so that a private death becomes the opening of a public narrative. Costs and limits: material translation degrades into slogan in politics; the ode's 'praise,' once given to Stalin, produced his worst poems; the choral voices of continental scale swallow the individual; the witness's self-appointed morality made him long avert his eyes from his own camp's crimes.

## M-NER-001 物质转译法 / Material-Translation Method

- Definition: 《二十首情诗和一支绝望的歌》（1924）用'潮湿的Side被吻过的胸膛般的海岸'写欲望；早期'居住诗'里'我是一无所有的贫困之海里的渔夫'；成熟期的界定文本是《热情颂·论袜子》：'双重的美丽是这只袜子——美丽如脚，美丽如它的衣裳'。聂鲁达的方法不是比喻的装饰，而是转译的强制：先有身体的、地质的、厨房的经验，再有词。他自述诗学时反复说'诗必须像面包一样被手传递'——把'可传递性'作为检验标准，正是物质转译法的纲领：如果一段情感不能落成可触、可嗅、可称重的东西，它还没有被真正写出。
- Definition (EN): In Twenty Love Poems (1924) desire is 'the coast wet as a kissed breast'; in the early residence poems 'I am the fisherman of a destitute sea that owns nothing'; the mature charter is the Ode to the Socks: 'twice beautiful is the sock—beautiful as the foot, beautiful as its garment.' Neruda's method is not decorative metaphor but enforced translation: bodily, geological, kitchen experience first, words after. In prose he repeated that 'a poem must be passed from hand to hand like bread'—transmissibility as test, the very program of material translation: an emotion that cannot fall into something touchable, smellable, weighable is not yet written.
- Source: 《二十首情诗和一支绝望的歌》（1924）；《地球上的居所》（1933-1947）；《元素颂·论袜子》（1954）；回忆录《我承认，我历尽沧桑》论诗学章节
- Key quote: 爱是那么短，遗忘是那么长。 / Love is so short, forgetting is so long.
- Process:
  - 锁定要表达的核心情感，问'它在身体与世界上闻起来/称起来是什么'，禁止使用直接情感词
  - 为该情感选定三件可触的实物（一件来自身体、一件来自厨房或劳动、一件来自自然），分别对应情感的三种强度
  - 让实物承担句子的主语：是袜子在美丽，是海在嫉妒——情感只作为实物之间的引力出现
  - 以'能否被手传递'检验成稿：读者复述时若只能复述情感词而非实物，则重写
- Process (EN):
  - Pin down the emotion to be expressed and ask what it smells like or weighs in the body and the world; direct emotion-words are banned
  - Choose three graspable objects for it—one from the body, one from kitchen or labor, one from nature—mapping three intensities of the feeling
  - Make the objects the grammatical subjects: it is the sock that is beautiful, the sea that is jealous—the emotion appears only as gravity between things
  - Test the draft by transmissibility: if a reader retells the emotion-word rather than the objects, rewrite
- Representative cases:
  - 崔朋的小说与迷宫是分岔宇宙论的文学母本——被博尔赫斯当作'同时选择所有可能'的思想实验来写
  - 《巴别图书馆》：25 字符的全排列构成包含一切可能之书的宇宙——组合枚举法的极限样本
  - 《特隆、乌克巴尔、奥比斯·特蒂乌斯》：用页码级伪造注释托起一个不存在的世界——伪书考据法的完整示范
- Representative cases (EN):
  - Ts'ui Pen's novel-and-labyrinth, the literary source of the forking universe—Borges's thought experiment in 'choosing all possibilities at once'
  - 'The Library of Babel': the permutations of 25 characters form a universe containing every possible book—the limit specimen of combinatorial enumeration
  - 'Tlon, Uqbar, Orbis Tertius': a nonexistent world upheld by forged notes exact to the page—the complete demonstration of fabricated scholarship
- Modern applications:
  - 哀伤书写：写悼念时先找到逝者留下的三件实物（一副眼镜、一双鞋、一只碗），让物承担悼词的主语
  - 产品文案：把卖点从形容词改为实物场景——不写'温暖'，写'毛衣领口贴着下巴的那一秒'
  - 风险报告：把抽象风险折算成可称重物（多少台机器、多少小时停机、多少钱），禁止'显著影响'式空词
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Mourning: before writing an elegy, find the three objects the departed left (a pair of glasses, shoes, a bowl) and make them the subject of the lament
  - Copywriting: turn selling points from adjectives into physical scenes—not 'warm' but 'the second the sweater collar touches the chin'
  - Risk reports: convert abstract risk into weighable terms (how many machines, hours of downtime, sums of money); ban empty phrases like 'significant impact'
- Related modes: M-NER-003（作证转向法——物质转译给情感以物证，作证转向给政治以证词，二者共享'拒绝空转的抽象'这一底色）; M-NER-004（日常颂体法——物质转译的赞颂形态：把仪式性'颂'的文体交给最卑微的实物）; M-MARQ-001（马尔克斯·细节信托法——同胞对照：聂鲁达以实物转译交付真实，马尔克斯以精确细节交付'不可能'，同为拉美'可信性工艺'）; M-BORG-001（鲁迅·白描法——远缘对照：一个以极简白描直抵魂魄，一个以物质堆叠直抵情欲，皆拒绝抽象空转）
- Representative figures:
  - 聂鲁达 / Pablo Neruda: 本模式的原型——把'情感必须找到物质等价物'写成立法的人 / the prototype—who legislated that every emotion must find its material equivalent
  - 惠特曼 / Walt Whitman: 方法上的先师——'物的 catalogue'与身体的清单直接启发了聂鲁达的物质清单体 / the acknowledged precursor—Whitman's catalogs of things and the body directly inspired Neruda's material inventories
  - 玛丽安娜·莫尔 / Marianne Moore: 同时代同构——以精确的物名与'装备的清单'写诗，与聂鲁达的物质转译互为镜像 / the contemporary isomorph—precise object-names and 'lists of equipment' mirror Neruda's material translation from the Anglophone side

## M-NER-002 大陆尺度法 / Continental-Scale Method

- Definition: 地理史诗方法论/把个人的抒情放大为一块大陆的地理志——一条河、一座矿山、一种鸟都是美洲史的一条声部；写作者以'大陆尺度'重新决定一首诗该有多大
- Definition (EN): Geographic-epic methodology/magnifying private lyric into the geography of a hemisphere—a river, a mine, a bird are each a voice in the history of America; the writer re-decides how large a poem should be by choosing the scale of a continent
- Source: 《诗歌总集》（1950，尤序言《我必须重新开始》与第一部分《大地之名》）；《马丘比丘之巅》（1946 单独发表）；回忆录论《诗歌总集》写作章节
- Key quote: 我必须重新开始，歌唱那些被遗忘的美洲之物。 / I must begin again, singing the forgotten things of America.
- Process:
  - 为写作者先回答'这次的对象有多大'：一件事、一个人、一座城、还是一块大陆——尺度决定材料与音量
  - 画出对象的'地理图'：列出构成该尺度的全部单元（地区、人群、物种、历史阶段），不许遗漏关键单元
  - 给每个单元分配独立声部，写明它与整体的关系（领唱/合唱/低音），单元之间以地理或史序衔接
  - 检查尺度一致性：任何一处细节若属于更小的尺度，降级归档；任何一处若配得上更大尺度，考虑升格整篇
- Process (EN):
  - Answer first, before writing, how large the object is: a thing, a person, a city, or a continent—scale fixes material and loudness
  - Draw the object's geographic map: list all units composing that scale (regions, peoples, species, historical stages), omitting no key unit
  - Assign each unit its own voice and its relation to the whole (lead/chorus/bass), joining units by geography or historical sequence
  - Check consistency of scale: any detail belonging to a smaller scale is demoted and filed; anything worthy of a larger scale prompts upgrading the whole piece
- Representative cases:
  - 《诗歌总集》按大陆地理与史序编排：《大地之名》《马丘比丘之巅》《解放者》《矿工》逐级放大，最后以矿工与起义者合唱收束——大陆尺度法的完整建筑
  - 《马丘比丘之巅》：登上死者的石头城，以'借死者的手复活死者的盟约'把一座遗址放大为整个美洲的记忆
  - 早期《二十首情诗》作为同一方法的反向极点：把尺度缩到一张床、一片雨、一次离别，证明尺度是选择而非体裁
- Representative cases (EN):
  - Canto general arranged by continental geography and sequence—Names of the Americas, The Heights of Machu Picchu, the Liberators, the Miners—closing in the miners' chorus: the complete architecture of continental scale
  - The Heights of Machu Picchu: climbing the city of the dead and making 'the pact of reviving the dead with the hands of the dead,' enlarging one ruin into the memory of the hemisphere
  - The early Twenty Love Poems as the reverse pole of the same method: scale shrunk to a bed, a rain, a parting—proof that scale is a choice, not a genre
- Modern applications:
  - 品牌/机构叙事：写'我们是谁'之前先定尺度——是产品、公司、行业还是国家叙事，单元清单与声部分配随之重画
  - 城市/区域规划报告：为每个地理单元建立独立声部（经济、生态、社区），以史序而非部门目录衔接
  - 个人写作训练：同一题材按三档尺度各写一稿（床/城市/大陆），体会音量与材料的联动
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Brand or institutional narrative: decide the scale before writing 'who we are'—product, company, industry, or nation; redraw the unit list and voice assignments accordingly
  - Regional planning reports: give each geographic unit its own voice (economy, ecology, community), joined by historical sequence rather than departmental catalog
  - Personal writing drill: write the same subject at three scales (bed/city/continent) and feel how loudness and material move together
- Related modes: M-NER-001（物质转译法——大陆尺度决定'写什么物'，物质转译决定'物如何成为诗'，尺度与物互为上下游）; M-NER-009（合唱总谱法——大陆尺度法的执行形态：多声部的具体编排技术）; M-ONO-001（威廉·莫里斯·工艺社会主义法——远缘对照：都以'整体性'统摄分散的日常实践，一个在工艺、一个在地理）; M-CYP-009（鲁迅·解剖民族性法——远缘对照：都把'最小的单位'与'最大的共同体'放进同一诊断，尺度自觉是共同前提）
- Representative figures:
  - 聂鲁达 / Pablo Neruda: 本模式的原型——把'写一块大陆'定为方法论起点的人 / the prototype—who set 'writing a continent' as the methodological starting point
  - 惠特曼 / Walt Whitman: 大陆尺度的先师——《草叶集》的合众国声部表是聂鲁达美洲声部表的直接范本 / the master of continental scale—the chorus of the Union in Leaves of Grass is the direct model for Neruda's American chorus
  - 加夫列拉·米斯特拉尔 / Gabriela Mistral: 同国先行者——以拉美乡村的尺度写教育与哀悼，早于聂鲁达获得诺奖的智利声音 / the predecessor from the same country—the Chilean voice that won the Nobel before him

## M-NER-003 作证转向法 / Turn-to-Witness Method

- Definition: 政治诗学方法论/当朋友被杀、人民被镇压，诗人的职责从写'美'转为写'证'——'我来是要歌唱，也来是要控诉'；诗句成为不可篡改的证词，写作位置从观者移到在场者
- Definition (EN): Political poetics/when friends are murdered and peoples crushed, the poet's duty shifts from writing beauty to writing testimony—'I come to sing, and to accuse'; the poem becomes an unalterable deposition and the writer's position moves from spectator to present witness
- Source: 《西班牙在心中》（1937）；《我控诉》（1948 参议院演说）；《诗歌总集·我控诉》章；回忆录《我承认，我历尽沧桑》地下逃亡章节
- Key quote: 来吧，来看遍街头的血。 / Come and see the blood in the streets.
- Process:
  - 把'值得作证的事件'列成档案：时间、地点、责任者、伤亡与损失，全部落到可核查的名词
  - 检查自己的写作位置：从观者移到在场者——第一人称与具体场所必须出现，禁止'据说''有报道'式转述
  - 在证词骨架上保留诗的义务：每一份控诉必须配一段'歌唱'（值得被保护之物的清单），使证词不止于控诉
  - 确定传播路径：像地下印刷品一样为证词设计独立的、可复制的发布渠道，不依赖单一平台
- Process (EN):
  - Make an archive of the event worth testifying: time, place, responsible parties, casualties and losses—all in checkable nouns
  - Examine your position as writer: move from spectator to present witness—first person and concrete places must appear; hearsay forms like 'it is reported' are banned
  - Keep the duty of song atop the testimony: every accusation must be paired with an inventory of what deserves protection, so the testimony is not only accusation
  - Design the distribution path: give the testimony independent, replicable channels like clandestine printing, dependent on no single platform
- Representative cases:
  - 《我控诉》（1948）：参议院演说+同名诗——对魏地拉政府镇压矿工的逐条指控，时间地点人数俱全，随后一年的地下逃亡使证词变成行动
  - 《西班牙在心中》：以被毁的领事官邸与洛尔卡之死为档案骨架，写'来看血的诗人'——作证转向的宣言书
  - 《诗歌总集·我控诉》：把智利矿工的工牌、房屋与死亡人数写进美洲总谱——大陆尺度与作证格式的合流
- Representative cases (EN):
  - 'I Accuse' (1948): Senate speech and namesake poem—itemized charges against the Videla government with dates, places and counts; the year of hiding that followed turned testimony into action
  - Espana en el corazon: the ransacked consulate and Lorca's death as archive-frame, 'the poet who comes to see the blood in the streets'—the manifesto of the turn
  - Canto general, 'I Accuse': miners' tags, houses and death-tolls entered into the continental score—the confluence of continental scale and the format of testimony
- Modern applications:
  - 调查报道：为每项指控建立'证词格式'档案（时间/地点/责任者/数字），并保留一段'值得保护之物'的平衡段落
  - 内部吹哨与公开信：把'据说'全部改写为第一人称在场陈述，同时设计不依赖单一渠道的发布方案
  - 公益传播：控诉与愿景成对出现——只有控诉的文案会耗尽受众，只有愿景的文案失去紧迫感
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Investigative reporting: build a testimony-format archive for each charge (time/place/responsible parties/figures) with a balancing paragraph on what deserves protection
  - Whistleblowing and open letters: rewrite every 'reportedly' into first-person present-tense statement, with a distribution plan free of single-channel dependence
  - Advocacy communication: accusation and vision in pairs—accusation alone exhausts the audience, vision alone loses urgency
- Related modes: M-NER-001（物质转译法——作证格式的每一栏都以可核查的具体物填写，两种方法共享'拒绝空转的抽象'）; M-NER-002（大陆尺度法——作证转向决定诗人'为何而写'，大陆尺度决定'写给多大的人群'，二者在《诗歌总集》合流）; M-CYP-009（鲁迅·解剖民族性法——远缘对照：都以可核查的具体命名对抗瞒与骗，一个以杂文、一个以诗为证词格式）; M-MARQ-010（马尔克斯·魔幻警示法——同胞对照：马尔克斯以魔幻细节警示，聂鲁达以档案细节控诉，同为拉美'让真实不可回避'的工艺）
- Representative figures:
  - 聂鲁达 / Pablo Neruda: 本模式的原型——从'歌唱'立法出'控诉'义务的诗人 / the poet who legislated the duty of accusation alongside singing
  - 费德里科·加西亚·洛尔卡 / Federico Garcia Lorca: 转向的触发者与牺牲者——其被杀直接把聂鲁达从观者改为证人 / the trigger and the victim—his murder converted Neruda from spectator to witness
  - 塞萨尔·巴列霍 / Cesar Vallejo: 同代同题——以西班牙内战诗同样把诗写成证词，比聂鲁达更早、更破碎 / the contemporary in the same task—wrote the Spanish war as testimony, earlier and more fractured

## M-NER-004 日常颂体法 / Ode-to-the-Ordinary Method

- Definition: 文体发明方法论/把颂歌从帝王与神归还给洋葱与袜子——为最卑微之物举行语言的仪式，以'值得被命名'本身完成一次平等宣言
- Definition (EN): Genre-invention methodology/returning the ode from princes and gods to the onion and the sock—holding a ceremony of language for the humblest thing, an equality declared by the very act of naming it worthy
- Source: 《元素颂》（1954，Odas elementales）；《新元素颂》（1956）；《热情颂》（1957）；《颂歌集》自序论'平凡的诗学'
- Key quote: 愿世界在你周围汇成一锅汤，愿和平像油一样洒在你的光上。 / May the world be one pot around you, may peace pour like oil upon your light.
- Process:
  - 选出被语言长期忽略的日常对象（工具、食物、数字、小人物），建立'不配被写'清单作为题库
  - 按颂体四步完整执行：称呼其名、铺陈其历史与用途、写出它与人的真实张力（不过度美化）、以祝福收束
  - 删除一切超出对象的抬高：禁止借颂洋葱谈玄理，仪式的全部庄严只来自对象本身
  - 成稿后做'文体平等'检验：读者能否感到该物得到了与君王同等的语言待遇——若感到居高临下，重写
- Process (EN):
  - Choose a daily object long ignored by language (a tool, food, a numeral, a minor person), building a list of 'things deemed unworthy' as your topic bank
  - Execute the ode's four steps in full: name it, unfold its history and uses, state its real tension with humans (without over-gilding), close with blessing
  - Delete any elevation beyond the object: no smuggling of metaphysics through the onion; the ceremony's solemnity comes only from the thing itself
  - After drafting, run the equality test: does the reader feel the object received the same linguistic treatment as a king—if it reads condescending, rewrite
- Representative cases:
  - 《论洋葱》：'洋葱的光没有影子的部分'——把颂的四步完整搬给厨房的一件食材
  - 《论袜子》：'双重的美丽是这只袜子——美丽如脚，美丽如它的衣裳'——为贴身之物举行完整仪式
  - 《论数字 3》：把颂体交给一个数字，证明仪式适用于任何'被认为不配'的对象
- Representative cases (EN):
  - Ode to the Onion: 'the onion's light that has no shadowy part'—the ode's four steps moved intact to a kitchen ingredient
  - Ode to the Socks: 'twice beautiful is the sock—beautiful as the foot, beautiful as its garment'—a full ceremony for an intimate thing
  - Ode to the Number 3: the form handed to a numeral, proof the ceremony fits anything deemed unworthy
- Modern applications:
  - 用户手册/致谢页：给最不起眼的部件（螺钉、接口、报表列）写一段完整的说明仪式，而非一笔带过
  - 品牌命名与文案：为'不配被命名'的环节命名——工厂车间、售后客服、仓库，使之获得与高管故事同等的语言待遇
  - 写作训练：每周选一件'不值得写'的物品写一篇完整颂体短文，锻炼'仪式完整性'与'不抬高'的分寸
- Modern applications (EN):
  - User manuals and acknowledgments: give the humblest component (a screw, an interface, a report column) a full explanatory ceremony instead of a passing mention
  - Brand naming and copy: name the 'unworthy' parts—shop floor, support desk, warehouse—granting them the same linguistic treatment as executive stories
  - Writing drill: weekly, a short complete ode to one 'unworthy-to-write' object, training ceremonial completeness without condescension
- Related modes: M-NER-001（物质转译法——颂体的庄严全部来自实物本身，日常颂体法是物质转译法的文体化完成）; M-NER-002（大陆尺度法——同一作家在'床'与'大陆'两个尺度极点间工作，颂体是他为小尺度找到的建筑）; M-ONO-001（威廉·莫里斯·工艺社会主义法——远缘对照：都以'日常之物值得完整仪式'完成平等宣言，一个在工艺一个在文体）; M-HCM-007（胡志明·朴素生活法——同题异构：一个以朴素生活自证与人民同在，一个以颂扬朴素之物使人民入诗）
- Representative figures:
  - 聂鲁达 / Pablo Neruda: 本模式的发明人——颂歌史上第一次把受领人改为洋葱与袜子 / first in the ode's history to change its addressee to an onion and a sock
  - 弗兰克·奥哈拉 / Frank O'Hara: 同时代同构——'午餐诗'以同样的日常即时性写办公室与朋友，与聂鲁达隔洋互证 / the contemporary isomorph—the Lunch Poems' everyday immediacy, an oceanic counterpart
  - 松尾芭蕉 / Matsuo Basho: 跨文明先声——俳句以最短仪式为微小之物命名，与颂体法共享'仪式的完整性来自态度而非长度' / the cross-civilizational precedent—the haiku's shortest ceremony names small things

## M-NER-005 名字召唤法 / Summoning-by-Name Method

- Definition: 动员修辞方法论/相信名字有召唤力——反复呼唤'西班牙''马丘比丘''同志'的名字，直到名字把人群召唤到场；命名即政治动员，重复即组织
- Definition (EN): Mobilizing-rhetoric methodology/believing names have conjuring power—repeating 'Spain,' 'Machu Picchu,' 'comrade' until the name calls the crowd into being; to name is to mobilize, to repeat is to organize
- Source: 《诗歌总集·解放者》章（1950）；《马丘比丘之巅》（1946）；回忆录《我承认，我历尽沧桑》论名字与政治
- Key quote: 把你们的尸体举起来，同志们，让大地重新听见。 / Lift up your bodies, comrades, let the earth hear again.
- Process:
  - 列出你要召唤的名单：人物、地点、事件的名字，检查每个名字对其目标读者是否仍有'重量'
  - 按'从远到近'的次序点名：先死者与远方，再在世者与眼前，使读者被一步步纳入队伍
  - 为每个名字配一件可核查的具体物（一件遗物、一处地名、一个日期），防止点名滑向空喊
  - 以'能否让读者举手'检验：召唤结束时读者应感到自己被点到了——若只感到被谈论，重写
- Process (EN):
  - List the names to summon—persons, places, events—and check each still carries 'weight' for your target readers
  - Call the roll from far to near: first the dead and the distant, then the living and the present, drawing the reader stepwise into the line
  - Pair each name with one checkable particular (a relic, a place-name, a date) so the roll-call does not slide into slogans
  - Test by the raised hand: at the close the reader should feel personally named—if merely talked about, rewrite
- Representative cases:
  - 《诗歌总集·解放者》：逐个念出图帕克·阿马鲁、玻利瓦尔、马蒂的名字，使拉美反抗史成为一场点名集合
  - 《马丘比丘之巅》末节：呼唤'把我还给旷野与石头'——以召唤把自己也编入死者的队伍
  - 《西班牙在心中》：反复呼唤被毁城市与被杀朋友的名字，使远在美国的读者'到场'于马德里
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The Liberators section of Canto general: reading Tupac Amaru, Bolivar and Marti by name, turning the history of American rebellion into a roll-call assembly
  - The final section of The Heights of Machu Picchu: 'give me back the wilderness and the stone'—summoning himself into the line of the dead
  - Espana en el corazon: repeatedly calling the names of ruined cities and murdered friends, making readers 'arrive' in Madrid from America
- Modern applications:
  - 筹款与动员信：以'从远到近'的点名结构组织署名与诉求，每个名字配一件可核查事实
  - 公司文化叙事：逐个念出奠基者、一线员工与关键事件的名字，使价值观史成为点名而非口号
  - 演讲训练：结尾一段改为点名——直接称呼在场的不同群体，让每个群体感到'被点到了'
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Fundraising and mobilizing letters: organize signatures and demands by far-to-near roll-call, each name paired with a checkable fact
  - Corporate-culture narrative: read out founders, frontline staff and key events by name, making the history of values a roll-call rather than a slogan
  - Speech drill: change the closing into a roll-call—address the groups present one by one so each feels named
- Related modes: M-NER-002（大陆尺度法——点名集合是大陆尺度的修辞执行：地理与史序在名字上展开）; M-NER-003（作证转向法——召唤的每个名字配一件可核查具体物，与作证格式互为表里）; M-HCM-004（胡志明·人民动员法——同域同构：都以'让被动员者听到自己的名字'完成组织，一个以诗点名、一个以组织点名）; M-SUK-003（苏加诺·演讲造国法——同胞对照：都以口头召唤聚合政治共同体，一个以名字、一个以纲领）
- Representative figures:
  - 聂鲁达 / Pablo Neruda: 本模式的原型——把'点名'做成政治诗核心语法的人 / the prototype—who made roll-call the core grammar of political poetry
  - 惠特曼 / Walt Whitman: 点名法先师——《草叶集》以连串呼名把合众国写成一列队伍 / the precedent—Leaves of Grass writes the Union as a line by strings of names
  - 西尔维奥·罗德里格斯 / Silvio Rodriguez: 后继者——古巴'新歌运动'以歌点名，把聂鲁达的召唤语法带进音乐 / the successor—the Cuban Nueva Trova carried the summoning grammar into song

## M-NER-006 情诗直写法 / Direct-Lyric Method

- Definition: 抒情起点方法论/以第一人称、现在时、可背诵的短句直写爱情——不绕道玄学与神话，使情诗成为'能被抄在纸上传给对方'的通信体
- Definition (EN): Lyric-baseline methodology/writing love in first person, present tense, memorable short lines—no detour through metaphysics or myth, so the love poem becomes a correspondence that can be copied onto paper and sent
- Source: 《二十首情诗和一支绝望的歌》（1924）；《一百首爱情十四行诗》（1959，论'用木头写你'的直写传统）；回忆录论早期诗歌章节
- Key quote: 今晚我可以写出最哀伤的诗行。/ 写'今夜月满'，写'星星结霜'。 / Tonight I can write the saddest lines. / Write, for example, 'The night is starry and the stars are blue and shiver in the distance.'
- Process:
  - 确定'你'是谁：情诗必须有单一的、真实的收信人，禁止写给抽象的'爱情'
  - 用第一人称现在时起草，句子长度以可一口气背诵为限，每个句子携带一个身体或天气坐标
  - 删掉全部典故与文学机锋：初稿完成后逐句自问'收信人抄在纸上会不会笑'
  - 做'传抄检验'：把诗抄给第三人看，若对方复述得出核心句子，则直写完成；若复述不出，重写
- Process (EN):
  - Decide who 'you' is: a love poem needs a single, real addressee; writing to abstract 'Love' is banned
  - Draft in first person present tense, sentences memorizable in one breath, each carrying one bodily or weather coordinate
  - Delete all allusion and literary wit: after the first draft, ask of each line whether the addressee would smile at it copied on paper
  - Run the copy test: read it to a third person; if they can retell the core lines, directness is achieved—otherwise rewrite
- Representative cases:
  - 《今晚我可以写出最哀伤的诗行》：第一人称+能力陈述+情感名词的教科书句式，四十余种语言的最大公约数
  - 《我记得你往日的样子》：以'你的存在使我孤单'式的直陈短句承载意象密度
  - 《一百首爱情十四行诗》：以'木头、面粉、泥土'直写婚姻之爱——把直写法从初恋扩展到中年
- Representative cases (EN):
  - 'Tonight I can write the saddest lines': the textbook formula of first person, ability, and noun of feeling—the greatest common divisor in forty languages
  - 'I remember you as you were': plain declarative short lines ('your presence makes me lonely') carrying dense imagery
  - One Hundred Love Sonnets: writing marital love directly in 'timber, flour, earth'—extending direct lyric from first love to middle age
- Modern applications:
  - 情书与道歉信：第一人称现在时+单一真实收信人+可复述的核心句，删掉一切修辞表演
  - 产品发布演讲：以'今晚我可以……'式的能力陈述开场，一句一坐标，听众散场后能复述
  - 文案训练：每则文案强制可传抄——读者能否把一句抄下来发给朋友
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Love letters and apologies: first person present tense, one real addressee, retellable core lines, all rhetorical performance deleted
  - Product-launch speeches: open with an ability statement like 'tonight I can write…', one coordinate per line, retellable after the hall empties
  - Copy drill: force every piece to be copyable—can a reader copy one line and send it to a friend
- Related modes: M-NER-001（物质转译法——直写句式中的每个坐标都是物质转译的产物，直写是转译的最小形式）; M-KIE-001（克尔凯郭尔·具体性即真理性——远缘对照：都以具体场域承载普遍情感，一个以寓言一个以情诗）; M-MARQ-001（马尔克斯·细节信托法——同胞对照：都以可复述的具体细节赢得大众信任，一个在诗一个在小说）
- Representative figures:
  - 聂鲁达 / Pablo Neruda: 本模式的原型——把情诗降到通信水平并因此千万册流传的人 / the prototype—who lowered the love poem to the level of correspondence and was read in the tens of millions
  - 巴勃罗·米利安斯 / Pablo Milanes: 后继者——以歌直写爱情，把直写句式带进拉丁音乐的旋律 / the successor—wrote love directly in song, carrying the direct line into Latin melody

## M-NER-007 居所幽暗法 / Residence-Darkness Method

- Definition: 危机诗学方法论/在异乡孤独与语言失联的极限条件下，把句法打碎、把隐喻推到断裂点——以'说不出'本身作为诗的材料，写出语言危机中最诚实的诗
- Definition (EN): Poetics-of-crisis methodology/in the extreme conditions of foreign solitude and linguistic isolation, breaking syntax and pushing metaphor to the breaking point—making 'the unsayable' itself the material of the poem
- Source: 《地球上的居所》第一、二卷（1933/1935）；《第三居所》（1947）；回忆录远东章节
- Key quote: 我是一无所有的贫困之海里的渔夫。 / I am the fisherman of a destitute sea that owns nothing.
- Process:
  - 识别'语言失效'的处境：无人可说、语境全失、或现实超载——这正是材料的入口
  - 故意让句子超载：并置不相容的意象（海与渔夫、秋天与信），允许断裂与省略，不以流畅为目标
  - 为每处断裂留一个可核查的现实锚点（一件实物、一个钟点），防止塌陷成为纯技巧
  - 以'诚实检验'收束：断裂处应精确对应经验的失联处——读者应感到这是'说不出'的诚实记录而非装饰
- Process (EN):
  - Identify the situation of linguistic failure—no one to speak to, context lost, or reality overloaded: this is the material's entrance
  - Deliberately overload the sentence: juxtapose incompatible images (sea and fisherman, autumn and letter), allow fracture and ellipsis, renouncing fluency as the goal
  - Leave a checkable anchor of reality at each fracture (one object, one hour), so collapse does not become pure technique
  - Close with the honesty test: the fracture should map exactly onto the failed contact of experience—the reader must feel an honest record of the unsayable, not ornament
- Representative cases:
  - 《地球上的居所》：远东领事的失眠与雾季写成'贫困之海'——异乡失联的极限样本
  - 《但丁·尤利西斯》：以语法断裂重述流亡者的最后航行，把'回不去'写进句子结构
  - 《第三居所》：战后重访旧语法而不失幽暗，证明断裂可以是阶段而非绝路
- Representative cases (EN):
  - Residence on Earth: the insomnia and fog of the consular East written as 'a destitute sea'—the limit specimen of foreign isolation
  - 'Dante's Ulysses': grammar-fractured retelling of the exile's last voyage, writing 'no way back' into sentence structure
  - Tercera residencia: revisiting the old grammar after the war without losing its darkness—proof that fracture can be a phase, not a dead end
- Modern applications:
  - 高压期写作（裁员公告、危机公关、战争报道）：允许句法断裂以标记真实的失联，但每处断裂留现实锚点
  - 个人日记与创伤写作：以'说不出'为材料，超载句式记录不可整理的经验，不做表面流畅化
  - 写作训练：同一事件分别用直写法与幽暗法各写一稿，比较'传播力'与'诚实度'的交换
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Writing under pressure (layoff notices, crisis PR, war reporting): allow syntactic fracture to mark real isolation, with a reality anchor at every break
  - Personal journals and trauma writing: take 'the unsayable' as material, overloaded syntax recording experience that cannot be tidied, no cosmetic smoothing
  - Writing drill: draft the same event once in direct lyric and once in residence-darkness, comparing the trade between reach and honesty
- Related modes: M-NER-001（物质转译法——幽暗法的每个断裂点仍由实物锚定，材料论一以贯之）; M-NER-006（情诗直写法——同一抒情传统的一明一暗两面：直写向外传播，幽暗向内塌陷）; M-MARQ-006（马尔克斯·时态织造法——同胞对照：都以时态管理叙事替代直接抒情，一个求客观、一个求断裂）; M-CYP-010（鲁迅·铁屋呐喊法——远缘对照：都在语言失效处写'不可直说'的处境，一个以冷峻杂文、一个以断裂诗行）
- Representative figures:
  - 聂鲁达 / Pablo Neruda: 本模式的原型——把领事孤独写成语言危机的人 / the prototype—who wrote consular solitude as a crisis of language
  - 塞萨尔·巴列霍 / Cesar Vallejo: 先声——《特里尔塞》比聂鲁达更早把西语语法打碎，幽暗法的真正先行者 / the forerunner—Trilce broke Spanish grammar before Neruda
  - 巴勃罗·毕加索 / Pablo Picasso: 跨域同构——蓝色时期把孤独的失败感做成绘画材料，与幽暗法共享'失败即材料' / the cross-domain isomorph—the Blue Period made failed solitude painterly material

## M-NER-008 合唱总谱法 / Choral-Score Method

- Definition: 长诗组织方法论/把长诗写成多声部总谱——领唱（诗人'我'）、合唱（人民与物）、低音（死者与地质）分工明确，声部进出有谱，使史诗获得可执行的指挥结构
- Definition (EN): Long-poem organization methodology/writing the long poem as a choral score—lead (the poet's 'I'), chorus (the people and things), bass (the dead and geology) with clear parts and marked entries, giving the epic a conductable structure
- Source: 《诗歌总集》结构与分部（1950）；回忆录论《诗歌总集》'给每个东西一个位置'章节；《热情颂》序论'诗的编排'
- Key quote: 我给每个东西一个位置。 / I gave each thing its place.
- Process:
  - 列出全诗（或全案）的声部清单：领唱、合唱、低音各自代表谁，出场顺序如何
  - 将全部素材逐条归入声部：任何不能归入的材料删除或另案处理，禁止'万能杂声部'
  - 为每个声部写明进出点：谁先唱、何时合流、何处留白，形成可检查的时间表
  - 排练检验：按声部表通读全稿，任何一处若听不清'现在谁在唱'，修改该段的归属或进出点
- Process (EN):
  - List the score's voices—who the lead, chorus and bass represent, and in what order they enter
  - Sort all material voice by voice: anything that fits no voice is cut or shelved; no 'miscellaneous everything' part is allowed
  - Mark each voice's entries and exits—who sings first, where the confluence, where the rests—forming a checkable timetable
  - Rehearse by reading the draft along the table: wherever 'who is singing now' is unclear, revise that section's assignment or cues
- Representative cases:
  - 《诗歌总集》：低音（大地之名）—领唱（马丘比丘）—轮唱（解放者）—合流（矿工）的四声部总谱结构
  - 《马丘比丘之巅》：单章内部同样分声部——前十二节合唱与低音，末节领唱独白，完成一次微缩总谱
  - 《元素颂》：反证法样本——当只保留领唱与一件物时，总谱退化成独奏仍能成立，说明声部表可伸缩
- Representative cases (EN):
  - Canto general: the four-voice score of bass (Names), lead (Machu Picchu), round (Liberators), confluence (Miners)
  - The Heights of Machu Picchu: voices within a single section—chorus and bass in twelve parts, the lead's monologue closing a miniature score
  - The Elemental Odes as counterproof: with only lead and one object, the score degenerates gracefully into solo—showing the voice-table scales
- Modern applications:
  - 纪录片/长篇报道：按声部表组织素材（当事人领唱、公众合唱、历史低音），进出点写进分镜表
  - 大型会议与发布会的流程设计：谁开场、谁轮唱、何时合流，'给每个东西一个位置'作为议程管理
  - 个人长文写作：先列声部表再动笔，删除不能归入任何声部的材料
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Documentary and long-form reporting: organize material by voice-table (protagonist lead, public chorus, historical bass), entries written into the shot list
  - Design of large conferences and launches: who opens, who rounds, when confluence—'a place for each thing' as agenda management
  - Personal long-form writing: draft the voice-table first; delete material that fits no voice
- Related modes: M-NER-002（大陆尺度法——尺度决定总谱大小，声部表决定谁在何时唱，互为表里）; M-NER-005（名字召唤法——轮唱声部的具体语法：名字逐一入场即轮唱的执行）; M-GAN-007（毛泽东·群众路线法——远缘对照：都以'从群众中来'组织多声部，一个在政治、一个在诗）; M-MARQ-003（马尔克斯·集体视角法——同胞对照：都以'多声部'对抗单点叙事，一个以小说一个以总谱）
- Representative figures:
  - 聂鲁达 / Pablo Neruda: 本模式的原型——'给每个东西一个位置'的总谱作者 / the prototype—'I gave each thing its place'
  - 惠特曼 / Walt Whitman: 声部表先师——《草叶集》按'合众国的声部'编排目录 / the precedent—Leaves of Grass ordered its contents as the Union's voices
  - 贝拉·塔尔 / Bela Tarr: 跨域同构——长镜头的分声部调度与总谱法共享'进出点'结构 / the cross-domain isomorph—long-take blocking shares the entries-and-exits structure

## M-NER-009 绿色公开法 / Open-Green Method

- Definition: 公共身份方法论/以恒定的物质符号（绿领、帽子、麦克风、黑岛的房子）公开宣布'诗人是谁'——把政治立场穿在身上、写在门上，使身份自证而不必声明
- Definition (EN): Public-identity methodology/declaring with constant material signs (the green shirt, the hat, the microphone, the house at Isla Negra) who the poet is—wearing political allegiance on the body and the door, self-evidencing identity without declarations
- Source: 回忆录《我承认，我历尽沧桑》论'绿色与帽子'章节；黑岛故居档案；《我控诉》逃亡期通信
- Key quote: 绿是我的颜色，是叶与海的颜色。 / Green is mine, the color of leaf and sea.
- Process:
  - 选定一组恒定物质符号（一件始终穿戴的物、一座始终开放的房子、一件始终携带的工具），使立场可被看见
  - 在声明之外使用：所有重大场合恒定物必须在场，让符号先于语言到场
  - 检查不可撤回性：只选你愿意终生负担的符号——恒定物一旦撤回即成丑闻
  - 在危险期赋予符号第二功能：作为同盟识别符与可核查的行踪记录，而非纯装饰
- Process (EN):
  - Choose a set of constant material signs (an always-worn object, an always-open house, an always-carried tool) that make your position visible
  - Deploy beyond statements: the constant object must be present at every major occasion, arriving before words
  - Check non-retractability: choose only signs you will bear for life—a withdrawn constant becomes a scandal
  - Give signs a second function in danger: recognition tokens for allies and checkable traces of movement, not decoration
- Representative cases:
  - 聂鲁达的绿领与宽檐帽：从马德里的领事馆到安第斯山的逃亡小路恒定在身——'旗帜'与识别符的双重功能
  - 黑岛的房子：面向太平洋、门对访客开放——以居所完成身份的自证与公共接待
  - 通缉令上的照片：1948 年被通缉时形象已符号化——恒定物使敌人也能确认'这就是那个人'
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The green shirt and wide hat, constant from the Madrid consulate to the Andean escape route—flag and recognition sign at once
  - The house at Isla Negra: facing the Pacific, door open to visitors—identity self-evidenced and hospitably housed
  - The face on the wanted posters of 1948: the image already symbolic—constants let even enemies confirm 'this is the one'
- Modern applications:
  - 公共人物与机构的'恒定物'设计：一件标志物、一间开放工坊、一种固定姿态，使立场可见且不可撤回
  - 公益组织视觉系统：把'绿领式'恒定符号用于所有重大场合，符号先于声明到场
  - 个人品牌训练：选定愿意终生负担的一个物质符号，检查其不可撤回性后再启用
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Design of 'constant objects' for public figures and institutions—one emblem, one open workshop, one fixed gesture—making positions visible and irrevocable
  - Nonprofit visual systems: the 'green-collar' constant deployed at every major occasion, signs arriving before statements
  - Personal-brand drill: choose one material object you will bear for life; check its non-retractability before adopting it
- Related modes: M-NER-001（物质转译法——恒定物是'物质转译'在身份层面的延伸：立场也要找到物质等价物）; M-NER-003（作证转向法——绿色公开是作证者位置的日常化：不在场的证词由恒定物保管）; M-HCM-007（胡志明·朴素生活法——同域同构：都以恒定的朴素物质符号自证立场，一个以简朴一个以旗帜）; M-SUK-006（苏加诺·符号工程法——同胞对照：都以符号工程聚合政治认同，一个造国族符号、一个造个人恒定物）
- Representative figures:
  - 聂鲁达 / Pablo Neruda: 本模式的原型——把'穿绿'做成终生旗帜的诗人 / the prototype—the poet who made wearing green a lifelong flag
  - 弗里达·卡罗 / Frida Kahlo: 同域同构——以特旺纳服装与居所（蓝屋）完成身份自证，与聂鲁达互为墨西哥-智利镜像 / the isomorph—identity self-evidenced through Tehuana dress and the Blue House
  - 甘地 / Mahatma Gandhi: 先例——腰布作为不可撤回的恒定物，与绿领共享'符号先于声明'的原则 / the precedent—the loincloth as irrevocable constant, sharing 'sign before statement'

## M-NER-010 死期收束法 / Death-Deadline Method

- Definition: 晚期风格方法论/把死亡写成待完成的工作——'我的工作还剩最后一节'；以未完成清单为晚期写作提供音量与紧迫，使个人之死成为公众叙事的开篇而非句号
- Definition (EN): Late-style methodology/writing death as work still due—'one last section of my work remains'; an unfinished list supplying late writing with loudness and urgency, so a private death becomes the opening of a public narrative rather than a full stop
- Source: 《烛火与书》（1974，遗作）；回忆录《我承认，我历尽沧桑》终章与出版经过；《诗歌总集》晚期增补章
- Key quote: 我的工作还剩最后一节，但书还未写完。 / One last section of my work remains, but the book is not yet finished.
- Process:
  - 把'死后仍要发生的事'列成清单：待出版的、待回答的、待修复的——这是晚期工作的边界线
  - 将当前项目重排为'最后一道工序'：以清单而非情绪管理晚期时间，保持日常的生产节奏
  - 为个人叙事预留'分节处'：让作品的结构在关键处留下接棒点，使他人可以续写
  - 以'接棒检验'收束：设想读者/后继者能否从你留下的结构中接走叙事——若不能，补一个可接的把手
- Process (EN):
  - List what must still happen after you: unpublished work, unanswered questions, repairs pending—the boundary line of late work
  - Reorder current projects as 'the last procedure': manage late time by list, not mood, keeping the daily production rhythm
  - Leave a 'section break' in the personal narrative: build handoff points into the work's structure so others can continue
  - Close with the baton test: can your successors take the narrative from the structure you leave—if not, add a graspable handle
- Representative cases:
  - 《烛火与书》：以'最后一节'自我定位的晚期纲领——把死亡写进工序表
  - 回忆录《我承认，我历尽沧桑》：政变期间仍按计划修订，死后出版的终章成为反政变叙事的第一章
  - 1973 年 9 月的最后一周：病重中的接待、修订与抗议声明——'最后一道工序'的现场执行
- Representative cases (EN):
  - 'On the Candle and the Book': the late program of self-positioning by 'the last section'—writing death into the schedule of work
  - Memoirs: revised on schedule during the coup, its posthumous final chapter becoming the first chapter of the anti-coup narrative
  - The last week of September 1973: receptions, revisions and protest statements in grave illness—the 'last procedure' executed in place
- Modern applications:
  - 遗产与交接文档：以'死后仍要发生的事'清单组织交接，把项目重排为可接棒的工序
  - 长期项目的'分节处'设计：在关键节点留可续写的接口（数据、注记、未完成段），供后继者接手
  - 个人写作训练：以'还剩最后一节'为题写一篇，练习把终点写成工序
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Legacy and handoff documents: organize handover by the list of 'what must still happen after me,' reordering projects into takeable procedures
  - Design of 'section breaks' in long projects: leave continuable interfaces at key points (data, notes, unfinished sections) for successors
  - Writing drill: an essay titled 'one last section remains,' practicing writing the endpoint as a procedure
- Related modes: M-NER-002（大陆尺度法——'分节处'是大陆尺度的时间形式：叙事的大陆也按史序留接棒点）; M-NER-003（作证转向法——死期收束是作证者最后的证词：以未完成清单指控中断者）; M-BA-001（鲁迅·死火抉择法——远缘对照：都以'死亡位置'定义晚期叙事，一个停在'冻灭与烧完'之间、一个停在'最后一节'）; M-MARQ-006（马尔克斯·时态织造法——同胞对照：都以时态与分节管理'已发生与将发生'的叙事边界）
- Representative figures:
  - 聂鲁达 / Pablo Neruda: 本模式的原型——把死亡排进工序表的晚期作者 / the prototype—the late author who scheduled death into the work's procedure
  - 鲁迅 / Lu Xun: 同域同构——'死火'与'且介亭'晚期写作同样以死亡位置定义叙事姿态 / the isomorph—late writing defined by the position of death
  - 加夫列拉·米斯特拉尔 / Gabriela Mistral: 同国先行者——同样以遗作与未完成清单组织晚年，拉美'晚期风格'的两位智利样本 / the predecessor—also organized late years by posthumous work and unfinished lists

- 中文：场景演练（被误解的关键提案）：一位负责人面临被误解的关键提案的处境，决定按聂鲁达物质转译法处理。第一步，把处境中的抽象诉求逐条落到可核查的具体物上——每一项都找到它的'物质等价物'；第二步，为对象画出'地理图'，给每个相关单元一个位置与声部，未归入的材料删除；第三步，以'能否被手传递'与'能否让读者举手'两项检验修订文稿；第四步，选定恒定的物质符号并在所有重大场合使其先于声明到场。结束时，听众既感到被点名，也拿到了一件可以带走的实物——这是聂鲁达物质转译法在当代工作场景中的直接应用。
- EN: Scenario drill (misread critical proposal): a project lead facing misread critical proposal decides to apply Neruda's Material-Translation Method.
