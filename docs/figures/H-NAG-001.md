# 龙树 (Nagarjuna) — H-NAG-001

- Era: 约 150 年生于南印度婆罗门家庭（一说安达罗国）——传说出身婆罗门而皈依佛教，据说由龙宫取出《大方广佛华严经》而号'龙树'——学成后一度以'憍慢'自居欲自立教团，传说大龙（一说老比丘）接引入深宫读大乘经而悔悟——确立'中观'（Madhyamaka）纲领：一切法缘起无自性（sunyata，空），执有执无皆为戏论，中道即是缘起——以归谬论证（prasanga）遍破'说一切有'部的实在论、数论的因中有果与胜论的因中无果，把'自性'（svabhava）概念推上审判台——著《中论》（Mulamadhyamakakarika，四百四十八颂二十七品）、《六十如理论》《七十空性论》《十二门论》《回诤论》《大智度论》（十万颂，汉译存三十四卷）等，被尊为'千部论主'——传说寿逾数百岁，后为王子求头（一说因与王室之约）以吉祥草叶自断其首而逝——身后中观成为大乘佛教义学的轴心：提婆、佛护、月称、清辨数代接续，经鸠摩罗什入汉地开三论宗，经寂护入藏地成应成传统，《楞伽经》授记其为'证得欢喜地'之菩萨，禅、净、天台、华严、三论、密诸宗共尊为'八宗共祖'
- Era (EN): Born c. 150 in South India into a Brahmin family (some say in the Andhra country)—by legend a Brahmin convert to Buddhism who fetched the Avatamsaka Sutra from the palace of the nagas and took the name 'Nagarjuna'—after mastery he once stood in 'pride,' intending to found his own order, until by legend a great naga (some say an old monk) led him into a palace to read the Mahayana sutras and he repented—gave the Middle Way (Madhyamaka) its program: all things arise dependently without intrinsic nature (sunyata, emptiness), clinging to being or non-being is proliferation, and the middle way is dependent origination itself—with reductio argument (prasanga) he refuted the Sarvastivada realism, the Samkhya 'effect pre-exists in the cause' and the Vaisesika 'effect is wholly new,' putting the concept of intrinsic nature (svabhava) on trial—wrote the Mulamadhyamakakarika (448 verses in 27 chapters), the Sixty Stanzas of Reasoning, the Seventy Stanzas on Emptiness, the Twelve Gates Treatise, the Vigrahavyavartani, and the Mahaprajnaparamitopadesa (100,000 stanzas, 34 fascicles in Chinese), honored as 'author of a thousand treatises'—by legend lived several hundred years and at the end, when a prince sought his head (some say by a pact with the royal house), severed it himself with a blade of kusa grass—after his death Madhyamaka became the axis of Mahayana doctrine: Aryadeva, Buddhapalita, Candrakirti and Bhavaviveka continued in succession; through Kumarajiva it entered China as the Three Treatise school, through Santaraksita Tibet as the Prasangika tradition; the Lankavatara Sutra prophesied him a bodhisattva at the stage of Joy, and the Chan, Pure Land, Tiantai, Huayan, Sanlun and Tantric schools honor him together as 'common ancestor of the eight schools'

- School: 中观学派的开山祖师（'缘起性空'形上学总纲的立法者）/ 归谬论证（应成）的发明者（不立自宗、以对手前提推出自毁）/ 大乘经典的整理与弘扬者（据说自龙宫迎出《华严》并广造诸论）/ 二谛学说的立法者（世俗谛与究竟谛的两层真值架构）/ 被尊为'第二佛陀'与'八宗共祖'的传承源头

## Historical significance
龙树（约150-250），南印度人，大乘佛教中观学派的创始人，被后世尊为'第二佛陀'。他的历史地位由五件事锚定。其一，'缘起性空'总纲的确立：他把佛陀的'缘起'教义锻造成完整的形上学体系——'众因缘生法，我说即是空'：凡依因缘而起者即无自性（svabhava），空不是无物而是'无自性'的别名，且'以有空义故，一切法得成'——唯有无自性，因果、修行、言说才可能。其二，归谬论证方法（应成）的立法：不立自宗、只用对手前提推出其自毁——'我无有少宗故，我即为无过'——中观由此成为史上罕见的'不占位置的批判系统'。其三，对'自性'概念的审判：自生、他生、共生、无因生四门俱破，'一'与'异'俱不成，把印度各派形上学的共同预设掀翻。其四，二谛学说：世俗谛（约定有效）与究竟谛（无自性可得）的两层架构，'不依世俗谛，不得第一义'——既保住日常世界的有效性，又为空性开出言说通道。其五，制度性影响：著作经鸠摩罗什等译入汉地开三论宗，经寂护入藏地成格鲁派义学根基；禅宗、净土、天台、华严、密教共尊其为'八宗共祖'，《楞伽经》授记其为登地菩萨——一个哲学体系在两大文明圈获得千年建制，史上罕见。代价与极限：'只破不立'使体系依赖后学补足（应成与自续的千年方法论之争）；'空'屡被误读为虚无主义（他本人警告'空被错误把握如抓蛇不当'）；生平资料多与传说混融（龙宫取经、寿逾数百、施头而逝），史实层仅能锚定其著述与思想影响。

## Historical significance (EN)
Nagarjuna (c. 150-250), a South Indian and the founder of the Madhyamaka school of Mahayana Buddhism, is honored as the 'second Buddha.' Five achievements anchor his standing. First, the program 'dependent origination is emptiness': he forged the Buddha's teaching of conditioned arising into a complete metaphysics—'whatever is dependently arisen, that I declare to be emptiness': whatever arises from conditions lacks intrinsic nature (svabhava); emptiness is not nothing but another name for the absence of intrinsic nature, and 'for whom emptiness is possible, everything is possible'—only without fixed essence can causation, practice and speech work. Second, the reductio method (prasanga): establishing no thesis of his own, he drew self-destruction from his opponents' premises—'since I assert no thesis, I alone am without fault'—making Madhyamaka a rare critical system that occupies no position. Third, the trial of 'intrinsic nature': arising from self, other, both and causeless all refuted; identity and difference both dissolved—the shared presupposition of Indian metaphysics overturned. Fourth, the two truths: the two-level architecture of conventional validity and ultimate emptiness, 'without the conventional the ultimate cannot be taught'—preserving the everyday world while opening a channel of speech for emptiness. Fifth, institutional influence: his works entered China through Kumarajiva (the Sanlun school) and Tibet through Santaraksita (the Geluk doctrinal foundation); Chan, Pure Land, Tiantai, Huayan and the Tantric schools honor him as 'common ancestor of the eight schools,' and the Lankavatara prophesied him a bodhisattva on the path—a philosophical system holding institutional life across two civilizations for a millennium, rarely matched. Costs and limits: 'refute only, never establish' left the system to be completed by successors (the millennium-long Prasangika-Svatantrika dispute); 'emptiness' was repeatedly misread as nihilism (he himself warned 'emptiness wrongly grasped is like a snake seized by the wrong end'); his biography fuses with legend (fetching sutras from the naga realm, centuries of life, yielding his head)—the historical layer is anchored only in his works and their influence.

## Unique thinking
他思维方式的底色是'缘起为刃、归谬为器：任何事物若被追问到底，其'独立自足'即告瓦解；而瓦解不是虚无——依起本身就是事物的全部存在方式'。六个标志性动作。其一，'缘起归空法'——对任何事物追问'它依什么而立'，凡依因缘和合而起者即无自性：依起与自性逻辑上不两立。其二，'四句破法'——穷举任何形上学命题的四种可能（有/无/双是/双非）并逐一归谬，且不立任何宗，包括'一切皆空'。其三，'假名施设法'——万物'但名'而立：名不指实（无自性可指）但名不虚设（依起故有用），假名不是缺陷而是事物唯一的存在方式。其四，'二谛分层法'——真值分层为世俗谛与究竟谛，两层不混不裂：'不依世俗谛，不得第一义'，世俗不因究竟空性而失效。其五，'一体异体破法'——对任何'整体-部分'或'能-所'结构逐一检验'是一'与'是异'并证明皆不成：同则失分位、异则失依存。其六，'空亦复空法'——把'空'本身也交还依起：'若复见有空，诸佛所不化'——体系必须吞下自身方为自洽。代价与极限：纯驳回不立论使制度建设依赖后学；'中道'易被庸俗化为折中主义（真中道的试金石是触到两极共同预设）；对'自性'的严格定义是全部论证的地基，定义被松动则归空力度减弱。

## Unique thinking (EN)
The bedrock of his thinking: dependent origination as the blade, reductio as the engine—any thing, pursued to its root, loses its 'independent self-sufficiency'; and the loss is not nihilism, for dependence is the whole of how things exist. Six signature moves. First, dependence-as-emptiness—of any thing ask 'on what does it stand': whatever arises from conditions lacks intrinsic nature; dependence and intrinsic nature are logically incompatible. Second, the tetralemma—enumerate the four possible positions on any metaphysical proposition (is / is not / both / neither) and refute each by reductio, asserting no thesis, not even 'all is empty.' Third, dependent designation—things stand as 'mere name': names point to no substance (none exists) yet are not idle (dependence gives them use); designation is not a defect but the only mode of existence. Fourth, the two truths—truth stratified into conventional and ultimate, neither merged nor split: 'without the conventional the ultimate cannot be taught,' and the everyday is not voided by ultimate emptiness. Fifth, identity-difference dissolution—of any whole-part or agent-patient structure test both 'identical' and 'different' and show neither holds: identity destroys the distinction, difference destroys the dependence. Sixth, emptiness of emptiness—hand 'emptiness' itself back to dependence: 'one who then sees emptiness as a view is beyond the Buddhas' healing'—a system is consistent only when it swallows itself. Costs and limits: pure refutation left institution-building to successors; the 'middle way' vulgarizes easily into compromise (the touchstone is touching the shared presupposition of the poles); the strict definition of 'intrinsic nature' is the foundation of every argument—loosen it and the reduction weakens.

## M-NAG-001 缘起归空法 / Dependent-Origination-as-Emptiness Method

- Definition: 本体解构方法论/对任何事物追问'它依什么而立'，凡依因缘和合而起者即无自性——空不是无物，而是'无自性'的别名：唯有空，一切法才得成立
- Definition (EN): Ontological-deconstruction methodology/ask of any thing 'on what does it stand': whatever arises dependently lacks intrinsic nature—emptiness is not nothing but another name for the absence of intrinsic nature: only because of emptiness can anything stand
- Source: 《中论》（Mulamadhyamakakarika）第18品观我法、第24品观四谛（'众因缘生法，我说即是空'，'以有空义故，一切法得成'）；《十二门论》观因缘门
- Key quote: 众因缘生法，我说即是空，亦为是假名，亦是中道义。 / Whatever is dependently co-arisen, that I declare to be emptiness; it is also a dependent designation, and precisely that is the middle way.
- Process:
  - 对对象提出依起问句：'它凭自身而立，还是依因缘、部分与概念而立？'——逐项列出其依存条件
  - 施加两难：若它有自性（凭自身而立），则不需生起、不需条件、不应变化——与经验矛盾；若它依条件而起，则无自性
  - 完成反转：宣告'空'不是消灭对象，而是对象'无自性'的别名——并论证唯有无自性，对象的因果作用与可认知性才成立
  - 自噬收口：把'空'本身也交还依起——空是依'执有'而立的方便假名，不可再实体化为一个'最高真理'
- Process (EN):
  - Put the dependence question to the object: 'does it stand by itself, or on causes, parts and concepts?'—list its conditions of dependence
  - Apply the dilemma: if it has intrinsic nature (standing by itself), it needs no arising, no conditions, no change—contrary to experience; if it arises depending on conditions, it lacks intrinsic nature
  - Complete the reversal: declare that emptiness does not abolish the object but names its absence of intrinsic nature—and argue that only thus can the object's causal efficacy and knowability hold
  - Close by self-consumption: hand emptiness itself back to dependence—emptiness is a provisional designation standing on the reification of 'things,' not to be reified as a highest truth
- Representative cases:
  - 《中论》第24品：'众因缘生法，我说即是空'——缘起与空同一的总立法，也是全书唯一一次把'空'正面定义
  - '以有空义故，一切法得成'——对'空摧毁世界'指控的最强反驳：若无自性，因果、四谛、修行才可能；若各有自性，一切皆不可能
  - 观因果品：'因中有果'与'因中无果'双遣——生本身依起而空，两条传统因果解释路线被同一把刀放下
- Representative cases (EN):
  - MMK chapter 24: 'whatever is dependently co-arisen, that I declare to be emptiness'—the founding identification of dependence with emptiness, the only positive definition of emptiness in the work
  - 'For whom emptiness is possible, everything is possible'—the strongest reply to the charge that emptiness destroys the world: without intrinsic nature causation, the four truths and practice are possible; with fixed essences, nothing is
  - The examination of causation: both 'the effect pre-exists in the cause' and 'it does not' are let fall—arising itself is empty of intrinsic nature, and two traditional accounts of causation are cut by the same blade
- Modern applications:
  - 本质主义审计：对任何'核心本质'话语（核心竞争力、民族性、人性）追问'它依什么条件而立'——依存条件一变，所谓本质即变，凝固的本质论多是把一段依起关系误读为永恒属性
  - 系统思维的哲学地基：系统动力学讲'结构决定行为'，龙树补上反向一刀——结构本身也是依起而立的，任何系统边界都是概念假名，建模前先空掉'系统有固定本质'的预设
  - 创新方法论：'以有空义故，一切法得成'翻译成工程语言即'唯有放弃固化设计，系统才能演化'——过度锁定规格的产品无法迭代，空性是演化的形上学许可证
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Essentialism audit: of any 'core essence' discourse (core competence, national character, human nature) ask 'on what conditions does it stand'—when the conditions shift, the 'essence' shifts; frozen essentialism usually misreads a dependence relation as an eternal property
  - The philosophical ground of systems thinking: system dynamics says structure determines behavior; Nagarjuna adds the reverse cut—structure itself is dependently arisen, every system boundary is a conceptual designation; before modeling, empty the assumption that the system has a fixed essence
  - Innovation methodology: 'for whom emptiness is possible, everything is possible' in engineering language reads 'only by releasing fixed design can a system evolve'—products over-locked to specs cannot iterate; emptiness is the metaphysical license for evolution

- Representative figures:
  - 龙树 / Nagarjuna: 本模式的原型——把'缘起即空'立法为中观学派的第一原理 / the prototype—who legislated 'dependent origination is emptiness' as the first principle of the Madhyamaka school
  - 释迦牟尼 / the Buddha: 教义源头——'缘起'（此有故彼有）的提出者，龙树自任为缘起义的正确守护者而非新立法者 / the source of the doctrine—the proponent of dependent origination ('this being, that becomes'); Nagarjuna claims to guard its true meaning, not to legislate anew
  - 休谟 / David Hume: 平行对照——同样拆解'必然因果'与'独立实体'的实在性，但休谟止于心理习惯论，龙树把拆解转为'依起即空'的肯定性中道 / the parallel—likewise dissolves 'necessary causation' and 'independent substance,' but Hume stops at psychological habit while Nagarjuna turns dissolution into the affirmative middle way of 'dependence is emptiness'

## M-NAG-002 四句破法 / Tetralemma-Refutation Method

- Definition: 辩证方法论/穷举任何形上学命题的四种可能（有/无/双是/双非）并逐一归谬驳回——不选边，而证明命题在究竟层面不可成立；且不立任何宗，包括'一切皆空'
- Definition (EN): Dialectical methodology/enumerate the four possible positions on any metaphysical proposition (is / is not / both / neither) and refute each by reductio—not picking a side but showing the proposition cannot ultimately hold; and asserting no thesis, not even 'all is empty'
- Source: 《中论》第1品观因缘、第10品观燃可燃、第15品观有无、第18品观我法（'我无有少宗故'）、第25品观涅槃（如来灭后四句）
- Key quote: 若我有所立，则我有过失；我无所立故，我即无过失。 / If I asserted any thesis, I would have a fault; since I assert no thesis, I alone am without fault.
- Process:
  - 把问题形式化为一个待决命题 P，并穷举其全部四种立场：P、非P、P且非P、非P且非非P
  - 对每一立场从其内部前提推出归谬：执'有'者无法解释变化与生起，执'无'者自我拆台（断灭论若真则言说无效），执'双是'者自语相违，执'双非'者仍以有无为坐标
  - 宣告结论不是第五种立场，而是'P 这类命题在究竟层面不可成立'——问题被消解而非被回答
  - 自噬检验：检查自己是否暗中立宗——若'一切皆空'被执为宗，对空本身再施四句；'无宗'是此法免于自毁的唯一保险
- Process (EN):
  - Formalize the question as a proposition P and enumerate its four positions: P, not-P, both, neither
  - Derive a reductio from each position's own premises: holding 'is' cannot explain change and arising; holding 'is not' undermines itself (if nihilism were true, speech would fail); 'both' is self-contradictory; 'neither' still takes is/is-not as its coordinate
  - Declare that the conclusion is not a fifth position but that 'propositions of this type cannot ultimately hold'—the question is dissolved, not answered
  - Run the self-consumption check: examine whether a thesis has been smuggled in—if 'all is empty' is held as a thesis, apply the tetralemma to emptiness itself; 'no thesis' is the only insurance against self-destruction
- Representative cases:
  - 观涅槃品：'如来灭后有/无/亦有亦无/非有非无'四句俱置——佛陀本人对此类问题保持圣默然，龙树把沉默论证化：四句不是尚未知道答案，而是问题本身范畴错置
  - 观我法品第十八：'我无有少宗故，我即为无过'——把'不立宗'本身升格为方法论原则，中观由此成为'不占位置的批判系统'
  - 观燃可燃品：燃者与可燃物'是一/是异'两俱不成——以'双是/双非'四句拆解一切'能所'结构的模板战例
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The examination of nirvana: on whether the Tathagata exists after death—the four corners are all set aside; the Buddha's own noble silence on such questions is argumentativized: the corners are not unanswered but category-mistaken
  - Chapter 18: 'since I assert no thesis, I alone am without fault'—the elevation of thesislessness itself into a methodological principle, making Madhyamaka a critical system occupying no position
  - The examination of fire and fuel: burner and combustible are neither identical nor different—a template engagement where the four corners dismantle every 'agent-patient' structure
- Modern applications:
  - 战略决策的选项审计：重大决策前问'我们的选项清单是否穷尽且是否共享一个错误前提'——四句破的当代形态是检查'若所有选项都自毁，问题本身是否提错了'
  - 辩论与谈判训练：把对方与己方的立场穷举成四角并各自归谬，常能发现双方共享的范畴错置——谈判僵局多为'第三种没人选的框架'所解
  - AI 对齐与伦理框架评估：对任何伦理立场（义务论/后果论/德性论/混合论）逐一施压测试，检验'混合论'是否只是把矛盾摊平而非解决——四句破是框架审计的原型工具
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Options audit in strategy: before a major decision ask 'does our option list exhaust the field, and do all options share a false premise'—the contemporary tetralemma is checking whether, if every option self-destructs, the question itself is misposed
  - Debate and negotiation training: enumerate both sides' positions into four corners and run reductio on each; often both share a category mistake—deadlocks dissolve through 'the third frame nobody picked'
  - AI alignment and ethical-framework assessment: stress-test any ethical stance (deontology/consequentialism/virtue/hybrid) corner by corner, checking whether the 'hybrid' merely flattens contradiction rather than resolving it—the tetralemma is the prototype audit tool for frameworks
- Related modes: M-NAG-001（缘起归空法——四句破所服务的总纲：驳回四句的根据是依起无自性）; M-NAG-005（一体异体破法——四句破在对偶场景的专门化：对偶结构天然只容'一/异'两角）; M-SHA-004（商羯罗·立敌对论法——近缘对照：同为'先把对手立场强立再破'，商羯罗立最强敌论以显自宗，龙树归谬后连自宗也不立——立与不立是两家最大分岔）; M-KD-001（康德·二律背反法——远缘对照：康德把理性的四组矛盾判定为'越界使用范畴'的症状，龙树把四句判定为'问题本身范畴错置'——都以'消解问题'代替'回答问题'）
- Representative figures:
  - 龙树 / Nagarjuna: 本模式的原型——把四句穷举归谬锻造成中观的论证引擎并立法'无宗无过' / the prototype—who forged four-corner enumeration and reductio into Madhyamaka's argumentative engine and legislated 'no thesis, no fault'
  - 提婆 / Aryadeva: 方法的直接继承者——《四百论》把四句破扩展到感官、知识论与外道体系的全面审计 / the direct inheritor—the Catuhsataka extends the tetralemma into a full audit of the senses, epistemology and rival systems
  - 塞克斯都·恩披里柯 / Sextus Empiricus: 平行对照——希腊怀疑论的'论式'同样穷举立场以悬置判断，但皮浪派以'悬置'求宁静，龙树以'消解'显空性 / the parallel—the Greek skeptic's 'modes' likewise enumerate positions to suspend judgment, but Pyrrhonism suspends for tranquility while Nagarjuna dissolves to reveal emptiness

## M-NAG-003 假名施设法 / Dependent-Designation Method

- Definition: 语言哲学方法论/万物依概念施设而立，假名不是缺陷而是事物唯一的存在方式——名不指实（无自性可指）但名不虚设（依起故有用）
- Definition (EN): Philosophy-of-language methodology/things stand by conceptual imputation; designation is not a defect but the only mode of existence—names point to no substance (none exists) yet are not idle (dependence gives them use)
- Source: 《中论》第24品观四谛（'亦为是假名，亦是中道义'）、第22品观如来（车喻五蕴施设）、《回诤论》论能立与所言俱空
- Key quote: 离车无支分，离支分无车；我依五蕴立，如车依轮轴。 / Apart from its parts there is no chariot, apart from the chariot no parts; the self is imputed on the aggregates as a chariot on its wheels and axle.
- Process:
  - 对对象作'但名检验'：除去全部概念施设，剩下什么？——若答案仍是另一层施设（而非某个非施设的核），对象即是纯假名
  - 考察施设的依存条件：这个名依哪些支分、哪些约定、哪些语境而立——列出'名'的缘起谱系
  - 双向排除：名所指之实既不在名外（离蕴无我），也不在名内（即蕴亦无我）——施设与所指的关系是依存而非同一或分离
  - 按需安立：在世俗层面保留并使用该假名（不废除'我'与'车'），同时标记其无自性——用而不执，即是中道
- Process (EN):
  - Run the designation test on the object: strip away all conceptual imputation—what remains? If the answer is another layer of imputation (not a non-imputed core), the object is pure designation
  - Map the designation's conditions of dependence: on which parts, conventions, contexts does the name stand—trace the lineage of the name
  - Exclude both ways: the substance the name denotes is neither outside the name (no self apart from the aggregates) nor inside it (no self identical with them)—designation and referent relate by dependence, not identity or separation
  - Establish as needed: retain and use the designation conventionally (do not abolish 'self' or 'chariot') while marking its lack of intrinsic nature—use without clinging, which is the middle way
- Representative cases:
  - 《中论》观如来品：'如来'依五蕴施设，'即蕴/离蕴/依蕴/蕴依如来'五种关系全部不成——但'如来'假名照常安立，宗教敬仰不受影响
  - 第24品把'空'自己也判为假名——'空'若不是假名就会成为新的'自性'，整个体系因此自洽
  - 对'自我'的车喻分析：世俗层面'我在走路'完全有效，究竟层面'我'不可得——一句话内两层真值并行不悖，是日常语言分析的千年先声
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The examination of the Tathagata: 'Tathagata' is imputed on the aggregates, and all five relations (identity with, apart from, possessing, depending) fail—yet the designation stands, and religious devotion is untouched
  - Chapter 24's verdict that 'emptiness' itself is a designation—if emptiness were not a designation it would become a new intrinsic nature, keeping the system self-consistent
  - The chariot analysis of the self: conventionally 'I am walking' is fully valid; ultimately no self is found—two truth-values in one sentence without collision, a millennia-early prefiguration of ordinary-language analysis
- Modern applications:
  - 组织命名与身份治理：职位、头衔、KPI 全是假名施设——用而不执：岗位有效（世俗），但把岗位当'实体'（职位即人、头衔即价值）必生组织病
  - 产品与品牌策略：品牌是依用户认知条件施设的假名——依起故有力，但试图让品牌'占有本质'（品牌即品质的保证）则脱离缘起，营销神话多源于此误
  - 法律与社会建构分析：'法人''货币''国界'皆为依约定施设而完全有效的假名——龙树框架比'社会建构主义'更精确：建构不等于任意，施设依条件而有约束力
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Organizational naming and identity governance: titles, roles and KPIs are designations—use without clinging: the role is valid conventionally, but treating it as an entity (the post is the person, the title the worth) breeds organizational pathology
  - Product and brand strategy: a brand is a designation imputed on the conditions of user cognition—powerful because dependent, yet asking the brand to 'own an essence' (the brand guarantees quality) departs from dependence; marketing myths are born of this error
  - Legal and social-construction analysis: 'legal person,' 'money,' 'border' are conventionally imputed and fully valid designations—Nagarjuna's frame is more precise than social constructivism: constructed does not mean arbitrary; imputations bind because they depend on conditions
- Related modes: M-NAG-001（缘起归空法——假名是缘起的语言面：依条件施设即依起，依起即无自性）; M-NAG-004（二谛分层法——假名在世俗谛的合法性由二谛框架正式授予）; M-LOC-001（洛克·观念论——远缘对照：洛克把普遍名词判为抽象观念的人造物，龙树把一切名词判为依起假名——同对'共相实在论'开刀，洛克保留观念层面，龙树连观念也施设）; M-WW-001（王维·诗画互证法——远缘对照：王维以'空山不见人'让名相在诗中自我消解，与龙树'但名'的语言观共享'名相不即是实在'的直觉）
- Representative figures:
  - 龙树 / Nagarjuna: 本模式的原型——把'假名'从修辞概念升格为存在论与语言哲学的核心立法 / the prototype—elevated 'designation' from a rhetorical term into the core legislation of ontology and philosophy of language
  - 月称 / Candrakirti: 方法的经典阐释者——《入中论》以车喻七相推求把假名施设法精致化为应成派的招牌 / the classic expositor—the Madhyamakavatara's sevenfold chariot analysis refined designation into the Prasangika hallmark
  - 维特根斯坦 / Ludwig Wittgenstein: 平行对照——后期维特根斯坦的'意义即使用'与假名依约定而生效同构，但维特根斯坦止于语言游戏，龙树把语言游戏接上缘起与空 / the parallel—the later Wittgenstein's 'meaning is use' is isomorphic with designation functioning by convention, but Wittgenstein stops at language games while Nagarjuna plugs them into dependent origination and emptiness

## M-NAG-004 二谛分层法 / Two-Truths Stratification Method

- Definition: 认识论方法论/真值分层为世俗谛与究竟谛，两层不混不裂——究竟须借世俗施设方可教授，世俗不因究竟空性而失效；冲突多为层级错位而非事实之争
- Definition (EN): Epistemological methodology/truth stratified into conventional and ultimate, neither merged nor split—the ultimate is teachable only through conventional imputation, and the conventional is not voided by ultimate emptiness; conflicts are mostly level-mismatch, not factual dispute
- Source: 《中论》第24品观四谛第8-10颂（'诸佛依二谛，为众生说法'，'不依世俗谛，不得第一义'）；《大智度论》论二谛方便
- Key quote: 诸佛依二谛，为众生说法：一以世俗谛，二第一义谛。若人不能知，分别于二谛，则于深佛法，不知真实义。 / The Buddhas teach the Dharma relying on two truths: the conventional truth of the world and the ultimate truth. One who does not know how to distinguish them cannot know the profound reality in the Buddha's teaching.
- Process:
  - 识别争论双方各自站在哪一层：把每个命题标记为世俗谛操作（约定、实践、制度）或究竟谛操作（自性审查）
  - 把'空与有之争'重写为层级错位：多数冲突是一方用究竟谛否证世俗命题、或另一方用世俗直觉抵御究竟审查——先纠层级再论内容
  - 执行借俗显真：通往究竟的任何进路必须用世俗材料搭建（语言、概念、修行次第），并承认这些材料本身无自性
  - 执行俗不废真：宣布究竟结论后逐项检查世俗义务是否仍有效——被取消的只是实体化执取，不是义务、因果与责任本身
- Process (EN):
  - Identify which level each side stands on: mark every proposition as conventional operation (agreement, practice, institution) or ultimate operation (intrinsic-nature scrutiny)
  - Rewrite the 'emptiness vs. being' dispute as level-mismatch: most conflicts are one side refuting conventional propositions with the ultimate, or the other resisting ultimate scrutiny with worldly intuition—correct the level before debating content
  - Apply the ladder rule: any route to the ultimate must be built of conventional material (language, concepts, stages of practice), and that material itself acknowledged as lacking intrinsic nature
  - Apply the non-voiding rule: after declaring an ultimate conclusion, check item by item that worldly obligations still hold—only reifying clinging is cancelled, not obligation, causation or responsibility itself
- Representative cases:
  - 《中论》第24品的总答辩：对手说'空则坏四谛'，龙树答'恰恰是空才让四谛成立'——冲突被重写为层级错位，论辩由此翻盘
  - 佛陀的日常言说：佛亦说'我'、说'众生'、说'取'——究竟证悟者仍完整使用世俗语言，是'俗不废真'的原始示范
  - 月称分世俗谛为'正世俗/倒世俗'（眼见二月是倒）——二谛框架内部的再立法，显示此法的可扩展性
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The grand defense of chapter 24: the opponent says emptiness destroys the four truths; Nagarjuna answers that only emptiness lets the four truths stand—the dispute is rewritten as level-mismatch and the debate turns
  - The Buddha's everyday speech: the Buddha too says 'I,' 'beings,' 'grasping'—the fully awakened one uses conventional language without remainder, the primal demonstration of non-voiding
  - Candrakirti divides conventional truth into the correct and the mistaken (the moon seen double is mistaken)—re-legislation inside the frame, showing its extensibility
- Modern applications:
  - 科学主义与人文价值之争的仲裁：'物理学没有自由意志'（究竟层操作）与'我选择了道歉'（世俗层操作）各居其层——二谛分层法是不取消任何一层的标准工具
  - 组织双重标准治理：公司章程（世俗谛）与'章程皆为临时约定'（究竟谛）并行——既维护规则效力，又防止规则被实体化为永恒教条
  - 心理咨询与哲学咨询：来访者的'我是个失败者'在世俗层是有效叙事、在究竟层是无自性标签——分层处理避免'空谈化解一切'的无效安慰与'同层硬顶'的对立
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Arbitrating scientism vs. humanistic values: 'physics has no free will' (ultimate-level operation) and 'I chose to apologize' (conventional-level operation) each hold at their level—the two truths are the standard tool that voids neither
  - Governance of organizational double standards: the charter (conventional) and 'all charters are provisional agreements' (ultimate) run in parallel—preserving the force of rules while blocking their reification into eternal dogma
  - Psychotherapy and philosophical counseling: the client's 'I am a failure' is a valid conventional narrative and an ultimate-level designation without intrinsic nature—leveling avoids both the empty comfort that dissolves everything and the head-on clash at one level
- Related modes: M-NAG-001（缘起归空法——二谛是空的防误读安全阀：究竟说空、世俗说有）; M-SHA-003（商羯罗·幻相层级法——近缘对照：同为真值分层，商羯罗分三级且究竟层是肯定的'唯一实在'，龙树分两层且究竟层不可作任何肯定陈述——分层数与终点性质是两家分岔）; M-KD-001（康德·现象与物自身——远缘对照：康德的界分是能力界限（我们只能认识现象），龙树的界分是真值界限（世俗与究竟皆可被知）——同为'两层世界'架构）; M-HAY-001（哈耶克·自发秩序法——跨域对照：哈耶克区分'设计的秩序'与'生成的秩序'并拒绝把后者还原为前者，与'拒绝用究竟谛取消世俗谛'共享'层级不可互相还原'的信条）
- Representative figures:
  - 龙树 / Nagarjuna: 本模式的原型——在《中论》第24品为二谛立法并给出'不依世俗谛不得第一义'的操作规则 / the prototype—legislated the two truths in chapter 24 and gave the operating rule 'without the conventional the ultimate cannot be taught'
  - 月称 / Candrakirti: 方法的精化者——把世俗谛再分正倒两层，处理'错乱认知'在世俗层的位置 / the refiner—subdivided the conventional into correct and mistaken, locating erroneous cognition within the conventional level
  - 康德 / Immanuel Kant: 平行对照——现象与物自身的两层架构与二谛同构，但康德以人类认识能力划界，龙树以真值与依起划界 / the parallel—phenomenon and thing-in-itself is structurally isomorphic with the two truths, but Kant bounds by the limits of human cognition while Nagarjuna bounds by truth-value and dependence

## M-NAG-005 一体异体破法 / Identity-Difference Dissolution Method

- Definition: 概念分析方法/对任何相依对偶逐一检验'是一'与'是异'并证明皆不成——同则失分位、异则失依存，拆除关系背后的实体承担者而不取消关系本身
- Definition (EN): Conceptual-analysis methodology/test 'identical' and 'different' on any dependent pair and show both fail—identity destroys the distinction, difference destroys the dependence; remove the substantial bearer behind the relation without cancelling the relation itself
- Source: 《中论》第10品观燃可燃、第2品观去来、第14品观和合、第18品观我法（我与蕴一异俱不成）、第20品观因果
- Key quote: 若燃是可燃，作作者则一；若燃异可燃，离可燃有燃。 / If the burning is the combustible, agent and act are one; if the burning is different from the combustible, it would burn apart from fuel.
- Process:
  - 识别对象中的相依对偶结构：能-所、整体-部分、主体-属性、因-果——列出对偶双方
  - 检验'是一'：若双方同一，其分位与功能是否消失？——通常消失（主体与对象混一，结构瓦解），故'是一'不成
  - 检验'是异'：若双方彻底相异而无关，依存关系是否还能成立？——通常不成立（离彼则此无用），故'是异'不成
  - 拆除实体化：宣告'关系真实、实体承担者不成立'——在世俗层保留关系运作，在究竟层拒绝将其读作两个或一个自性物
- Process (EN):
  - Identify the dependent pair in the object: agent-patient, whole-part, subject-property, cause-effect—name both sides
  - Test identity: if the two are one, do their stations and functions vanish?—usually yes (agent and patient merge, structure collapses), so identity fails
  - Test difference: if the two are utterly unrelated, can the dependence still hold?—usually not (apart from the other, this is idle), so difference fails
  - Remove the reification: declare 'the relation is real, the substantial bearer is not established'—keep the relation working conventionally while refusing, ultimately, to read it as two or one intrinsically-natured things
- Representative cases:
  - 观燃可燃品：燃者与可燃柴'一异俱不成'——全书最完整的一异检验示范，后世以'刀与斫、火与薪'反复重演
  - 观我法品：'我'与五蕴一异俱不成——佛教无我论证的标准形态，被《入中论》七相推求精致化
  - 观去来品：去者与去法一异俱破——对'动作主体'的实体性直觉的第一课，运动学上'现在去'不可得的著名分析
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The examination of fire and fuel: burner and combustible are neither identical nor different—the fullest demonstration of the test, replayed ever after as 'knife and cutting, flame and firewood'
  - The examination of self and aggregates: the self is neither identical with nor different from the five aggregates—the standard form of the Buddhist no-self argument, refined into Candrakirti's sevenfold analysis
  - The examination of going: the mover and the going are both dissolved—the first lesson on the substantiality of 'agent of an action,' with the famous analysis that 'present going' cannot be found
- Modern applications:
  - 组织架构实体化诊断：'部门'与'人'、'岗位'与'能力'常被实体化为自性物——一异检验可拆解'市场部（异于个人）'与'市场部即张三（同一）'两种执，回到关系性安立
  - 产品一体化 vs 拆分的战略检验：'软硬一体'（一）与'彻底解耦'（异）两极都不成立时，说明应回到依存关系的具体条件设计——封闭生态与彻底开放是同一刀下的不同安立
  - 心理边界咨询：'我与我的情绪是一'（被情绪吞没）与'我与情绪完全相异'（压抑隔离）两俱不成——健康的安立是'情绪依条件而起、与我依存相关而非同一'，龙树框架给出中道的表述
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Diagnosing organizational reification: 'department' and 'person,' 'role' and 'competence' get reified into intrinsically-natured things—the one/different test dissolves both 'marketing is other than individuals' and 'marketing is Zhang San,' returning to relational establishment
  - Strategic testing of integration vs. unbundling: when 'integrated hardware-software' (one) and 'total decoupling' (different) both fail, the answer lies in designing the concrete conditions of dependence—closed ecosystem and total openness are different establishments under the same blade
  - Boundary counseling: 'I am my emotion' (being swallowed) and 'my emotion is wholly other' (suppression) both fail—the healthy establishment is 'emotion arises depending on conditions, related to me by dependence yet not identical'; Nagarjuna's frame gives the middle-way formulation
- Related modes: M-NAG-002（四句破法——一异破是四句在对偶场景的专门化：对偶天然只容两角）; M-NAG-001（缘起归空法——一异俱不成正是'依起无自性'的对偶形态：关系依起而立，故无实体承担者）; M-SHA-002（商羯罗·梵我同一法——反向来向对照：商羯罗把'我与梵'判为一（同一），龙树把'我与蕴'判为不一不异——同一命题上'判一'与'破一异'的正面对照）; M-IBR-001（伊本·路世德·双真法——远缘对照：伊本·路世德以'哲学与宗教双真'化解一与多的张力，龙树以'不一不异'拆除张力双方的实体化——两种'不站极端'的技术路线）
- Representative figures:
  - 龙树 / Nagarjuna: 本模式的原型——以燃可燃、去来、我与蕴等章节把一异检验立法为中观的标准刀法 / the prototype—legislated the one/different test as the Madhyamaka standard blade through the chapters on fire and fuel, going, and self and aggregates
  - 月称 / Candrakirti: 方法的精致者——七相推求（一、异、能依、所依、具有、积聚、形状）把二角检验扩展为七角 / the refiner—the sevenfold analysis (identity, difference, basis, dependent, possession, aggregation, shape) extends the two-corner test to seven
  - 柏拉图 / Plato: 反向对照——《巴曼尼得斯篇》同样以一异难题自我拷问相论，但柏拉图以分有说救相，龙树以一异俱破拆实体 / the counterpoint—the Parmenides likewise torments the theory of Forms with the one/many puzzle, but Plato saves the Forms by participation while Nagarjuna dissolves substance with both corners failing

## M-NAG-006 无生论证法 / No-Arising Argument Method

- Definition: 因果批判方法论/审查'生起'本身：自生、他生、共生、无因生四门皆闭——结论是无自性物之'生'，生起只是依起关系的世俗描述
- Definition (EN): Causal-critique methodology/submit arising itself to scrutiny: from self, other, both, or causeless—four gates all shut; the conclusion is no arising of intrinsic things, arising being the conventional description of dependence
- Source: 《中论》第1品观因缘（'诸法不自生，亦不从他生，不共不无因'）；《七十空性论》论生住灭；《十二门论》观生门
- Key quote: 诸法不自生，亦不从他生，不共不无因，是故知无生。 / Things do not arise from themselves, nor from another, nor from both, nor without cause—therefore know that there is no arising.
- Process:
  - 把'X 产生于 Y'的因果主张分解为四种可能结构：X 本在 Y 中（自生）、X 与 Y 自性相异（他生）、两者结合（共生）、无需因（无因生）
  - 逐门施加归谬：自生则生无所生且再生无穷；他生则自性相异即无关，一切应生一切；共生则两过并具；无因则因果与修行皆溃
  - 宣告四门俱闭后的结论：'无生'——不是否认事件发生，而是否认'自性物被产生'这一本体论解读；生起重新安立为依起关系的世俗描述
  - 与二谛配套：在世俗谛保留'生'的全部日常用法（生育、制造、发生），在究竟层注明其无自性——防止'无生'被读作虚无主义
- Process (EN):
  - Decompose the causal claim 'X arises from Y' into four possible structures: X pre-exists in Y (self), X differs in intrinsic nature from Y (other), both combined, no cause needed
  - Run reductio at each gate: self-arising produces nothing and regresses infinitely; other-arising makes unrelatedness entail everything-arising-from-anything; both fails doubly; causeless collapses causation and practice
  - Declare the conclusion with all gates shut: no arising—not a denial that events occur but a denial of the ontological reading 'an intrinsic thing is produced'; arising is re-established as the conventional description of dependence
  - Pair with the two truths: keep every everyday use of 'arising' conventionally (birth, manufacture, occurrence) while marking its lack of intrinsic nature ultimately—so 'no arising' is not read as nihilism
- Representative cases:
  - 《中论》第一品的开门之作：对数论'自生'的'已有再生则无穷'归谬——用一个反问摧毁印度最精致的世代自性论
  - 对他生的'一切生一切'归谬：若自性相异者可以互生，则稻种应生豆芽、黑暗亦应生光明——归谬推理的教科书示范
  - 佛护与清辨的用法之争：同一组四门论证，佛护只用归谬（应成），清辨要求自立量（自续）——方法论争论本身成为中观思想史的主干线索
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The opening engagement of chapter 1: the regress reductio against the Samkhya 'self-arising'—one question dismantles India's most refined theory of pre-existence
  - The 'anything from anything' reductio against other-arising: if difference in intrinsic nature permits production, rice seeds should sprout mung beans and darkness produce light—a textbook demonstration
  - The Buddhapalita-Bhavaviveka dispute over usage: the same four gates, used as pure reductio (Prasangika) or recast in autonomous syllogism (Svatantrika)—the methodological dispute became a main line in the history of Madhyamaka
- Modern applications:
  - 创业'从0到1'话语审查：产品不是'无中生有'（无因生）、不是'创始人天赋自带'（自生）、不是'市场机会决定一切'（他生）——四门审查逼出'依起'的真话：成功依团队、时机、资本、技术条件的合和
  - 科学发现的哲学表述：新理论不是从旧理论'他生'（范式不可通约时归谬即触发）、也不是'自生'（观察渗透理论）——把'发现'安立为依起事件，消解'天才史观'与'纯累积史观'的两极
  - 因果推断的方法论自省：统计学因果推断永远只能给出依起关系（条件概率改变）而非'产生'——龙树四门是'相关非因果'直觉的形上学表述
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Auditing the 'zero-to-one' startup discourse: products do not arise causelessly, are not born of founder genius (self), are not dictated by market opportunity alone (other)—the four gates force the truth of dependence: success arises from the convergence of team, timing, capital and technology
  - Philosophical formulation of scientific discovery: new theories neither arise 'from another' (the incommensurability reductio fires) nor 'from themselves' (observation is theory-laden)—establishing discovery as a dependence event dissolves both the genius view and the pure-accumulation view
  - Methodological self-audit of causal inference: statistical causality ever yields only dependence relations (changes in conditional probability), never 'production'—Nagarjuna's four gates are the metaphysical formulation of 'correlation is not causation'
- Related modes: M-NAG-001（缘起归空法——无生论证是缘起归空的因果面：四门俱闭反证唯有依起）; M-NAG-002（四句破法——四门生是四句框架在'生起'问题上的专门化穷举）; M-AQU-001（阿奎那·五路证明法——反向来向对照：阿奎那从因果链回溯第一因，龙树审查因果链每一环的'生'而四门俱闭——同以因果为题，一个立第一因，一个拆生起本身）; M-UM-001（休谟·因果怀疑法——近缘对照：休谟拆因果的必然性而止于习惯性期待，龙树四门俱闭后把因果重新安立在世俗谛——拆解后'立什么'是两家分岔）
- Representative figures:
  - 龙树 / Nagarjuna: 本模式的原型——《中论》开篇即以四门审查立法'无生'为中观的第一辩题 / the prototype—opened the Mulamadhyamakakarika by legislating 'no arising' through the four-gate audit as Madhyamaka's first thesis
  - 佛护 / Buddhapalita: 归谬用法的大师——只用归谬不立自宗，被月称尊为应成派的真正立脚点 / the master of reductio usage—refuting without establishing his own position, honored by Candrakirti as the true footing of the Prasangika
  - 清辨 / Bhavaviveka: 自续用法的大师——把四门重构为自立量三支论式，触发千年方法论之争并使论证标准更严 / the master of autonomous usage—recasting the four gates in independent syllogism, triggering the millennium dispute and sharpening the standards of argument

## M-NAG-007 中道行持法 / Middle-Way Practice Method

- Definition: 实践方法论/中道不是两极之间的折中点，而是对两极共同预设（自性见）的放弃——看出两极同犯一错，两极同时脱落，行持落在依起上
- Definition (EN): Practical methodology/the middle way is not a compromise point between extremes but the abandonment of their shared presupposition (the view of intrinsic nature)—seeing both poles commit the same error, both fall away, and practice lands on dependence
- Source: 《中论》第1品归敬颂（'离有无二边'）、第15品观有无、第24品观四谛（'亦是中道义'）、第26品观十二因缘
- Key quote: 不生亦不灭，不常亦不断，不一亦不异，不来亦不出——能说是因缘，善灭诸戏论，我稽首礼佛。 / Neither arising nor ceasing, neither permanent nor annulled, neither one nor different, neither coming nor going—such is dependent origination, the appeasement of all proliferation; to that Buddha I bow.
- Process:
  - 把眼前的立场冲突表述为一对极端：常/断、一/异、来/去、决定/随机——确认两极的表述方式
  - 寻找两极的共同预设：两极共享的那个未审查前提是什么？（通常是'存在自性物'或'存在唯一正确的本体描述'）
  - 放弃共同预设而非选边：宣告两极在'有自性'坐标上同时不成立，把行动安立在依起关系上——既非常亦非断的新描述
  - 转入行持：在规范上（依缘施设而有效的伦理）、行动上（条件合和中的参与项）、修持上（还灭链的逐环松动）落实'依起'姿态
- Process (EN):
  - Formulate the conflict as a pair of extremes: eternal/annihilated, one/different, coming/going, determined/random—state both poles' terms
  - Hunt the shared presupposition: what unexamined premise do both poles share? (usually 'an intrinsically-natured thing exists' or 'one correct ontological description exists')
  - Abandon the presupposition, not one pole: declare both poles untenable on the 'intrinsic nature' axis and establish action on dependence—a description neither eternal nor annihilated
  - Carry into practice: in norms (ethics valid as conditioned designation), in action (a participating condition within a convergence), in cultivation (loosening the ceasing chain link by link)—the stance of dependence
- Representative cases:
  - 《中论》归敬颂的'八不'总纲：不生不灭、不常不断、不一不异、不来不去——全书立场的浓缩宣言，中道行持的纲领文本
  - 观十二因缘品：无明灭则行灭……生灭则老死灭——把中道接到还灭实践：既然是依起链而非宿命，逐环可松动
  - 对'恶取空'的预防性警告：'不能正观空者，钝根则自害'——中道行持的反面教材：把空当作放弃一切行动的理由即堕断边
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The eight negations of the homage verse: no arising, no ceasing, no permanence, no annihilation, no identity, no difference, no coming, no going—the condensed manifesto and program text of middle-way practice
  - The examination of the twelve links: with ignorance ceased, formations cease ... with birth ceased, aging-and-death cease—the middle way wired into practice: being a dependence chain and not fate, every link can be loosened
  - The preventive warning against 'wicked emptiness': 'who observes emptiness wrongly, the dull destroy themselves'—the negative exhibit of the method: taking emptiness as grounds to abandon all action falls into annihilationism
- Modern applications:
  - 政策设计的'第三轴'思考：计划经济/自由市场、集权/分权等两极争论，先找共同预设（'存在唯一正确的制度本体'），放弃后转向条件设计——哈耶克与科斯的方案在龙树框架里都是'依起安立'的样本
  - 冲突调解的中道技术：把'你错我对'表述为两极，引导双方看见共同预设（如'零和'假设）——放弃预设而非各让一步，和解因此不是折中而是升维
  - 个人决策的极端审计：'全押'与'不动'两极共同预设'机会是自性物'（要么必成要么永失）——看出依起（条件可造、后果可分批承担），行动落在中间动态而非端点
- Modern applications (EN):
  - The 'third axis' in policy design: in plan-versus-market, centralization-versus-devolution, first find the shared presupposition ('one correct institutional ontology exists'); abandoning it turns attention to condition-design—Hayek's and Coase's schemes are both samples of 'establishing on dependence' in Nagarjuna's frame
  - The middle-way technique in mediation: formulate 'you are wrong, I am right' as poles and guide both sides to see the shared presupposition (say, a zero-sum assumption)—abandoning the presupposition rather than each conceding a step, settlement becomes dimensional, not a split of differences
  - Extreme auditing in personal decisions: 'all-in' and 'hold still' share the presupposition that 'the opportunity is an intrinsic thing' (either bound to succeed or lost forever)—seeing dependence (conditions can be made, costs can be staged), action lands in the dynamic middle, not an endpoint
- Related modes: M-NAG-001（缘起归空法——中道的正面表述：依起即中道，空假名即是中道义）; M-NAG-002（四句破法——中道的辩证面：四句俱遣之后所行即是中道）; M-AR-001（亚里士多德·中庸法——近缘对照：亚氏中道在同一尺度上取适度值，龙树中道拆除尺度本身——'取中间值'与'弃坐标轴'是两种中道的分岔）; M-NER-008（聂鲁达·合唱总谱法——跨域对照：聂鲁达在个人抒情与集体政治两极间拒绝选边而以'合唱总谱'兼收两者，与'放弃预设而非选边'共享超越两极的姿态）
- Representative figures:
  - 龙树 / Nagarjuna: 本模式的原型——以八不归敬颂立法'中道即缘起'，学派因此得名中观 / the prototype—legislated 'the middle way is dependent origination' in the eight-negation homage verse, giving the school its name Madhyamaka
  - 释迦牟尼 / the Buddha: 教义源头——初转法轮'离苦乐二边'的中道原初表述，龙树把它从伦理升格为本体论 / the source of the doctrine—the Buddha's original 'leaving both extremes of indulgence and mortification,' which Nagarjuna elevated from ethics to ontology
  - 亚里士多德 / Aristotle: 对照系——中庸的适度值方案与中观的弃坐标方案并行于两大文明，是'两种中道'的经典对位 / the contrast system—the Aristotelian mean and the Madhyamaka abandonment-of-axis run in parallel across two civilizations, the classic counterpoint of 'two middle ways'

## M-NAG-008 空亦复空法 / Emptiness-of-Emptiness Method

- Definition: 元方法论/把方法论自身结论再次作为审查对象：空依执有而立，执有既去空亦不留——'若复见有空，诸佛所不化'：体系必须吞下自身方为自洽
- Definition (EN): Metamethodology/make the methodology's own conclusion the next object of scrutiny: emptiness stands on the clinging to being and departs when the clinging departs—'one who then sees emptiness as a view is beyond healing': a system is consistent only when it swallows itself
- Source: 《中论》第13品观行（'大圣说空法，为离诸见故'）、第24品观四谛（'实无不空法，何得有空法'）、第18品观我法（'有无既无故，戏论息'）
- Key quote: 大圣说空法，为离诸见故；若复见有空，诸佛所不化。 / The great sage taught emptiness to free us from all views; one who then sees emptiness as a view is beyond the healing of the Buddhas.
- Process:
  - 识别体系的顶层概念：本方法依赖的最核心、最不可让渡的那个词是什么？（如'空'、'理性'、'效率'）
  - 审查顶层概念的施设结构：它依什么对立面或什么语境而立？——找到'空'依'不空'式的对称项
  - 执行自噬：证明当体系的目标达成（执有既去、问题已解），顶层概念失去对立面而无指称——必须随境况一起放下
  - 建立弃用条款：把'此概念用毕即弃'写进体系自身（如维特根斯坦的撤梯比喻），并设计对'二阶执取'（把弃用也执为真理）的持续提醒机制
- Process (EN):
  - Identify the system's top-level concept: the most central, least negotiable word on which the method rests (say, 'emptiness,' 'reason,' 'efficiency')
  - Audit the concept's imputation structure: on what opposite or context does it stand?—find the symmetrical term ('emptiness' on 'non-empty')
  - Execute the self-consumption: show that when the system's goal is achieved (clinging gone, problem solved), the top-level concept loses its opposite and its referent—it must be laid down with the situation
  - Install a sunset clause: write 'this concept is discarded after use' into the system itself (as Wittgenstein's ladder), with a standing reminder mechanism against second-order clinging (clinging to the discarding itself)
- Representative cases:
  - 《中论》第13品'空如对治药'的立法：空为破执而设，执破则空不当留——'若复见有空，诸佛所不化'成为整个大乘传统的自检格言
  - 第24品的逻辑表述：'实无不空法，何得有空法'——'空'因失去对立面而成为无指称假名，自否定获得严格论证形式
  - 禅宗'逢佛杀佛'与佛陀筏喻（法尚应舍，何况非法）：筏喻是此法的原始版本，禅宗将其推向戏剧化极致
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The chapter-13 legislation of emptiness as remedy: emptiness is set up to break clinging and must not remain once clinging breaks—'one who sees emptiness as a view is beyond the Buddhas' healing' became the self-audit maxim of the whole Mahayana
  - The chapter-24 logical formulation: 'since nothing non-empty exists, how could emptiness exist?'—emptiness becomes a referentless designation for losing its opposite, the self-negation given strict argumentative form
  - The Chan 'meet the Buddha and kill the Buddha' and the Buddha's raft simile (the Dharma itself is to be let go, let alone what is not-Dharma)—the primal version, dramatized to the extreme by Chan
- Modern applications:
  - 组织变革的'工具退役'制度：数字化转型、OKR、敏捷都是'对治药'——制度上写明退役条件（如'OKR 成熟即转入常规管理'），防止工具本身成为新官僚教条
  - 科学方法论的自省：'可证伪性'标准自身的适用边界（数学、历史学是否豁免）——波普尔体系需要龙树式自噬：方法论标准不得把自己豁免于审查
  - AI 安全的对齐递归问题：'对齐目标'本身可能被错执（reward hacking 是'把药执为食'的机器版本）——目标用毕即弃、分层递归审查，是'空亦复空'的技术翻译
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Sunset clauses for organizational tools: digital transformation, OKR and agile are all remedies—institutionalize retirement conditions ('once OKR matures, fold into routine management') so tools do not become the new bureaucratic dogma
  - Self-audit of scientific methodology: the applicability of 'falsifiability' itself (do mathematics and history get exemptions?)—Popper's system needs the Nagarjunian swallow: a methodological standard may not exempt itself from scrutiny
  - The recursive problem in AI alignment: alignment objectives can be mis-clung to (reward hacking is the machine version of 'clinging to the medicine as food')—objectives discarded after use, with layered recursive review, is 'emptiness of emptiness' in technical translation
- Related modes: M-NAG-001（缘起归空法——自噬的上游：空依缘起而立，故空亦复空）; M-NAG-002（四句破法——'无宗无过'原则是自噬的论证形态：不立宗则无可被驳）; M01（董仲舒·天人感应法——反向对照：董仲舒把体系锚定在不可质疑的'天'上，龙树拆除一切锚点包括自己的——两种体系自稳策略（锚定 vs 自噬）的经典对照）; M-WZT-010（武则天·无字碑留白法——反向对照：武则天以无字碑拒绝为自己撰写定论、把解释权交给后世，龙树以自噬拆除一切锚点——两种'不为自己立言'的收束策略）

## M-NAG-009 戏论止息法 / Prolificulation-Ceasing Method

- Definition: 认知收敛方法论/概念增殖是痛苦的认知根源，其原动力是执取——止息之道不是少说话而是松动占有欲：执取驱动增殖，增殖巩固执取，双向刹车
- Definition (EN): Cognitive-convergence methodology/conceptual proliferation is the cognitive root of suffering, powered by clinging—the way of ceasing is not fewer words but loosened possessiveness: clinging drives proliferation and proliferation entrenches clinging, a two-way brake
- Source: 《中论》第18品观我法（'有无既无故，戏论息'）、第25品观涅槃（'戏论灭'之定义）、《六十如理论》论戏论与寂灭
- Key quote: 有无既无故，戏论亦息——诸法实相者，心行言语断。 / With being and non-being gone, proliferation ceases—the true nature of things is where thought and speech are cut off.
- Process:
  - 识别增殖状态：检查当前思维是否在少数概念轴（对错、得失、有无）上反复生成立场——增殖的征兆是'新立场总在旧立场上繁殖'
  - 定位驱动源：问'这个概念游戏在为哪种占有欲服务？'——想赢的辩论、怕失去的身份、想固化的利益，戏论背后总有执取
  - 松动执取而非压制概念：对驱动源做处理（承认失去的可能、撤销身份绑定），让增殖失去能源——不与念头搏斗，断其供给
  - 验证止息：增殖停止的标志不是'无观点'而是'观点不再自我繁殖'——仍可言说、仍可行动，但每一句话都是工具而非新阵地
- Process (EN):
  - Recognize proliferation: check whether thought is endlessly spawning positions on a few axes (right/wrong, gain/loss, is/is-not)—the symptom is 'new positions breeding on old ones'
  - Locate the driver: ask 'which possessiveness does this concept game serve?'—a debate to win, an identity defended, an interest to freeze; behind proliferation there is always clinging
  - Loosen the clinging, not the concepts: treat the driver (acknowledge possible loss, unbind identity) so proliferation loses its fuel—do not fight the thoughts, cut the supply
  - Verify cessation: proliferation has stopped not when 'no views remain' but when views no longer breed—one still speaks and acts, but every sentence is a tool, not a new position
- Representative cases:
  - 《中论》第18品的'心行言语断'：诸法实相被定义为概念活动的止息处——全书归谬在治疗学上的总收口
  - 第25品对涅槃的界定：'戏论灭'即寂灭——涅槃不是另一个世界而是增殖停止的这个心，治疗学意义的终极化
  - 归谬的方法学设计：让对手在每一分支撞墙而非直接告知答案——增殖自然息止的'体验设计'，是认知行为疗法'苏格拉底式提问'的千年先声
- Representative cases (EN):

  - Chapter 25's definition of nirvana as 'the ceasing of proliferation'—nirvana is not another world but this very mind with proliferation stopped, the ultimate statement of the therapeutic reading
  - The methodological design of reductio: letting the opponent hit walls at every branch rather than telling the answer—a designed experience of natural cessation, a millennia-early ancestor of Socratic questioning in cognitive therapy
- Modern applications:
  - 信息过载与注意力管理：刷屏、立场站队、无限争论是 prapanca 的当代形态——龙树诊断说'少刷'不是解法，'松动身份绑定'（观点不等于我）才是断供

- Modern applications (EN):
