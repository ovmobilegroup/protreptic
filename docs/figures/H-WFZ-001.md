## 一、历史定位

王夫之（1619-1692），字而农，号姜斋，湖南衡阳人，晚年隐居衡阳石船山著述，世称船山先生，明末清初三大思想家之一。明亡后举兵抗清，失败后终身不仕，遁迹石船山四十余年，著书百余种、四百余卷。哲学上以张载气学为宗，建立'理者气之理''天下惟器'的气一元论，彻底批判程朱'理先气后'；提出'动静皆动''天地之化日新'的动态宇宙观与'趋时更新'的历史进化论；认识论上'能必副其所'，确立中国哲学史上最明确的符合论真理观；知行观上'知行相资以为用'，批判朱熹知先行后与阳明知行合一；伦理上'人欲之各得即天理之大同'，重建理欲关系；历史观上'理势合一''于其变观其通'，为古代历史哲学之巅峰。谭嗣同称'五百年来学者，真通天人之故者，船山一人而已'。

**代表著作**: 《张子正蒙注》（气一元论宗旨所在）、《周易外传》（哲学体系主建筑）、《尚书引义》（认识论与知行论）、《读通鉴论》（三十卷历史哲学巨著）、《宋论》、《读四书大全说》（理欲之辨、理势合一）、《思问录》（内外篇哲学箴言）、《俟解》《噩梦》《黄书》

**籍贯/身份**: Hengyang County, Huguang (now Hengyang, Hunan)；中国（明末清初）；汉族

**人物概述**: 王夫之（1619-1692），明末清初三大思想家之一，字而农，号姜斋，世称船山先生，湖南衡阳人。其思想以'天下惟器'的气一元论、'理势合一'的历史哲学、'能必副其所'的符合论认识论、'知行相资'的知行辩证法、'人欲之各得即天理之大同'的理欲重建为核心，建立中国古代最系统的辩证法与历史哲学体系，被谭嗣同誉为'五百年来真通天人之故者'。

## 三、十大思维模式 (M-WFZ-001 ~ M-WFZ-010)

### M-WFZ-001 理气相即法

- **英文名**: Principle-Qi Unity Method (Li-Qi Xiangji)
- **类别**: 本体论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 1 | **研究稿ID**: M-WAN-001
- **领域**: 气本论/本体论 / Qi Ontology / Metaphysics
- **出处**: 《读四书大全说》卷十、《周易外传》
- **定义**: 理不是悬于气之上的抽象实体，而是气自身运行的条理与秩序。理与气互为体用，气是理的载体，理是气的法则——二者不可分割，理不离气，气不能无理。此法从根本上否定了朱熹'理在气先'的超越化理本论，确立理气一元的一体观。'理即是气之理，气当得如此便是理，理不先而气不后。'
- **定义(EN)**: Principle (li) is not an abstract entity suspended above qi, but the internal order and rhythm of qi's own operations. Principle and qi are mutually constitutive: qi is the substrate of principle, principle is the law of qi. They cannot be separated—there is no principle apart from qi, and qi cannot exist without principle. This method fundamentally rejects Zhu Xi's 'principle precedes qi' transcendent metaphysics, establishing a monistic li-qi unity. 'Principle is simply the principle of qi; when qi ought to be thus, that is principle; principle does not precede and qi does not follow.'
- **关键概念**: 理在气中、气外无理、理气相即、理不先气不后、气本论
- **原文依据**: 尽天地之间，无不是气，即无不是理也。理即是气之理，气当得如此便是理，理不先而气不后。
  > Between heaven and earth, nothing is not qi, and therefore nothing is not principle. Principle is simply qi's principle; when qi ought to be thus, that is principle.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 识别现象背后的气的运行方式——不是追问'抽象之理'，而是观察'具体之气'
  2. 从气的条理中发现理——理不在气外，而在气自身秩序之中
  3. 以理气相即的框架批判'理先气后''离气言理'的超越化倾向
  4. 在理气统一的框架内理解事物的结构、功能与变化
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Identify the operational mode of qi behind phenomena—inquire not about 'abstract principle' but observe 'concrete qi'
  2. Discover principle within qi's order—principle is not outside qi but in qi's own structure
  3. Critique transcendent tendencies ('principle before qi', 'principle apart from qi') through the li-qi unity framework
  4. Understand the structure, function, and change within the integrated li-qi framework
- **代表性案例**:
  - 船山批判朱熹'理在气先'：若理在气先，则理成为'悬隔'的抽象实体，无法解释具体事物的生成——理必须内在于气方能起作用
  - 船山论'太和'：太和不是超越的理，而是气的最充实状态；理即在气的太和之中显现
  - 反例警示：若将理理解为独立于气的超验法则（如柏拉图式的理念），则理成为'悬宕的形而上学物'，无法指导具体实践
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Chuanshan criticizes Zhu Xi's 'principle before qi': if principle precedes qi, it becomes an abstract entity suspended in transcendence, unable to explain the generation of concrete things—principle must be immanent in qi to be operative
  - Chuanshan on 'Taihe' (Great Harmony): Taihe is not a transcendent principle but the fullest state of qi; principle manifests within qi's Taihe
  - Counterexample: treating principle as a transcendent law independent of qi (like Platonic Forms) makes it a 'metaphysical dangler' that cannot guide concrete practice
- **当代应用**:
  - 制度设计：制度（理）必须贴合人性现实（气），不能空谈理想
  - 系统思维：结构（理）内在于系统（气），不可割裂
  - 教育改革：教育方法（理）必须基于学习者实际（气）
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Institutional design: institutions (li) must align with human reality (qi), not abstract ideals
  - Systems thinking: structure (li) is immanent in the system (qi), not separable
  - Education reform: pedagogical methods (li) must be grounded in learners' actual conditions (qi)
- **关联模式**: M-WFZ-002、M-WFZ-006、M-WFZ-008

### M-WFZ-002 天下惟器法

- **英文名**: Only Concrete Things Exist Method (Tianxia Wei Qi)
- **类别**: 存在论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 2 | **研究稿ID**: M-WAN-002
- **领域**: 道器论/反先验论 / Dao-Qi Theory / Anti-Transcendentalism
- **出处**: 《周易外传》卷五
- **定义**: 天下万物皆为具体之'器'，道不能脱离器而独立存在。'无其器则无其道'——先有具体的酒杯，才有'饮酒之道'；先有具体的车马，才有'驾车之道'。形而上不是'无形之上'，而是'已有形之后'的抽象层面。此法是对佛老'道在虚/寂'与理学'道在气先'的双重否定，确立了以具体存在为本原的唯物论立场。
- **定义(EN)**: All things in the world are concrete 'qi' (vessels/containers); dao (the Way) cannot exist independently apart from qi. 'Without the vessel, there is no way'—first there must be a specific wine cup before there is the 'way of drinking wine'; first there must be specific carriages before there is the 'way of driving'. The metaphysical is not 'above the formless' but the abstract dimension that comes after concrete forms exist. This method is a double negation of Buddhist-Daoist 'dao in emptiness/silence' and Neo-Confucian 'dao prior to qi', establishing a materialist position that takes concrete existence as primary.
- **关键概念**: 天下惟器、无其器则无其道、道器合一、据器而道存、形而上非无形
- **原文依据**: 天下惟器而已矣。道者器之道，器者不可谓之道之器也。无其器则无其道。
  > All under heaven consists only of concrete vessels. Dao is the dao of vessels; vessels cannot be called the vessels of dao. Without the vessel, there is no dao.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 从具体器物/现象出发，而非从抽象概念出发
  2. 追问：这个'道'依附于什么具体的'器'？没有这个器，道是否存在？
  3. 在实践中发现道的运作——道在器中起用，而非在器外悬设
  4. 拒绝脱离具体情境的空谈玄理
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Start from concrete objects/phenomena, not from abstract concepts
  2. Ask: what specific 'qi' does this 'dao' depend upon? Without this qi, does dao exist?
  3. Discover dao's operations in practice—dao functions within qi, not suspended outside it
  4. Refuse to speculate on empty metaphysics detached from concrete situations
- **代表性案例**:
  - 船山论'形而上'：形而上不是无形之物，而是'已有形之后'的抽象层面——先有具体的车马，才能抽象出'驾车之道'
  - 批判佛老：佛家说'道在寂'，道家说'道在虚'，皆将道理解为脱离具体存在的抽象本体——船山指出这是'标离器之名以自神'
  - 方法论意义：在解决问题时，必须先找到'器'（具体的条件、工具、对象），才能谈论'道'（方法、规律）
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Chuanshan on 'the metaphysical': it is not something formless, but the abstract dimension that comes after forms exist—first there must be concrete carriages before one can abstract the 'way of driving'
  - Critique of Buddhism-Daoism: Buddhists say 'dao is in silence,' Daoists say 'dao is in emptiness'—both reduce dao to an abstract entity detached from concrete existence; Chuanshan points out this is 'naming separation from vessel to elevate oneself as divine'
  - Methodological implication: when solving problems, one must first identify the 'vessel' (concrete conditions, tools, objects) before discussing the 'dao' (methods, principles)
- **当代应用**:
  - 产品开发：从用户具体需求（器）出发，而非从抽象愿景出发
  - 政策制定：政策（道）必须基于具体国情社情（器），不能照搬他国模式
  - 工程设计：方法论必须依托具体工具与条件，不可空中楼阁
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Product development: start from users' concrete needs (qi), not abstract visions
  - Policy making: policy (dao) must be grounded in concrete national/social conditions (qi), not copied blindly from other countries
  - Engineering design: methodology must be anchored in concrete tools and conditions, not built in the air
- **关联模式**: M-WFZ-001、M-WFZ-005、M-WFZ-009

### M-WFZ-003 日新之化法

- **英文名**: Daily Renewal Method (Rixin Zhihua)
- **类别**: 变化论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 3 | **研究稿ID**: M-WAN-003
- **领域**: 变化观/发展论 / Theory of Change / Development
- **出处**: 《思问录·外篇》
- **定义**: 天地万物处于永恒的更新之中，今日非昨日，今年非去年。变化不是简单的循环，而是真正的'日新'——新故相推，日生不滞。人的身心亦然，每日皆有新我。此法从根本上否定了'复古退化'的历史观，也为变革与创新提供了本体论依据：新本身就是宇宙的法则，守旧即是逆势。
- **定义(EN)**: All things in heaven and earth are in constant renewal; today is not yesterday, this year is not last year. Change is not mere circular repetition but genuine 'daily newness'—the old and new push each other forward, day after day without stagnation. Human body and mind are the same: each day brings a new self. This method fundamentally negates the 'restore antiquity, degenerate' view of history, and provides ontological grounding for reform and innovation: newness itself is the universe's law, and conservatism runs against the current.
- **关键概念**: 天地之化日新、新故相推、日生不滞、日新之命、变化无常
- **原文依据**: 天地之化日新。今日之风雷非昨日之风雷，是以知今日之日月非昨日之日月也。
  > The transformation of heaven and earth renews daily. Today's wind and thunder are not yesterday's wind and thunder; hence we know today's sun and moon are not yesterday's sun and moon.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 识别当前事物的'新故关系'：哪些是正在消亡的旧事物，哪些是正在生长的新事物
  2. 顺应'日新'之势，主动迎接变化而非抗拒
  3. 在变化中把握'新故相推'的节奏：不断淘汰过时之物，培育新生之力
  4. 将变化视为常态，以开放心态面对不确定性
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Identify the 'old-new relationship' of current phenomena: what is dying, what is growing
  2. Go with the flow of 'daily renewal,' actively embracing change rather than resisting it
  3. Grasp the rhythm of 'old giving way to new': eliminate the obsolete, cultivate the emergent
  4. Treat change as the norm and face uncertainty with openness
- **代表性案例**:
  - 船山批判复古论：夏后之法不行于商周，成周之规不施于后世——每个时代有每个时代的'日新之化'，泥古薄今是违背天道
  - 个体层面：耳目日生视、耳日生听、心日生思——人的认知能力每天都在更新，昨日之知不足以解今日之问
  - 反例警示：固守旧模式拒绝变化者，如同刻舟求剑，终将随波逐流而无所适从
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Chuanshan critiques antiquarianism: Xia's institutions could not govern Shang and Zhou; Chengzhou's models could not be applied to later generations—each era has its own 'daily renewal,' and clinging to the past defies the Way of Heaven
  - Individual level: eyes see fresh daily, ears hear fresh daily, mind thinks fresh daily—yesterday's knowledge cannot solve today's questions
  - Counterexample: those who cling to old models and refuse change are like the man who marks his boat to find his lost sword—lost to the current with no anchor
- **当代应用**:
  - 企业转型：主动迭代而非等待危机倒逼
  - 个人成长：终身学习，每日精进
  - 技术创新：拥抱不确定性，持续试错
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Corporate transformation: proactive iteration rather than waiting for crisis to force change
  - Personal growth: lifelong learning, daily improvement
  - Technological innovation: embrace uncertainty, continuous experimentation
- **关联模式**: M-WFZ-004、M-WFZ-007、M-WFZ-010

### M-WFZ-004 继善成性法

- **英文名**: Continue-Good-to-Cultivate-Nature Method (Jishan Chengxing)
- **类别**: 人性论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 4 | **研究稿ID**: M-WAN-004
- **领域**: 人性生成论/发展心理学 / Human Nature Formation / Developmental Psychology
- **出处**: 《周易外传·系辞上》《尚书引义·太甲二》
- **定义**: 人性不是先天固定、一成不变的实体，而是在持续实践中日日生成的过程。天赋之善只是潜能（善端），必须通过后天的'继'——即不断的学习、实践、修养——才能将潜能变为现实的人性。'不成未有性，不继不能成'——没有持续的努力，人性就无法完成。此法既反对告子的'生之谓性'（纯生物本能），也反对理学'天命之性'的静态先验观。
- **定义(EN)**: Human nature is not a fixed, unchanging entity given at birth, but a process continuously formed through ongoing practice. Innate goodness is merely potential (the sprout of good); it must be realized through postnatal 'continuation'—that is, continuous learning, practice, and cultivation. 'Without completion, there is no nature; without continuation, completion is impossible'—without sustained effort, human nature cannot be fulfilled. This method opposes both Gaozi's 'nature is what is born with' (pure biological instinct) and the Neo-Confucian view of 'heavenly-mandated nature' as a static a priori given.
- **关键概念**: 性日生日成、继善成性、习与性成、动态人性论、后天养成
- **原文依据**: 性者，生理也，日生则日成也。天命者，岂但初生之顷命之哉！
  > Nature is the principle of life; it is formed day by day. Can the heavenly mandate be only at the moment of birth?
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 承认人性是可塑的、动态的，而非先天固定的
  2. 识别当前'性'的状态：哪些潜能已经展开，哪些尚未激活
  3. 通过持续实践（继）将善的潜能转化为现实的人性
  4. 不断调整环境与习性，使性在新的条件下继续生成
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Acknowledge that human nature is malleable and dynamic, not fixed at birth
  2. Identify the current state of 'nature': which potentials are activated, which remain dormant
  3. Transform good potential into actual human nature through continuous practice ('continuation')
  4. Constantly adjust environment and habits so nature continues to form under new conditions
- **代表性案例**:
  - 船山论'命日受，性日生'：人每天从天地接受新的气化，每天形成新的人性——人性是一生不断生成的过程，而非出生时一次性决定的
  - 反对'悬一性于初生之顷'：如果人性在出生时已完全确定，则后天努力毫无意义——这是宿命论
  - 正向应用：教育的本质就是'继善成性'——通过持续引导，将人的潜能发展为现实德行
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Chuanshan on 'mandate received daily, nature formed daily': each day humans receive new qi from heaven and earth, each day forming new human nature—nature is a lifelong process of becoming, not once-determined at birth
  - Critique of 'fixing nature at the moment of birth': if human nature were fully determined at birth, postnatal effort would be meaningless—that is fatalism
  - Positive application: the essence of education is 'continuing good to cultivate nature'—through sustained guidance, human potential develops into actual virtue
- **当代应用**:
  - 教育：因材施教，持续养成
  - 心理治疗：人格可塑，非命运注定
  - 自我发展：终身成长，打破'固定型思维'
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Education: teach according to aptitude, nurture continuously
  - Psychotherapy: personality is malleable, not fated
  - Self-development: lifelong growth, breaking 'fixed mindset'
- **关联模式**: M-WFZ-003、M-WFZ-005、M-WFZ-006

### M-WFZ-005 行可兼知法

- **英文名**: Practice-Encompasses-Knowledge Method (Xing Ke Jian Zhi)
- **类别**: 认识论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 5 | **研究稿ID**: M-WAN-005
- **领域**: 知行关系/实践认识论 / Knowledge-Action Relation / Pragmatic Epistemology
- **出处**: 《尚书引义·说命中二》
- **定义**: 实践（行）是认识（知）的基础、来源和检验标准。实践可以涵盖认识的功能（行可兼知），但认识不能替代实践（知不可兼行）。陆王心学'以知为行'将认识等同于实践，禅学'知有是事便休'将认识视为终点——船山均予批驳。真正的知识必须在实践中验证、完善和完成。知与行各有功能、相互补充（知行相资），但行的地位优先。
- **定义(EN)**: Practice (xing) is the foundation, source, and test of knowledge (zhi). Practice can encompass the function of knowledge (practice encompasses knowledge), but knowledge cannot replace practice (knowledge cannot encompass practice). Lu-Wang school's 'treating knowledge as practice' reduces practice to cognition; Chan Buddhism's 'knowing the matter is enough' treats knowledge as the end—Chuanshan refutes both. Genuine knowledge must be verified, refined, and completed in practice. Knowledge and practice each have their function and complement each other (mutual assistance), but practice holds priority.
- **关键概念**: 行可兼知、知不可兼行、知行相资、行先知后、实践优先
- **原文依据**: 行可兼知，而知不可兼行。知行相资以为用，并进而有功。
  > Practice can encompass knowledge, but knowledge cannot encompass practice. Knowledge and practice supplement each other in function; advancing together yields results.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 识别问题：当陷入'空想'或'知而不行'时，追问——我是否把认识当成了实践？
  2. 以实践为起点：先动手做，在做的过程中获得真知
  3. 在实践中检验认识：想法是否正确，必须回到实践中验证
  4. 知行并进：认识指导实践，实践深化认识，循环上升
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Identify the problem: when stuck in 'empty speculation' or 'knowing without doing,' ask—am I mistaking knowledge for practice?
  2. Start from practice: act first, gain genuine knowledge through doing
  3. Test knowledge in practice: verify whether ideas are correct by returning to practice
  4. Advance knowledge and practice together: knowledge guides practice, practice deepens knowledge, cycling upward
- **代表性案例**:
  - 船山批判陆九渊'发明本心'：只向内求心，不向外实践，则'知有是事便休'——认识停留在表面，无法真正解决问题
  - 船山批判王阳明'知行合一'：将知等同于行，以内心的'良知'替代外在的实践——这是'销行以归知'，将实践消解为认识
  - 正向案例：大禹治水——不是先'知'治水之道再行，而是在治水实践中逐步认识水之规律，边做边学
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Chuanshan criticizes Lu Jiuyuan's 'discover the original mind': seeking only inward without outward practice leads to 'knowing the matter is enough'—knowledge stays superficial, unable to truly solve problems
  - Chuanshan criticizes Wang Yangming's 'unity of knowledge and action': equating knowledge with practice, replacing outward practice with inner 'innate knowing'—this is 'dissolving practice into knowledge'
  - Positive case: Yu the Great taming the floods—not knowing the way to control water first then acting, but gradually understanding water's laws through the practice of flood control, learning while doing
- **当代应用**:
  - 创业：先做最小可行产品验证假设，而非过度规划
  - 学习：在做中学，边做边学
  - 科研：假设必须在实验中验证
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Entrepreneurship: build MVP to test hypotheses first, not over-plan
  - Learning: learn by doing
  - Research: hypotheses must be verified through experimentation

### M-WFZ-006 理在欲中法

- **英文名**: Principle-in-Desire Method (Li Zai Yu Zhong)
- **类别**: 价值论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 6 | **研究稿ID**: M-WAN-006
- **领域**: 理欲之辨/伦理学 / Principle-Desire Debate / Ethics
- **出处**: 《读四书大全说》卷八
- **定义**: 天理不是与人欲对立的抽象法则，而是内在于人欲之中的合理秩序。正当的欲望（公欲）本身就是天理的体现——饥食渴饮、男女居室，这些基本需求在合理的节制下即是天理。只有'私欲'（过度的、损人利己的欲望）才是应当去除的对象。此法是对程朱'存天理灭人欲'的根本修正：灭的是'私欲'而非'人欲'。
- **定义(EN)**: Heavenly principle (tianli) is not an abstract law opposed to human desires (yuyu), but the rational order immanent within human desires. Legitimate desires (public desires) are themselves manifestations of heavenly principle—hunger and eating, thirst and drinking, male and female—the basic needs, when properly moderated, are heavenly principle. Only 'private desire' (excessive, self-serving desires) is what ought to be eliminated. This method is a fundamental correction to Cheng-Zhu's 'preserve heavenly principle, eliminate human desires': what is eliminated is 'private desire,' not 'human desire' itself.
- **关键概念**: 天理寓于人欲、理欲统一、公欲即天理、去私欲而非灭人欲、理在欲中
- **原文依据**: 礼虽纯为天理之节文，而必寓于人欲以见……故终不离人而别有天，终不离欲而别有理也。
  > Ritual, though purely the measured expression of heavenly principle, must be embedded in human desires to manifest itself. Therefore, heaven is never separate from humanity, and principle is never separate from desire.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 区分'正当欲望'（公欲）与'过度私欲'（私欲）
  2. 确认正当欲望的合理性：饥食渴饮是人性之常，不必羞耻
  3. 去除的是'过'与'私'——超出合理限度的欲望，而非欲望本身
  4. 在节制中实现理欲统一：让欲望在合理的轨道上运行
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Distinguish 'legitimate desires' (public desires) from 'excessive private desires' (private desires)
  2. Affirm the rationality of legitimate desires: hunger and thirst are natural human conditions, not shameful
  3. Eliminate 'excess' and 'selfishness'—desires beyond reasonable limits, not desire itself
  4. Achieve li-yu unity through moderation: let desires operate within rational channels
- **代表性案例**:
  - 船山批判'存天理灭人欲'：若灭尽人欲，则人性沦为空虚寂灭——没有欲望的生命如同枯木死灰，毫无生机
  - 船山论'公欲'：饥食渴饮、男女居室，这些基本需求是'公欲'，人人共有，正当合理；君子与小人在此层面是平等的
  - 反例警示：将一切欲望视为恶（如某些禁欲主义宗教），导致对人性的压抑和对生命的否定
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Chuanshan critiques 'preserve heavenly principle, eliminate human desires': if all desires are eliminated, human nature becomes empty and dead—life without desire is like dry wood and cold ashes, devoid of vitality
  - Chuanshan on 'public desire': hunger and eating, thirst and drinking, male and female—the basic needs are 'public desires' shared by all, legitimate and reasonable; gentlemen and commoners are equal in this respect
  - Counterexample: treating all desire as evil (as in certain ascetic religions) leads to suppression of human nature and denial of life
- **当代应用**:
  - 消费伦理：正当消费是合理的，过度消费才是问题
  - 企业管理：满足员工正当需求（公欲）而非压制
  - 公共政策：尊重民众合理欲望，疏导而非堵截
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Consumer ethics: legitimate consumption is reasonable; overconsumption is the problem
  - Enterprise management: satisfy employees' legitimate needs (public desires), do not suppress
  - Public policy: respect citizens' reasonable desires, channel rather than block

### M-WFZ-007 理势合一法

- **英文名**: Principle-Tendency Unity Method (Li Shi He Yi)
- **类别**: 历史哲学 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 7 | **研究稿ID**: M-WAN-007
- **领域**: 历史观/政治哲学 / Philosophy of History / Political Philosophy
- **出处**: 《读通鉴论》《宋论》
- **定义**: 历史发展有其内在规律（理），也有不可逆转的趋势（势）。理是必然性，势是必然性的展开过程——二者不可割裂。'时异则势异，势异则理亦异'——每个时代有其独特的理势结构，不能用古人之理裁断今人之势。此法既反对'泥古复古'的退化史观，也反对'历史虚无'的偶然论，提供了一种辩证的历史分析方法。
- **定义(EN)**: Historical development has its internal laws (li/principle) and irreversible tendencies (shi/trend). Principle is necessity; tendency is the unfolding of necessity—they cannot be separated. 'When times differ, tendencies differ; when tendencies differ, principles differ as well'—each era has its unique li-shi structure; one cannot judge current tendencies by ancient principles. This method opposes both 'clinging to antiquity' degenerative historicism and 'historical nihilism' contingency theory, offering a dialectical method for analyzing history.
- **关键概念**: 理势合一、时异势殊、势因理成、因时制宜、历史必然性
- **原文依据**: 势者，非他，时而已矣。理者，物之固然，事之所以然也。理势不可以两截观。
  > Tendency is nothing other than the conditions of the time. Principle is what things inherently are and why events happen as they do. Principle and tendency cannot be viewed as two separate things.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 识别当下的'时'（时代条件）：经济、政治、文化等结构性因素
  2. 分析'势'的形成：哪些力量在推动历史向某个方向运动
  3. 追问'理'：这个趋势背后的必然性是什么
  4. 因时制宜：根据当前理势结构制定策略，而非套用古人之法
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Identify the current 'time' (era conditions): economic, political, cultural structural factors
  2. Analyze the formation of 'tendency': which forces are pushing history in a particular direction
  3. Inquire into 'principle': what necessity lies behind this tendency
  4. Adapt to the times: formulate strategy based on current li-shi structure, not mechanically applying ancient methods
- **代表性案例**:
  - 船山论郡县制：郡县制取代封建制是'势'的必然，不是圣人有意设计——'气运流转，日趋于一'，历史大势不可阻挡
  - 船山论'复古'之非：夏后之法不行于商周，成周之规不施于后世——时代不同，势理不同，泥古必败
  - 反例警示：用三代以上之理裁断秦汉以后之世，如同'以古人之规矩，裁今人之方圆'，必生龃龉
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Chuanshan on the commandery-county system: the replacement of feudalism by commanderies was the necessity of 'tendency,' not the deliberate design of sages—'the flow of qi turns steadily toward unity,' the great trend of history cannot be resisted
  - Chuanshan on the futility of 'restoring antiquity': Xia's methods could not govern Shang and Zhou; Chengzhou's models could not be applied later—times differ, li-shi differ, clinging to antiquity leads to defeat
  - Counterexample: judging the post-Qin world by Three Dynasties principles is like 'using ancient compass and square to cut modern wood'—inevitably producing friction and failure
- **当代应用**:
  - 战略规划：审时度势，顺势而为
  - 政策制定：因时制宜，不照搬他国模式
  - 个人抉择：认清时代大势，做出符合时势的选择
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Strategic planning: assess the times and tendencies, act with the current
  - Policy making: adapt to the times, do not blindly copy other countries' models
  - Personal decision-making: understand the great trend of the era, make choices consistent with it
- **关联模式**: M-WFZ-002、M-WFZ-003、M-WFZ-009

### M-WFZ-008 两端一致法

- **英文名**: Dual-Polarities-Unity Method (Liang Duan Zhi Yi)
- **类别**: 辩证法 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 8 | **研究稿ID**: M-WAN-008
- **领域**: 矛盾论/辩证思维 / Contradiction Theory / Dialectical Thinking
- **出处**: 《周易外传》《张子正蒙注》
- **定义**: 任何事物都包含对立的两极（两端），两极相互对立又相互依存，最终达成统一（一致）。反对执一端而废另一端（如执理废欲、执静废动）。矛盾双方不是零和博弈，而是互以相成的辩证统一——没有阴就没有阳，没有反对就没有支持。最高明的思维不是选择一端，而是在两极的张力中找到动态平衡。
- **定义(EN)**: Any thing contains opposing poles (two ends); the poles oppose each other yet mutually depend on each other, ultimately achieving unity (consistency). This method opposes choosing one pole and discarding the other (e.g., valuing principle while discarding desire, valuing stillness while discarding movement). Contradictory aspects are not zero-sum games but dialectical unities—without yin there is no yang, without opposition there is no support. The highest thinking does not choose one pole but finds dynamic balance within the tension between the two.
- **关键概念**: 阴阳相成、两端一致、相反相仇、无终相敌、动态平衡
- **原文依据**: 阴阳各成其象，则相为对；刚柔、寒温、生杀，必相反而相为仇，而其互以相成，无终相敌之理。
  > Yin and yang each form their images, thus standing in opposition; hardness and softness, cold and warmth, generation and destruction—all necessarily oppose and conflict, yet they mutually complete each other, with no ultimate logic of mutual destruction.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 识别问题中的对立面（两端）：如理与欲、静与动、已知与未知
  2. 分析两端的对立关系：它们如何相互冲突、相互排斥
  3. 发现两端的统一关系：它们如何相互依存、相互成就
  4. 在两端之间寻找动态平衡点——不是选择一端，而是在张力中把握整体
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Identify the opposites (two ends) in the problem: e.g., principle and desire, stillness and movement, known and unknown
  2. Analyze the opposition between the two ends: how do they conflict with and exclude each other
  3. Discover the unity between the two ends: how do they mutually depend on and complete each other
  4. Find the dynamic balance point between the two ends—not choosing one, but grasping the whole within the tension
- **代表性案例**:
  - 船山论理欲：理与欲不是对立的，而是'天理人欲，同源异流'——正当的欲望即是天理的体现，去除私欲而非去除欲望本身
  - 船山论动静：动中有静，静中有动——'静者静动，非不动也'，静止是运动的特殊状态，而非运动的否定
  - 反例警示：执理废欲（程朱后学的禁欲主义）或执欲废理（纵欲主义）都是'执一隅而遗其全'的错误
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Chuanshan on principle and desire: principle and desire are not opposed but 'same source, different streams'—legitimate desire is itself the manifestation of heavenly principle; eliminate private desire, not desire itself
  - Chuanshan on stillness and movement: movement contains stillness, stillness contains movement—'stillness is quiet movement, not non-movement'; stillness is a special state of movement, not its negation
  - Counterexample: valuing principle while discarding desire (asceticism of Cheng-Zhu followers) or valuing desire while discarding principle (hedonism) are both errors of 'grasping one corner and losing the whole'
- **当代应用**:
  - 矛盾管理：在冲突双方中寻找共赢点
  - 决策思维：避免非此即彼的二元选择
  - 创新思维：在对立概念的张力中产生新方案
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Conflict management: find win-win points between opposing parties
  - Decision-making: avoid false dichotomies
  - Innovative thinking: generate new solutions from the tension between opposing concepts
- **关联模式**: M-WFZ-001、M-WFZ-006、M-WFZ-007

### M-WFZ-009 鉴古酌今法

- **英文名**: Examine-the-Past-to-weigh-the-Present Method (Jian Gu Zhuo Jin)
- **类别**: 史学方法论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 9 | **研究稿ID**: M-WAN-009
- **领域**: 历史哲学/实践智慧 / Philosophy of History / Practical Wisdom
- **出处**: 《宋论》《读通鉴论》
- **定义**: 研究历史的目的在于为当下提供智慧，而非泥古不化。历史不是重复的模板（不可简单套用古人方案），而是提供情境化的洞察——每个时代有其特殊性（时），必须'因时制宜'。但历史中有恒常的理（治乱之几），可以通过研究具体历史事件来把握。此法既是历史研究方法，也是政治决策方法：既不复古，也不忘本。
- **定义(EN)**: The purpose of studying history is to provide wisdom for the present, not to slavishly imitate the past. History is not a repeated template (one cannot simply apply ancient solutions); it provides contextualized insights—each era has its particularity ('time'), and one must 'adapt to the times.' But within history there are constant principles (the mechanisms of governance and chaos), which can be grasped through studying specific historical events. This method is both a method of historical research and a method of political decision-making: neither reverting to antiquity nor forgetting one's roots.
- **关键概念**: 鉴古酌今、因时制宜、一代之治各因其时、治乱之几、历史洞察
- **原文依据**: 一代之治，各因其时，建一代之规模以相扶而成治。
  > The governance of each dynasty follows its own time, building the scale of that era to support and achieve order.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 研究具体历史事件，而非抽象历史规律
  2. 识别历史情境中的'时'（时代条件）与'势'（发展趋势）
  3. 从中提炼可以借鉴的'理'（恒定规律），而非照搬具体方案
  4. 将历史智慧应用于当下，但根据当前时势调整具体策略
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Study specific historical events, not abstract historical laws
  2. Identify the 'time' (era conditions) and 'tendency' (development trend) within historical contexts
  3. Extract transferable 'principles' (constant laws) from history, not copy specific solutions
  4. Apply historical wisdom to the present, but adjust specific strategies according to current conditions
- **代表性案例**:
  - 船山《读通鉴论》：通过详细分析秦汉至唐末的历代兴亡，提炼出'理势相成'的历史规律——每个时代的治乱都有其特定的理势结构
  - 船山《宋论》：批评宋代'泥古'之弊——宋代士大夫空谈三代，却不知如何治理当下——鉴古的真正目的是'酌今'
  - 反例警示：简单套用'古代经验'解决当代问题（如主张恢复封建制），是'以古人之规矩，裁今人之方圆'，必生弊病
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Chuanshan's 'Reflections on the Comprehensive Mirror': through detailed analysis of rise and fall from Qin to Tang, he extracts the historical law of 'li-shi mutual formation'—each era's governance or chaos has its specific li-shi structure
  - Chuanshan's 'Discussion on Song': criticizes Song dynasty's 'clinging to antiquity'—Song scholars talked idly about the Three Dynasties but did not know how to govern the present—the true purpose of examining antiquity is 'to weigh the present'
  - Counterexample: simply applying 'ancient experience' to solve contemporary problems (e.g., advocating restoration of feudalism) is 'using ancient compass and square to cut modern wood'—inevitably producing dysfunction
- **当代应用**:
  - 政策制定：借鉴历史经验，但不照搬
  - 商业决策：从案例研究中提炼规律，而非复制模式
  - 个人成长：以史为鉴，但活在当下
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Policy making: learn from historical experience without copying blindly
  - Business decisions: extract patterns from case studies, not replicate models
  - Personal growth: use history as mirror, but live in the present

  4. Create new interpretations so the classical spirit speaks again in contemporary context
