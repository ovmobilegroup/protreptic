# 迈蒙尼德 (Maimonides) — H-MAI-001

- Era: 1138 年生于科尔多瓦一个拉比世家——其祖上可溯至 Rabbi Yehuda Hanasi（《密西拿》编纂者）的宫廷——父迈蒙（Maimon）是其最早的《塔木德》教师——1148 年穆瓦希德王朝攻陷科尔多瓦，强令犹太人改宗或流亡，全家开始了长达十余年的颠沛流亡：先后避居阿尔梅里亚、休达，约 1159 年定居摩洛哥非斯（其间据后世争议性记载曾被迫表面改宗伊斯兰教）——1165 年经圣地巴勒斯坦短暂朝觐耶路撒冷（他立誓每年禁食纪念登临圣殿山之日）后定居埃及福斯塔特——以其弟大卫溺亡印度洋（约 1170 年，兄弟合营珠宝贸易、大卫是家中经济支柱）为转折，大病一场后转而以行医为业——凭医术先后成为法蒂玛/阿尤布宫廷御医（传统认为侍奉过维齐尔法迪勒，与萨拉丁朝廷有交集），据传萨拉丁本人曾邀其入侍而为其婉拒——同时被推举为埃及犹太社区的精神领袖（Nagid，约 1190 年代正式受任），以书信裁决从也门到马格里布的犹太律法争端，著名的《也门书信》安抚了受弥赛亚运动与迫害双重搅扰的也门社群——1168 至 1178 年间以十年之功完成《密西拿托拉》（Mishneh Torah）：把浩如烟海的《巴比伦塔木德》判决编纂为十四编、千余章的无引注法典——约 1185 至 1190 年用犹大-阿拉伯语（Judeo-Arabic）写成《迷途指津》（Dalalat al-Ha'irin / The Guide for the Perplexed），1204 年后由塞缪尔·伊本·提本译为希伯来文——1204 年逝世于福斯塔特/开罗，遗体运葬提比里亚——墓志铭云'从摩西到摩西，无人如摩西'——身后其十三信条被编入犹太祈祷书（Yigdal 颂），《迷途指津》引发延续数十年的'迈蒙尼德之争'（Maimonidean controversy），反对方焚烧其著作，而经院哲学的托马斯·阿奎那则直接吸收其'否定属性'学说
- Era (EN): Born 1138 in Córdoba into a rabbinic family that traced itself to the court of Rabbi Yehuda Hanasi, editor of the Mishnah—his father Maimon was his first Talmud teacher—in 1148 the Almohads took Córdoba and forced Jews to convert or flee, and the family began more than a decade of wandering: Almería, Ceuta, and by about 1159 Fez in Morocco (where, according to a later and disputed account, they lived outwardly as converts to Islam)—in 1165, after a brief pilgrimage through the Holy Land (he vowed an annual fast for the day he ascended the Temple Mount), he settled in Fustat, Egypt—the drowning of his brother David in the Indian Ocean around 1170 (the two traded jewels, and David had been the family's support) broke his health and turned him to medicine—by his skill he became physician at the Fatimid/Ayyubid court (traditionally to the vizier al-Fadil, within Saladin's orbit), and is said to have declined an invitation to serve Saladin in person—at the same time he was recognized as the spiritual head (Nagid) of Egyptian Jewry, adjudicating halakhic disputes by letter from Yemen to the Maghreb, his famous Epistle to Yemen consoling a community shaken by persecution and a messianic agitation—between 1168 and 1178 he completed the Mishneh Torah: the vast Babylonian Talmud codified into fourteen books, over a thousand chapters, citation-free—about 1185–1190 he wrote The Guide for the Perplexed (Dalalat al-Ha'irin) in Judeo-Arabic, translated into Hebrew after 1204 by Samuel ibn Tibbon—he died at Fustat/Cairo in 1204 and was buried in Tiberias—his epitaph: 'From Moses to Moses, none arose like Moses'—his Thirteen Principles entered the Jewish prayer book (the Yigdal hymn); the Guide ignited a Maimonidean controversy lasting decades, in which opponents burned his works while Thomas Aquinas absorbed his doctrine of negative attributes into scholastic philosophy

- School: 犹太律法系统化（halakhic codification）的集大成者（《密西拿托拉》十四编法典）/ 犹太哲学亚里士多德化的完成者（《迷途指津》以理性哲学重释托拉）/ '否定神学'在亚伯拉罕传统中的经典立法者（上帝只有否定属性）/ 犹太教十三信条的制定者（首次为犹太信仰立教义清单）/ 中世纪医学伦理与养生学的著述家（《养生法》《格言》的作者）

## Historical significance
迈蒙尼德（Moses ben Maimon, 1138-1204），中世纪最伟大的犹太哲学家、律法学家、医师与社区领袖，后世尊称 Rambam。他的历史地位由五件事锚定。其一，律法的总编纂：《密西拿托拉》把千万言的《塔木德》判决熔铸为结构分明的十四编法典，不注出处、不容歧义，使散落在辩论中的'活的律法'第一次拥有了可检索的总纲——它本身就是'从浩繁裁决到公理化法典'的方法论示范。其二，信仰的教义化：十三信条第一次为犹太教立下'最低共识'清单，把'何为必须相信'从无尽辩论中析出，Yigdal 颂至今在每个犹太会堂吟诵——'信仰清单'这一形式本身是他首创的方法论装置。其三，《迷途指津》的哲学重释：以亚里士多德哲学系统重释托拉，处理'律法语言与科学理性相冲突时诚实的智识人如何自处'——为'迷失在托拉字面与哲学真理之间的人'立法。其四，否定神学的经典表述：关于上帝我们只能说'祂不是什么'（非物体、非偶性、不可比拟），一切肯定属性皆是行为的转述或观念的类比——此说经译本传入拉丁世界，直接塑造了阿奎那的上帝论。其五，典范人格的建制：医师、法官、法典家、哲学家四位一体，'从摩西到摩西，无人如摩西'的墓志铭把其个人确立为流散犹太文明的制度性支柱。代价与极限：法典化被批评为扼杀塔木德式的开放辩论（卡罗《舒尔汉阿鲁赫》以同样体例反驳并最终取代其司法地位）；十三信条在其身后引发持久的正统性之争（哈西德·克雷斯卡斯为复活概念辩护、后世拉比否认信条的强制力）；《迷途指津》刻意晦涩（'以七种含义写作'），引发两个世纪解释权的混战，反迈蒙尼德派两度焚烧其著作。

## Historical significance (EN)
Moses ben Maimon (Maimonides, Rambam, 1138-1204) was the greatest medieval Jewish philosopher, halakhist, physician and communal leader. Five achievements anchor his standing. First, the total codification of the law: the Mishneh Torah melted millions of words of Talmudic decision into a fourteen-book code, citation-free and deliberately univocal, giving the 'living law' scattered through debate its first searchable master plan—the work is itself a demonstration of method, from unbounded litigation to an axiomatic code. Second, the creedalization of faith: the Thirteen Principles were the first 'minimal consensus' list in Judaism, extracting 'what must be believed' from endless disputation; the Yigdal hymn is still sung in every synagogue—the very form of a 'creed list' is his invention. Third, the philosophical reinterpretation of the Guide for the Perplexed: Aristotelian philosophy brought into systematic contact with Torah, legislating for the honest intellectual caught between the law's language and scientific truth. Fourth, the classic statement of negative theology: of God we may say only what He is not (not a body, not an accident, incomparable); every positive attribute is a report of action or an analogy of ideas—transmitted into Latin through translation, it directly shaped Aquinas's doctrine of God. Fifth, the institution of the exemplary persona: physician, judge, codifier and philosopher in one body—the epitaph 'from Moses to Moses, none like Moses' fixed the man as an institutional pillar of the diaspora. Costs and limits: codification was attacked as strangling Talmudic open debate (Caro's Shulchan Arukh answered in the same genre and ultimately displaced his juridical primacy); the Thirteen Principles provoked a lasting controversy over orthodoxy (Hasdai Crescas defended resurrection against them; later rabbis denied their binding force); and the Guide, written in studied obscurity ('written in seven senses'), ignited two centuries of interpretive warfare, with opponents burning his works twice.

## Unique thinking
他思维方式的底色是'系统的总归拢与极限的诚实言说：真理若有体系就必须能被编纂成法典，而体系的顶端（上帝）恰恰是体系无法言说之物——思维的纪律是先归拢一切可归拢者，再在顶点处拒绝言说'。六个标志性动作。其一，'公理化归拢法'——把无边界的辩论海归拢为分编、分章、条目的封闭法典：先分类，再排序，再消除歧义，最后正文零引注（引注是辩论的遗迹，法典要的是裁决本身）。其二，'信条析出法'——从无尽神学争议中析出'最低共识'：任何争论必先问'这是核心信仰还是可争的边缘'，用编号清单把必须相信与可以讨论切开。其三，'否定的属性法'——对不可比拟者，肯定性言说必然使其沦为被造物；诚实路线只有否定：逐项说'祂不是什么'，逼近而不指认。其四，'寓言解码法'——圣典中与理性冲突的字面陈述必须读作寓言（'上帝的手'、'上帝后悔'），解码的钥匙由两条优先级决定：理性不可违背者按理性读，理性可违背者按字面读；一个陈述若字面可能，禁止寓言化。其五，'中间道路法'——德性与性情皆取两极之间的中道，唯谦卑一项例外（应取极端之低）；这条伦理几何学把'德的修养'变成对两个极端坐标的持续测量。其六，'双真分程法'——哲学理性与启示托拉各有管辖：哲学管辖'存在是什么'，托拉管辖'人该怎么活'；冲突处理先重读后协调，协调不成则守托拉而不弃理性。代价与极限：归拢式法典在结构上压制异议（卡罗的司法反扑与塔木德派的不满同源于此）；信条化把活信仰变成清单，其身后正统性战争证明清单本身就是争议源；《迷途指津》的自设密码使传播依赖解码授权，两派焚烧其书是密码传播模式的暴力终局。

## Unique thinking (EN)
The bedrock of his thinking: total systematization joined to honest speech at the limit—if truth has a system it must be codifiable like a law code, yet the apex of the system (God) is precisely what the system cannot say; the discipline of thought is to gather all that can be gathered, then at the summit refuse to speak. Six signature moves. First, axiomatic gathering—reduce the boundless sea of debate to a closed code of books, chapters and entries: classify, order, disambiguate, and finally write with zero citations (citations are the residue of debate; a code wants the decision itself). Second, creedal extraction—from endless theological dispute extract the 'minimal consensus': before any argument, ask whether it touches a core belief or a contestable margin, and cut the must-believe from the debatable with a numbered list. Third, negative attributes—of the incomparable, affirmative speech demotes God to a creature; the honest route is negation: say item by item what He is not, approaching without pointing. Fourth, allegorical decoding—literal statements of scripture that clash with reason must be read as parables ('the hand of God,' 'God repented'), the decoding key fixed by a two-term priority: what reason cannot violate is read rationally, what it may violate is read literally; and a statement possible as literal must not be allegorized. Fifth, the middle way—virtue and temperament take the mean between two poles, with humility the single exception (the lower extreme is prescribed); this moral geometry turns cultivation of character into the continuous measurement of two extreme coordinates. Sixth, the double-truth division—reason and Torah each hold jurisdiction: philosophy governs 'what exists,' Torah governs 'how one shall live'; on conflict, reread first and harmonize second, and failing harmony, keep Torah without abandoning reason. Costs and limits: the gathering code structurally suppresses dissent (Caro's juridical counterattack and the Talmudists' discontent share this root); creedal listing turned living faith into a checklist, and the posthumous wars over orthodoxy prove the list itself is the controversy; the Guide's self-set cipher made transmission depend on licensed decoding—the two burnings of his books were the violent endgame of that ciphered circulation.

## M-MAI-001 公理化归拢法 / Axiomatic Gathering Method

- Definition: 知识系统化方法论/四步归拢：分类（按生命历程与义务对象切分）、排序（条目自成闭环）、消歧（同题异判给出单一裁决）、零引注（剥离辩论史，呈现活的律法而非律法的考古）
- Definition (EN): Knowledge-systematization methodology/four-step gathering: classification (cut by life-course and objects of duty), ordering (entries self-contained), disambiguation (one ruling per contested case), zero citations (excise the history of debate—present the living law, not its archaeology)
- Source: 《密西拿托拉》卷首'致读者书'与全书结构；《密西拿评注》卷首论知识分类；《逻辑技艺论》论科学与知识的划界
- Key quote: 让读者从这部书中获得全部口传律法的结论，无需在辩论的海洋中打捞——本条例无引注，因为引注属于辩论，而这部书属于裁决。 / Let the reader take from this work the upshot of the whole oral law without dredging the ocean of debate—this code carries no citations, for citations belong to debate, and this book belongs to the ruling.
- Process:
  - 划定领域并选轴：把要归拢的知识域整体圈出，选一条贯穿全部材料的主轴（生命历程、义务对象、决策阶段），拒绝按材料原貌的偶然结构归档
  - 四步归拢：分类切分到自然层级→每个单元排序为闭环（定义→细则→边界案例）→同题异判裁定唯一结论→剥离论证史只留结论
  - 加装'单义条款'：对每一条裁决标注其权威级别（共识/多数/个人判断），把归拢者的裁定权显式化而非隐蔽化
  - 预留更新接口：明示'若出现更强证据，此条可改'——法典的封闭性只封闭呈现形式，不封闭修正通道
- Process (EN):
  - Bound the domain and choose the axis: circle the whole field of knowledge to be gathered, choose one axis that runs through all the material (life-course, objects of duty, decision stages), and refuse to archive by the accidental shape of the sources
  - Gather in four steps: classify into natural tiers→order each unit as a closed loop (definition→rules→borderline cases)→adjudicate divergent authorities to one conclusion→excise the argumentation, keep only the ruling
  - Install the univocity clause: mark each ruling's authority grade (consensus/majority/private judgment), making the gatherer's adjudicating power explicit rather than hidden
  - Leave an update interface: state that 'on stronger evidence this entry may be revised'—the code's closure closes the form of presentation, not the channel of correction
- Representative cases:
  - 《密西拿托拉》的整体架构：二百五十万言塔木德熔铸为十四编千余章——'从任何一条进入即可执行'的可检索法典
  - 十三信条作为信条领域的同款归拢：从无尽神学争议中析出编号的最低共识清单
  - 《养生法》：把体液医学与个人体质、气候、起居归拢为可执行的宫廷医嘱——医学的同款四步法
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The architecture of the Mishneh Torah: two and a half million words of Talmud melted into fourteen books, a thousand chapters—a searchable code executable from any single entry
  - The Thirteen Principles as the same gathering in the field of creed: a numbered minimal-consensus list extracted from endless theological dispute
  - The Regimen of Health: humoral medicine, constitution, climate and habit gathered into executable court medical instructions—the four steps applied to medicine
- Modern applications:
  - 组织知识库建设：把散落在会议记录、邮件、口头惯例中的决策归拢为结构化手册——分类选轴、条目闭环、消除'我们一般都这么办'式的多义性，并标注每条规则的权威级别
  - 大型代码库/运维手册的重构：从'考古式文档'（满是历史讨论与作废方案）改写为'裁决式文档'——正文只呈现当前有效结论，讨论史移入变更日志
  - 标准与合规文件的编写：同题异判处必须裁定唯一结论并显式标注'此为公司裁决，非行业共识'，防止归拢者的个人判断伪装成客观标准
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Organizational knowledge bases: gather decisions scattered through meeting notes, email and oral custom into a structured manual—choose the axis, close each entry, eliminate 'we usually do it this way' ambiguity, and mark each rule's authority grade

## M-MAI-002 信条析出法 / Creedal-Extraction Method

## M-MAI-003 否定的属性法 / Negative-Attributes Method

- Definition: 极限言说方法论/给宗教语言立总规则：肯定言说只许三种用途（行为转述、观念类比、否定逼近），逐项说'祂不是什么'排除误认——排除完毕剩下的不可排除者即方向，但不可被言说
- Definition (EN): Methodology of speech at the limit/one master rule for religious language: positive speech only as report-of-action, analogical attribution, or negation—say item by item what He is not to exclude misidentifications; the unexcludable remainder is the direction, itself unsayable
- Source: 《迷途指津》第一部第 50-60 章论属性；《密西拿托拉》知识编'托拉基础'第 1-3 章的非物体化表述；'论改宗'章对属性问题的先行论述
- Key quote: 关于祂，否定的言说是接近，肯定的言说却是远离——你每说出一个肯定的谓词，你就把祂变成了一个别的东西。 / Of Him, negative speech draws near and affirmative speech recedes—with every positive predicate you utter, you turn Him into something else.
- Process:
  - 先立'不可指认者'：确认讨论对象属于'肯定描述必然降格'的类型（绝对、终极、不可比拟者）——此后一切语言操作在此约束下进行
  - 审计既有语言：把领域内全部肯定陈述分类——哪些是行为转述、哪些是观念类比、哪些是必须改正的字面误认
  - 逐项否定排除：对每一候选'理解'说'不是这个'——排除创造神、可感物、复合体、可比较者，每次排除一个误认
  - 守住剩余者：排除完毕后明确宣布'剩下的不可被言说'——把不可言说本身写为体系的一条正式结论而非失败标记
- Process (EN):
  - First fix 'the unpointable-at': confirm the object belongs to the type where affirmative description necessarily demotes (the absolute, the ultimate, the incomparable)—all speech-operations proceed under this constraint
  - Audit the existing language: classify every affirmative statement in the field—reports of action, analogical attributions, or literal misidentifications to be corrected
  - Exclude by successive negation: say 'not this' of each candidate understanding—exclude creator-god, sensible object, composite, comparable being, one misidentification per negation
  - Hold the remainder: when the exclusions are complete, declare that 'what remains cannot be said'—writing unsayability as a formal conclusion of the system, not a mark of failure
- Representative cases:

## M-MAI-004 寓言解码法 / Allegorical-Decoding Method

- Definition: 文本调和方法论/三道闸门的受控解码：冲突检测（字面与理性/感官不可调和才启动）→字面优先（字面可能者禁止寓言化）→排序解码（理性原则先于文本细节、整体教义先于孤立字句）
- Definition (EN): Textual-reconciliation methodology/three-gate controlled decoding: conflict detection (open only on irreconcilable clash with reason or the senses)→literal priority (what is possible literally must not be allegorized)→ordered decoding (reason's principles before textual detail, whole doctrine before isolated words)
- Source: 《迷途指津》第一部导论与第 2-27 章的拟人经文逐条处理；第一部第 59 章论字面与寓言的边界；致提本的信论'七种含义'

  - 列出'不可动摇者'：先写死理性已确立者与感官已观察者的清单——这是解码程序中唯一的硬约束，也是全部闸门的地基
  - 冲突检测：逐条比对文本字面义与不可动摇清单——冲突则进入解码通道，无冲突则字面放行，永不预支寓言
  - 按优先级解码：冲突案例按固定优先级重读——理性原则先于字句、整体教义先于孤立文本，解码产物必须回写进整体教义自洽
  - 给解码留审计痕迹：每个寓言化决策标注其依据（理性原则/感官观察）与反例边界（字面可能性为何不成立）——使解码可被质疑而非解释者独断
- Process (EN):
  - List the unshakables: write down first what reason has proved and what the senses have observed—the single hard constraint of the decoding program and the ground of all gates
  - Detect conflict: compare each text's literal sense with the unshakable list—conflict enters the decoding channel, no conflict passes literally; allegory is never prepaid
  - Decode by priority: conflict cases are reread by fixed priority—reason's principles before words, whole doctrine before isolated text—and the decoding product must write back into doctrinal coherence
  - Leave an audit trail: mark each allegorical decision with its ground (rational principle / sensory observation) and the boundary of counterexamples (why literal possibility fails)—so decoding is contestable, not the interpreter's fiat

  - '七种含义'的分层设计：《迷途指津》表层与深层并行——同一文本对大众给虔诚、对智识人给哲学，解码权按训练分层授权

## M-MAI-005 中间道路法 / Middle-Way Method

- Definition: 德性几何学方法论/性情是后天倾向而非被造判决——每项性情定位为两极间可测中点，修养即持续测量与回调；初学者可过冲训练换回稳；谦卑是唯一取极端的例外
- Definition (EN): Moral-geometry methodology/temperament is acquired disposition, not creation's verdict—each temperament is a measurable midpoint between poles, and cultivation is continuous measurement and correction; beginners may overshoot to the opposite pole for stability; humility is the single extreme-prescribed exception
- Source: 《密西拿托拉》知识编'德性篇'第一章论性情与中道；同篇末章论'把谦卑修到极低'与怒的全量禁绝
- Key quote: 性情人皆有之，方向各异——智者的功课是让自己的每一项性情都稳稳站在中点，唯有谦卑，要走到最低的那一端去。 / Every man has temperaments, each tending a different way—the wise man's work is to hold every temperament steady at its midpoint; humility alone must be walked to the lowest end.
- Process:
  - 拒绝'本质判决'：把待改的性情从'我就是这种人'改写为'我目前站在某两极之间的某点'——先消解身份化，再谈调整
  - 定位两极并写判据：为该性情找出两端，把中点写成可自我检验的行为判据（'在什么情境、以多大强度、能否被劝回'）
  - 选择训练路径：中点难稳者用过冲训练（刻意走向对立极一段时期再回调）；每个训练周期末以判据复测坐标
  - 执行例外条款：对谦卑类目标直接取极端（修到极低），对愤怒类目标执行零容忍（任何增量都改）——两条特殊规程不适用中点逻辑
- Process (EN):
  - Refuse the verdict of essence: rewrite the temperament to be changed from 'this is what I am' to 'I currently stand at some point between two poles'—dissolve identity-fixation before adjustment
  - Fix the poles and write the test: find both extremes, and phrase the midpoint as a behavioral test one can run on oneself (in what situations, at what intensity, capable of being talked back down)
  - Choose the training path: if the midpoint cannot be held, use overshoot training (deliberately toward the opposite pole for a period, then correct back); re-measure the coordinate against the test at each cycle's end
  - Execute the exception clauses: for humility-type targets take the extreme directly (to the utmost low); for anger-type targets enforce zero tolerance (every increment corrected)—two special protocols outside the midpoint logic
- Representative cases:
  - '德性篇'开篇的立法：性情被宣布为可训练的倾向——希腊中道说被法典化为犹太律法的第一条德性条款
  - 愤怒的特殊条款：'德性篇'把怒判为'任何量都应避免'的性情——中点几何学内的单点否决
  - 对吝啬者的训练建议：先教其挥霍式施予数月、再回调至慷慨——过冲换回稳的可操作示范
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The legislation opening Hilkhot De'ot: temperament declared trainable disposition—Greek doctrine of the mean codified as the first clause of Jewish virtue law
  - The special clause on anger: anger judged 'a temperament whose every quantity should be avoided'—a single-point veto inside the midpoint geometry

## M-MAI-006 双真分程法 / Double-Truth Division Method

- Definition: 管辖权划分方法论/先立管辖权地图再处理冲突：哲学管辖'存在'、托拉管辖'应然'——冲突走固定三步（重读→协调→双重归档），双重归档非骑墙：理性证明优先于字面，诫命层永远按托拉执行
- Definition (EN): Jurisdiction-partition methodology/draw the jurisdictional map before handling conflicts: philosophy governs 'existence,' Torah governs 'the ought'—conflicts follow a fixed three steps (reread→harmonize→double-file); double-filing is not fence-sitting: rational demonstration outranks the literal, while the commandment-layer is always executed per Torah
- Source: 《迷途指津》第一部导论与第二部论创世的冲突处理；致提本的信论全书目的；《密西拿托拉》知识编'偶像崇拜法'开篇论理性与诫命
- Key quote: 理智与托拉不是两个对手——它们分治两个国度；唯有在边界上相撞时，让证明先说话，让诫命照常执行。 / Intellect and Torah are not two adversaries—they govern two realms; only when they collide at the border, let the demonstration speak first and the commandment stand in force as always.
- Process:
  - 先画管辖权地图：在处理任何具体冲突前，先明确两个权威各管什么（事实层归理性、应然层归托拉/制度底线），禁止在冲突现场临时划界
  - 固定三步处理：冲突案例先重读（误读/误用检测）→再协调（找两不歪曲的第三义）→不成则双重归档（登记'不可裁决'并按实践权威执行）
  - 给两域设权重规则：在可裁决处理性证明优先于字面文本；在应然层，托拉/制度权威不因事实结论动摇——权重规则本身写死，不逐案摇摆
  - 预防滑坡：明示'双重归档'与'两真并存'的区别——归档是暂缓裁决并保留唯一实践标准，不是承认两套互斥真理都真

  - Guard against the slide: state the difference between double-filing and 'two truths side by side'—filing defers the verdict while keeping one practical standard; it does not concede that two mutually exclusive truths are both true
- Representative cases:

## M-MAI-007 医学理性诊疗法 / Medical-Rational-Diagnosis Method

- Definition: 专业实践方法论/诊疗三特征：个体变量先行（处方针对变量组合而非病名）、养生优先于药（预防为最高档，药物是干预而非默认）、管辖权服从（职业伦理高于仪式义务，行医不因安息日中止）
- Definition (EN): Professional-practice methodology/three features of diagnosis: individual variables first (prescriptions target variable-combinations, not disease names), regimen before drugs (prevention the top tier; drugs the intervention, not the default), subordination to jurisdiction (professional ethics outrank ritual; medicine does not pause for the Sabbath)
- Source: 《养生法》开篇论个体变量与气候；《论哮喘》《论痔疮》的个体化处方；《密西拿评注》论 pikuach nefesh；《格言》的医学伦理条款
- Key quote: 同一疾病在不同的人身上是不同的病——医师的功课是先记下这个人的体质、气候与起居，再谈药石。 / The same disease in different persons is a different disease—the physician's work is to write down first this person's constitution, climate and habits, and only then speak of drugs.
- Process:
  - 先记录变量清单：对每一个案建立个体变量档案（体质、年龄、气候、职业、起居节律、情绪状态），拒绝直接对'病名'开方
  - 按变量组合推断：在理性框架内推断该变量组合下的干预方向——同一诊断在不同组合下允许相反处方，处方必须可执行到日常动作
  - 按优先级排序干预：养生（饮食/睡眠/运动/情绪）为第一档，药物为养生失守后的第二档——预防写在治疗方案的最前面
  - 执行管辖权服从：专业义务的边界由伦理总则（生命优先、怜悯优先）裁定——仪式与程序义务不得中止专业响应
- Process (EN):
  - Record the variable list first: build an individual variable file for every case (constitution, age, climate, occupation, daily rhythm, state of mind), refusing to prescribe off the disease name
  - Infer by variable-combination: within the rational frame infer the direction of intervention for this combination—the same diagnosis may take opposite prescriptions under different combinations, and prescriptions must be executable down to daily actions
  - Rank interventions by priority: regimen (food, sleep, exercise, passions) as the first tier, drugs as the second after regimen fails—prevention written at the head of the treatment plan
  - Execute subordination to jurisdiction: the bounds of professional duty are set by the ethical code (life first, compassion first)—ritual and procedural obligations may never suspend the professional response
- Representative cases:
  - 《养生法》为维齐尔法迪勒而作：开篇即用一整章记录法迪勒的体质、气候与起居——'个体变量先行'的完整示范
  - 把'情绪过激'列为致病变量并开心理处方（情绪管理规则）——中世纪医学把心理变量写入生理处方的先行案例
  - 安息日行医的裁决传统：pikuach nefesh 使生命救护优先于一切仪式——管辖权服从在专业层的应用
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The Regimen of Health written for the vizier al-Fadil: an entire opening chapter recording al-Fadil's constitution, climate and habits—the full demonstration of 'individual variables first'
  - Listing 'excess of the passions' among pathogenic variables and prescribing for it psychologically (rules of emotion management)—a medieval precedent of writing psychological variables into physiological prescriptions
  - The ruling tradition of practicing on the Sabbath: pikuach nefesh makes rescue of life outrank all ritual—the professional application of subordination to jurisdiction
- Modern applications:
  - 临床与健康的个体化实践：建立'变量档案先行'的诊疗/教练流程（体质、环境、作息、情绪先记录，方案后开），并把生活方式干预排在药物/技术干预之前——预防优先的结构
  - 专业服务的伦理管辖：给高价值客户（权力、资本）服务的顾问/医师建立'伦理总则高于客户意愿'的明文边界——防止专业判断被强者倾斜
  - 复杂系统的'变量组合诊断'：运维/安全/管理问题不按'故障名'处理，先记录系统变量组合（负载、气候般的部署环境、人员节律），同症不同因允许相反修法
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Individualized practice in clinical and health work: build the 'variable-file first' workflow (constitution, environment, sleep, mood recorded before any plan is issued), with lifestyle intervention ranked before drugs or technology—a prevention-first structure
  - Ethical jurisdiction in professional services: for consultants and physicians serving high-value clients (power, capital), a written boundary that 'the ethical code outranks the client's wishes'—keeping professional judgment from tilting toward the strong
  - Variable-combination diagnosis in complex systems: treat operations/security/management problems not by 'fault name' but after recording the system's variable combination (load, deployment climate, human rhythms); same symptom, different cause, opposite fix allowed

## M-MAI-008 书信裁决法 / Epistolary-Arbitration Method

- Definition: 分布式治理方法论/把权威写成文书：三段结构（事实诊断→法理引据→行动指令）+ 两种文体（安抚型缓裁决、裁决型直入法理）——诊断最详因其决定后续；引据须可被第三方复核；指令带适用边界
- Definition (EN): Distributed-governance methodology/write authority as document: three-part structure (fact-diagnosis→legal grounding→action directive) plus two registers (consoling slows the ruling; adjudicating enters the law directly)—the diagnosis fullest because everything after depends on it; grounding verifiable by third parties; directives carry boundaries of application
- Source: 《也门书信》全文结构；《论改宗者书信》的裁决型示范；《论迫害》对被迫改宗者的法理处理；书信集首尾对其权威来源的自述
- Key quote: 我不能到你们那里去，但法理可以——让这封信带上诊断、引据与指令：诊断若错，引据无益；引据若不可复核，指令无权。 / I cannot come to you, but the law can—let this letter carry diagnosis, grounding and directive: if the diagnosis errs the grounding profits nothing; if the grounding cannot be verified the directive holds no authority.
- Process:
  - 先诊断后裁决：对每一宗远程事务，先花最大篇幅复述并重估来件的处境与诉求——诊断段未写完前禁止进入裁决
  - 把引据写到可复核精度：裁决所依赖的每条权威（条文、先例、信条）引用到当地执行者可以独立查验的程度——文书的质量=可被第三方检验
  - 区分文体并防错配：判断来件是求安抚还是求裁决，选对应文体；安抚型先共情缓指令，裁决型直入法理少铺垫
  - 给指令加边界与时限：行动指令写明适用条件与失效情形，并预防'情境文书被当永久教条'——文书的复用风险在发出时就要对冲
- Process (EN):
  - Diagnose before ruling: for every remote matter, spend the fullest space restating and re-estimating the situation and plea—no ruling may begin before the diagnosis is complete
  - Cite the grounding to verifiable precision: every authority the ruling rests on (text, precedent, principle) cited to the degree that local executors can independently check—the quality of the document = third-party checkability
  - Distinguish registers and prevent mismatch: judge whether the incoming letter seeks consolation or a ruling, and choose the register accordingly—consoling opens with empathy and slows the directive; adjudicating enters the law with little preamble
  - Bound and date the directive: state the conditions of application and the cases of expiry, guarding against 'the situation-specific letter becoming permanent dogma'—the reuse risk must be hedged at the moment of dispatch
- Representative cases:
  - 《也门书信》：对受迫害与假弥赛亚双重搅扰的也门社群，先诊断'双重苦难'、再引先知权威、末给'禁食祈祷代替绝望'的指令——三段结构的最完整示范
  - 《论改宗者书信》：对被迫改宗者的身份裁决直入法理（被迫的行为不算背教）——裁决型文体的标杆
  - 对马格里布、黎凡特各地争端的书信群：同一框架在不同语境的批量复用——文书治理对肉身到场的替代
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The Epistle to Yemen: for a community shaken by persecution and a false messiah at once—diagnosing the 'double suffering,' grounding in prophetic authority, closing with 'replace despair with fasting and prayer'—the fullest display of the three-part structure
  - The Epistle on Conversion: the identity ruling for forced converts entering the law directly (forced acts are not apostasy)—the benchmark of the adjudicating register
  - The letter-groups to the Maghreb and the Levant: the same frame reused across contexts in bulk—documentary governance substituting for bodily presence
- Modern applications:
  - 远程团队的管理文书：对无法到场的分布式团队，重要决定写成三段文书（处境诊断→依据→带边界的指令），并按对方需求选'安抚型/裁决型'文体——杜绝'只下指令不做诊断'的失效管理
  - 法务与合规的书面意见书：意见书的权威=引据可复核精度；给每条结论标注适用边界与失效条件，防止意见书被后人当万能条款引用
  - 开源社区/论坛的仲裁与公告：置顶公告采用'诊断（当前冲突的真实结构）→依据（治理章程条款）→指令（带时限的行动）'三段式，且区分安抚帖与裁决帖
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Management documents for remote teams: for the distributed team one cannot reach, write major decisions as three-part documents (situation diagnosis→grounds→bounded directives), choosing the consoling or adjudicating register by what the other side needs—ending the failed management of 'orders without diagnosis'
  - Written opinions in legal and compliance work: the opinion's authority = the verifiable precision of its grounding; mark each conclusion's boundary of application and expiry, so an opinion is not cited later as a universal clause
  - Arbitration and announcements in open-source communities and forums: pinned notices in the three-part form (diagnosis of the real structure of the conflict→grounds in the governance charter→time-bounded directives), with consoling and adjudicating posts kept distinct
- Related modes: M-MAI-002（信条析出法——书信裁决的弹药库：也门信的安抚工程直接调用信条框架诊断信仰动摇）; M-MAI-001（公理化归拢法——文书的母方法：书信裁决之所以可行，因为裁决所引的权威已被法典归拢成可检索条文）; M-CIC-002（西塞罗·文书先行法——最强先例：同以文书对无法到场的政治世界行使权威，西塞罗面向元老院同侪、迈蒙尼德面向流散共同体——'共和文书'与'裁决书信'之分）; M-MAI-010（典范收束法——书信的人格效力：远程权威最终靠'写信者是谁'的可信度支撑，收束于典范人格的建制）

## M-MAI-009 渐近阶梯法 / Asymptotic-Ladder Method

- Definition (EN): Spiritual-growth methodology/rewrite 'knowing God' as a triple asymptotic ladder: knowing what He is not (verifiable advance by exclusion)→two grades of love (climbing from interest-driven to purely object-directed)→the intellect ever advancing (perfection is a process, not an arrival)—growth as infinite approximation, each rung testable, the summit forever open
- Source: 《迷途指津》第三部第 51-54 章（末章'以理智认识理智对象'）；《密西拿托拉》'忏悔篇'第十章论两级爱；'德性篇'论爱与认知的互养

## M-MAI-010 典范收束法 / Exemplar-Closure Method

- Definition: 体系落地方法论/把法典、信条、哲学、医术收束于一个可模仿的完整人格：体系的可靠性论证走'此人已按此体系完整生活'的路线；收束点必须可模仿而非神迹化——人格被神化之日即收束失效之时
- Definition (EN): System-landing methodology/gather code, creed, philosophy and medicine into one imitable complete person: the system's reliability argued via 'this man has lived the system completely'; the closure-point must stay imitable, not deified—the day the person is deified is the day the closure fails
- Source: 迈蒙尼德书信集对其多重身份的自述；《密西拿托拉》'德性篇'对智者人格的规范性描述；其墓志铭与门徒记载的生平规范
- Key quote: 体系最后的论证不在书里，在生活里——若这套生活行不通，写它的那只手也应受审。 / The system's final argument is not in the books but in the life—if this life cannot be lived, the hand that wrote it stands for judgment too.
- Process:
  - 先建体系的互证结构：让体系的各组成领域（规范、信念、认知、实践）共享同一批方法气质，使它们在同一人身上能互相验证而非各自为政
  - 把人格立为收束点：明确宣布体系的最终验证形式是'一个完整人格的生活'，并以自己的公开实践（可查证的行为记录）作答
  - 守住可模仿性下限：收束人格的生活方式必须普通人在其条件下可部分复制——写明取舍（兼任的代价、拒绝的诱惑），禁止神迹化叙事
  - 预防捆绑风险：预先声明'典范的私德争议不自动构成体系反证'，并把体系的命题层与人格层分开审计——收束一旦失效，命题层仍应可独立检验
- Process (EN):
  - Build the mutual-verification structure first: make the system's constituent fields (norms, beliefs, cognition, practice) share one set of method-temperaments, so they verify one another in a single person instead of going their own ways
  - Erect the person as closure-point: declare that the system's final form of verification is 'the life of one complete person,' and answer with one's own public practice (verifiable records of conduct)
  - Hold the floor of imitability: the exemplar's way of life must be partially replicable by ordinary people under their conditions—write down the trade-offs (the cost of holding offices together, the temptations refused), and forbid hagiography
  - Hedge the bundling risk: state in advance that 'controversies over the exemplar's private conduct do not automatically refute the system,' and audit the propositional and personal layers separately—so even if closure fails, the propositions remain independently testable
- Representative cases:
  - 四重身份的互证：法典的归拢气质出现在《养生法》的变量清单，信条的最低共识出现在《也门书信》的安抚工程——同一方法气质贯穿四域
  - '从摩西到摩西，无人如摩西'的墓志铭：把人格本身立为体系的最后一条论证——典范收束的公共仪式化
  - 安息日行医的公开实践：以可查证的行为（不因仪式中止专业响应）为'专业义务高于仪式义务'作人格层证明
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The mutual verification of four identities: the gathering temperament of the code reappears in the Regimen's variable-lists, the creed's minimal consensus in the Yemen letter's consoling engineering—one method-temperament across four fields
  - The epitaph 'from Moses to Moses, none like Moses': the person himself erected as the system's final argument—the public ritualization of exemplar-closure
  - The public practice of practicing medicine on the Sabbath: a verifiable conduct (no ritual suspends the professional response) as personal-layer proof that professional duty outranks ritual
- Modern applications:
  - 创始人型组织的体系落地：把方法论、价值观、产品哲学收束于创始人公开可查证的生活实践（而非口号），同时预先声明'人格争议与命题真伪分开审计'——防止人设崩塌连带摧毁体系
  - 学术传统的师承建制：导师以'可模仿的生活形态'（作息、兼任、取舍）而非神化形象收束学派，并写明取舍代价——模仿可复制，崇拜必失真
  - 公共知识分子的立身设计：主张与其生活互相验证（公开行为记录可查），并为'私德争议不自动构成观点反证'预设辩护边界——既立典范又留审计缝隙
- Modern applications (EN):
  - System-landing in founder-led organizations: gather methodology, values and product philosophy into the founder's publicly verifiable lived practice rather than slogans, while declaring in advance that 'character controversies and propositional truth are audited separately'—so a persona collapse does not destroy the system
  - Institution-building in academic lineages: the mentor closes the school as 'an imitable form of life' (routine, offices held, trade-offs made) rather than a deified image, with the costs written down—imitation replicates, worship distorts
  - Self-design for public intellectuals: let claims and life verify one another (public records of conduct checkable), while pre-setting the boundary that 'private-conduct controversies do not automatically refute the ideas'—raising an exemplar while leaving an audit seam
- Related modes: M-MAI-001（公理化归拢法——收束的内部结构：四域共享的归拢气质是人格互证得以成立的前提）; M-MAI-005（中间道路法——人格层的日常工艺：典范的'如何生活'正是中道坐标的终身执行）; M-AUG-001（奥古斯丁·信理辩护法——平行对照：同以人格为体系的支撑（奥古斯丁的《忏悔录》），但奥古斯丁的收束是'转依叙事'，迈蒙尼德的收束是'持续兼任'——'皈依收束'与'执业收束'之分）; M-CC-001（孔子·问对诘合法——跨文明平行：同为'体系收束于可模仿人格'，孔子以礼乐日常收束、迈蒙尼德以法典—医术兼任收束——两者共同示范'人格即证明'的方法族）

### C-MAI-010 (M-MAI-010)
