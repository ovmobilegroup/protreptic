# 奥罗宾多 (Aurobindo) — H-AUR-001

- Era: 1872 年生于加尔各答婆罗门家庭，五岁起随兄赴英受教十四年（曼彻斯特、伦敦圣保罗学校、剑桥国王学院），精通希腊拉丁与数种欧洲语文而几乎不识母语——1893 年回国入巴罗达土邦任职，从行政官到大学院教授，自学梵文与印度经典——1905 年孟加拉分治后投身民族运动，先密谋武装起义，后公开办《向母亲致敬》（Bande Mataram）成为第一个公开要求完全独立（purna swaraj）的印度政治家——1907 年因煽动罪被诉而获释，1908 年因阿利普尔密谋案入狱一年，狱中修瑜伽而得'无处不在的克里希那、无物不梵'的灵性觉醒——1909 年无罪开释后发表著名的乌塔帕拉演说，短暂复办《业瑜伽》（Karmayogin）与《法》（Dharma）——1910 年'奉上界之命'悄然赴法属本地治里，此后四十年不再公开介入政治——1914-1921 年与法国人格力埃（Paul Richard）创办哲学月刊《阿雅亚》（Arya），连载产出《神圣人生论》《瑜伽综合论》《吠陀的秘密》《薄伽梵歌论》《人类统一的理想》等全部主要著作——1926 年'悉地日'宣布超心（Overmind）降入物理意识后退隐，此后仅在每年四个达善日露面，修道院事务交密那·阿尔法萨（'母亲'）主持——1950 年逝世于本地治里——身后'整体瑜伽'经萨特普雷姆续写《母亲的议程》传入欧美，奥罗维尔实验城成为其'人类统一'理想的制度遗存
- Era (EN): Born 1872 in Calcutta into a Brahmin family, sent to England at five and schooled there for fourteen years (Manchester, St. Paul's School London, King's College Cambridge)—master of Greek, Latin and several European languages yet almost ignorant of his mother tongue—returned in 1893 to serve in the Baroda state, from administrator to college professor, teaching himself Sanskrit and the Indian classics—after the 1905 Partition of Bengal threw himself into the national movement: secret preparation for armed insurrection first, then public journalism through Bande Mataram, becoming the first Indian politician to demand complete independence (purna swaraj) openly—prosecuted for sedition in 1907 and acquitted, imprisoned in 1908 for a year in the Alipore conspiracy case, where he practiced yoga and had the realization of 'Krishna everywhere, the Divine in everything'—released in May 1909, delivered the famous Uttarpara speech, briefly ran the weeklies Karmayogin and Dharma—in April 1910, 'by a command from above,' quietly went to French Pondicherry and for the next forty years took no public part in politics—founded the philosophical monthly Arya in 1914 with the Frenchman Paul Richard, serializing the works that became his books: The Life Divine, The Synthesis of Yoga, The Secret of the Veda, Essays on the Gita, The Ideal of Human Unity—on 'Siddhi Day' 1926 announced the descent of the Overmind into the physical and withdrew, seen only on four darshan days a year, entrusting the ashram to Mirra Alfassa ('the Mother')—died in Pondicherry in 1950—after his death Integral Yoga passed to Europe and America through Satprem's The Agenda of the Mother, and the experimental city of Auroville stands as the institutional legacy of his 'ideal of human unity'

- School: 整体瑜伽（Purna Yoga）的创立者（个体解放之外更求世界转化的灵性进路）/ '意识演化'哲学的立法者（精神内卷—物质—生命—心智—超心智的演化总纲）/ 民族运动'极端派'的理论家（完全独立的第一个公开主张者）/ 印度文明精神解释的复兴者（《吠陀的秘密》《薄伽梵歌论》的经典重释者）/ 三重转化与超心智降临学说的提出者

## Historical significance
奥罗宾多·高士（1872-1950），印度革命家、哲学家、瑜伽行者与诗人，二十世纪最有体系的印度精神思想家。他的历史地位由五件事锚定。其一，'意识演化'总纲的立法：把吠陀的'梵'与达尔文的'演化'焊接成一套完整宇宙论——精神'内卷'入物质，意识经物质、生命、心智逐层重新涌现，而人类心智只是过渡阶段，演化的下一站是'超心智'（Supermind）——灵性传统向后看黄金时代、向上看来世解脱，他向前看：地球上将有意识的新种。其二，整体瑜伽的开创：传统四道（智、爱、业、王）皆求个体解脱出世界，他把四道综合并加上第五要素——神圣意识降入身体与物质生活，目标是转化地上的生命而非逃离它。其三，三重转化方法：心灵转化（发现内在灵魂）、灵性转化（向上层意识开敞）、超心智转化（下降改写心智生命身体的本性）——一条有次第、可执行的世界转化工艺。其四，民族运动的理论突破：第一个公开要求完全独立、把民族主义表述为'神的工'、乌塔帕拉演说把狱中觉醒化为'一切皆是梵'的政治神学——四十年政治生涯与四十年隐修在同一人身上衔接，革命者与先知同体。其五，文本建制：《神圣人生论》《瑜伽综合论》《人类循环论》以及两万四千行的史诗《莎维德丽》构成一个自足的思想体系，经阿罗频多修道院与奥罗维尔两大制度载体持续运作至今。代价与极限：超心智降临被批评为不可证伪的形上学许诺（他本人承认第三重转化'或需数世纪'）；生平被后世灵性营销反复简化，双面人生的革命史实层常被遮蔽；'民族主义即宗教'的表述在其身后被各方挪用。

## Historical significance (EN)
Aurobindo Ghose (1872-1950), Indian revolutionary, philosopher, yogi and poet, was the most systematic Indian spiritual thinker of the twentieth century. Five achievements anchor his standing. First, the legislation of 'the evolution of consciousness': welding the Vedic Brahman onto Darwinian evolution into a complete cosmology—Spirit 'involutes' into matter, consciousness re-emerges through matter, life and mind, human mind is only a transitional stage, and evolution's next station is the 'Supermind'—where spiritual traditions looked back to a golden age or upward to otherworldly liberation, he looked forward: a new species of consciousness on this earth. Second, the founding of Integral Yoga: the traditional four paths (jnana, bhakti, karma, raja) all seek individual liberation out of the world; he synthesized them and added a fifth element—the descent of divine consciousness into the body and material life, aiming at transforming life on earth, not fleeing it. Third, the method of the triple transformation: psychic (the discovery of the inner soul), spiritual (opening to the higher planes), supramental (the descent that rewrites mind, life and body)—a staged, executable craft of world-transformation. Fourth, the theoretical breakthrough in the national movement: the first public demand for complete independence, nationalism stated as 'the work of God,' the Uttarpara speech turning the prison realization into the political theology of 'all is Brahman'—four decades of politics and four decades of seclusion joined in one man, revolutionary and seer in a single body. Fifth, textual institution-building: The Life Divine, The Synthesis of Yoga, The Human Cycle, and the 24,000-line epic Savitri form a self-standing system, carried to this day by two institutions, the Sri Aurobindo Ashram and Auroville. Costs and limits: the supramental descent is criticized as an unfalsifiable metaphysical promise (he himself conceded the third transformation 'might take centuries'); his life is repeatedly flattened by later spiritual marketing, the revolutionary half of his double life often occluded; and 'nationalism as religion' has been appropriated by many hands since.

## Unique thinking
他思维方式的底色是'演化即灵性、灵性即工程：神性不是彼岸而是尚未抵达的此岸前方——思想的任务不是描述已成的世界而是为未成的世界开路'。六个标志性动作。其一，'内卷—演化双程法'——任何'突现的新事物'都不是无中生有：在物质中沉睡的必在演化中觉醒，向下内卷多深、向上涌现就有多远的对应阶梯。其二，'过渡存在定位法'——把'当前状态'重新定义为'中间阶段'：人不完备不是缺陷而是工件，判断任何现状先问'它处在哪个过程上'。其三，'三重转化分程法'——把不可企及的大目标拆成三个递进可执行的层级，每层有自己的入口、障碍与完成判据，逐层打通。其四，'整体统摄法'——拒绝把任一真实面向（知识、爱、行动、身体）当成全部：四道综合、身心智灵四层并修，综合不是并列而是让每一道在下一次转化中各就其位。其五，'降入法'——一切高级状态的检验不在其上的清晰而在其下的落地：意识必须降入最稠密的物质层才算完成，'高而未降'只是体验不是成就。其六，'人类统一收束法'——个体的完满与整体的统一是同一件事的两面，个人修行最终必须收束为世界性建制（修道院、奥罗维尔）。代价与极限：体系高度依赖'内卷'这个形上学前提，前提若不成立则整套演化阶梯悬空；'超心智'词汇的可操作性在弟子传承中递减；转化时间表（数世纪）使体系在实践中依赖象征性建制而非可验证成果。

## Unique thinking (EN)
The bedrock of his thinking: evolution is spirituality and spirituality is engineering—the Divine is not beyond but ahead, in a not-yet-arrived region of this world; thought's task is not to describe a finished world but to open the road for an unfinished one. Six signature moves. First, the involution-evolution double pass—nothing genuinely new arises from nothing: what sleeps in matter must waken in evolution; the depth of the downward involution sets the length of the upward ladder. Second, transitional-being positioning—redefine 'the current state' as 'an intermediate stage': human incompleteness is not a defect but a work in progress; of any present condition first ask 'at what point in which process does it stand.' Third, the staged triple transformation—break an unreachable great goal into three ascending executable levels, each with its own entrance, obstacle and completion test, cleared level by level. Fourth, integral synthesis—refuse to make any real facet (knowledge, love, action, body) the whole: synthesize the four paths, cultivate body, life, mind and spirit together; synthesis is not juxtaposition but giving each path its station in the next transformation. Fifth, the descent test—the proof of any higher state lies not in its clarity above but in its landing below: consciousness must descend into the densest material layer to count as achieved; 'high but not descended' is experience, not attainment. Sixth, human-unity closure—the perfection of the individual and the unity of the whole are two faces of one thing; personal practice must finally close into world-institutions (the ashram, Auroville). Costs and limits: the whole ladder hangs on the metaphysical premise of involution—without it the stages float; the operationality of 'supramental' vocabulary thins down the line of successors; and a timetable of centuries leaves the system resting on symbolic institutions rather than verifiable results.

## M-AUR-001 内卷演化双程法 / Involution-Evolution Double-Pass Method

- Definition: 演化本体论方法论/向下内卷与向上涌现互为倒放：物质中沉睡着生命，生命中沉睡着心智，心智中沉睡着超心智——新事物是旧沉睡物的觉醒，内卷深度决定涌现阶梯的长度
- Definition (EN): Evolutionary-ontology methodology/involution downward and evolution upward are one pass played in reverse: life sleeps in matter, mind in life, Supermind in mind—the new is the awakening of an old sleeper, and the depth of involution sets the length of the emergence ladder
- Source: 《神圣人生论》第二部论'宇宙与实在'及'演化与involution'诸章；《瑜伽综合论》论演化的目的；《阿雅亚》月刊连载原初语境
- Key quote: 心智不是演化的最后一级——在它之上还有更高的意识等着涌现：内卷入物质者，必自物质中涌现。 / Mind is not the last grade of evolution—above it waits a higher consciousness yet to emerge: what was involuted into matter must emerge out of matter.
- Process:
  - 对'突现的新事物'提问：它真的是无中生有，还是某种已在场之物的觉醒？——追认其'内卷存根'（哪些潜能早已沉睡在场）
  - 重建双程阶梯：把当前状态放回下行与上行两个方向中——它由哪些层隐没而来，又将向哪些层涌现而去
  - 推演下一站：既然每一层都沉睡着上一层的潜能，问'当前层的深处还沉睡着什么'——把过渡性写成结构位置
  - 给演化留阻力账：识别涌现路径上的'黑暗力量'（惯性、返祖、偶然），不把方向论写成无痛的进步论
- Process (EN):
  - Interrogate the 'emergent new': is it truly ex nihilo or the awakening of something already present?—trace its involutionary deposits (which potentials long slept in place)
  - Rebuild the double-pass ladder: situate the current state in both directions—out of which layers it involuted, toward which layers it may evolve
  - Project the next station: since each layer sleeps on the potential of the next, ask 'what still sleeps in the depths of the current layer'—write transitionality as a structural position
  - Book the resistance: identify the 'dark forces' on the emergence path (inertia, atavism, chance) so the doctrine of direction does not become a painless progressivism

## M-AUR-003 三重转化分程法 / Triple-Transformation Staging Method

- Definition: 目标工程化方法论/把不可企及的大目标拆成三个递进层级，每层有入口、障碍与完成判据——次第不可跳越、总目标不折价：给不可能的目标画甘特图
- Definition (EN): Goal-engineering methodology/break the unreachable goal into three ascending levels, each with entrance, obstacle and completion test—the sequence cannot be skipped and the total aim is undiluted: a Gantt chart for the impossible
- Source: 《瑜伽综合论》论三重转化与修行次第；《神圣人生论》第三部论'走向更高的意识'；《母亲的议程》对转化次第的实测记录
- Key quote: 转化的路径有三程：先寻得心灵，再开向高处的光，最后让光降入泥土——每一程都不可跳越，每一程都以下一程为完成。 / The path of transformation has three stages: first find the soul, then open to the light above, last let the light descend into the clay—no stage may be skipped, and each is completed only by the next.
- Process:
  - 把总目标显式写出，并承认其'一次性不可执行'——写下'为什么单次跳跃不够'（哪些层面单跳触及不到）
  - 划分递进层级：每一层只解决总目标的一个切片，并为其定义入口（怎么进入）、主要障碍（怎么失败）与完成判据（怎么算过）
  - 声明次第与依赖：明确哪些层级必须先于哪些层级，以及越级的已知风险——把'不可跳越'写成规程而非建议
  - 设验证与交付点：每层有可观察的阶段性完成物，总目标设长期里程碑——既保持目标不折价，又让进度可被检验
- Process (EN):
  - Write out the total goal explicitly and concede it 'unexecutable in one pass'—state why a single leap is insufficient (which layers a leap cannot reach)
  - Divide the ascending levels: let each level solve one slice of the total goal, defining its entrance (how to enter), chief obstacle (how it fails) and completion test (what counts as cleared)
  - Declare sequence and dependency: make explicit which levels must precede which, and the known risks of skipping—write 'non-skippable' as protocol, not advice
  - Set verification and delivery points: each level gets observable stage deliverables, the total goal long-range milestones—keeping the aim undiluted while making progress testable
- Representative cases:
  - 《瑜伽综合论》的三程立法：心灵—灵性—超心智各配入口与判据——'神圣人生'第一次获得工程化的路径
  - 对'中间地带'的警告：未完成心灵转化而强开上层者得到混杂体验而非转化——次第规程的负面教材驱动立法
  - 1926 悉地日与长期工程：超心降入物理被宣布后，后续二十四年用于第三程的静默工作——分程法的时间尺度自觉
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The triple-stage legislation of The Synthesis of Yoga: psychic, spiritual and supramental each given entrance and test—the first engineering path for 'divine life on earth'
  - The warning about the 'intermediate zone': forcing the higher levels without the psychic work yields mixed experiences, not transformation—the legislation driven by negative exhibits of skipped stages
  - Siddhi Day 1926 and the long project: after announcing the Overmind's descent into the physical, the next twenty-four years given to silent work on the third stage—the staging method's self-conscious timescale
- Modern applications:
  - 组织级大目标的分程治理：把'数字化转型'这类十年目标拆为三程（流程电子化—数据驱动—智能化），每程配入口、障碍与验收判据，并明文禁止跳程
  - 个人大目标的反挫败设计：把'成为领域顶尖'拆为三程并接受'第三程或需十年'——既不因遥远而放弃，也不因急躁而越级（越级对应'拔苗式学习'的失败模式）
  - AI 能力路线图的阶段化：把'通用智能'类总目标拆为有依赖关系的层级，每层有判据与已知风险清单——直接复用'次第不可跳越'与'中间地带'两个概念
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Stage governance for organization-level goals: break a ten-year aim like 'digital transformation' into three stages (process digitization—data-driven—intelligent), each with entrance, obstacle and acceptance test, skipping expressly forbidden
  - Anti-frustration design for personal goals: break 'becoming a leader in a field' into three stages and accept that 'the third may take a decade'—neither abandoning the aim for its distance nor skipping levels out of impatience (skipping maps to the known failure mode of hothouse learning)
  - Staged roadmaps for AI capability: decompose goals like 'general intelligence' into dependent levels with tests and risk lists per level—reusing 'non-skippable sequence' and 'intermediate zone' as working concepts
- Related modes: M-AUR-001（内卷演化双程法——分程的路线图来自双程阶梯：'下一层'由演化结构给定）; M-AUR-005（降入检验法——第三程的完成判据正是降入：高而不降不算过）; M-WJM-004（威廉·詹姆斯·实用主义兑现检验法——远缘对照：詹姆斯以兑现价值检验观念并坚持多元反等级，奥罗宾多给出严格的层级图谱与次第——'多元并置'与'层级分程'的两种意识处理）; M-CC-021（朱熹与阿奎那对照组·格物穷理渐进法——跨域对照：以渐进积累通达超越性真理、为顿悟立法次第，与三重转化的分程工艺同构——两种'渐修至顿'的工程学）

## M-AUR-004 整体统摄法 / Integral-Synthesis Method

- Definition: 系统综合方法论/四道各有所见亦各有局限——综合不是并列折中而是升层统摄：让每一道在更高的统一中各就其位，产出任何单道单独给不出的结果
- Definition (EN): Systemic-synthesis methodology/each path sees something and each has limits—synthesis is not juxtaposed compromise but ascent-level integration: give each path its station in a higher unity and yield what no single path could give alone
- Source: 《瑜伽综合论》第一部分论'四道综合'的原则；《神圣人生论》对东西方知识体系的统摄处理；《薄伽梵歌论》对行动—知识—虔爱三道在'有为而无执'中的统一
- Key quote: 真正的综合不是各让一步，而是升一层让各方全保——每一道都是整体的一个器官，砍去任何一道，整体即残。 / True synthesis does not split differences but ascends a level keeping all parties whole—each path is an organ of the whole; cut one and the whole is maimed.
- Process:
  - 盘点'各有所见'：列出竞争各方各自真实抓住的面向——先承认每一方的局部正确，再谈综合
  - 找出或构建更高的统一结构：能同时安放各方的那个框架是什么？——若找不到现成的，就立法新建（如'意识演化'统摄科学与灵性）
  - 为每一方写分工规则：它在统一结构中负责什么、边界在哪、与谁衔接——'各就其位'要能回答'这一方何时让位'
  - 检验扩容产出：综合后是否出现了任何一方单独给不出的新目标或新能力？——没有扩容产出的'综合'即退回并列，重新升层
- Process (EN):
  - Inventory what each party truly sees: list the real facet each competitor holds—grant every party its local correctness before speaking of synthesis
  - Find or build the higher unifying structure: which framework can house all parties?—if none exists, legislate a new one (as 'the evolution of consciousness' housed science and spirit)
  - Write the division-of-stations rule: what each party is responsible for in the unified structure, its boundaries, its hand-offs—'each at its station' must answer 'when does this party yield'
  - Test for enlargement: does the synthesis yield a new goal or capability none of the parties could give alone?—an 'integration' without enlargement has fallen back into juxtaposition; ascend a level again
- Representative cases:
  - 《瑜伽综合论》的四道统摄：智、爱、业、行（身体）各就其位——'整体瑜伽'得名于此操作
  - 《薄伽梵歌论》的重新解读：把'行动—知识—虔爱'三道统一于'有为而无执'——以统摄取代传统'哪道最尊'的裁决
  - 东西方知识的统摄处理：科学与灵性被安放在'意识演化'的同一结构中——《神圣人生论》的写作结构本身即示范
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The fourfold synthesis of The Synthesis of Yoga: knowledge, love, action and the body each at its station—the operation from which 'Integral Yoga' takes its name
  - The rereading of the Gita: knowledge, devotion and action unified in 'work without clinging'—synthesis replacing the traditional adjudication of 'which path is supreme'
  - The integral treatment of Eastern and Western knowledge: science and spirituality housed in the one structure of the evolution of consciousness—The Life Divine's very architecture as demonstration
- Modern applications:
  - 学科交叉与团队组建：把竞争方法论（定量/定性、工程/设计）放进更高的统一问题结构并写分工规则，以'扩容产出'检验是否真综合——防止' interdisciplinary'沦为并列贴标签
  - 产品路线的多目标治理：性能/成本/体验各有所见——升层到'用户任务的完成结构'里各就其位，而非在原层各让一步
  - 个人能力栈设计：'深度专精'与'广度通识'之争以统摄处理：专精为照明、通识为联结、输出为外显——并为三者写清衔接与让位规则
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Interdisciplinary team design: put rival methodologies (quant/qual, engineering/design) inside a higher unifying problem structure with written division-of-stations rules, and test for enlargement output—so 'interdisciplinary' does not degenerate into labeled juxtaposition
  - Multi-objective product governance: performance, cost and experience each see something—ascend to 'the structure of the user's task completion' and give each its station, rather than conceding steps at the original level
  - Personal capability-stack design: treat the depth-vs-breadth debate by synthesis—specialization as illumination, generalism as bond, output as expression—with clear hand-off and yield rules among the three
- Related modes: M-AUR-003（三重转化分程法——统摄给出'哪些要兼修'，分程给出'按什么次序'）; M-AUR-009（对话综合法——统摄在'文本与思想交锋'层面的操作）; M-HEG-001（黑格尔·辩证扬弃三段法——近缘对照：黑格尔的对立统一也升层保全双方（Aufhebung），但黑格尔的升层由矛盾逻辑驱动、终点固定；奥罗宾多的升层由'整体全景'驱动、结构开放——两种综合的引擎）; M-JBS-006（凯恩斯·多方案综合法——跨域对照：凯恩斯综合货币与财政两套政策工具，奥罗宾多综合四道修行——同为'拒绝在既有选项里选边'的工程，一在政策一在修行）
- Representative figures:
  - 奥罗宾多 / Sri Aurobindo: 本模式的原型——以'整体'为名把四道修行与东西方知识立法为升层统摄 / the prototype—legislated the four paths and the two hemispheres of knowledge into ascent-level synthesis under the banner of 'integral'

  - 黑格尔 / G. W. F. Hegel: 哲学对照——扬弃（Aufhebung）同样升层保全对立双方，是统摄操作的严格哲学版本 / the philosophical parallel—sublation likewise ascends a level keeping both opposites, the strict philosophical version of the synthesis operation

## M-AUR-005 降入检验法 / Descent-Verification Method

- Definition: 成果验证方法论/高级状态的唯一有效检验是降入最稠密日常层并改写它——上层体验再清晰，'高而未降'只是体验不是成就
- Definition (EN): Achievement-verification methodology/the only valid test of a higher state is descending into the densest everyday layer and rewriting it—however clear the higher experience, 'high but not descended' is experience, not attainment
- Source: 《瑜伽综合论》论'降入身体'与瑜伽的转化目标；《神圣人生论》论'神圣生命的落地'；晚年书信'把瑜伽带进细胞'（Letters on Yoga）
- Key quote: 除非光降入泥土并改写泥土，否则它只是高处的一场照明——把瑜伽带进细胞。 / Unless the light descends into the clay and rewrites the clay, it is only an illumination upstairs—bring the yoga into the cells.
- Process:
  - 识别系统的'最稠密层'：问'哪里最难改变'——身体的习惯、组织的琐务流程、财务的现金流，找到变化阻力最大处
  - 把高层成果投影到稠密层：该成果在这个层上应留下什么痕迹？——写出具体的可观察改变（行为、流程、数字）
  - 执行痕迹检验：到稠密层实地检查——痕迹为零或只在上层可见，即判'高而未降'，成果记为'体验'而非'成就'
  - 规划降入工程：为高层成果设计下行路线（哪些载体、哪些步骤把它带进最稠密层），并以稠密层的改写度作为最终验收
- Process (EN):
  - Identify the system's densest layer: ask 'where is change hardest'—bodily habit, the organization's routine procedures, financial cash flow; locate the point of maximal resistance
  - Project the higher achievement onto the dense layer: what traces should it leave there?—write out the concrete observable changes (behaviors, procedures, numbers)
  - Run the trace test: inspect the dense layer on the ground—zero traces, or traces visible only upstairs, means 'high but not descended'; record the result as 'experience,' not 'attainment'
  - Plan the descent work: design the downward route for the higher achievement (which carriers, which steps bring it into the densest layer), and accept the dense layer's degree of rewriting as the final验收
- Representative cases:
  - 对传统'高层体验'判据的立法性否决：三摩地与狂喜被降格为'体验'——唯一的成就判据改为降入身体与日常的改写
  - 晚年'把瑜伽带进细胞'：把体系的最稠密检验层一路下压到生理层——降入检验的极致版本
  - 《母亲的议程》的实测记录：三十年逐日记录降入身体过程的反弹、失败与微进展——降入检验费时性的第一手数据
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The legislative veto of the traditional 'higher experience' criterion: samadhi and ecstasy demoted to 'experience'—the only attainment criterion rewritten as descent and the rewriting of body and daily life
  - The late-life 'bring the yoga into the cells': pressing the system's densest test-layer all the way down to the physiological—the extreme version of the descent test
  - The field records of The Agenda of the Mother: thirty years of daily entries on the descent into the body, its backlashes, failures and micro-progress—the first-hand data on how long descent takes
- Modern applications:
  - 战略落地的验收标准：战略共识会（高层体验）不算完成，唯当战略降入周例会、考核指标与一线流程并改写它们——'痕迹检验'直接用于判断变革是否真实发生
  - 学习成果验证：'听懂了/震撼了'不算学会，唯当知识降入'最稠密层'（默认操作习惯、每天的产出物）——把'体验与成就'区分制度化
  - 制度落地评估：新制度降入基层日常操作（表格、审批动线）才算建成——'高而未降'的制度即为文件
- Modern applications (EN):

## M-AUR-006 层级意识地图法 / Consciousness-Level Mapping Method

  - Consult the layer's failure-mode library: what typically fails at this layer (over-reduction at analysis, over-certainty at intuition)—guard against in-layer failure before considering ascent

## M-AUR-007 行动瑜伽伏藏法 / Action-Yoga Entrustment Method

- Definition: 事业交接方法论/使命转向时对旧事业执行'封存交托'：确信成果已不依赖本人后，把事业转为自主续存状态并转身投入更高优先级——不推倒、不恋栈、不弃前功
- Definition (EN): Enterprise-handover methodology/at a mission pivot, execute 'seal and entrust' on the old enterprise: certain the outcome no longer depends on you, convert it to a self-sustaining state and turn to the higher priority—demolishing nothing, clinging to nothing, abandoning no prior gain
- Source: 《奥罗宾多生平》论 1910 年赴本地治里之抉择（'奉上界之命'）；书信集对'为何退出政治'的自辩；乌塔帕拉演说对'事业归于大势'的表述

- Process:
  - 执行'依赖性审计'：旧事业的成败现在依赖多少本人不可替代的投入？——若关键路径已可由他人与大势承担，记录依据
  - 设计封存结构：把事业转为'他人可续、大势可成'的自主状态——明确交托对象（人、制度、已注入的精神），不留悬空
  - 完成转身立法：以更高优先级或更高召唤为转身提供合法性（对内对外都讲清楚），并声明'非弃乃托'以保全旧事业的名义价值
  - 迁移资产清单：列出旧事业中可迁移的技能、网络与信誉，显式接入新工作——防止转身变成净损失
- Process (EN):
  - Run the dependence audit: how much of the old enterprise's success now depends on irreplaceable personal input?—if the critical path can be carried by others and the trend, document the grounds
  - Design the sealing structure: convert the enterprise to a self-sustaining state 'continuable by others, completable by the trend'—name the entrusted objects (people, institutions, the spirit already instilled), leaving nothing suspended
  - Legislate the turn: ground the pivot in a higher priority or calling (stated clearly inwardly and outwardly), declaring 'not abandonment but entrustment' to preserve the old enterprise's nominal value
  - Move the asset list: inventory the transferable skills, networks and reputation from the old work and wire them explicitly into the new—so the turn is not a net loss
- Representative cases:
  - 1910 年赴本地治里：判'独立已由大势保证'后封存革命事业——报纸交同侪、网络还其逻辑、本人转入静默工作，转身全程不弃笔力与组织力
  - 《业瑜伽》《法》的交托：离岸前把刊物明确移交（尼维蒂塔接编）——封存结构中'交托对象明确'的示范
  - 革命经验向写作的迁移：民族运动时期的政论笔力全数转入《阿雅亚》六年的密集产出——'不弃前功'的资产迁移实证
- Representative cases (EN):

  - The entrustment of Karmayogin and Dharma: before leaving, the journals explicitly handed over (Nivedita taking the editorship)—a model of 'named entrusted objects' in the sealing structure
  - The migration of revolutionary experience into writing: the polemical pen of the national years carried whole into six dense years of the Arya—empirical proof of 'no abandonment of prior gains'
- Modern applications:
  - 创始人退居的规范流程：先做依赖性审计（公司离开我是否仍能成），再设计封存结构（明确的交托对象与机制），最后公开立法转身——为创始人退出提供'非弃乃托'的叙事与操作框架

## M-AUR-008 静默权变法 / Silent-Force Method

- Definition: 影响力方法论/退出前台后以静默力量持续作用于全局——通信、原则、精神影响为常态，干预保留为按需触发的例外：领袖不在场而原则在场
- Definition (EN): Influence methodology/after leaving the front stage, keep acting on the whole through silent force—letters, principles and spiritual influence as the norm, intervention held as an on-demand exception: the leader absent, the principle present
- Source: 书信集（Letters on Yoga）对'静默行动'的表述；1940 年与 1942 年两次公开表态文本；《奥罗宾多生平》论'退隐中的注视与干预'
- Key quote: 我不在场，但并非无所作为——静默的作用比喧哗的干预更深入：原则代替我出席。 / I am absent, but not inactive—the silent action reaches deeper than noisy intervention: the principle attends in my place.
- Process:
  - 设计静默通道：退出前台前，先建好不在场也能持续作用的结构（书面原则、定期仪式、可传阅的通信）——使'退'不等于'断'
  - 预设干预判据：写明何种级别的事件必须打破静默（存亡级、原则级），以及以什么身份发声——把'例外'制度化以防失语或滥用
  - 建立组织防火墙：静默影响不得经由组织利益传导——个人原则与机构立场分离，保证影响的纯洁性与可信度
  - 克制执行：静默是常态，触发是例外——每次触发前后都对照预设判据复盘，防止'例外'膨胀为'新常态'
- Process (EN):
  - Design the silent channels: before leaving the front stage, build the structures that keep working in absence (written principles, periodic rites, circulate-able letters)—so 'withdrawal' is not 'severance'
  - Preset the intervention criteria: write down which order of event must break the silence (existential, principled) and in what capacity one speaks—institutionalize the 'exception' against both speechlessness and abuse
  - Build the organizational firewall: the silent influence must not travel through organizational interest—keep personal principle and institutional stance separate to preserve the influence's purity and credibility
  - Execute with restraint: silence is the norm, the trigger the exception—review each trigger against the preset criteria before and after, lest the 'exception' swell into a 'new normal'
- Representative cases:
  - 四十年退隐的结构：数千通书信、每年四次达善、对世界局势的持续注视——静默影响通道的完整建制
  - 1940 年致信支持盟军：以个人身份在文明存亡节点打破静默——干预例外的唯一档案化示范
  - 修道院政治防火墙：坚拒国大党会议主席等公职请求、修道院与政治无涉——静默影响不被组织利益污染的制度保障
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The forty-year withdrawal's structure: thousands of letters, four darshan days a year, a continuous watch on world affairs—the full apparatus of the silent channels
  - The 1940 letter in support of the Allies: breaking the silence, in a personal capacity, at a civilizational juncture—the sole archival demonstration of the intervention exception
  - The ashram's political firewall: refusing the Congress presidency and keeping the ashram apart from politics—the institutional safeguard keeping silent influence uncontaminated by organized interest
- Modern applications:
  - 创始人/高管退居二线的影响力设计：书面原则与定期沟通为常态影响，'重大节点发声'预设判据并保持个人身份与公司立场分离——避免'垂帘听政'与'彻底失联'两个失败形态
  - 公共知识分子的立场管理：以系统性著作为静默影响主体，把'就重大事件表态'留为有判据的例外——保护长期公信力
  - 董事会与顾问的'不干预治理'：明确'何时必须发声'的触发清单（合规崩坏、存亡决策），其余时候以原则文件而非个人干预起作用
- Modern applications (EN):

  - Position management for public intellectuals: systematic writing as the main body of silent influence, leaving 'statement on major events' as a criterion-governed exception—protecting long-run credibility

## M-AUR-009 对话综合法 / Dialogical-Synthesis Method
