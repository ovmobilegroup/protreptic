# 世宗大王 Sejong — H-SEJ-001（Sejong the Great, 世宗大王）

- 编号: H-SEJ-001
- 年代: 1397-1450
- 学派/身份: 朝鲜王朝第四代君主与儒教国家制度的总设计师 / 文字发明家（训民正音——东亚唯一由在位君主亲创的表音文字）/ 实学精神的先行者（农书、医书、天文观测全部'实测本土化'）/ 科举-官僚体系内的大规模知识工程组织者

## 生平纪事 (era)
1397-1450 : 1397 生于汉阳（首尔），太宗李芳远第三子，幼名忠宁——性情温厚却体弱多病，少时读书'手不释卷至目疾'，太宗反而不予储位之望——1418 太宗让位，忠宁以次子（兄让宁世君因狂乱被废）继位，是为世宗——即位头四年实权仍在太上王太宗之手，1422 太宗死方得亲政——亲政即设集贤殿，把年轻学者养在自己身边：问对、修典、议政三合一——1419 己亥东征扫荡对马倭寇；1431 推行调剂法（义仓改革）救荒——1433 年颁行《农事直说》，派农业官下乡实测各地气候土性写成本土农书——1434 铸'甲寅字'金属活字（此前 1403 太宗已有癸未字，世宗两度改良）——1443 年创制训民正音二十八字，1446 年正式颁布：'国之语音异乎中国，与文字不相流通，故愚民有所欲言而终不得伸其情者多矣……新制二十八字，欲使人人易习，便于日用耳'——文武百官与两班士林激烈反对（崔万理《驳正音疏》），世宗不为所动——同一年代：测定七政仪与日晷、编《七政算》、创水运浑天仪、定《医方类聚》365 卷、《乡药集成方》、令容像所绘制历代君臣像、设书云观观测天象——1442 年起目疾与风湿日重，仍以口授令侍读代笔——1450 年正月崩于永膺大君府，遗命'不迁葬'简葬——朝鲜五百年文教制度，皆此三十年的骨架——后世尊称'大王'（the Great），朝鲜历史上唯一获此谥号者；韩文节（10 月 9 日）即纪念训民正音颁行

### Era (English)
1397-1450 : born in Hanyang (Seoul) in 1397, third son of King Taejong, the boy Chungnyeong was mild but sickly—read until his eyes failed, and his father held out no hope of the throne—in 1418 Taejong abdicated; after his elder brother the Crown Prince was deposed for derangement, Chungnyeong, the second son, succeeded as Sejong—the first four years of his reign real power remained with the retired Taejong, and only in 1422, on his father's death, did he rule in his own name—at once he founded the Hall of Worthies (Jiphyeonjeon), raising young scholars at his side: inquiry, compilation, and policy combined—1419 the Gihae eastern expedition crushed the wako pirates of Tsushima; in 1431 the Joryak granary reform saved harvests—in 1433 came the Nongsa Jikseol ('Straight Talk on Farming'), written by agricultural officers sent down to measure each region's soil and climate—the cast-bronze printing type of 1434 ('Gapjin type') improved the font his father had cast in 1403—in 1443 he created the twenty-eight letters of Hunminjeongeum, promulgated in 1446: 'The speech of our country differs from that of China and does not match its characters; therefore the foolish common people, though they wish to speak, cannot at last express their meaning… I have made twenty-eight letters, wanting everyone to learn them easily and use them daily'—ministers and scholar-officials fiercely opposed it (Choe Manri's memorial of rebuttal); Sejong did not yield—in the same years: the armillary sphere and sundial measured, the Chiljeongsan astronomical tables compiled, the water-driven orrery invented, the 365-volume Medical Encyclopedia and the Treasury of Native Medicines ordered, portraits of kings and ministers painted, the Royal Observatory set to work—from 1442 eye disease and rheumatism worsened, yet he dictated to scribes—he died in the first month of 1450 in the mansion of Prince Yeongeung, ordering a simple burial—the skeleton of five hundred years of Korean learning is these thirty years—posterity titles him 'the Great,' the only king of Joseon so honored; Hangul Day (October 9) commemorates the promulgation

## 历史地位 (historical_significance)
李裪（1397-1450），朝鲜王朝第四代王，庙号世宗，朝鲜历史上唯一的'大王'。他的历史地位由四个工程锚定。其一，文字创制：训民正音二十八字由他亲自参定原理并主持颁行——在'文字即士族特权'的汉字文明圈内，他公开宣布文字的目的'欲使人人易习，便于日用'，把书写权从两班下放到全民；这是人类历史上罕见的由最高统治者亲自设计并顶住士林反对强制推广的表音文字工程，五百年后成为韩国的国家认同基座。其二，'实测'治国：他拒绝照抄《齐民要术》式的中原农书，派官下乡量测各地气候土性写成本土《农事直说》；编医书先查'乡药'本土药性；修天文先铸浑天仪、日晷亲测，再编《七政算》——一切知识以'验于本土'为准绳，是实证方法在国家层面的首次系统实践。其三，知识工程的官僚化：集贤殿学士、观象监、校书馆、容像所……他为每类知识设立专职机构与人才管道，把'著书立说'变成可持续的国家职能——金属活字两度改良、365 卷《医方类聚》成书，都是制度产粮而非个人兴致。其四，民本 ruler 的日常化：调剂法救荒、义仓改定、免徭恤刑、'为民上者，不可不先知民事'的批答——他把'民惟邦本'从经典口号做成可操作的行政纪律。代价与张力同样真实：崔万理为首的两班反对表音文字'有违前例'，世宗以王权强推——文字的民主化依赖君主的集权；己亥东征的血债、对女真与倭寇的强硬，与他的文教理想并存；'言语与文字不相流通'的痛苦由他终结，却也把朝鲜的汉语汉籍依赖再延续数百年。但方法论遗产极清晰：一个君主可以把自己国家的知识生产设计成一套'发现问题—设立机构—实测本土—颁布推广—留监督'的完整流水线，而全部工程的验收标准只有一条——愚夫愚妇用不用得上。

### Historical Significance (English)
Yi Do (1397-1450), the fourth king of Joseon, temple name Sejong, the only 'the Great' in Korean history. His standing rests on four engineering feats. First, script creation: the twenty-eight letters of Hunminjeongeum were personally worked out in principle by him and promulgated under his authority—inside the Chinese-character civilization, where literacy was a yangban privilege, he publicly declared the script's purpose 'wanting everyone to learn it easily and use it daily,' devolving writing from the aristocracy to the whole people; it is a rare case in human history of a supreme ruler personally designing a phonetic script and forcing it past the opposition of his own scholar-officials, and five centuries later it is the foundation of Korean national identity. Second, governing by field-measurement: refusing to copy medieval Chinese farming manuals, he sent officers down to measure each region's climate and soil and write a native 'Straight Talk on Farming'; medical compilations began by checking native herbs; astronomy was fixed by casting his own armillary spheres and sundials before the tables were drawn—every body of knowledge tested against local reality, the first systematic state-level practice of the empirical method. Third, the bureaucratization of knowledge production: the Hall of Worthies, the Observatory, the proofreading office, the portrait bureau—institutions and talent pipelines for each kind of knowledge, turning scholarship into a sustainable function of state—the two improved metal-type fonts and the 365-volume Medical Encyclopedia are institutional harvests, not private hobbies. Fourth, the daily practice of a people-first ruler: granary reform, tax relief, lenient punishment, and the rescript 'the ruler of the people must first know the people's affairs'—'the people are the root of the state' made into administrative discipline. The tensions were real: Choe Manri and the literati fought the new script as a breach of precedent, and Sejong pushed it through with royal power—the democratization of writing rode on monarchical centralization; the blood debt of the Tsushima expedition and his hard line with Jurchens and pirates coexisted with his civil ideal; the pain of 'speech not matching writing' he ended, though dependence on Chinese books endured for centuries. Yet the methodological legacy is crisp: a ruler can design his country's knowledge production as a full pipeline—spot the problem, found the institution, measure locally, promulgate, keep oversight—whose sole acceptance test is whether the humblest man and woman can use it.

## 独特思维方式 (unique_thinking)
他的思维方式的底色是'一切知识必须验于本土、达于日用'：世宗的一生是一部'如何把王权变成知识工程局'的教程。五个标志性动作。其一，'发明之前先诊断'——创制正音之前，他先写下病案：'国之语音异乎中国，与文字不相流通，故愚民有所欲言而终不得伸其情者多矣'——不是先造工具再找用途，而是先把'谁的痛苦、痛在哪里'写成需求文档；其二，'实测先于文献'——农书不抄中原，派官下乡量气候土性；医书不抄《证类本草》，先查乡药；天文不抄大统历，先铸仪亲测——把'验于本土'定为一切知识的第一道关卡；其三，'机构承载知识'——集贤殿养学者、观象监制仪器、校书馆刊书籍，他不以个人聪明治学，而以机构让知识可持续地产出与传承；其四，'分级沟通'——对两班用汉字文言颁《农事直说》，对庶民用正音谚解颁法典佛经——同一内容按受众重新编码，语言本身被他当作治理界面；其五，'顶住精英否决'——崔万理上疏斥正音'有违前例'，他逐条批驳后仍强令推行：真有用的发明，推广期必然遭遇旧制度的免疫反应，君主的职责是做免疫反应的阻尼器。代价与极限：文字民主化借王权强推，为后世留下'开明专制'的辩论；东征对马与其民本理想并置；他个人的集权使一切工程系于一人，世宗死后正音一度沉寂近五十年才被中宗朝重新启用。但他给自己的判词刻在文字颁行的本身：'欲使人人易习，便于日用'——把'人人'与'日用'四个字写进国家工程的验收单。

### Unique Thinking (English)
The bedrock of his thinking: all knowledge must be verified locally and reach daily use—Sejong's life is a tutorial on 'turning royal power into a bureau of knowledge engineering.' Five signature moves. First, diagnose before you invent—before creating the script he wrote the case history: 'the speech of our country differs from that of China and does not match its characters; the foolish common people, though they wish to speak, cannot express their meaning'—not a tool seeking a use, but a written statement of whose pain, where. Second, measurement before citation—farming manuals not copied from China but field-measured by dispatched officers; medicines not copied from the great Chinese herbals but checked against native drugs; astronomy not copied from the Yuan calendar but fixed by instruments he had cast—the test 'verified in our own land' stands as the first gate for every body of knowledge. Third, institutions carry knowledge—the Hall of Worthies raised scholars, the Observatory cast instruments, the proofreading bureau printed books; he did not lecture from personal brilliance but made knowledge flow through institutions. Fourth, tiered communication—to the literati, Chinese-script texts; to the commoners, hangul glosses of laws and sutras—the same content re-encoded by audience; language itself became an interface of governance. Fifth, withstand elite veto—Choe Manri's memorial condemned the script as breach of precedent; Sejong answered it point by point and enforced it anyway: a genuinely useful invention always triggers the immune reaction of the old order, and the sovereign's duty is to be that reaction's damper. Limits: the democratization of writing relied on centralized royal power, leaving the 'enlightened autocracy' debate to posterity; the Tsushima expedition sits beside his people-first ideal; and all the engineering leaned on one man—after his death the script fell dormant for nearly fifty years before being revived. But his own verdict is engraved in the promulgation itself: 'wanting everyone to learn it easily and use it daily'—'everyone' and 'daily use' written into the acceptance form of a state project.

### 1. M-SEJ-001 日用编码法 / Daily-Use Encoding Method

**定义**：设计方法论/发明的验收标准不是技术先进而是最底层的人用不用得上——先写'谁的痛苦、痛在哪里'的需求诊断，再让工具向'人人'与'日用'收敛

**Definition**：design methodology/an invention's acceptance test is not technical advance but whether the humblest person can use it—first write the needs diagnosis of whose pain hurts where, then converge the tool toward 'everyone' and 'daily use'

**历史现场 (domain)**：1443 年的汉阳：世宗已亲政二十一年，集贤殿的年轻学者们围绕着他。他做了一件东亚君主史上没有先例的事：不是修一部汉文典籍，而是发明一种给不识字的人用的文字。他先写诊断书——《训民正音》序言开篇不是讲音韵学，而是讲病案：'国之语音异乎中国，与文字不相流通，故愚民有所欲言而终不得伸其情者多矣……予为此悯然，新制二十八字，欲使人人易习，便于日用耳。'二十八个字母，每个字母几天可以学会；设计原理（象形喉舌齿唇、阴阳清浊）供学者研究，但验收标准只有一条：'人人易习，便于日用'。他的全部国家工程共用这条验收单：《农事直说》不写成理论农学，写成农民照着做的操作手册；法典颁正音谚解本，让被告听得懂自己犯了什么法；佛经谚解让僧尼信徒直接读。他甚至给日用反过来立法——教百姓用韩文写状子、记账、写家信，文字的用途清单本身就是推广方案

**Domain (English)**：Hanyang, 1443: Sejong has ruled in his own name for twenty-one years, young scholars of the Hall of Worthies around him. He does something without precedent among East Asian monarchs: instead of compiling another classical text, he invents a script for people who cannot read. He begins not with phonology but with a case history—the preface of Hunminjeongeum: 'The speech of our country differs from that of China and does not match its characters; the foolish common people, though they wish to speak, cannot at last express their meaning… I, grieved by this, have newly made twenty-eight letters, wanting everyone to learn them easily and use them daily.' Twenty-eight letters, each learnable in days; the design principles (pictographic throat-tongue-teeth-lip, yin-yang and clear-muddy) serve the scholars, but the acceptance test is one line: 'everyone learns it easily, uses it daily.' The same acceptance form runs his whole state project: Straight Talk on Farming is not theoretical agronomy but an operations manual farmers can follow; the legal code is issued in hangul gloss so the accused understands his own offense; sutras are glossed so monks and laity read directly. He even legislates for daily use in return—teaching people to write petitions, keep accounts, send letters in hangul; the list of uses is itself the diffusion plan

**方法论深描 (definition)**：世宗日用编码法的核心命题是：好发明的定义不在发明者一侧而在使用者一侧——'最底层的人能不能用它'是比'技术是否先进'更硬的验收标准；而要达到这个标准，工程必须从'谁的痛苦、痛在哪里'的需求诊断开始，而不是从'我能造什么'开始。操作纪律有四条。第一，先写病案再开药方：把问题写成'某某群体在某事上受苦'的具体陈述——世宗的'愚民有所欲言而终不得伸其情'就是范文；写不出具体受苦者与具体受阻环节的发明，多半是发明者的自我表达。第二，把验收标准压缩成一句可检验的话：'人人易习，便于日用'——人人（覆盖面）＋易习（学习成本）＋日用（使用频率），三个可测变量；验收单越短，工程越不跑偏。第三，按最不熟练的用户设计：字母表不是给音韵学家的论文而是给农夫的账本——用最不熟练的用户做设计基准，熟练者自然在包络内。第四，为使用立法：新工具不会自动被用，要给用途清单（状子、账本、家信、谚解经书）——用途清单就是推广方案。深意有三重。其一，它把'民主化'从政治概念翻译成设计约束：易学性、覆盖面、日用频率是三个工程指标，不靠宣言。其二，它解释了文字改革的成败史：同时期的其他表音尝试死在'给谁用'没有答案。其三，它对精英工程的免疫：凡是验收标准写成'先进''完善''体系化'的工程，最终受益者都会漂移回工程建造者。适用边界：适用于面向大众的工具、制度、教育产品；对专业工具（外科器械、精密仪器），'最笨用户'标准需换成'最紧张用户'标准。现代对应物：UX 设计的'新手优先'原则、plain language 运动、无障碍设计（curb-cut 效应）、联合国'简易语言'版文件。

**Method (English)**：The core proposition of the daily-use encoding method: a good invention is defined on the user's side, not the inventor's—'can the humblest person use it' is a harder acceptance test than 'is it technically advanced'; and to meet it, the project must begin from a needs diagnosis of whose pain hurts where, not from 'what can I build.' Four disciplines. First, write the case history before the prescription: state the problem as a concrete claim—a named group suffering a named obstruction; Sejong's 'the foolish common people cannot express their meaning' is the model essay; an invention that cannot name its sufferer is mostly the inventor's self-expression. Second, compress the acceptance test into one checkable sentence: 'everyone learns it easily, uses it daily'—coverage, learning cost, frequency of use, three measurable variables; the shorter the acceptance form, the less the project drifts. Third, design for the least practiced user: the alphabet is a farmer's ledger, not a phonologist's monograph—using the least practiced user as the design baseline, the skilled fall inside the envelope. Fourth, legislate for use: new tools are not used automatically—issue a list of uses (petitions, accounts, letters, glossed sutras); the list of uses is the diffusion plan. Three layers of depth. First, it translates 'democratization' from a political concept into design constraints: learnability, coverage, daily frequency are engineering metrics, not proclamations. Second, it explains the history of script reform: contemporary phonetic attempts died where 'for whom' had no answer. Third, it immunizes against elite engineering: projects whose acceptance test reads 'advanced,' 'complete,' 'systematic' drift their benefits back to the builders. Boundaries: it suits mass-facing tools, institutions, and education; for professional tools (surgical instruments, precision devices), swap 'the clumsiest user' for 'the most stressed user.' Modern counterparts: UX's beginner-first principle, the plain-language movement, accessibility design (the curb-cut effect), UN Easy Read documents.

**出处**：《训民正音》序（1446）'国之语音异乎中国'章；《训民正音解例》郑麟趾序；世宗实录二十五年九月关于正音颁布与崔万理驳论的记事

**关键概念 / Key Concepts**：
- 验收标准在使用者一侧
- 先写病案再开药方
- 人人易习便于日用三变量
- 按最不熟练用户设计
- 用途清单即推广方案
- 民主化是设计约束不是宣言

**金句**：欲使人人易习，便于日用耳——验收单上没有'先进'二字，只有'人人'与'日用'。
**Key quote**：Wanting everyone to learn it easily and use it daily—the acceptance form holds no word for 'advanced,' only 'everyone' and 'daily use.'

**操作流程 / Process**：
1. 写病案：把问题写成具体陈述——谁、在什么事上、被什么卡住；写不出具体受苦者的工程暂停
2. 定验收单：把成功标准压缩成一句可检验的话——覆盖面、学习成本、使用频率三个变量一个不缺
3. 基准下移：用最不熟练的用户做设计基准——熟练者的需求自动包含在内，反之不成立
4. 减法迭代：每轮审查砍掉'给专家看'的部分——字母表里的冗余、农书里的引经据典
5. 为使用立法：公布用途清单——状子、账本、家信，让'用'本身成为推广渠道
6. 回访验收：定期检查最底层用户的真实使用率而非满意度——使用率不达标的部件回炉

**Process (English)**：
1. Write the case history: state the problem concretely—who, at what task, blocked by what; suspend any project that cannot name its sufferer
2. Fix the acceptance form: compress success into one checkable sentence—coverage, learning cost, frequency, all three present
3. Lower the baseline: design for the least practiced user—the skilled fall inside automatically; the reverse never holds
4. Subtract by iteration: each round cuts what exists 'for the experts'—redundancies in the alphabet, classical citations in the farm manual
5. Legislate for use: publish the list of uses—petitions, accounts, letters—making use itself the diffusion channel
6. Audit by return visit: track the humblest users' real usage rate, not satisfaction—parts below target go back to the furnace

**代表案例 / Representative Cases**：
- 1446 年训民正音颁布：二十八字'聪明者一朝可解，愚者旬月可学'——按最笨用户设计的字母表，五百年后成为一国文字

**现代应用 / Modern Applications**：

- Open-source software: the first screen of the README addresses someone new to the field—adoption and contributor counts share one curve

### 2. M-SEJ-002 本土实测法 / Local-Verification Method

**定义**：认识论方法论/一切知识在采用前必须'验于本土'——文献、外来体系、权威结论只是假设，本地实测数据才有裁决权

**Definition**：epistemological methodology/every body of knowledge must be 'verified in our own land' before adoption—texts, foreign systems, and authorities are hypotheses; local measured data holds the verdict

**历史现场 (domain)**：1433 年《农事直说》的成书过程是这条方法的标本：世宗拒绝命儒臣抄撮《齐民要术》《四时纂要》交差——那些书写的是华北的节气与土壤。他派 '劝农官' 下乡，逐道逐郡记录：本地何时浸种、何时下秧、用什么堆肥、轮作什么——按实测数据写成朝鲜自己的农书，并注明'各随其土宜'（按各地土性调整）。同一纪律贯穿医政：《乡药集成方》不是抄《证类本草》，而是先查'乡药'——朝鲜本土药材的真实药性与产地；天文不抄大统历完事，先铸简仪、浑天仪、日晷、圭表，书云观日夜观测，然后才重编《七政算》。世宗实录里反复出现同一条批答逻辑：'中国之法有不可行于我国者'——外来体系不是敌人，是无权裁决的证人

**Domain (English)**：The making of Straight Talk on Farming in 1433 is the method's specimen: Sejong refused to have his scholars copy the Qimin Yaoshu and the Sizuan Zuan—books of North China's seasons and soils. He sent agricultural officers into every province and county to record: when seeds soak locally, when seedlings go out, what compost is used, what rotates with what—the measured data became Korea's own farm manual, annotated 'let each follow its own soil.' The same discipline runs through medicine: the Treasury of Native Medicines does not copy the Chinese great herbal but first checks the native drugs—the real properties and sources of Korean materia medica; astronomy does not end with copying the Ming calendar—first the armillary sphere, the simplified instrument, the sundial, and the gnomon are cast and the Observatory watches day and night, and only then are the astronomical tables redrawn. One rescript recurs in the Annals: 'there are Chinese methods that cannot work in our country'—the foreign system is not an enemy, merely a witness without a vote

**方法论深描 (definition)**：世宗本土实测法的核心命题是：知识的管辖权在数据而不在出处——一部书写得多权威、一个体系来自多大的文明，都只是'待验证的假设'；在本地条件下重复观测之后，本地数据才有裁决权。操作纪律有四条。第一，把文献降格为假设：引用《齐民要术》时不问'圣贤怎么说'而问'这在我们地里灵不灵'——出处提供候选答案，不提供结论。第二，建设实测能力：派官下乡、铸仪器、设观象台——没有自己的测量工具，'验于本土'就是空话；实测能力先于实测结论。第三，分域建档：实测按地域、土性、药性分卷记录（《农事直说》'各随其土宜'）——拒绝用一个均值抹平差异，本地数据的颗粒度就是治理的颗粒度。第四，允许推翻权威：实测结果与经典冲突时，改经典不改数据——世宗修历改律皆循此例。深意有三重。其一，它是'移植迷信'的解毒剂：任何制度、技术、教义跨文化移植前先问本地数据。其二，它把国家从'文明圈的学生'变成'自己的实验站'——朝鲜由此有了自己的农学、医学、天文学，而非汉籍的注释业。其三，它给后发者一条合规的学习路径：学上游可以，验收必须自己来。适用边界：适用于可观测、可重复的领域（农学、医学、工程、经济政策）；对纯演绎学科（数学、逻辑），'本土实测'退化为'本地可理解性'检验。现代对应物：循证医学与本地临床数据、A/B 测试对最佳实践的中和、跨国公司的'本地化验证'流程、农业推广系统的田间试验网络。

**Method (English)**：The core proposition of the local-verification method: jurisdiction over knowledge belongs to data, not to provenance—however authoritative a text, however great the civilization a system comes from, both stand only as 'hypotheses pending verification'; after repeated observation under local conditions, local data holds the verdict. Four disciplines. First, demote texts to hypotheses: cite the Qimin Yaoshu not as 'what the sages say' but as 'does this work in our fields'—provenance offers candidate answers, never conclusions. Second, build measurement capacity first: officers into the provinces, instruments cast, observatories manned—without your own instruments, 'verify locally' is empty speech; capacity precedes conclusions. Third, archive by domain and by region: measurements filed by soil, by climate, by drug ('let each follow its own soil')—refuse one mean to flatten differences; the granularity of local data is the granularity of governance. Fourth, permit overturning authority: where measurement conflicts with the classics, revise the classics, not the data—Sejong's calendar and legal reforms both follow this rule. Three layers of depth. First, it is the antidote to transplant superstition: ask for local data before any institution, technology, or doctrine crosses cultures. Second, it turns a nation from 'student of a civilization' into 'its own experimental station'—Korea thereby gained its own agronomy, medicine, and astronomy, not a commentary industry on Chinese books. Third, it gives latecomers a compliant path of learning: learn from upstream as you like, but the acceptance test must be run by you. Boundaries: it suits observable, repeatable domains (agronomy, medicine, engineering, economic policy); for purely deductive fields it degrades to a 'local intelligibility' test. Modern counterparts: evidence-based medicine with local clinical data, A/B testing neutralizing best practice, multinational localization validation, the farm-trial networks of agricultural extension.

**出处**：《农事直说》序与各道记录体例（1433）；世宗实录十五年关于劝农官派遣的记事；《乡药集成方》序（1433）；《七政算》进呈记事（1444）与书云观观测记事

**关键概念 / Key Concepts**：
- 知识的管辖权在数据不在出处
- 文献降格为假设
- 实测能力先于实测结论
- 分域建档拒绝均值抹平
- 实测与经典冲突改经典
- 外来体系是无权裁决的证人

**金句**：中国之法有不可行于我国者——书可以抄，地不能抄；一切结论，验于本土之后才算数。
**Key quote**：There are Chinese methods that cannot work in our country—books may be copied, land may not; every conclusion counts only after local verification.

**操作流程 / Process**：
1. 列假设：把要采用的知识（农法、药性、历法、制度）列成待验证清单——出处权威性只影响先验概率不影响结论
2. 建能力：先配置本地测量手段——下乡的官、铸出的仪、记录的表格——没有工具的验证是空谈
3. 实测采集：按统一格式在真实条件下重复观测——本地何时浸种、本地药材何性
4. 分域建档：数据按地域与对象分卷——'各随其土宜'，一个均值不许抹掉颗粒度
5. 裁决冲突：实测与文献冲突时以实测为准——修经典、改律历，不改数据
6. 定期复测：气候、药源、政策环境随时间漂移——设复测周期，让'本地结论'保持本地

**Process (English)**：
1. List the hypotheses: put the knowledge to be adopted—methods, drug properties, calendars, institutions—on a verification checklist; provenance moves priors, never verdicts
2. Build capacity: configure local measurement first—officers into the field, instruments cast, forms printed; verification without instruments is talk
3. Collect by measurement: repeated observation under real conditions in a uniform format—when seeds soak here, what this native drug does
4. Archive by domain: file data by region and object—'each follows its own soil'; no mean may erase granularity
5. Adjudicate conflicts: where measurement meets the classics, measurement wins—revise classics and laws, never the data
6. Re-measure on schedule: climate, drug sources, and policy drift with time—set re-testing cycles so local conclusions stay local

**代表案例 / Representative Cases**：
- 《农事直说》：劝农官逐道实测'何时浸种、何时下秧'——朝鲜第一部按本国土壤写的农书，增产效果立竿见影
- 《乡药集成方》：先查朝鲜本土药材的真实药性与产地，再编医方——本土药材第一次获得国家级档案
- 《七政算》：不抄大统历，先铸简仪浑天仪日晷圭表日夜观测，再据实测重编——朝鲜天文学的出生证明

- The Treasury of Native Medicines: native drugs' real properties and sources checked before formulas compiled—the first state archive of indigenous materia medica

- The Annals rescript: 'there are Chinese methods that cannot work in our country'—a standing reservation clause on imported institutions

**现代应用 / Modern Applications**：
- 跨国扩张：进入新市场前设'本地验证期'——总部的增长模型只是假设，本地数据出来前不谈复制
- 循证管理：行业最佳实践（谷歌式 OKR、亚马逊式会议）进入公司前先做本地试点——'验于本土'的管理版

- 个人学习：读任何方法论后先做小型本地实验——'试跑两周再改自己的流程'是这条方法的个人版
- Cross-border expansion: a local validation period before entering a market—headquarters' growth model is a hypothesis until local data arrives
- Evidence-based management: industry best practice (Google-style OKRs, Amazon-style memos) passes a local pilot before adoption—the managerial form of 'verify locally'
- Agricultural and medical extension: rebuild farm-trial and local-clinical networks—make local conclusions infrastructure, not projects
- Personal learning: after any methodology book, run a small local experiment—'try it two weeks before rewiring your workflow' is the personal edition

### 3. M-SEJ-003 机构养士法 / Institution-as-Incubator Method

**定义**：组织方法论/不以个人聪明而以机构承载知识生产——为每类知识设立专职机构与人才管道，让产出可持续、可积累、可传承

**Definition**：organizational methodology/carrying knowledge production by institutions rather than personal brilliance—a dedicated body and talent pipeline for each kind of knowledge, making output sustainable, cumulative, and inheritable

**历史现场 (domain)**：1420 年代起，世宗把王宫一角变成知识园区：集贤殿设于宫内，一批二十岁上下的青年学者（申叔舟、成三问、崔恒、李垲……）被养在那里，每日问对——君主与学者对坐论学议政，修典、制礼、发明相互喂养。但集贤殿只是其中一件：观象监专管天文历法与仪器；校书馆专管刊印（金属活字归它运营）；容像所专管绘制；医局专管医书与药业。他为每一类知识配上'机构＋人才管道＋考核'：集贤殿学士有进讲义务也有议政权利，观象监有观测值班表，校书馆有字库维护职责。世宗实录里他的用人批答反复出现同一逻辑：'人才不养则不至，不至则事不成'——他甚至亲自过问某个学者的俸禄与升迁，让机构里的产粮者比外廷的辩士更有前途。三十年下来，朝鲜第一次拥有了不依赖君主个人精力而运转的知识体系——世宗死后，这些机构继续出书、造仪、传教

**Domain (English)**：From the 1420s Sejong turned a corner of the palace into a knowledge campus: the Hall of Worthies inside the walls, a cohort of young scholars in their twenties (Sin Sukju, Seong Sammun, Choe Hang, Yi Gae) kept there, in daily audience—ruler and scholars sitting face to face over learning and policy, compilation, rites, and invention feeding one another. The Hall was only one piece: the Royal Observatory for astronomy, calendars, and instruments; the proofreading bureau for printing (the metal type ran under it); the portrait bureau for painting; the medical office for medical texts and the drug trade. Each body of knowledge got 'institution + talent pipeline + assessment': Hall scholars owed lectures and held the right to counsel; the Observatory kept observation duty rosters; the proofreading bureau maintained the type library. One logic recurs in his personnel rescripts: 'talent unfostered does not come, and without it nothing is accomplished'—he personally checked a scholar's salary and promotion, so that the producers inside the institutions out-promised the debaters outside. After thirty years, Joseon for the first time owned a knowledge system that ran without the king's personal energy—after his death these bodies went on printing, casting, and teaching

**方法论深描 (definition)**：世宗机构养士法的核心命题是：知识生产的可持续性取决于承载它的机构，而不取决于天才的个人——天才是脉冲，机构才是电网；没有机构的知识生产随天才死亡而中断，有机构的知识生产可以穿越王朝更替。操作纪律有四条。第一，按知识门类设机构而非按任务设小组：观象监不是'修历项目组'而是一个长期部门——门类机构的生命周期长于任何单一任务。第二，养士先于用士：给青年学者俸禄、宫内席位与每日问对——养的过程本身就是生产过程（问对即咨询）；不供养只索取，人才管道三年枯竭。第三，给机构配置考核与保障：观测有值班表、刊印有字库维护、学士有升迁通道——没有考核的知识机构会变成养闲院，没有保障的知识机构留不住人。第四，机构之间建接口：医书用校书馆刊印、农书用观象监的节气数据——知识体系的价值等于接口的密度。深意有三重。其一，它把'智库'从临时幕僚升级为常设制度——集贤殿是世界上最早的常设宫廷智库之一。其二，它解释了世宗工程的全部产出率：365 卷《医方类聚》不可能靠兴趣完成，只能靠机构产粮。其三，它给出'个人品牌知识工作者'时代最容易被遗忘的教训：可持续的产出需要你为自己建机构（哪怕是一个人的机构——固定的流程、档案与节奏）。适用边界：适用于长期、大体量、可积累的知识与工程生产；对快速试错的探索期项目，机构化过早会杀死灵活性。现代对应物：企业研究院（贝尔实验室、Xerox PARC、DeepMind）、常设的政府咨询委员会、开源基金会、个人知识管理系统。

**Method (English)**：The core proposition of the institution-as-incubator method: the sustainability of knowledge production depends on the institution that carries it, not on the genius of the individual—a genius is a pulse; an institution is the grid—without institutions, knowledge production dies with the genius; with them, it survives dynastic change. Four disciplines. First, found institutions by discipline of knowledge, not task forces by project: the Observatory is not a 'calendar-fixing squad' but a standing department—the life of a disciplinary institution outlives any single task. Second, foster before you use: salary, a seat inside the palace, daily audiences for young scholars—the fostering is itself production (the audience is consultation); demand without供养 exhausts the pipeline in three years. Third, attach assessment and security to each body: duty rosters for observation, type-library maintenance for printing, promotion tracks for scholars—knowledge institutions without assessment become lounges; without security they leak people. Fourth, build interfaces between institutions: medical books printed at the proofreading bureau, farm manuals fed by Observatory phenology—the value of a knowledge system equals the density of its interfaces. Three layers of depth. First, it upgrades the 'think tank' from temporary staff to standing institution—the Hall of Worthies is among the world's earliest standing court think tanks. Second, it explains the entire yield of Sejong's projects: a 365-volume encyclopedia is finished by institutions, never by enthusiasm. Third, it delivers the lesson most forgotten in the age of the personal brand: sustainable output requires building an institution of your own—even a one-person institution: fixed process, archive, cadence. Boundaries: it suits long-horizon, large-volume, cumulative knowledge and engineering production; for rapid-exploration phases, premature institutionalization kills flexibility. Modern counterparts: corporate research labs (Bell Labs, Xerox PARC, DeepMind), standing government advisory councils, open-source foundations, personal knowledge management systems.

### 4. M-SEJ-004 正音减笔法 / Minimal-Script Method

**定义**：符号设计方法论/为最大覆盖面做最小符号集——用系统生成（辅音按发音部位象形、元音按天地人）替代符号堆砌，让'几天学会'成为设计指标

**Definition**：symbol-design methodology/a minimal symbol set for maximal coverage—systematic generation (consonants picturing the articulation organs, vowels heaven-earth-human) replacing symbol accretion, making 'learnable in days' a design metric

**历史现场 (domain)**：汉字有数万之数，谚文最终只有二十八个字母：十七个辅音、十一个元音。设计原理写在《训民正音解例》里：'象形而字倣古篆'——ㄱ画舌根闭喉之形，ㅁ画口形，ㅅ画齿形，ㅇ画喉形：辅音的形状就是发音器官的解剖图；'ㅏ'取天圆、'ㅡ'取地平、'ㅣ'取人立：元音以天地人为骨架——一个普通人不需要语言学训练就能从字母形状反推发音方法。更狠的是减法：已有的音用已有字母加笔画表示，不另造新符；字母组合成音节块，视觉上仍像汉字方块，迁移成本降到最低。这套符号系统在 1446 年颁布时被士林讥为'与倭人格子文同'，但它在五百年里证明了一件事：符号的竞争力是学习成本，不是血统与美感的竞争力。二十八个字母覆盖了朝鲜语的全部语音——用最少的符，覆盖最大的面

**Domain (English)**：Chinese characters number in the tens of thousands; the vernacular script ended at twenty-eight letters: seventeen consonants, eleven vowels. The design principle is written in the Explanations: 'pictorial in form, modeled on the ancient seal'—ㄱ draws the tongue root closing the throat, ㅁ the closed lips, ㅅ the teeth, ㅇ the throat: the consonant shapes are an anatomical chart of the speech organs; 'ㅏ' takes heaven's roundness, 'ㅡ' earth's level line, 'ㅣ' a person standing: vowels built on heaven-earth-human—an ordinary person, needing no linguistic training, can reverse the pronunciation from the letter's shape. The subtraction is harder still: sounds already covered are expressed by adding strokes to existing letters, no new signs; letters combine into syllabic blocks that still look square like characters, minimizing transfer cost. When promulgated in 1446 the literati mocked it as 'like the lattice script of the wako,' but five hundred years proved one thing: a script's competitiveness is its learning cost, not its pedigree or beauty. Twenty-eight letters cover all the phonology of Korean—fewest symbols, widest coverage

**方法论深描 (definition)**：世宗正音减笔法的核心命题是：符号系统的优劣由学习成本裁决——而压低学习成本的最强杠杆不是简化单个符号，而是让符号'成系统'：用户学会规则就等于学会全部符号，符号数量与认知负担脱钩。操作纪律有四条。第一，先定覆盖面再定符号集：先穷举要编码的全部对象（朝鲜语全部音节），再设计能生成它们的系统——顺序颠倒的符号设计会漏掉长尾。第二，让符号自带说明书：字母形状编码其功能（发音部位象形）——符号即文档，学习从记忆变成推理。第三，向既有系统做兼容减法：音节块视觉上像汉字——新系统寄生在旧视觉习惯上，迁移摩擦最小化。第四，接受最笨用户的复训成本：'愚者旬月可学'写进设计文档——设计指标不是专家效率而是初学时长。深意有三重。其一，它给出'生成式设计'的早期范本：设计物不是符号清单而是符号生成器。其二，它解释了为什么谚文战胜了僧侣文字与吏读等所有竞争方案：学习成本是最公平的竞争力。其三，它提示所有'简化'的真正含义：简化不是删符号，是造系统。适用边界：适用于符号系统、命令语言、API 设计、图标系统；对非系统性对象（地名、专名）减笔法不适用。现代对应物：iCon/emoji 的组合生成、正则表达式、编程语言的语法系统性、Dvorak 与其他'按认知设计'的键盘。

**Method (English)**：The core proposition of the minimal-script method: symbol systems are judged by learning cost—and the strongest lever for lowering it is not simplifying single symbols but making symbols a system: learn the rule and you have learned every symbol, decoupling symbol count from cognitive load. Four disciplines. First, fix coverage before the symbol set: enumerate everything to be encoded (every Korean syllable), then design the generator—reversing the order leaves a long tail uncovered. Second, let the symbol carry its own manual: letter shapes encode their function (articulation pictographs)—the symbol is the documentation; learning shifts from memorization to inference. Third, subtract compatibly into the incumbent: syllable blocks look square like characters—the new system parasitizes old visual habits, minimizing transfer friction. Fourth, accept the slowest learner's retraining cost: 'the slow learn in ten days' written into the design document—the metric is beginner hours, not expert efficiency. Three layers of depth. First, it is an early specimen of generative design: what is designed is not a symbol list but a symbol generator. Second, it explains why hangul beat the clerical scripts and idu and every rival: learning cost is the fairest competitive edge. Third, it redefines 'simplification': not deleting symbols, but building a system. Boundaries: it suits symbol systems, command languages, APIs, icon sets; for non-systematic objects (place names, proper nouns) it does not apply. Modern counterparts: composable emoji and icons, regular expressions, the systematicity of programming-language syntax, Dvorak and other cognitively designed keyboards.

- 向旧视觉习惯做兼容减法

**操作流程 / Process**：

**Process (English)**：
1. Enumerate coverage: list every object to be encoded—every phoneme, command, scenario—the long tail may not be dropped

**代表案例 / Representative Cases**：
- ㄱㅁㅅㅇ的解剖学：辅音字母画出喉舌齿唇的发音部位——零训练者可从形状反推发音，世界文字史上孤例

### 5. M-SEJ-005 分级沟通法 / Tiered-Encoding Method

**定义**：治理方法论/同一内容按受众重新编码——对精英用文言、对庶民用正音，语言与载体本身成为治理的界面

**Definition**：governance methodology/re-encoding the same content by audience—classical Chinese for the elite, the vernacular script for commoners; language and medium themselves become interfaces of governance

**历史现场 (domain)**：世宗的朝廷运转在两种语言上：对两班士林，诏敕、《实录》、经筵讲论全部用汉文文言——那是精英层的'行业语言'，文言的精度服务于制度辩论。但下行文书走另一条通道：法典《六典》与诰令出正音谚解本，让不通汉文的守令与百姓直接听懂法律；《佛说菩萨内戒经》等经书组织僧俗用谚语翻译刊行，让乡村佛堂直接读经；1444 年他甚至下令'凡刑狱词状'允许以谚文书写——百姓第一次可以用自己的文字打官司。同一项政策双语发行：《农事直说》既出汉文儒学版（供劝农官引用），又以口诀谚解下田头。他不假装一种语言能服务所有人——'文字不相流通'的痛苦他记得清清楚楚，治理界面必须按接收方切换

**Domain (English)**：Sejong's court ran on two languages: to the literati, edicts, the Annals, and lecture debates all in classical Chinese—the elite's professional language, its precision serving institutional argument. But downward documents took another channel: the Six Codes and royal ordinances issued in vernacular gloss so magistrates and subjects without Chinese could hear the law directly; sutras translated and printed in hangul for monks and laity, so village halls read scripture directly; in 1444 he even ordered that 'petitions in criminal and civil cases' be accepted written in hangul—for the first time people could sue in their own script. One policy, bilingual release: Straight Talk on Farming issued in the classical edition (for agricultural officers to cite) and glossed for the field. He did not pretend one language could serve everyone—he remembered 'speech not matching writing' precisely; the interface of governance must switch by receiver

**方法论深描 (definition)**：世宗分级沟通法的核心命题是：内容的意义不由发送方的语言决定，而由接收方能否解码决定——'说清楚'不是修辞品质，是按受众重新编码的工程任务；同一内容可以有多个编译版本，且都是正本。操作纪律有四条。第一，先画受众图谱：把信息接收方按语言能力、职业、读写习惯分层（两班/守令/农夫）——没有受众图谱的'统一发文'必然只对一层有效。第二，逐层重编码而非逐层简化：谚解不是把文言'写简单点'，而是用另一套符号系统完整重写——重编码保结构，简化保结论，前者不失真。第三，为每层指定正本：汉文版与谚解版并列公布——庶民版本不是'低级版'而是同级正本，避免沟通中的版本歧视。第四，接收方反向上行通道：允许百姓用谚文写状子——分级沟通是双向的，接收方的语言也是发送方的耳朵。深意有三重。其一，它把'沟通'从发送方的表达问题重构为接收方的解码工程——这与克尔凯郭尔间接沟通法共享同一颗北极星。其二，它揭示了语言权力的再分配机制：谁掌握重编码权，谁就掌握政策的实际含义。其三，它对'一刀切公告'的现代病给出药方：同一政策发三个版本（专家版/执行版/公众版）不是冗余，是精确。适用边界：适用于政策发布、文档体系、产品文案、教育分层；对需要统一法理基准的法律文本，重编码必须以'语义等价性审查'护航。现代对应物：合规文件的'通俗版＋完整版'双发、API 文档的 tutorial/reference 分离、新闻通稿与技术白皮书并存、双语字幕的分级字幕组。

**Method (English)**：The core proposition of the tiered-encoding method: a message's meaning is decided not by the sender's language but by whether the receiver can decode it—'clarity' is not a rhetorical virtue but an engineering task of re-encoding by audience; one content may have several compiled versions, all canonical. Four disciplines. First, draw the audience map first: stratify receivers by language ability, occupation, and reading habits (literati / magistrates / farmers)—uniform issuance without an audience map works for one stratum only. Second, re-encode per stratum rather than simplify: the vernacular gloss is not 'the classical written simpler' but a complete rewrite in another symbol system—re-encoding preserves structure; simplification preserves only conclusions, and distorts. Third, name a canon for each stratum: the classical and vernacular editions issued side by side—the commoner's edition is not a 'junior version' but a co-equal canon, preventing version discrimination in communication. Fourth, an upstream channel for receivers: petitions accepted in hangul—tiered communication is bidirectional; the receiver's language is also the sender's ear. Three layers of depth. First, it reconstructs communication from the sender's expression problem into the receiver's decoding project—sharing Kierkegaard's polestar. Second, it exposes the redistribution mechanism of linguistic power: whoever holds the right of re-encoding holds the practical meaning of policy. Third, it prescribes the modern disease of the one-size-fits-all announcement: one policy, three versions (expert / executor / public)—not redundancy but precision. Boundaries: it suits policy releases, documentation systems, product copy, educational stratification; for legal texts requiring a unified doctrinal baseline, re-encoding must be escorted by semantic-equivalence review. Modern counterparts: compliance documents issued as plain version plus full version, API docs split into tutorial and reference, press release alongside technical white paper, tiered fan-subtitling.

**出处**：世宗实录二十六年'刑狱词状许用谚文'记事；《六典》谚解与诰令颁行记事；《训民正音》颁行后经书谚译的组织记事（释宗灿、信眉等僧人参与译经）

**关键概念 / Key Concepts**：
- 意义由接收方解码决定
- 重编码优于简化
- 每层正本并列
- 接收方需要上行通道
- 语言是治理界面
- 受众图谱先于发文

**金句**：同一部法，两个正本——对两班是文言，对百姓是正音；治理的精度不在写得多深，而在对方解得开。
**Key quote**：One law, two canons—classical for the literati, vernacular for the people; governance is precise not when written deep but when decoded by its receiver.

**操作流程 / Process**：
1. 画受众图谱：按解码能力把接收方分层——语言、职业、读写习惯三维度
2. 逐层重编码：为每层用其母符号系统完整重写内容——禁止'把文言写简单点'式的降级
3. 并列正本：各层版本同时公布、效力等同——庶民版不是注释版
4. 语义等价审查：多版本间设对照校验——重编码失真的条款回炉
5. 开上行通道：接收方可以用自己的语言回复与申诉——沟通双车道
6. 按反馈迭代：追踪各层'解得开吗'的真实数据——重编码版本按解码失败率修订

**Process (English)**：
1. Draw the audience map: stratify receivers by decoding ability—language, occupation, reading habits, three dimensions
2. Re-encode per stratum: a complete rewrite in each stratum's own symbol system—the downgrade of 'write it simpler' is barred
3. Parallel canons: all versions issued together with equal force—the commoner's edition is not a commentary
4. Semantic-equivalence audit: cross-checking between versions—clauses distorted in re-encoding go back to the furnace
5. Open the upstream lane: receivers reply and appeal in their own language—communication on two tracks
6. Iterate by feedback: track real data of 'can each stratum decode this'—re-encoded versions revised by decoding-failure rate

**代表案例 / Representative Cases**：

**现代应用 / Modern Applications**：

### 6. M-SEJ-006 免疫阻尼法 / Immune-Damping Method

**定义**：变革方法论/新发明必然触发旧制度的免疫反应——提前识别否决者、逐条批驳、以制度权威压住反应，让创新穿过推广期的死亡谷

**Definition**：change methodology/a genuinely new invention always triggers the old order's immune reaction—identify the vetoers early, rebut point by point, damp the reaction with institutional authority, and carry the innovation through the adoption valley

**历史现场 (domain)**：1444 年《训民正音》尚未颁布，反对已经成军：集贤殿出身的崔万理上《驳正音疏》，措辞极重——'自古九州之内，风土虽异，未有因方言而别为文字者'、'若流中国，或有非议之者，岂不有愧于事大慕华'——反对的实质是文字即礼制：新文字将让工商贱隶'琐琐皆得其志'，纲常与科举秩序被动摇。世宗的应对是教科书级的免疫阻尼：不废止、不妥协、也不情绪化，逐条批驳——'谕礼曹：聪明者一朝可解，愚者旬月可学，其以此训之'；把反对者的职位（崔万理外放）与正音的推广（诰令、佛经、词状）并行推进。他知道士林的抵抗不会消失，所以不指望说服，而指望'反应存在但被压住'——正音在他生前全面运行，五十年沉寂后又在中宗朝复起，工程穿透了君主的生命周期

**Domain (English)**：In 1444, before Hunminjeongeum was even promulgated, the opposition had formed: Choe Manri, a product of the Hall of Worthies himself, submitted the memorial of rebuttal in the gravest terms—'within the nine provinces, though customs differ, no one has ever made a separate script from a local dialect,' and 'if word reaches China, will we not be ashamed before the admirers of the great'—the substance of the opposition was that script is ritual: a new script would let merchants and menials 'all gain their wishes,' shaking the moral order and the examinations. Sejong's response was textbook immune-damping: neither abolish, nor compromise, nor vent—he rebutted point by point ('instruct the Board of Rites: the clever grasp it in a morning, the slow in ten days; teach them so'); moved Choe out of office while pushing the script's uses (ordinances, sutras, petitions) forward in parallel. He knew the literati's resistance would not vanish, so he did not count on persuading anyone—he counted on the reaction existing but being damped—the script ran fully in his lifetime, fell silent for fifty years, and rose again under Jungjong: the project crossed the monarch's lifespan

**方法论深描 (definition)**：世宗免疫阻尼法的核心命题是：有用的新事物遭遇旧制度的免疫反应不是危险信号而是成功信号——反应强度与发明的威胁度成正比；变革者的任务不是消除反应（不可能），而是像阻尼器一样吸收震荡、保护工程穿过推广期。操作纪律有四条。第一，预判免疫源：推广前识别'谁的利益与身份被触动'（世宗面对的既是利益也是文明认同）——免疫反应的形状由被触动者决定。第二，接受反对的正当性并逐条回应：崔万理的疏有真实的礼制逻辑，世宗逐条批驳而不嘲笑——嘲笑只加固免疫。第三，区分'可安抚的'与'必须压住的'：给反对者体面（外放而非杀戮），给工程强制力（诰令与考试）——安抚与压制同时用。第四，让工程在反应期内持续产出可见成果：佛经印出来、状子收进来——免疫反应最怕的不是论证而是既成事实。深意有三重。其一，它把'被批评'重新定价：没人反对的改革多半没碰到真问题。其二，它给出变革者与系统的关系学：你是入侵者，学会做阻尼器而非锤子。其三，它提醒变革者设置'续命结构'——工程要能穿过你本人的政治周期，五十年后的复起需要活着的机构。适用边界：适用于组织变革、技术采用、制度创新；对需要共识的大规模政治转型，强压反应可能积累反噬。现代对应物：新技术采用曲线中的'死亡之谷'管理、开源项目的 RFC 争论治理、企业变革中的'反对者清单'管理法、疫苗与公共卫生推广中的回音室应对。

**Method (English)**：The core proposition of the immune-damping method: a useful novelty meeting the old order's immune reaction is not a danger sign but a success sign—the reaction's strength scales with the invention's threat; the reformer's task is not to eliminate the reaction (impossible) but, like a damper, to absorb the oscillation and protect the project through its adoption valley. Four disciplines. First, predict the immune sources: before diffusion, identify whose interests and identities are touched (Sejong faced both interest and civilizational identity)—the reaction's shape is set by the touched. Second, accept the opposition's legitimacy and answer it point by point: Choe's memorial had real ritual logic; Sejong rebutted each clause without mockery—mockery only hardens immunity. Third, separate the 'pacifiable' from the 'suppressible': dignity for opponents (reassignment, not execution), coercive force for the project (ordinances and examinations)—soothe and press simultaneously. Fourth, keep visible output flowing during the reaction: sutras printed, petitions received—what the immune reaction fears is not argument but accomplished fact. Three layers of depth. First, it reprices 'being criticized': a reform nobody opposes has probably missed the real problem. Second, it gives the reformer a relational science of the system: you are the invader—learn to be a damper, not a hammer. Third, it warns the reformer to build a life-support structure: the project must cross your own political cycle; the revival fifty years later needed living institutions. Boundaries: it suits organizational change, technology adoption, institutional innovation; for mass political transformation requiring consensus, heavy damping may bank a backlash. Modern counterparts: managing the 'chasm' in technology adoption curves, RFC-governance of open-source disputes, the 'opposition checklist' in corporate change, countering echo chambers in public-health rollout.

**出处**：世宗实录二十五年九月崔万理《驳正音疏》全文与世宗批答；《训民正音》颁布记事与礼曹谕令；中宗朝正音复起的制度背景（后见）

**关键概念 / Key Concepts**：
- 免疫反应是成功信号
- 反应强度与威胁度成正比
- 逐条批驳不嘲笑
- 安抚与压制并用
- 既成事实胜过论证
- 工程要穿过变革者的政治周期

**金句**：被围攻不是危险，是工作的证明——我不需要反对者闭嘴，只需要他们的反应压不住工程的推进。
**Key quote**：Being besieged is not danger but proof of work—I need not the critics' silence, only that their reaction fails to stop the project's advance.

**操作流程 / Process**：
1. 预判免疫源：推广前列出'谁的利益与身份被触动'——利益受损者与认同焦虑者分组建档
2. 逐条批驳：对正式反对逐条回应——不嘲笑、不降格、不留断章取义的把柄
3. 分轨处理：可安抚者给体面（调岗、保留地位），必须压住者用制度权力（诰令、考核）
4. 持续产出：反应期内让工程持续放出可见成果——既成事实是最强的阻尼剂
5. 保护产粮者：明确保护工程执行者免受政治牵连——阻尼器保护的不是理念是执行层
6. 设续命结构：让工程拥有独立于你本人周期的机构与文档——你的离场不等于工程离场

**Process (English)**：
1. Predict the immune sources: before diffusion, list whose interests and identities are touched—file the interest-lost and the identity-anxious separately
2. Rebut point by point: answer formal opposition clause by clause—no mockery, no strawman, no quotemining to seize on
3. Split the tracks: dignity for the pacifiable (reassignment, rank kept), institutional force for the suppressible (ordinances, assessment)
4. Keep output flowing: during the reaction, release visible results continuously—accomplished fact is the strongest damping agent
5. Shield the executors: explicitly protect the project's operators from political entanglement—the damper protects not the idea but the execution layer
6. Build the life-support structure: give the project institutions and documents independent of your own cycle—your exit must not be the project's exit

**代表案例 / Representative Cases**：
- 崔万理《驳正音疏》全文在录、世宗逐条批答——朝鲜史上最高级别的公开技术辩论，以工程推进而非说服获胜收场

### 7. M-SEJ-007 民情仪表法 / Populace-as-Gauge Method

**定义**：决策方法论/把底层民众的真实生存状况当作政策的第一仪表——'为民上者不可不先知民事'，救荒、税赋、刑狱皆先读表再动手

**Definition**：decision methodology/taking the real living conditions of the lowest people as the primary gauge of policy—'the ruler of the people must first know the people's affairs'; famine relief, taxation, and justice all read the gauge before acting

**历史现场 (domain)**：世宗实录里最密集的一类记事是灾荒：某道歉收、米价几何、流民几口——每次他都先读仪表再下诏。1431 年的调剂法是仪表读数的直接产物：义仓旧制只在灾年放粮，腐坏严重且远水不解近渴；他把义仓粮'调剂'于民间放贷收息、利息滚入赈济本金——用金融工程让救荒可持续。他要求各道按月上报'民情文案'：物价、疾苦、词讼——把自己变成全国民情的仪表盘持有者。他的批答有一句自我立法：'为民上者，不可不先知民事'——民事不是奏章里的事后汇报，而是仪表读数的事前采集。连刑狱也一样：他令京外囚徒案卷定期上呈亲自勾决，减免拷讯——司法的仪表也是民情

**Domain (English)**：The densest entries in Sejong's Annals are famine: such a province's failed harvest, the rice price, the number of displaced households—each time he read the gauge before the edict. The Joryak granary reform of 1431 was a direct product of gauge readings: the old granary system released grain only in famine years, rotting badly and arriving too late; he 'joryak-ed' the granary stock—lending it out at interest with the interest rolled into the relief principal—financial engineering to make relief sustainable. He required every province to file monthly 'reports on the people's condition': prices, hardships, litigation—making himself the holder of the realm's condition dashboard. One rescript is self-legislation: 'the ruler of the people cannot but first know the people's affairs'—the people's condition is not a report after the fact but a reading taken before. Justice too: he ordered case files of prisoners outside the capital submitted periodically for his own review, easing torture and interrogation—the gauge of the law is also the condition of the people

**方法论深描 (definition)**：世宗民情仪表法的核心命题是：统治的质量取决于仪表的精度与读数频率——'民情'不是抽象的民本理念，而是一套必须持续采集、定期读数、直接驱动决策的数据系统；不读表的民本只是修辞，读表的民本才是工程。操作纪律有四条。第一，定义仪表读数：把'民生'拆成可上报的具体变量（米价、灾情、词讼、流民数）——模糊的'民不聊生'不可操作。第二，固定采集频率：月报制度——仪表的价值在于连续读数，一次性调研只能拍快照。第三，读表者直接持权：君主亲读、亲勾、亲批——仪表数据若只到中间层，决策就被中间层的转述污染。第四，读数驱动制度而非驱动个案：调剂法不是一次放粮而是一次制度更新——好的仪表读数应当改变系统参数，而不是仅救一次急。深意有三重。其一，它是'民本'的操作化：从《尚书》的敬德保民到月报米价，理念必须翻译成数据管道才有治理效力。其二，它给出统计治国的早期形态：朝鲜的民情月报比现代统计制度早五百年，功能同构。其三，它警告仪表腐蚀：当上报者知道读数决定赏罚，数据本身会被优化——世宗以亲决与抽查对冲。适用边界：适用于面向大规模人群的治理与产品运营；对高度依赖专业判断的领域（外交、战略），仪表读数只是输入之一。现代对应物：企业 NPS 与用户健康分、疫情数据仪表盘、Grievance 与申诉系统、政府'接诉即办'。

**Method (English)**：The core proposition of the populace-as-gauge method: the quality of rule depends on the gauge's precision and reading frequency—'the people's condition' is not an abstract ideal of benevolence but a data system that must be continuously collected, periodically read, and directly drive decisions; benevolence without readings is rhetoric; with readings, engineering. Four disciplines. First, define the readings: decompose 'livelihood' into reportable variables (rice price, disaster, litigation, displaced households)—a vague 'the people suffer' is not actionable. Second, fix the collection frequency: the monthly report—a gauge's value lies in continuous readings; one-off surveys take only snapshots. Third, the reader holds power directly: the king reads, ticks, and rescripts himself—data stopping at the middle layer is polluted by paraphrase. Fourth, let readings drive institutions, not anecdotes: the Joryak reform was a system update, not one relief act—good readings should change system parameters, not merely patch an emergency. Three layers of depth. First, it operationalizes 'the people as the root': from the Documents' 'revere virtue, protect the people' to monthly rice prices—an ideal must be translated into a data pipeline to govern. Second, it is an early form of government by statistics: Joseon's condition reports predate modern statistical systems by five centuries, functionally isomorphic. Third, it warns of gauge corrosion: when reporters know readings determine rewards, the data itself gets optimized—Sejong hedged with personal review and spot checks. Boundaries: it suits governance and product operations over large populations; in domains resting on expert judgment (diplomacy, strategy), gauge readings are one input among several. Modern counterparts: NPS and user-health scores, epidemic dashboards, grievance systems, 'complaints handled immediately' government platforms.

- 民本是数据管道不是理念

**操作流程 / Process**：
1. 定义读数：把抽象关切拆成可上报变量——价格、灾情、词讼、人口流动，每项有计量口径

6. 公开反馈：把读数与响应公布给被测量者——仪表的透明本身就是治理信用

**Process (English)**：
1. Define the readings: decompose abstract concern into reportable variables—price, disaster, litigation, migration—each with a metric
2. Fix the frequency: set the collection cycle (monthly)—a gauge that does not read continuously is no gauge
3. Read and rescript directly: the decision-maker reads the raw readings—no gauge through layers of paraphrase
4. Hedge corrosion: spot checks and cross-validation coexist—when reporters know data will be audited, they dare not prettify
5. Let readings change institutions: after every anomalous reading ask 'which system parameter updates'—beyond relief, repair the system
6. Publish the loop: disclose readings and responses to the measured—gauge transparency is itself governance credit

### 8. M-SEJ-008 组合生成法 / Combinatorial-Generation Method

**定义**：创新方法论/不造无限工具而造可组合的最小单元——字母拼成音节、字块拼成版面、机构拼成体系，组合爆炸的收益归用户

**Definition**：innovation methodology/build not infinite tools but minimal composable units—letters into syllables, blocks into pages, institutions into systems; the yields of combinatorial explosion accrue to the user

**历史现场 (domain)**：训民正音最深的一层设计是'拼'：二十八个字母不是二十八个字，而是二十八个可组合的积木——辅音+元音+收音拼成方块音节，音节拼成词，词拼成无限的句子。用户学二十八个字母，得到的是整个语言的表达能力——组合的收益全部归用户而非设计者。同一思路贯穿世宗工程：金属活字是'可组合的印刷'（一个字模印无限文本）；《七政算》吸收伊斯兰历法与明朝历法时可组合地取舍（内篇用大统历框架、外篇用回回历数据）；集贤殿、观象监、校书馆三个机构拼成一条'研究—观测—刊印'流水线。世宗设计系统的方式惊人一致：把大问题拆成最小可组合单元，把组合权交给使用者——他从不试图替用户穷举

**Domain (English)**：The deepest layer of Hunminjeongeum's design is 'composition': twenty-eight letters are not twenty-eight characters but twenty-eight composable blocks—consonant, vowel, and final composing a square syllable, syllables composing words, words infinite sentences. The user learns twenty-eight letters and gains the expressive power of the whole language—the yield of combination accrues wholly to the user, not the designer. The same logic runs through the Sejong projects: metal type is 'composable printing' (one punch, unlimited text); the astronomical tables absorbed Islamic and Ming calendars compositely (inner chapters on the Ming framework, outer on Islamic data); the Hall of Worthies, the Observatory, and the proofreading bureau compose into a research-observation-printing pipeline. Sejong's way of designing systems is strikingly uniform: decompose the large problem into minimal composable units, and hand the right of combination to the user—he never tried to enumerate for the user

**方法论深描 (definition)**：世宗组合生成法的核心命题是：可扩展性的来源不是工具的数量而是单元的可组合性——设计者的任务止于提供最小积木与组合规则，穷举与封顶是设计者的傲慢；组合爆炸的收益应当归用户。操作纪律有四条。第一，识别最小正交单元：把系统拆到'再拆就失去功能'的颗粒度（辅音/元音、单字模、单机构职能）——单元不正交，组合就会塌缩。第二，先给规则再给库存：组合规则（音节拼合法）比单元库存更重要——规则是算法，库存是数据。第三，把组合权交给用户：设计者不预设合法组合的白名单——用户的组合越过设计者想象，系统才算活。第四，为组合失败设计兜底：音节块拼不出新音时允许加笔——系统要有逃生舱。深意有三重。其一，它解释了谚文的百年适应性：新词、外来语、网络语言全部'长'进了系统——设计者死了五百年系统还在吸收新对象。其二，它是平台思维的古代形态：提供积木与规则，让生态长出设计者想不到的东西。其三，它对'大而全'产品哲学的否决：穷举式产品在需求变化时全部重写，组合式产品只加单元。适用边界：适用于语言、软件、组织、金融工具等可符号化的领域；对不可分解的整体性对象（一件瓷器、一台手术），组合法降级为'模块化'。现代对应物：Unix 管道哲学、乐高与 Minecr­aft 的组合生态、乐理十二音、API 组合与微服务。

**Method (English)**：The core proposition of the combinatorial-generation method: extensibility comes not from the number of tools but from the composability of units—the designer's job ends at providing minimal blocks and composition rules; enumeration and caps are the designer's arrogance, and the yield of combinatorial explosion should accrue to the user. Four disciplines. First, identify minimal orthogonal units: decompose the system to the grain at which further division loses function (consonant/vowel, one punch, one bureau's office)—non-orthogonal units collapse under composition. Second, rules before inventory: composition rules (how syllables assemble) matter more than unit stock—rules are the algorithm, inventory the data. Third, hand the right of combination to the user: the designer sets no whitelist of legal compositions—the system is alive only when users combine beyond the designer's imagination. Fourth, design an escape hatch for failed compositions: when a block cannot spell a new sound, strokes may be added—systems need an escape hatch. Three layers of depth. First, it explains hangul's century-scale adaptability: new words, loanwords, and internet language all 'grew' into the system—the designer dead five hundred years, the system still absorbing new objects. Second, it is platform thinking in ancient form: provide blocks and rules; let the ecosystem grow what the designer never imagined. Third, it vetoes the philosophy of the all-in-one product: exhaustive products are rewritten when demand shifts; compositional products only add units. Boundaries: it suits symbolizable domains—language, software, organizations, financial instruments; for unanalyzable wholes (a porcelain vase, one operation), it degrades into modularity. Modern counterparts: the Unix pipe philosophy, the compositional ecologies of Lego and Minecraft, twelve-tone theory, API composition and microservices.

**出处**：《训民正音解例》'合字解'——初声中声终声的组合规则；《七政算》内外篇对大统历与回回历的可组合取舍；金属活字铸造与校书馆刊印制度的记事

**关键概念 / Key Concepts**：
- 可组合性是扩展性之源
- 最小正交单元
- 规则比库存重要
- 组合权归用户
- 组合失败要有逃生舱
- 设计者穷举是傲慢

**金句**：我不给你无限的字，我给你二十八个字母和拼法——其余的句子，由你们自己长出来。
**Key quote**：I give you not infinite characters but twenty-eight letters and the rules of assembly—the rest of the sentences, you will grow yourselves.

**操作流程 / Process**：
1. 拆到正交：把系统分解为最小功能单元——每单元只做一件事且不依赖其他单元的内部结构
2. 定组合规则：写清单元如何合法拼接——规则越简单覆盖越大
3. 交出组合权：不设合法组合白名单——用户的新组合默认有效，出问题再修规则
4. 留逃生舱：为规则覆盖不到的边角预留扩展机制——加笔、外挂、例外条款
5. 测试组合爆炸：抽样验证'单元数×组合规则'能否覆盖真实需求长尾——覆盖率不达标回到第一步
6. 冻结积木：单元与规则稳定后避免频繁改动——组合生态的信任来自积木的稳定

**Process (English)**：
1. Decompose to orthogonality: break the system into minimal functional units—each does one thing and depends on no other's internals
2. Fix composition rules: state how units legally assemble—the simpler the rule, the wider the coverage
3. Hand over the right of combination: no whitelist—users' new compositions are valid by default; fix rules only when problems arise
4. Leave an escape hatch: reserve an extension mechanism for corners the rules miss—added strokes, plugins, exception clauses
5. Test the combinatorial explosion: sample whether 'units × rules' covers the real demand tail—failing coverage returns you to step one
6. Freeze the blocks: once units and rules stabilize, avoid frequent change—the trust of a compositional ecology comes from stable blocks

### 9. M-SEJ-009 工程留档法 / Engineering-Archive Method

**定义**：知识管理方法论/每项工程同步产出三件套——成果、原理说明（解例本）、执行档案——让后人能复现、能检修、能接续

**Definition**：knowledge-management methodology/every project ships a three-piece set—the deliverable, a principles explanation (the Explanations), and execution records—so successors can reproduce, maintain, and continue

**历史现场 (domain)**：《训民正音》颁布时附带了一件史无前例的东西：《训民正音解例》——由郑麟趾等集贤殿学士撰写的原理说明书：制字解（为什么字母长成这样）、合字解（怎么拼）、用字解（怎么用）、韵会要义（与汉音韵的关系）——这不是宣传册，是让任何后人能独立复现与检修这门文字的'源代码注释'。同一纪律出现在每项工程：《农事直说》每条农法标注实测来源；《七政算》写明每张表的数据出处与算法；《医方类聚》逐卷列编纂人与所据文献。而王朝层面还有第三件套的放大器：《朝鲜王朝实录》——世宗一朝实录逐日记事，工程决策的背景、争论（崔万理疏全文在录）、批答全部留档。五百年后韩国的语言学家仍能从解例本推演字母设计的每一步——工程死了，档案让工程随时可以被重新启动

**方法论深描 (definition)**：世宗工程留档法的核心命题是：工程的完成度等于'可被完全陌生者接手'的程度——只交付成果的工程是半成品；完整的工程交付物是三件套：成果本体（正音）、原理文档（解例）、执行档案（实录）——三者共同保证工程可复现、可检修、可接续。操作纪律有四条。第一，原理文档与成果同步交付：解例本与正音同月颁布——原理写晚了，细节就死了。第二，为'检修'写档而非为'汇报'写档：解例解释'为什么这样设计'（制字解）而非'我们多么努力'——档案的读者是未来的维修者不是当下的领导。第三，把争论一并归档：崔万理的反对疏与世宗的批答都进实录——争议的完整记录比胜利叙事更值钱，后人需要知道工程踩过哪些坑。第四，档案接入执行链：农法标注实测来源、历表写明算法出处——档案不是图书馆的孤本而是执行现场的参考文献。深意有三重。其一，它把'知识传承'从师徒口授升级为工程文档——韩国语言学因此拥有世界文字史上最完整的创制记录。其二，它解释了正音五十年沉寂后仍能复起：解例本在手，工程随时可重启——文档是工程的休眠舱。其三，它对'人走茶凉'的组织病给出解药：让工程不再依赖任何个体的记忆。适用边界：适用于需要长期维护与传承的工程与制度；对快速迭代的探索型工作，过度留档会拖慢节奏。现代对应物：开源项目的 README/文档/决策记录（ADR）、科学的可复现性运动（数据与代码公开）、组织 runbook 与维基、设计系统的 tokens 文档。

**Method (English)**：The core proposition of the engineering-archive method: a project's completeness equals the degree to which a total stranger could take it over—delivering only the artifact is a half-delivery; the full deliverable is the three-piece set: the artifact itself (the script), the principles document (the Explanations), the execution record (the Annals)—together guaranteeing the project can be reproduced, maintained, and continued. Four disciplines. First, principles ship with the artifact: the Explanations promulgated the same season as the script—write the principles late and the details die. Second, write for maintainers, not for reporting: the Explanations explain 'why designed this way,' not 'how hard we worked'—the archive's reader is the future repairer, not the current boss. Third, archive the disputes too: Choe's opposition and Sejong's rebuttals both entered the Annals—a complete record of controversy outvalues any victory narrative; successors need to know which pits the project fell into. Fourth, wire the archive into execution: farm methods annotated with measured sources, tables stating algorithms—the archive is not a library relic but a working reference at the execution site. Three layers of depth. First, it upgrades knowledge transfer from oral lineage to engineering documentation—Korean linguistics thereby owns the most complete script-creation record in world history. Second, it explains the script's revival after fifty silent years: with the Explanations in hand the project can restart anytime—the document is the project's dormancy pod. Third, it answers the organizational disease of 'the person leaves, the warmth leaves': the project no longer depends on any individual's memory. Boundaries: it suits long-lived engineering and institutions; for fast-iterating exploration, over-documenting slows the beat. Modern counterparts: open-source README/docs/ADRs, the reproducibility movement (open data and code), organizational runbooks and wikis, design-token documentation.

**Process (English)**：

### 10. M-SEJ-010 接班伏笔法 / Succession-Foreshadowing Method

**定义**：传承方法论/重大工程在建成时同步埋下'再次启用'的接口——机构、文档、教育渠道三线并埋，让工程能穿越创建者的离场与低潮

**Definition**：legacy methodology/at completion, simultaneously bury 're-activation' interfaces for every major project—institution, document, and education channels laid in parallel—so the project can survive the founder's exit and the trough

**历史现场 (domain)**：世宗在工程建成时同步做了三件'埋线'的事。第一线，机构与人才：正音颁行时，创制团队的核心（成三问、申叔舟、崔恒）都已在集贤殿占据固定官职——工程长在机构里而非长在国王身上。第二线，文档：《解例本》把设计原理完整成文——即使机构被清洗，纸面上的原理仍可独立传世（后来解例本果然成为唯一的幸存火种）。第三线，应用场景：诰令、佛经、词状、童蒙读物全面使用正音——数百万普通人的读写习惯本身就是最深的伏笔：精英可以停止推广，但受过训的百姓不能被取消。1450 年世宗死，文宗继位一年而卒，端宗幼年即位，1453 年癸酉靖难、1456 年死六臣——集贤殿被血洗，正音推广全面停摆近五十年。但三线伏笔逐一起效：解例本在，原理在；机构虽清洗，文脉士人（后来的中宗朝士林）在；百姓已会用——中宗朝起正音全面复起，谚解本、小说、家信最终让韩文成为韩国的国家文字。世宗埋下的不是成果，是'随时可以重新开始'的全部条件

**Domain (English)**：At each project's completion Sejong simultaneously laid three lines of buried wire. The first, institutions and talent: at promulgation the creation team's core (Seong Sammun, Sin Sukju, Choe Hang) already held fixed posts in the Hall of Worthies—the project grew in the institution, not in the king. The second, documents: the Explanations fixed the design principles in writing—even if the institution were purged, the paper principles could survive independently (the Explanations indeed became the sole surviving ember). The third, use cases: ordinances, sutras, petitions, and children's primers all employed the script—millions of ordinary people's reading habit is itself the deepest buried wire: the elite could stop promoting, but the trained population could not be unmade. In 1450 Sejong died; Munjong reigned a year and died; the child Danjong succeeded; in 1453 the coup of Suyang, in 1456 the death of the six martyred ministers—the Hall of Worthies was purged in blood and the script's promotion halted for nearly fifty years. But the three lines fired in sequence: the Explanations preserved the principles; though purged, the literati line survived (the scholars of Jungjong's court); and the people already knew how to read—from Jungjong onward the script revived fully, and glosses, fiction, and letters at last made hangul the national script. What Sejong buried was not the result but all the conditions for starting again at any time

**方法论深描 (definition)**：世宗接班伏笔法的核心命题是：工程的真正风险不在建成而在离场——创建者被替代、被清洗、或仅仅被遗忘之后，工程能否续命取决于建成时埋下的'重启接口'；接口有三条线：机构（有人在推进）、文档（原理在纸上）、用户（习惯在民间）——三线并埋者穿越低潮，单线者随创建者一同死亡。操作纪律有四条。第一，把工程长进机构：核心成员的官职、俸禄、考核与工程绑定——'人对'必须制度化为'岗位对'。第二，原理必须纸面化：设计文档独立于任何组织而可传世——组织可以被摧毁，一份好的解例本摧毁不了。第三，制造不可逆的用户基础：让最底层的人养成使用习惯——精英的政治风向改变不了百万人的手指记忆。第四，为低潮期选'最小火种'：预先判断哪条线最可能幸存（世宗时代是纸面文档），把最高密度的工作投入那条线。深意有三重。其一，它把'传承'从道德期望改写为工程冗余：不是指望后人贤明，而是让工程对后人的贤明不敏感。其二，它解释了为什么有些发明死了而有些活过清洗：活过的都有民间的使用基础。其三，它对'创始人是最大风险'的 Startup 病给出解药：创始人的最大责任是让自己变得可替代。适用边界：适用于长期工程、制度建设、开源项目与文化事业；对本质上依赖天才个人魅力的艺术工程，伏笔法只能保住文档保不住风格。现代对应物：开源项目的 BDFL 退休与基金会化、公司的'无人懂系统'风险治理、品牌资产中'用户习惯'对营销预算的替代、科学学派的制度传承。

**Method (English)**：The core proposition of the succession-foreshadowing method: a project's true risk lies not in completion but in departure—after the founder is replaced, purged, or merely forgotten, the project's survival depends on the 'restart interfaces' buried at completion; the interfaces run on three lines: the institution (people still advancing it), the document (principles on paper), the users (habits among the populace)—bury all three and the project crosses the trough; bury one and it dies with the founder. Four disciplines. First, grow the project into the institution: core members' posts, salaries, and assessments bound to it—'right people' must be institutionalized into 'right positions.' Second, put the principles on paper: a design document that can outlive any organization—an organization can be destroyed; a good Explanations cannot. Third, build an irreversible user base: let the lowest people form the habit—the elite's political winds cannot reverse a million fingers' memory. Fourth, choose the minimal ember for the trough: predict in advance which line will most likely survive (in Sejong's era, the paper document) and invest the highest density of work in that line. Three layers of depth. First, it rewrites 'legacy' from moral hope to engineering redundancy: not hoping successors are wise, but making the project insensitive to their wisdom. Second, it explains why some inventions die while others survive purges: the survivors all had a popular base of use. Third, it answers the startup disease of 'the founder is the greatest risk': the founder's greatest duty is to make himself replaceable. Boundaries: it suits long-horizon projects, institutions, open source, and cultural works; for artistic projects resting on personal charisma, the method can preserve documents but not style. Modern counterparts: open source's BDFL retirement and foundation-ization, corporate governance of 'no one understands the system' risk, user habit replacing marketing budget in brand equity, institutional lineage of scientific schools.
