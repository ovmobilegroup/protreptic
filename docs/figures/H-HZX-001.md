## M-HZX-001 天下主权法 / Sovereignty of Tianxia Method

天下（人民）是政治主体的根本，君主只是天下的客人和服务者。颠覆了'君权神授'的传统逻辑：不是天立君以治民，而是民本位、君末位。'原君'篇指出，君主最初的设立是为了天下人的利益，而非相反。若君主以天下利益为私，则君主身份本身即构成对政治秩序的破坏。

Tianxia sovereignty inverts the founding question of political order. Where the mandate-of-heaven tradition asks 'who is authorized to rule,' Huang Zongxi's 'Origin of the Ruler' asks 'why does rulership exist at all'—and answers: in the beginning, each person pursued private benefit while public goods went uncultivated and public harms unremoved; someone emerged who labored ten-million-fold for all under heaven yet enjoyed none of the benefit himself. Such a post is one no one would naturally want—which means rulership is a burden contracted for the sake of the ruled, not a prize seized for the ruler. From this premise three consequences follow with mechanical force. First, the subject-object inversion: Tianxia (the people) is the principal (zhu) and the ruler merely the guest (ke), the hired steward of the common good—so a ruler who treats the realm as private estate has not merely misgoverned; he has dissolved the very office he occupies, becoming, in Huang's most famous verdict, 'the great harm of Tianxia.' Second, legitimacy is audited by benefit-flow: the sole test of any regime is whether the lived welfare of the millions improves, which converts political philosophy from a theology of authorization into an accounting of public benefit. Third, the argument is historical as well as logical—comparing the Three Dynasties' 'all-under-heaven as principal' with the post-Qin 'one family as principal' yields a degeneration narrative that makes the critique of autocracy a reading of history, not a utopian wish. Methodological signature: locate the origin of an institution in the function it was contracted to perform, then measure every current claimant against that function—the office belongs to the served, never to the server.



**反面警示 / Counterexample:** 反例警示：若把'天下为主'误读为无政府的多数直接统治，则失去制度中介的民本论会滑向暴民政治——黄宗羲同时强调学校议政与治法框架（见 M-HZX-002/003），主权在民必须落在制度通道里，否则只是换个名字的私天下

Counterexample: Counterexample: reading 'the people as principal' as unmediated majoritarian rule forfeits the institutional channel and slides into mob politics—Huang simultaneously requires school deliberation and a framework of good law (M-HZX-002/003); popular sovereignty must land in institutional channels, or it is private rule of the realm under a new name

## M-HZX-002 治法优先法 / Rule-of-Law Priority Method

优良制度（治法）优先于个人品德（治人）。与儒家传统'有治人无治法'相反，黄宗羲指出后世的'非法之法'（专制律令）才是真正的祸根，没有好的制度框架，再贤明的君主也无法实现善治。这是中国思想史上首次系统论证制度优先于个人的政治主张。

Institutional priority reverses the Confucian default that 'there are governing men, but no governing laws.' Huang's 'Origin of Law' argues the opposite sequence: 'you zhi fa er hou you zhi ren'—good law must come first, and only then can good men govern. The argument turns on a distinction between two kinds of law. The law of the Three Dynasties was 'law of Tianxia': established for the people, sparse because it needed only to channel public benefit. The law since Qin-Han is 'law of one family': designed to secure a dynasty's private holding, dense and punitive precisely because it fears the ruled—and such 'non-law law' (fei zhi fa) does not fail to produce good governance; it actively manacles 'the hands and feet of all under heaven,' making every office a garrison of private interest. Three operational rules follow. First, diagnose the law's owner: ask for any institution not whether it is well-administered but whose interest its rules encode—public law serves the realm, private law serves the house. Second, distrust virtue-first remedies: since institutions shape incentives before character can act, moral cultivation of occupants cannot repair a structure whose rewards point the wrong way. Third, reform the framework before the personnel: replacing rulers inside a 'one-family law' only re-staffs the cage. Methodological signature: when outcomes are chronically bad, treat the rule-structure—not the individuals caught in it—as the primary suspect, and audit whom the rules were written to serve.



**反面警示 / Counterexample:** 反例警示：制度优先不等于制度万能——若把'治法'推到极端，会产出只见规则不见人的法条主义，人才凋零、组织僵死；黄宗羲的次序是'先法治后治人'而非'法治代治人'，制度重建之后仍需学校养士与君子执政（M-HZX-003）来激活框架

Counterexample: Counterexample: institutional priority pushed to the extreme yields legalism that sees rules but not people—talent withers and organizations ossify; Huang's order is 'law first, then men,' not 'law instead of men'—after structural reform, school-cultivated scholars and worthy officials (M-HZX-003) are still needed to bring the framework alive

## M-HZX-003 学校议政法 / School Deliberation Method

学校不应仅是教育机构，更应是政治议政的场所。'必使治天下之具皆出于学校'，学校成为评判是非、议决政策的公共空间。太学祭酒（校长）地位等同宰相，皇帝亦需出席学校参与讨论。这是中国思想史上最接近近代议会制度的构想，将'公其非是于学校'作为制约君权、实现公共理性讨论的制度设计。

School deliberation converts education into the structural check on power. Huang's 'Schools' chapter demands that 'all instruments for governing Tianxia originate from schools'—not as a slogan about literacy but as a constitutional design: the court's right-and-wrong must be settled publicly where scholars deliberate, not privately where the ruler wills. The design has three load-bearing parts. First, an independent academic estate: the Grand Learning's director (jijiu) holds rank equal to the chancellor and is chosen for scholarly standing, so the authority to judge policy is institutionally separate from the authority to execute it. Second, adjudication power over the throne: the director 'dots the vermilion'—imperial proposals take legal effect only after school review, and when the emperor errs the school ' disputes him in open assembly,' making criticism a statutory procedure rather than a punishable boldness. Third, a nested network: provincial and county schools review local officials each term, so supervision runs bottom-up through the whole polity, not merely top-down. What emerges is the closest Chinese approximation to a deliberative assembly: public reason given a building, a budget, and a veto. Methodological signature: wherever power concentrates, endow a standing, independent body of qualified judges with the formal right to pass public verdict on it—surveillance by opinion must be made into procedure, or it stays bravery.



**反面警示 / Counterexample:** 反例警示：议政机构若被行政收编或被单一学派垄断，'公其非是'立刻退化为新一重的御用肯定——黄宗羲的一本万殊原则（M-HZX-005）要求学校内部保持多元争鸣；没有内部多元的学校议政只是换了讲台的独断

Counterexample: Counterexample: once the deliberative body is captured by the executive or monopolized by one school, 'public judgment' degrades into a new layer of courtly approval—Huang's one-essence-many-manifestations principle (M-HZX-005) requires plural contest inside schools; deliberation without internal pluralism is merely dictatorship from a different lectern

## M-HZX-004 理气一元法 / Li-Qi Monism Method

反对程朱理学'理在气先'的二元论，主张'理即是气之理'，理不能离气而独存。宇宙本原是一气流行，理只是气的运行规律而非独立实体。'盈天地皆心也，变化不测，不能不万殊'，心与气、理与气统一于物质性的气本原。这一立场奠定了明清之际唯物主义哲学的转向。

Li-qi monism strikes at the reifying move in Cheng-Zhu metaphysics. Against the claim that principle (li) precedes material force (qi), Huang insists 'li is simply the li of qi'—pattern is the patterned behavior of some substance, and there is no principle floating where nothing flows. The cosmos is one qi in endless circulation; what scholastics had promoted into a transcendent entity is only its regularity. Two extensions give the doctrine its width. Inward: 'the whole of heaven and earth is heart-mind,' changeful and inexhaustibly various—mind is not a disembodied principle over against the world but the mobile, per-varying aspect of the one flux; the moral law lives inside processes, not above them. Outward: Huang practiced the claim—his calendrical and astronomical work treats the heavens as calculable process, and he read the classics through computed ephemerides, so cosmology, philology, and natural study corroborate one monist picture. The stance founds the materialist turn of early Qing philosophy: explanations must descend to the carrier, never ascend to a hypostatized abstraction. Methodological signature: when an explanation invokes an entity nobody can locate, ask what concrete process its name abbreviates—and relocate the regularity back into the process that bears it.



**反面警示 / Counterexample:** 反例警示：气一元若滑向粗糙还原论，会把礼乐、价值、规范一并化约为'不过是气'，从而失去 normativity 的立足点——黄宗羲保留'盈天地皆心'的心学维度，正是为了给道德实践留出位置；一元论须能安放规范，而非取消规范

Counterexample: Counterexample: qi-monism sliding into crude reductionism dissolves ritual, value, and norm into 'nothing but qi,' losing any footing for normativity—Huang's retention of the 'all heaven and earth is heart-mind' dimension reserves standing for moral practice; monism must accommodate norms, not cancel them

## M-HZX-005 一本万殊法 / One Essence Many Manifestations Method

真理（一本）通过无数个体化路径（万殊）显现，学术发展不应强求统一标准。编纂《明儒学案》时，黄宗羲主张'有一偏之见，有相反之论，学者于其不同处正宜着眼理会'，反对'以水济水'的同质化。各学派宗旨不同但皆求道，这正是'一本而万殊'的学术生态观。这一方法论突破了'独尊一途'的学术专制传统。

One-essence-many-manifestations is Huang's charter for intellectual pluralism, written into the general principles of the 'Ming Confucian Learning Cases.' Its core ruling: 'the way of learning is that what each person can genuinely put to use is true'—truth is verified at the point of practice by the individual pathway, not decreed by a single orthodoxy. From this, pluralism becomes a duty of scholarship rather than a tolerated mess. Different schools embody different partial insights (yi pian zhi jian) and even opposing theses (xiang fan zhi lun); the compiler's task is precisely to 'attend to where they differ,' because the divergences are where the Dao's ungraspable totality shows itself. Huang's polemic target is homogenization—'pouring water into water': a tradition that only repeats itself has abandoned inquiry. He faulted Sun Qifeng's 'Lineage of Principle Studies' for indiscriminate aggregation without discernment, and instead demanded that each case be distilled from the thinker's complete works, preserving the school's own aim (zongzhi) without dilution. The result is an academic ecology: many roots, one living tree; differences are data, not noise. Methodological signature: treat disagreement inside a field as information about the field's structure—preserve and stage the dissenting positions at full strength, and let their friction, not a referee's verdict, reveal the shared ground.



**反面警示 / Counterexample:** 反例警示：'万殊'若无'一本'统摄，多元主义会退化为相对主义的价值虚无——任何立场都等价，批评失去依据；黄宗羲的一本万殊是'万殊中见一本'，多元必须仍受'各人自用得着者为真'的实践检验约束

Counterexample: Counterexample: 'many manifestations' without a unifying 'one essence' decays into relativist nihilism—all positions equally valid, criticism groundless; Huang's formula is 'seeing the one within the many,' and plurality remains bound by the practical test 'what each can genuinely use is true'

## M-HZX-006 经世应务法 / Practical Statecraft Method

学问的根本目的在于'经世应务'——解决现实社会问题，而非空谈心性。'学必原本于经术而后不为蹈虚，必证明于史籍而后足以应务'，将经学研究与历史考证结合，以史证经、以经驭史，最终服务于治国平天下的实践目标。这是对明代王学末流空疏学风的直接批判。

Practical statecraft defines what knowledge is for. Huang's twin maxim—'learning must be rooted in the classics to avoid treading on emptiness, and verified in the historical record to be adequate for managing affairs'—builds a two-stage pipeline for scholarship: classical study supplies the normative framework (so argument has an authorized lineage, not private invention), and historical verification supplies the empirical test (so doctrine can actually touch institutions, finance, law, defense). The pipeline was aimed at the late-Ming Wang-school's terminal disease: luminous talk about mind and nature while the dynasty fell. Three working rules follow. First, every thesis must name the affair it serves—learning that cannot be pointed at a live problem is ornament. Second, the classics are read through the histories and the histories through the classics: the canonical text constrains cherry-picked precedent, and the record keeps exegesis from drifting into pure gloss. Third, synthesis is judged by use: Huang's own forty-year compilation of the 'Ming Wen Hai' was explicitly an archive for statecraft, and the 'Waiting for the Visitor in Darkness' was written as an institutional repair manual for a dynasty yet to come. Methodological signature: bind every intellectual product to a verifiable chain—normative root, empirical check, live problem—and reject as empty whatever cannot complete the circuit from text to affair.



**反面警示 / Counterexample:** 反例警示：'应务'若被推到极端会反噬学术——凡不能立刻兑换成政策的知识都被斥为无用，则数学、天文、考据等'远水'皆废；黄宗羲本人精历算、重考据（M-HZX-007），恰证明'经世'须以'不问近利'的基础研究为蓄水池，急用先学终将无学可用

Counterexample: Counterexample: 'managing affairs' pushed to the extreme devours scholarship—knowledge not instantly convertible into policy is dismissed as useless, and the 'slow waters' of mathematics, astronomy, and philology are cut off; Huang's own calendrical and textual scholarship (M-HZX-007) proves statecraft needs unprofitable basic research as its reservoir—salvage-first learning ends with no learning left to apply

## M-HZX-007 史料考辨法 / Historical Source Criticism Method

以严密的考证方法辨别史料真伪，反对盲从权威和片面记载。'国史取详年月，野史取当是非，家史备官爵世系'，根据不同史料类型确定取舍标准。用天文学推算验证《春秋》日食记录，以历法知识判定古文《尚书》真伪，体现了'实事求是'的实证精神。重视一手材料，反对'谀墓单辞'。

Historical source criticism is Huang's machinery for making truth claims about the past auditable. Its premise is typological: different genres of record carry different reliabilities for different questions—'official histories supply detailed chronology, unofficial histories preserve contemporary judgment of right and wrong, family histories complete the offices and genealogies'—so the critic first sorts sources by function before weighing any single testimony. Three operations then run. Cross-interrogation (tanbo canzheng): conflicting accounts of the same event are set against each other, and the critic names which records deserve trust and why—at the Ming-Qing transition, amid a flood of self-serving chronicles, he shortlists only those that survive corroboration. Instrumental verification (lisuan zhengjing): specialized knowledge is deployed as an independent check—Huang recomputed the Spring and Autumn Annals' solar eclipses with Han and Yuan calendar systems to test the record's credibility, and his calendrical work fed Yan Ruoqu's demonstration that the Old Text Documents were forged. Bias filtration: 'hearsay errors and the mouths of love and hate' are actively subtracted—flattering epitaphs, factional slanders, and retrospective pieties are treated as contamination, not evidence. Methodological signature: no testimony is above audit—triangulate genre, cross-examine accounts, and where possible check the record against an independent instrument, keeping only what survives all three.



**反面警示 / Counterexample:** 反例警示：考辨若无最后落点，会沦为无限怀疑主义——一切史料皆可疑，则历史书写不可能；黄宗羲的怀疑是'汰伪存真'的工具性怀疑，以可验证的独立标准（历算、交叉比证）收束怀疑，最终产出可信结论而非悬置

Counterexample: Counterexample: criticism without a terminus collapses into unlimited skepticism—if every source is suspect, no history can be written; Huang's doubt is instrumental 'discarding the false to keep the true,' converging via independent standards (calendrical computation, cross-testimony) on trustworthy conclusions rather than suspension

## M-HZX-008 黄宗羲定律法 / Huang Zongxi's Law Method

税制改革往往导致税负反弹的规律性现象：每次简化税制、合并税种的改革，短期内确实减轻了农民负担，但随后新增名目繁多的附加税又会将负担推到比改革前更高的水平。'积累莫返之害'揭示了制度性腐败的内在逻辑——统治者在税制改革中并非真正让利，而是通过'并税式改革'将隐性剥削合法化后，再叠加新的盘剥。这一规律超越了时代，适用于分析任何制度变革中的利益分配问题。

Huang Zongxi's Law names the ratchet of fiscal reform: every simplification briefly relieves the taxpayer, then relaunches the burden above its old level. Writing on land tax, Huang observed the sequence across the Tang two-tax reform and the Ming Single Whip: a proliferation of levies is consolidated into one simpler tax at a headline rate that genuinely lowers the total; but once the old exactions are forgotten as a baseline, new surcharges accrete outside the consolidation—levies justified by emergencies that outlive the emergency, fees restored under fresh names—until 'the accumulated harm cannot be returned' (ji lei mo fan zhi hai). The mechanism, not malice, does the work: consolidation resets the reference point and legitimizes the surviving burden as 'the whole tax,' and the erased memory of earlier exactions becomes unclaimed space for new ones; each cycle compounds, so the floor rises ratchet-wise and never falls back. Qin Hui, naming the pattern 'Huang Zongxi's Law' in the 1990s, showed it holds from the Tang through the Qing—and wherever reform merges levies without simultaneously binding the state against re-accretion. Methodological signature: when a simplification reform is proposed, audit the reference point it resets—ask not whether burdens fall today, but what prevents tomorrow's new levies from standing on the erased baseline; a consolidation without a ratchet-lock is a promise of relapse.



**反面警示 / Counterexample:** 反例警示：定律不是宿命论——把'积累莫返'读成'改革必然失败'会为不改革辩护；真正的对策是黄宗羲自己给出的治法路径（M-HZX-002）：改革必须连'不得再加派'的约束装置一并写入制度，并税而无锁扣机制，反弹只是时间问题

Counterexample: Counterexample: the law is not fatalism—reading 'irreversible accumulation' as 'reform always fails' becomes an apology for not reforming; the real remedy is Huang's own institutional path (M-HZX-002): a consolidation must write its anti-accretion lock ('no new levies') into the structure itself—merge taxes without the lock, and rebound is only a matter of time

## M-HZX-009 夷夏之辨法 / Yi-Xia Distinction Method

以文化（华夏文明）而非种族血统作为区分'华夏'与'夷狄'的标准。'自三代以后，乱天下者无如夷狄矣'，但黄宗羲的'夷夏之辨'并非单纯的种族主义，而是强调文明的先进性与落后性。当夷狄具备文明秩序时，可以接受其统治；当华夏丧失文明时，则等于夷狄。这一文化主义取向使其区别于狭隘的民族主义。

The Yi-Xia distinction as Huang wields it is cultural, not racial: the line between civilized center and outer barbarism runs through conduct and order, not blood. His famous grief—'since the Three Dynasties, none has devastated Tianxia like the Yi and Di'—is a lament over conquest's destruction of civilization, yet the operative criterion he preserves is civilizational attainment: when the outer people establish ordered, humane governance they move toward Xia, and when the inner people forfeit civilized order they become Yi in fact. Three positions follow. Anti-essentialism: no lineage either owns civilization or is barred from it—barbarism is a performance, not an essence. Cultural primacy: the transmission of learning, rites, and historical memory outranks regime continuity—the dynasty can fall, but the school, the archive, and the scholarly estate must carry on; Huang's own life enacted this, refusing Qing office while teaching, compiling, and corresponding freely. Critical openness: civilization is judged by results, so superior practice anywhere demands acknowledgment—he accepted the precision of Western astronomy and called for 'converging Chinese and Western learning,' letting the cultural standard override the political boundary. Methodological signature: when judging a group or an era, apply the civilizational test—order, learning, humane treatment—rather than descent, and keep the standard consistent enough to indict one's own side first.



**反面警示 / Counterexample:** 反例警示：文化标准若被无限抬高，会反向沦为'教化即征服'的扩张正当化——凡我文明即统治之权；黄宗羲的界线在于'以文明论接受，不以文明论征服'，一旦文化判准被用来论证武力统治的正当，它就背叛了自身

Counterexample: Counterexample: raising the cultural standard infinitely high turns it into a license for 'civilizing conquest'—our civilization justifies our rule; Huang's line is 'accept by civilization, never conquer by civilization'—the moment the cultural criterion argues for armed domination, it betrays itself

## M-HZX-010 会众合一法 / Synthesizing the Many Method

在尊重各家'一偏之见'的基础上，通过'会众以合一'的归纳方法，从多元学术传统中萃取共同精神。与'一本万殊法'互为表里：一本万殊是分析视角（从共性见差异），会众合一是归纳视角（从差异见共性）。编《明儒学案》时，'皆从全集纂要钩玄'——从每位学者的全集中提炼其宗旨，既不抹杀个性，又揭示共性，实现了'万殊归一本'的学术综合。

Synthesizing-the-many is the inductive complement to Huang's pluralist epistemology: where one-essence-many-manifestations analyzes the whole into its legitimate divergences, this method reassembles the divergences into a higher unity without erasing them. Its image is hydraulic: 'a scholar a thousand years later must synthesize the many into one—from valley to river, from river to sea'—understanding flows along channels, gathering without amalgamating. The procedure, refined in the 'Ming Confucian Learning Cases,' has three steps. Full collection: nothing is judged before its complete works are gathered—summary judgment on excerpts is disqualified by construction. Essence-extraction (zuan yao gou xuan): from each corpus the compiler distills the school's governing aim, quoting and arranging rather than rewriting, so each position speaks in its own strongest form. Convergence-without-flattening: with all aims set side by side, the deep structure common to them becomes visible—'the many returning to the one'—a unity that is discovered in the material, not imposed on it. The contrast case defines the method: Sun Qifeng's compilation failed by 'indiscriminate aggregation without discernment,' which is synthesis skipped; the opposite failure is sectarian uniformity, which is difference skipped. Huang's synthesis requires both. Methodological signature: to master a plural field, first gather exhaustively, then let each part state itself at full strength, and only then read the convergent pattern across them—a synthesis built this way commands assent because every party recognizes itself in it.



**反面警示 / Counterexample:** 反例警示：'会众合一'若先立结论再剪裁材料，归纳就退化为循环论证——所谓'贯通'只是自己预设的影子；黄宗羲的次序不可逆：全收在前、钩玄在中、合一在后，任何一步省略，合一即是虚构

Counterexample: Counterexample: if 'synthesizing the many' fixes the conclusion first and tailors evidence to fit, induction degrades into circular reasoning—the claimed 'convergence' is only the shadow of its own premise; Huang's order is irreversible: exhaustive collection first, extraction second, convergence last—omit any step and the unity is fiction
