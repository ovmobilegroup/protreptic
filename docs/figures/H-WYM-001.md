## 一、历史定位

王阳明（1472—1529），名守仁，字伯安，浙江余姚人，明代最杰出的思想家、教育家、军事家，心学集大成者。弘治十二年进士，因得罪刘瑾谪贬贵州龙场驿丞，于龙场悟道，确立'心即理'宗旨。后任南赣巡抚平定匪乱，仅三十五日平定宁王朱宸濠之乱，创立'致良知'学说。晚年提出'知行合一''四句教'（无善无恶心之体，有善有恶意之动，知善知恶是良知，为善去恶是格物），天泉证道确立晚年定论。其学上承陆九渊'心即理'，下启泰州学派与日本阳明学，打破朱子学'析心与理为二'的格局，强调'心外无物、心外无理'，圣人之道吾性自足。用兵'此心不动，随机而动'，以良知用兵，声东击西、攻心为上。代表作《传习录》《大学问》《王文成公全书》。

**代表著作**: 《传习录》、《大学问》、《王文成公全书》

**籍贯/身份**: Shaoxing Prefecture, Yuyao County (now Yuyao, Zhejiang)；中国（明）；汉族

**名言**: Unity of knowledge and action: knowing is the beginning of action, action is the completion of knowing; Extending innate knowledge: that bit of innate knowing is your own criterion; Unmoved mind acting on circumstances; Four-sentence teaching: Mind substance is neither good nor evil, intention stirring has good and evil, innate knowing knows good and evil, rectifying affairs is doing good and removing evil

**历史影响**: Wang Yangming (1472-1529), courtesy name Shouren, from Yuyao, Zhejiang, was the Ming dynasty's most distinguished philosopher, educator, and military strategist, and the crowning synthesizer of the School of Mind. A jinshi of 1499, he was exiled to Longchang, Guizhou after offending eunuch Liu Jin; there he attained enlightenment, establishing that 'mind is principle.' As Grand Coordinator of southern Jiangxi he suppressed banditry, and in just thirty-five days quelled Prince Ning's rebellion, developing his doctrine of 'extending innate knowledge.' His final teaching crystallized in the Four-Sentence Teaching and the Tianquan verification. Breaking Zhu Xi's separation of mind and principle, he taught that there is nothing outside the mind; the way of the sage is self-sufficient in one's own nature. In war he practiced 'an unmoved mind, acting according to circumstances.' Major works: Chuanxilu, Da Xue Wen, and the Complete Works.

## 三、十大思维模式 (M-WYM-001 ~ M-WYM-010)

### M-WYM-001 心即理法

- **英文名**: Mind-Is-Principle Method
- **类别**: 本体论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 1 | **研究稿ID**: M-WAN-001
- **领域**: 道德形上学/主体性 / Moral Metaphysics / Subjectivity
- **出处**: 《传习录》卷上
- **定义**: 心之本体即是天理，理不在心外，不必向外求索。良知即是天理，天理即是良知。此法是阳明心学的本体论根基，将道德的根源从外在规范转向内在主体，使道德判断获得了不可动摇的自主性。所谓'心即理'，并非否定外在事物的客观规律，而是指道德价值的判断标准不在外物，而在人心之良知。
- **定义(EN)**: The original substance of the mind is Heavenly Principle; principle does not exist outside the mind, no need to seek it externally. Liangzhi (innate knowing) is Heavenly Principle, and Heavenly Principle is Liangzhi. This is the ontological foundation of Yangming's School of Mind, shifting the root of morality from external norms to inner subjectivity and giving moral judgment an unshakable autonomy. 'Mind is principle' does not deny the objective laws of external things, but indicates that the standard for moral evaluation resides in the liangzhi of the human heart.
- **关键概念**: 心即理、良知即天理、心外无物、心外无理、道德主体性
- **原文依据**: 心即理也。天下又有心外之事，心外之理乎？
  > Mind is principle. Is there anything outside the mind, or any principle outside the mind, in the world?
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 识别向外求理的惯性思维
  2. 体认心之本体即是天理
  3. 以良知为判断是非的最终依据
  4. 在日用常行中印证心即理
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Identify the habitual tendency to seek principle externally
  2. Apprehend that the original substance of mind is Heavenly Principle
  3. Take liangzhi as the ultimate criterion for judging right and wrong
  4. Verify mind-is-principle in daily affairs
- **代表性案例**:
  - 王阳明年轻时无视朱熹'即物穷理'的教导，对着竹子'格'了七天七夜，病倒后领悟理不在外物而在内心，由此开启心学之路
  - 阳明答弟子问：'草碍吾庭，去之可乎？'曰：'草有妨碍，理亦宜去，去之而已。偶未即去，亦不累心。'——去草之事是理，但理在心上，不在草上
  - 反例警示：若把'心即理'理解为否定一切客观规律，则会陷入主观唯心主义的狂禅——阳明强调心即理是针对道德价值而言，并非否认物理世界的客观性
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - In his youth, Wang ignored Zhu Xi's teaching of 'investigating things to exhaust principle,' staring at bamboo for seven days and nights until he fell ill, then realized principle was not in external things but in the mind, thus beginning his path of the School of Mind
  - When asked whether to remove grass blocking his courtyard, Wang replied: 'If grass is an obstacle, principle dictates its removal — just remove it. If you cannot yet remove it, let it not disturb the mind.' The principle of removing grass is in the mind, not in the grass itself
  - Counterexample: reading 'mind is principle' as denying all objective laws leads to subjective idealist Zen-like arrogance — Wang meant this specifically for moral values, not for denying the objectivity of the physical world
- **当代应用**:
  - 价值判断的自主性
  - 道德教育的内在化
  - 企业文化中的价值观内化
  - 个人成长中的自我认同
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Autonomy of value judgment
  - Internalization of moral education
  - Value internalization in corporate culture
  - Self-identity in personal growth
- **关联模式**: M-WYM-002、M-WYM-003、M-WYM-005

### M-WYM-002 知行合一法

- **英文名**: Unity-of-Knowing-and-Acting Method
- **类别**: 认识论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 2 | **研究稿ID**: M-WAN-002
- **领域**: 知行关系/实践哲学 / Knowledge-Action Relationship / Practical Philosophy
- **出处**: 《传习录》卷上
- **定义**: 知与行是本体的两面，不可割裂。知是行的主意，行是知的功夫；知是行之始，行是知之成。真知必能行，不行不足谓之知。一念发动处，便即是行了。此法是阳明对当时学者'知而不行'弊病的有力针砭，强调道德认知必须落实为道德实践，二者本是一事。
- **定义(EN)**: Knowing and acting are two sides of one substance and cannot be separated. Knowing is the intention of acting; acting is the effort of knowing. Knowing is the beginning of acting; acting is the completion of knowing. True knowing necessarily leads to action; without action, it is not truly knowing. The moment a thought arises, it is already action. This is Wang's powerful corrective to the contemporary flaw of 'knowing without acting,' emphasizing that moral cognition must be implemented as moral practice, and the two are fundamentally one thing.
- **关键概念**: 知行本体、知是行之始、行是知之成、一念发动、真知必行
- **原文依据**: 知是行的主意，行是知的功夫；知是行之始，行是知之成。
  > Knowing is the intention of acting; acting is the effort of knowing. Knowing is the beginning of acting; acting is the completion of knowing.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 识别知而不行的自我欺骗
  2. 体认知行本是一体
  3. 在念头上察识善恶
  4. 将道德认知落实为道德实践
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Identify self-deception of knowing without acting
  2. Apprehend that knowing and acting are one substance
  3. Discern good and evil at the level of thought
  4. Implement moral cognition as moral practice
- **代表性案例**:
  - 阳明说：'真知即所以为行，不行不足谓之知。'如知孝必已行了孝，知君必已行了忠，未有知孝而不行孝者
  - 有人问：'目前人是知温清奉养的，如何不行？'阳明曰：'他不知，只是不会。若能知，未有不知行的。'——不知温清之节，故不行温清；不知奉养之宜，故不行奉养
  - 反例警示：若把知行合一理解为'知了就行'而忽略实践的艰难，则会陷入轻率的行动主义——阳明强调的是知行在本体上的一致性，而非取消实践的艰巨性
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Wang said: 'True knowing is that which enables acting; without acting, it cannot be called knowing.' For example, to truly know filial piety means one has already practiced it; to know loyalty means one has already been loyal — there is no one who knows filial piety yet does not practice it
  - Someone asked: 'People nowadays know the proper ways to warm and serve their parents, so why don't they practice it?' Wang replied: 'They do not truly know; they simply lack the ability. If they truly knew, they would not fail to act.' — not knowing the proper methods of warming and serving means not practicing them
  - Counterexample: reading unity-of-knowing-and-acting as 'once known, automatically done' while ignoring the difficulty of practice leads to reckless activism — Wang emphasizes the ontological consistency of knowing and acting, not the elimination of practice's arduousness
- **当代应用**:
  - 克服知行脱节
  - 行为改变的策略
  - 教育中的实践导向
  - 组织执行力提升
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Overcoming knowledge-action gap
  - Strategies for behavior change
  - Practice-oriented education
  - Enhancing organizational execution
- **关联模式**: M-WYM-001、M-WYM-003、M-WYM-004

### M-WYM-003 致良知法

- **英文名**: Extending-Innate-Knowing Method
- **类别**: 工夫论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 3 | **研究稿ID**: M-WAN-003
- **领域**: 修养方法论/道德实践 / Cultivation Methodology / Moral Practice
- **出处**: 《传习录》卷中
- **定义**: 致吾心之良知于事事物物，使事事物物皆得其理。良知是心之本体，是人人本具的是非准则。致良知，就是将此内在的是非准则推至事事物物，使一切行为皆符合良知的要求。此法是阳明晚年所提的学问宗旨，'吾平生讲学，只是致良知三字'，是心学的最高概括和最终归宿。
- **定义(EN)**: Extend the liangzhi (innate knowing) within one's mind to all things, so that all things receive their proper order. Liangzhi is the original substance of the mind, the innate criterion of right and wrong present in every person. Extending innate knowing means applying this inner criterion to all affairs, so that all actions accord with liangzhi. This is the ultimate teaching principle Wang proposed in his later years: 'In my life of teaching, I have only spoken these three words — extend innate knowing.' It is the highest summary and final destination of the School of Mind.
- **关键概念**: 致良知、良知即天理、推致、是非之心、学问头脑
- **原文依据**: 致吾心之良知于事事物物也。吾心之良知，即所谓天理也。致吾心良知之天理于事事物物，则事事物物皆得其理矣。
  > Extend the liangzhi of my mind to all things. The liangzhi of my mind is what is called Heavenly Principle. To extend the Heavenly Principle of my liangzhi to all things is so that all things receive their proper order.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 确认良知是本具的是非准则
  2. 在念念中觉察良知之发用
  3. 将良知推致于事事物物
  4. 使一切行为皆符合良知的要求
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Confirm that liangzhi is the innate criterion of right and wrong
  2. Discern the functioning of liangzhi in each thought
  3. Extend liangzhi to all affairs and things
  4. Bring all actions into accord with the demands of liangzhi
- **代表性案例**:
  - 阳明答弟子问格物：'格者，正也，正其不正以归于正也。'格物不是穷究外物之理，而是正心之物——使意之所在之事归于正
  - 阳明说：'尔那一点良知，是尔自家的准则。尔意念着处，他是便知是，非便知非，更瞒他一些不得。此便是格物的真诀，致知的实功。'
  - 反例警示：若把致良知理解为纯粹的内省冥想而忽略外在实践，则会落入禅定式的空修——阳明强调致良知必须在事上磨练，在人情事变中体认
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - When asked about Gewu (investigating things), Wang replied: 'Ge means rectify — to rectify what is not upright and return it to uprightness.' Investigating things is not exhausting the principle of external objects, but rectifying the matters of the mind
  - Wang said: 'That point of liangzhi in you is your own standard. When your thoughts arise, it knows what is right, knows what is wrong, and cannot be deceived even slightly. This is the true secret of Gewu and the real work of extending knowledge'
  - Counterexample: reading extending innate knowing as pure introspective meditation while ignoring external practice falls into Zen-like empty cultivation — Wang emphasizes that extending liangzhi must be practiced in affairs, discerned in human relationships and worldly events
- **当代应用**:
  - 道德直觉的培养
  - 决策中的良知判断
  - 自我监督与反思
  - 领导力的道德根基
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Cultivation of moral intuition
  - Liangzhi-based judgment in decision-making
  - Self-supervision and reflection
  - Moral foundation of leadership
- **关联模式**: M-WYM-001、M-WYM-002、M-WYM-004

### M-WYM-004 念念察识法

- **英文名**: Discerning-Each-Thought Method
- **类别**: 工夫论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 4 | **研究稿ID**: M-WAN-004
- **领域**: 心性修养/自我觉察 / Mind-Nature Cultivation / Self-Awareness
- **出处**: 《传习录》卷上
- **定义**: 在每一个念头的发动处保持良知的觉察，善念即行之，恶念即克之。此法是致良知工夫的具体操作，强调在意念初动之时就要保持警觉，因为一念发动处便即是行了。善念须当即扩充推行，恶念须当即克制消除，不可任其潜滋暗长。
- **定义(EN)**: Maintain the discernment of liangzhi at the moment each thought arises: promote good thoughts, suppress evil thoughts. This is the specific operation of extending innate knowing, emphasizing vigilance at the initial movement of thought, because the moment a thought arises, it is already action. Good thoughts must be immediately expanded and practiced; evil thoughts must be immediately suppressed and eliminated, not allowed to grow secretly.
- **关键概念**: 念念察识、一念发动、克己、善念扩充、恶念克制
- **原文依据**: 善念发而知之，而充之；恶念发而知之，而遏之。
  > When a good thought arises, one knows it and expands it; when an evil thought arises, one knows it and restrains it.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 在念头初动时保持警觉
  2. 以良知判别念头的善恶
  3. 善念当即推行，恶念当即克制
  4. 持之以恒，不间断察识
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Maintain vigilance when thoughts first arise
  2. Discern good and evil of thoughts through liangzhi
  3. Immediately practice good thoughts, immediately suppress evil thoughts
  4. Persevere without interruption in discernment
- **代表性案例**:
  - 阳明教导弟子：'克己须要扫除廓清，一毫不存方是。有一毫在，则众恶相引而来。'——强调在念头上用功，须彻底清除私欲，不可留一丝一毫
  - 有人问：'静时意思亦好，及遇事时便不能。'阳明曰：'此只是用功不力。静时功夫，亦须有事上磨练方可。'
  - 反例警示：若把念念察识理解为压抑正常情感和欲望，则会陷入枯寂的禁欲主义——阳明强调的是在念头上用功，而非消灭念头本身
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Wang instructed disciples: 'Suppressing oneself requires sweeping and clearing completely; not leaving the slightest trace is correct. If even a hair's breadth remains, all evils will be drawn together.' — emphasizing work on thoughts, one must thoroughly eliminate selfish desires without leaving the smallest trace
  - Someone asked: 'When alone in stillness my thoughts are good, but when events arise I cannot maintain them.' Wang replied: 'This is simply insufficient effort. Stillness practice must also be tempered through affairs'
  - Counterexample: reading discerning each thought as suppressing normal emotions and desires leads to ascetic emptiness — Wang emphasizes working on thoughts, not eliminating thoughts themselves
- **当代应用**:
  - 正念冥想
  - 情绪管理
  - 习惯养成
  - 自我监督
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Mindfulness meditation
  - Emotional management
  - Habit formation
  - Self-supervision
- **关联模式**: M-WYM-002、M-WYM-003、M-WYM-005

### M-WYM-005 龙场悟本法

- **英文名**: Longchang Enlightenment Method
- **类别**: 认识论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 5 | **研究稿ID**: M-WAN-005
- **领域**: 认知转折/顿悟方法 / Cognitive Turning Point / Sudden Enlightenment Method
- **出处**: 《王阳明年谱》
- **定义**: 在极端困境中超越生死执念，回归内心，体认'圣人之道，吾性自足'。正德元年（1506年），王阳明被贬至贵州龙场，在瘴疠之地、生死边缘，日夜端居澄默，终于一夜大悟，认识到向之求理于事物者皆是误也。此法是阳明心学的源头，也是其在绝境中实现认知突破的经典案例。
- **定义(EN)**: Transcending the obsession with life and death in extreme adversity, returning to the inner self, and apprehending 'the Way of the sage is fully present within my nature.' In 1506, Wang was exiled to Longchang in Guizhou, where in a land of miasma facing death and life, he sat in meditation day and night. Finally, in one night he had a great enlightenment, realizing that seeking principle in external things had all been erroneous. This is the source of Yangming's School of Mind and a classic case of cognitive breakthrough in desperate circumstances.
- **关键概念**: 龙场悟道、吾性自足、困境突破、生死超越、向内求索
- **原文依据**: 圣人之道，吾性自足，向之求理于事物者误也。
  > The Way of the sage is fully present within my nature; seeking principle in external things was the error all along.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 处于极端困境，放下对外在的依赖
  2. 日夜端居澄默，向内观照
  3. 超越生死执念，直面生命本质
  4. 忽然大悟，体认圣人之道吾性自足
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. In extreme adversity, let go of dependence on external things
  2. Sit in still meditation day and night, turning inward
  3. Transcend the obsession with life and death, face the essence of existence
  4. Suddenly achieve great enlightenment, apprehend that the Way of the sage is fully within
- **代表性案例**:
  - 龙场悟道后，阳明豁然贯通，从此'始知圣人之道，吾性自足'，不再向外求索，开启了心学的伟大事业
  - 阳明自述悟道过程：'其夜宿龙冈庵，忽中夜大悟格物致知之旨，不觉呼跃而起，从者皆惊。'
  - 反例警示：若把龙场悟道理解为神秘主义的个人体验，则会错失其方法论意义——悟道的关键在于放下对外在权威的依赖，回归内心的主体性
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - After the Longchang enlightenment, Wang achieved sudden breakthrough, realizing 'the Way of the sage is fully within,' no longer seeking externally, thus beginning the great enterprise of the School of Mind
  - Wang described the process: 'That night at Longgang Hermitage, suddenly in the middle of the night I had a great enlightenment on the meaning of Gewu and zhizhi, involuntarily leaping up in excitement, startling my followers.'
  - Counterexample: reading Longchang enlightenment as mystical personal experience misses its methodological significance — the key is letting go of dependence on external authority and returning to the subjectivity of the inner self
- **当代应用**:
  - 逆境中的认知突破
  - 人生转折的应对
  - 创造性思维的激发
  - 自我认知的重构
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Cognitive breakthrough in adversity
  - Coping with life turning points
  - Stimulating creative thinking
  - Reconstructing self-understanding
- **关联模式**: M-WYM-001、M-WYM-006、M-WYM-008

- **类别**: 工夫论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 6 | **研究稿ID**: M-WAN-006
- **领域**: 心性修养/境界论 / Mind-Nature Cultivation / Theory of Realm
- **出处**: 《传习录》卷下
- **定义**: 无善无恶心之体，有善有恶意之动，知善知恶是良知，为善去恶是格物。四句教是阳明晚年对心学体系的完整概括，涵盖了本体、发用、工夫三个层面：心之本体超越善恶对待，意念发动则有善恶之分，良知具有辨别善恶的能力，格物就是为善去恶的实践工夫。此法是阳明心学的总纲，也是其后学分化的重要节点。
- **定义(EN)**: The substance of mind transcends good and evil; when thoughts arise, there is good and evil; innate knowing discerns good and evil; Gewu is the practical work of promoting good and eliminating evil. The Four-Sentence Teaching is Wang's late-life complete summary of the School of Mind, covering ontology, manifestation, and practice: the substance of mind transcends the duality of good and evil, thoughts bring the distinction of good and evil, liangzhi has the capacity to discern good and evil, Gewu is the practical work of promoting good and eliminating evil. This is the general outline of Yangming's School of Mind and an important node in the later differentiation of his disciples.
- **关键概念**: 无善无恶、心之体、意之动、良知、格物
- **原文依据**: 无善无恶心之体，有善有恶意之动，知善知恶是良知，为善去恶是格物。

- **操作步骤**:
  1. 体认心之本体超越善恶对待
  2. 觉察意念发动时的善恶分别
  3. 以良知判别是非善恶
  4. 在为善去恶中实践格物
- **操作步骤(EN)**:

  2. Discern the distinction of good and evil when thoughts arise
  3. Use liangzhi to judge right and wrong
  4. Practice Gewu in promoting good and eliminating evil
- **代表性案例**:
  - 天泉证道时，阳明向钱德洪、王畿宣讲四句教，叮嘱二人'以后再不可更此四字'，显示此教法的根本性和重要性
  - 四句教中'无善无恶心之体'并非否定道德，而是指心体本身超越善恶对待，是绝对的至善；'有善有恶意之动'说明善恶来自意念的发动
  - 反例警示：若把'无善无恶'理解为道德虚无主义，则会滑向纵欲的借口——阳明强调的是心体的超越性，而非否定善恶的价值判断
- **代表性案例(EN)**:

  - In the four sentences, 'the substance of mind transcends good and evil' does not deny morality, but indicates that the mind's substance transcends the duality of good and evil, being absolute perfect goodness; 'when thoughts arise, there is good and evil' shows that good and evil come from the movement of thoughts
  - Counterexample: reading 'transcending good and evil' as moral nihilism leads to the excuse for indulgence — Wang emphasizes the transcendence of the mind's substance, not the denial of value judgment regarding good and evil

### M-WYM-007 事上磨练法

  - Counterexample: reading tempering in affairs as busyness that ignores inner cultivation leads to the trap of mere activity — Wang emphasizes maintaining conscious liangzhi in affairs, not being dragged along by them

### M-WYM-008 人人成圣法

- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Confirm that liangzhi is innately present in everyone

- **代表性案例**:

### M-WYM-009 心外无物法

- **定义(EN)**: Nothing exists outside the mind; no affairs exist outside the mind; no principle exists outside the mind. The meaning and value of things only manifest under the illumination of the mind. The metaphor of flowers in the rock: 'When you have not yet looked at this flower, the flower and your mind return to silence together; when you come to look at this flower, its color suddenly becomes clear.' - **操作步骤(EN)**:

  4. Let each thing attain its proper place under the mind's illumination
- **代表性案例**:

## 五、跨引用

- **H-ZHX-001** (vs): 心即理 vs 性即理：王阳明批评朱子'析心与理为二'，主张理不在事物而在吾心；朱子格物穷理是外向认识论，阳明格物正念头是内向实践论。这是宋明理学'理学'与'心学'两系的分水岭。
  > Mind-is-Principle vs Nature-is-Principle: Wang criticized Zhu Xi's splitting of mind and principle; Zhu's investigation of things is outward-directed epistemology, Wang's is inward-directed praxis. This is the watershed between Neo-Confucian rationalism and the School of Mind.
- **H-LJY-001** (inheritance): 王阳明承陆九渊'心即理''六经注我'之旨，以'致良知'完成心学体系化；陆发其端而未畅，阳明扩充到底，故并称'陆王心学'。
  > Wang inherited Lu Jiuyuan's 'mind is principle' and 'the classics annotate me,' completing the systematization of the School of Mind through 'extending innate knowledge'; hence the joint name Lu-Wang.
- **H-KZ-001** (inheritance): 王阳明自述'所学皆由孔子之统绪'，知行合一与万物一体之仁直承孔子'吾道一以贯之'与仁学传统。
  > Wang claimed his learning continued Confucius's line: unity of knowledge and action and the benevolence of all-things-one-body carry forward 'my way is strung on one thread' and the ren tradition.
- **H-HZX-001** (philosophical_debate): 王阳明'满街都是圣人'的人人皆可为圣论，与黄宗羲以《明夷待访录》批判君主专制一脉相通：黄宗羲师承刘宗周，刘宗周承阳明蕺山一系，阳明学的心性平等观为明清批判思想提供了内在资源。
  > Wang's doctrine that 'the whole street is full of sages' connects to Huang Zongxi's critique of despotism: Huang, heir of Liu Zongzhou in the Yangming line, drew on the Yangming view of moral equality of all minds.
- **H-DZS-001** (vs): 良知内在自律 vs 天人感应他律：董仲舒以超验之天制约君权，王阳明以人人内在良知作为道德最高法庭。外在权威与内在权威构成中国政治伦理思想史的两极。
  > Innate-conscience autonomy vs heaven-human heteronomy: Dong constrained rulers through transcendent Heaven; Wang made each person's innate moral knowing the supreme court. External versus internal authority as two poles of Chinese ethical-political thought.
- **H-SMQ-001** (comparison): 王阳明'心外无物'与司马迁'究天人之际'：两者都把探究焦点从外在天命转向人的世界——司马迁转向历史人事，王阳明转向本心良知。
  > Wang's 'nothing outside the mind' and Sima Qian's 'probing heaven and humanity': both shifted inquiry from external mandate to the human world—Sima to history and affairs, Wang to the mind's innate knowing.
