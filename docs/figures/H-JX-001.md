## 一、历史定位

纪弦（1913-2013），原名路逾，笔名路易士、青空律，原籍陕西周至，生于河北清苑。台湾现代派诗歌运动的奠基人与倡导者，被誉为'台湾三老之首'。1953年创办并主编《现代诗》季刊，正式发起台湾现代诗运动。他提出著名的'六大信条'——主张'横的移植'（向西方现代主义学习）而非'纵的继承'（中国传统诗学），强调知性在诗歌创作中的主导地位。代表作《你的名字》以'用了世界上最轻最轻的声音，轻轻地唤你的名字每夜每夜'闻名于世。2013年7月22日在美国加州逝世，享年101岁。

## 二、核心思想

- "横的移植"——向西方现代主义学习
- 知性之强调——理性思辨在诗歌中的核心地位
- "纯粹的诗"——区别于散文的诗之本体论
- 存在主义是现代诗的源流

- 原文: 现代诗是横的移植，不是纵的继承。
  > Modern poetry is horizontal transplantation, not longitudinal inheritance.
- 出处: 《现代诗》季刊·六大信条之一

## 四、现代场景应用 (C-JX-001 ~ C-JX-010)

### C-JX-001 / C-JX-001E - "横的移植"——台湾现代诗运动中的跨文化借鉴案例

- **C-JX-001**: 纪弦在1950年代台湾诗坛提出'横的移植'理论，主张直接向西方现代主义诗歌学习（如波德莱尔、兰波、艾略特等），而非沿着中国传统诗学的纵向脉络继承。这一理念通过《现代诗》季刊推广，从根本上改变了台湾诗歌的发展方向，成为华语现代诗史上的里程碑事件。
- **C-JX-001E**: In the 1950s, Ji Xian proposed his 'horizontal transplantation' theory in Taiwan's poetry scene, advocating direct learning from Western modernist poets (Baudelaire, Rimbaud, Eliot) rather than following the longitudinal lineage of traditional Chinese poetics. This concept, promoted through his Modern Poetry quarterly, fundamentally changed the direction of Taiwanese poetry and became a milestone in the history of Chinese-language modern poetry.

### C-JX-002 / C-JX-002E - "知性之强调"——诗歌创作中理性思辨的主导地位案例

- **C-JX-002**: 纪弦在《现代诗》六条信条中明确提出'新诗是知性的强调'，主张诗歌创作不应仅凭感性抒情，而应融入理性思辨与智性思考。这一理念体现在他的诗作如《你的名字》中，表面轻柔的语言下蕴含着对存在、记忆与情感深度的哲学思考。
- **C-JX-002E**: Ji Xian explicitly stated in his six commandments that 'new poetry is the emphasis on intellectuality,' arguing that poetry should not rely solely on emotional lyricism but should incorporate rational reflection and intellectual thought. This is embodied in works like 'Your Name,' where gentle language conceals philosophical depth about existence, memory, and emotional resonance.

### C-JX-003 / C-JX-003E - "纯粹的诗"——区分诗歌与散文的本体论案例

- **C-JX-003**: 纪弦主张'现代诗是纯粹的诗，区别于散文的诗'，强调诗歌应有其独立的本体价值与审美自律性。这一理论立场使台湾现代诗从传统抒情中解放出来，转向对语言、形式和意象的纯粹探索，影响了后续几代台湾诗人的创作取向。
- **C-JX-003E**: Ji Xian asserted that 'modern poetry is pure poetry, distinct from prose-poetry,' emphasizing poetry's independent ontological value and aesthetic autonomy. This theoretical stance liberated Taiwanese modern poetry from traditional lyricism, shifting toward pure exploration of language, form, and imagery, influencing subsequent generations of Taiwanese poets.

### C-JX-004 / C-JX-004E - 存在主义诗学——现代诗歌的思想资源案例

- **C-JX-004**: 纪弦主张存在主义是现代诗的重要源流，将萨特、加缪等存在主义哲学思想引入诗歌创作。他的诗中常以'独狼'自喻，表达个体的孤独、自由与反抗精神，将存在主义关于人类处境的思考转化为深刻的诗学表达。
- **C-JX-004E**: Ji Xian argued that existentialism is a crucial source of modern poetry, introducing Sartre and Camus's philosophical ideas into poetic creation. His poems often use the 'lone wolf' metaphor, expressing individual loneliness, freedom, and resistance — transforming existentialist reflections on the human condition into profound poetic expression.

### C-JX-005 / C-JX-005E - "诗是人类生活中的高扬部分"——诗歌超越性价值的理论建构案例

- **C-JX-005**: 纪弦在其六条信条中提出'诗是人类生活中的高扬部分'，认为诗歌不应只是生活的记录或反映，而应超越日常经验，达到精神层面的升华与提升。这一理论为台湾现代诗确立了崇高的价值定位，使诗歌成为人类精神自由的载体。
- **C-JX-005E**: In his six commandments, Ji Xian proposed that 'poetry is the elevated part of human life,' asserting that poetry should transcend everyday experience and reach spiritual elevation. This theory established a lofty value position for Taiwanese modern poetry, making poetry the vehicle of human spiritual freedom.

### C-JX-006 / C-JX-006E - "六个信条"——系统化现代诗学理论建构案例

- **C-JX-006**: 纪弦在《现代诗》季刊中系统提出六大信条：横的移植、知性强调、纯粹的诗、存在主义源流、高扬部分、新的人类。这六个信条构成了完整的现代诗学理论体系，从方法论（横的移植）到本体论（纯粹的诗），再到价值论（高扬部分），为台湾现代诗建立了系统的理论框架。
- **C-JX-006E**: Ji Xian systematically proposed six commandments in Modern Poetry: horizontal transplantation, emphasis on intellectuality, pure poetry, existentialist source, elevated part, and new humanity. These six commandments formed a complete modern poetic theory system — from methodology (horizontal transplantation) to ontology (pure poetry) and axiology (elevated part) — providing a systematic theoretical framework for Taiwanese modern poetry.

### C-JX-007 / C-JX-007E - "独狼"精神——孤独独立的艺术人格案例

- **C-JX-007**: 纪弦在诗中常以'独狼'自喻，表达其不随流俗、独立特行的艺术人格。他不认同群居式的创作模式，坚持个体的独特视角和表达方式，这种'独狼'精神成为台湾现代诗坛的精神象征，影响了无数追求艺术独立的年轻诗人。
- **C-JX-007E**: Ji Xian frequently identified himself as a 'lone wolf' in his poems, expressing an artistic persona that refused to conform and stood independent of crowds. Rejecting group-style creative models, he insisted on individual perspective and unique expression. This 'lone wolf' spirit became a spiritual symbol of Taiwan's modern poetry scene, influencing countless young poets pursuing artistic independence.

### C-JX-008 / C-JX-008E - 蓝星论战——现代诗路线争鸣案例

- **C-JX-008**: 纪弦与蓝星诗社（覃子豪、余光中等）之间的论战是台湾现代诗史上的重要事件。蓝星诗社主张'抒情传统'与'民族精神'，反对纪弦'横的移植'理论。这场论战促进了台湾现代诗的多元化发展，使不同诗学理念在碰撞中深化，推动了诗歌创作的繁荣。
- **C-JX-008E**: Ji Xian's debates with the Blue Star Poetry Society (Qin Zihao, Yu Guangzhong et al.) were pivotal events in Taiwan's modern poetry history. Blue Star advocated 'lyrical tradition' and 'national spirit,' opposing Ji's 'horizontal transplantation.' This debate promoted Taiwan modern poetry's多元化 (diversity), deepening poetic creation through clashing philosophies.

### C-JX-009 / C-JX-009E - "新的人类"——诗歌面向现代人的价值取向案例

- **C-JX-009**: 纪弦在第六信条中提出'新诗必须属于新的人类（现代人类）'，主张诗歌应面向现代社会的人及其生存境遇。这一理念使台湾现代诗从古典抒情传统转向对现代人精神状态的关注，为诗歌注入了时代感和现实关怀。
- **C-JX-009E**: In his sixth commandment, Ji Xian declared that 'new poetry must belong to new humanity (modern humans),' advocating that poetry should address modern society's people and their existential circumstances. This shifted Taiwanese modern poetry from classical lyricism toward attention to modern humans' spiritual conditions, infusing poetry with contemporary relevance and realistic concern.

### C-JX-010 / C-JX-010E - 终身创作——以持续实践验证诗学理念的案例

- **C-JX-010**: 纪弦从1930年代活跃于上海文坛，到1953年在台湾创办《现代诗》季刊，再到晚年仍不断创作出版诗集（如90岁时的《年方九十》），以跨越近一个世纪的持续创作实践验证其诗学理念。他90岁时仍保持活跃思维与丰富想象力，'仍然非常美'，成为终身创作的典范。
- **C-JX-010E**: From his active years in 1930s Shanghai literary circles, to founding Modern Poetry quarterly in Taiwan (1953), and continuing creative output into old age (including 'Fifty at Ninety' published at age 90), Ji Xian validated his poetic theory through nearly a century of continuous creative practice. Even at 90, he maintained active thinking and rich imagination — 'still very beautiful' — setting an example of lifelong creative dedication.
