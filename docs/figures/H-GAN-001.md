## M-GAN-001 萨提亚格拉哈真理坚持法 / Satyagraha (Truth-Force Adherence)

把对抗的重心从'压倒对手'改写为'唤醒对手'：satya（真理）+agraha（坚持），即以对真理的执着替代对武力的执着——对手不是敌人，而是'同陷错途的受难者'；抗争的目标不是消灭他，而是让他看见共同错在哪。要害有三：其一，真理先行——任何行动之前先确认自己站在可验证、可公开陈述的真相一边，撒谎一次则 satyagraha 整体作废；其二，对手非敌——把对手当作要转化（convert）而非击败（defeat）的对象，故胜利的定义是'对手心服'而非'对手屈服'；其三，自愿受苦——以自己承受代价（入狱、没收、绝食）而非让对手付出代价来显示真理的分量，苦难成为论证本身。方法论签名：弱者的力量不由刀剑担保，而由'我愿为此真理先赴苦'的可信度担保——真理不是喊出来的，是以身试出来的。

Satyagraha rewrites the very objective of an unequal contest: not to overpower the opponent but to awaken him. Gandhi coined the word from satya (truth) and agraha (holding firmly) in South Africa in 1906, deliberately rejecting 'passive resistance' as too negative—the practitioner does not resist passively; he adheres to truth actively, and the opponent is not an enemy but a fellow human being trapped in the same error, to be converted rather than defeated. Three disciplines give the mode its force. First, truth before action: before any campaign the actor must verify, and be willing to state publicly and testably, that his side stands on facts—one deliberate lie voids the entire method, because the whole apparatus runs on credibility rather than coercion. Second, the opponent is convertible: victory is defined as the opponent's own conscience conceding, not his will being crushed, which is why Gandhi sought settlements—like the Gandhi-Irwin Pact—that let the adversary walk away with dignity intact. Third, voluntary suffering is the argument: instead of making the opponent pay a price, the satyagrahi absorbs one—jail, confiscation, fasting—so the visible cost of holding truth becomes the proof of truth; suffering is not collateral damage but the medium of persuasion itself. This inverts the standard logic of power: the weak, lacking armies and treasuries, possess in their own bodies a currency the strong cannot counterfeit—the willingness to lose for what is true. Signature: the weak do not borrow force; they spend credibility—and suffering freely accepted is the only proof of conviction that no treasury can purchase and no bayonet can refute.

流程: 真相核验：行动前把己方事实陈述写清、公开、可验——凡经不起对账的诉求立即撤回 → 对象转化：把对手定位为'需被唤醒的共同受难者'而非敌人，为每一轮对抗预留心服的出口 → 以身试理：不向对手索取代价，而自愿承担入狱、没收、绝食等代价，让承受本身成为论证 → 心服收官：胜利以对手良知认可为准（谈判、让步、修法），不以单方面屈服为终点

金句: 真理之力不是压倒对手，而是唤醒对手——我愿先于任何人承受这真理的代价。

## M-GAN-002 非暴力不合作法 / Non-Violent Non-Cooperation (Ahimsa Discipline)

把'不合作'从情绪化的抵制升级为一门有纪律的工程学：ahimsa（不害）不是消极不动，而是对暴力手段的绝对禁运——同时以成千上万普通人'撤回合作'来冻结统治机器的运转：不买英货、不上法院、不进官办学校、不受封号。统治依赖被治者的配合，撤回配合即是最深的合法性质疑。要害有三：其一，非暴力是纪律不是天性——受训者须事先宣誓、演练挨打不还手，'非暴力者与暴力者同样勇敢且须更勇敢'；其二，不合作有梯度——从退还封号、抵制英货到抗税，按训练程度递进，未受训者不得参与高危层级；其三，合作是权力的来源这一判断把斗争场从战场挪到日常——每个消费者、学生、律师都天然握有武器。方法论签名：统治机器每转一圈都要有人上油——不合作者不发一枪，只拒绝再上油；机器越大，断油越致命。

Non-cooperation upgrades boycott from an outburst of anger into a disciplined engineering of withdrawal: ahimsa (non-injury) is not passivity but a total embargo on violent means, exercised alongside the mass refusal of cooperation that keeps any regime running—buy no foreign cloth, staff no courts, attend no official schools, accept no titles. Every government, Gandhi argued, rests on the cooperation of the governed; withdraw the cooperation and the deepest question of legitimacy is posed without a single shot. Three disciplines carry the mode. First, non-violence is a drill, not a temperament: volunteers take vows beforehand and rehearse taking blows without returning them, because 'the non-violent are as brave as the violent—braver,' and an untrained crowd is a mob that will convert boycott into riot within an hour. Second, non-cooperation is graduated: from returning honors and boycotting goods up through tax refusal, each rung demands a higher level of training, and participants below the required grade are barred from the dangerous rungs—escalation is rationed by preparation. Third, the premise that cooperation is the source of power moves the battlefield into ordinary life: every consumer, student, and lawyer already holds the weapon, which is why a movement of spinners and school-boycotters could shake an empire with no arsenal at all. At its 1920-22 peak the campaign made colonial courts, councils, and cloth markets visibly malfunction across a subcontinent. Signature: every machine needs someone to oil it—cooperators fire no shot; they simply stop oiling, and the larger the machine, the more fatal the drought.

流程: 权力断源：先画出统治机器的'合作依赖清单'（货、法、学、衔、税），选定普通人零门槛可断的一环 → 训练在前：参与者先宣誓受训——挨打不还手、不侮辱对手、不藏武器，未受训者不得上场 → 梯度升级：从低危（退衔抵货）到高危（抗税抗命）逐级放行，每级以训练度为准入门槛 → 违纪即停：任何暴力出轨立即中止相应行动并公开担责——纪律是方法的氧气，断氧即熄火

金句: 非暴力不是弱者的遮羞布，而是比暴力更难的纪律——不发一枪，让机器自己停转。

## M-GAN-003 自苦感化法 / Tapasya (Self-Suffering as Moral Pressure)

把冲突的代价从对手身上移到自己身上：tapasya（自苦）以绝食为最高形式——不强迫、不威胁，只把自己的生命押在论点之上，让'我为此真理而死'的可见性成为对手良知与国际舆论无法卸载的压强。要害有三：其一，绝食是对自己人的武器——甘地明言绝食不能用来迫使敌人就范，只能针对'与自己有情感纽带者'（同志、同教、被统治者），因为其机理是爱的债而非力的压；其二，先礼后苦——绝食前必先穷尽说理、谈判、请愿，且把理由、诉求、期限全部公开，使自苦可被检验而非表演；其三，不成功便成仁的真诚度由历史担保——1932年反对分划贱民选区的绝食推进至濒死，逼出《浦那协定》，制度为此改写。方法论签名：把账单开给自己——当对手看见你已把自己逼到墙角，他面对的不再是威胁而是镜子：不公若继续，他将被写进你倒下的那一页。

Tapasya moves the price of a conflict off the opponent and onto oneself: fasting is its highest form—no force, no threat, only one's own life staked on the claim, so that the visible readiness to die becomes a pressure no opponent's conscience and no international audience can unload. Three disciplines make it work rather than theatrical. First, the fast is a weapon aimed at one's own side: Gandhi insisted a fast must never coerce an enemy, only those bound to him by ties of love or duty—comrades, co-religionists, his own people—because its mechanism is the debt of love, not the leverage of force; fasting at an adversary is blackmail, fasting before one's own is testimony. Second, persuasion first, suffering last: every fast followed exhausted reasoning, negotiation, and petition, with the reasons, the demand, and the deadline published in advance so the self-suffering is auditable rather than staged—a fast without a stated, narrow, rescindable condition is suicide, not method. Third, sincerity is vouched for by history, not rhetoric: the 1932 fast against separate electorates for the Depressed Classes was carried to the edge of death until the Poona Pact rewrote the electoral arrangement; the willingness to go through with it was what converted moral theater into constitutional fact. The mode converts the weakest body into the strongest instrument: hunger is a resource the poorest can mint, and unlike violence it cannot be answered with counter-violence—only with conscience or with the humiliation of watching a man die. Signature: bill yourself first—when the opponent sees you have cornered yourself, he faces not a threat but a mirror: if the injustice continues, history will record it on the page where you fell.

流程: 穷尽说理：绝食前把谈判、请愿、公开辩论全部走完——自苦是最后手段而非开场白 → 限定对象：只对'与自己有情感/义务纽带者'绝食（同志、同教、本国民众），不对敌人做胁迫 → 公示条款：把理由、具体诉求、解除条件、期限全部公开，使绝食可审计、可叫停、不可诬为表演 → 以身践诺：把承受推进到真实不可逆（濒死亦不破例），让对手面对'良知或耻辱'的二元选择

金句: 绝食的矛头永远向内——我以自己的饥饿向自己的同胞讨要他们的良知。

## M-GAN-004 道义资本蛰伏积累法 / Latent Accumulation of Moral Capital

敌强我弱时不急于总攻，而以合法、局部、可撤回的小步行动系统性地积累三样弱者唯一能积累的资本：案例（每次小胜都是方法与正当性的公开演示）、组织（每次行动训练出一批受过纪律淬炼的骨干）、声誉（对'守法与诚信'的长期自持使日后更大的不服从自带合法性）。南非二十一年即是教科书式的蛰伏期：从1894年成立纳塔尔印度人大会递请愿，到1906年才第一次主动犯法，中间12年只做合法积蓄。要害有三：其一，蛰伏不是无所作为而是定向积蓄——每一步都写进方法学档案（甘地在《印度舆论》上持续刊载实验记录）；其二，'可撤回'是试错的前提——小步行动保持随时可停，失败不透支信用；其三，升级有时点判据：当对手以更大压迫回应合法请愿（1906《亚洲法》），即是道义资本足以支撑'公开犯法'的信号。方法论签名：弱者打不起总攻，但每一场小仗都在为最后的总攻存本钱——存的是案例、骨干与声誉，这三样火种越积，火才烧得越旺。

When the enemy is strong and you are weak, do not rush the general offensive; instead accumulate—through legal, local, retractable small actions—the only three forms of capital a weak side can build: cases (every small victory is a public demonstration of method and legitimacy), cadres (every action trains a batch of discipline-hardened organizers), and reputation (long self-restraint within the law makes later, larger disobedience arrive pre-legitimized). Gandhi's twenty-one years in South Africa are the textbook latent phase: from founding the Natal Indian Congress and petitioning in 1894 to the first deliberate law-breaking only in 1906, twelve years of purely legal accumulation preceded the first satyagraha. Three disciplines govern the accumulation. First, latency is directed, not dormant: every step is logged—his paper Indian Opinion functioned as a running lab notebook of experiments, so each small campaign compounded method rather than merely mood. Second, retractability is the precondition of trial and error: small steps stay stoppable at any point, so failures cost attention but never credit. Third, escalation has a trigger test: when the opponent answers legal petition with heavier repression (the 1906 Asiatic Registration Act), that response is the signal that accumulated moral capital can now underwrite open defiance—escalate on the opponent's escalation, not on one's own impatience. When Gandhi returned to India in 1915, he spent his first years in local campaigns—Champaran, Ahmedabad, Kheda—precisely to re-run the accumulation loop on the national stage before the 1920 general offensive. Signature: the weak cannot afford a general offensive, but every skirmish deposits principal for it—cases, cadres, and reputation are the kindling; the larger the pile, the hotter the final fire.

流程: 小步定标：以合法、局部、可撤回为约束，设计每一步小行动并预先写明其要积累的是案例还是骨干还是声誉 → 档案化：每次行动的过程、结果、教训持续公开刊载，使个人经验升为可复制的方法学 → 守边界蓄信：长期严格守法与守诺，为日后更大的不服从预存合法性 → 以对手升级定升级：当对手以压迫回应合法请愿时，才把道义资本兑换为公开犯法的下一阶段

金句: 弱者存不了军火，但存得了火种——每一场小仗存下的案例、骨干与声誉，就是总攻那天的燃料。

## M-GAN-005 运动自我净化纠错法 / Movement Self-Purification and Error Correction

把运动自身的失范当作最高级的纠错信号：群众中出现暴力，不是'被煽动的外部事件'，而是运动内部道德训练的失败——追责先向内。1922年乔里·乔拉示威者烧死22名警察，甘地当月宣布无限期中止整个全国非合作运动并公开自责：宁可交还已到手的政治 momentum，也不让方法在带病的肌体上继续。要害有三：其一，标准内化——非暴力纪律是运动的'存在理由'而非'可谈判的策略选项'，故纪律失守即停机检修，而非'下不为例'；其二，以公开忏悔替代内部甩锅——甘地多次公开自认早期对非洲人的种族偏见、自承实验失败，把个人过失摆上公共检视台，使纠错成为组织的示范行为；其三，纠错的代价由领导者先付——中止运动的最大损失落在甘地个人声望上，他先担，骨干才敢效仿。方法论签名：宁可停摆，不可带病运转——运动的信用不是由胜利养大，而是由'失范即自罚'养大的。

Treat a movement's own lapses as its highest-grade error signal: when violence breaks out among the people, it is not an externally instigated incident but a failure of the movement's internal moral training—accountability points inward first. When demonstrators burned a police station at Chauri Chaura in February 1922 and 22 officers died, Gandhi suspended the entire national non-cooperation movement within the month and confessed his own responsibility publicly: he would rather hand back a political momentum already in hand than let the method run on a diseased body. Three disciplines define the practice. First, the standard is constitutive, not tactical: non-violent discipline is the movement's reason for being, not a negotiable option—so a breach means halt-and-repair, never 'don't let it happen again.' Second, public confession replaces internal blame-shifting: Gandhi repeatedly owned his early racial prejudice toward Africans and his failed 'experiments,' placing personal faults on the public examination table so that self-correction became the organization's demonstrated behavior rather than its hidden etiquette. Third, the leader pays the correction's price first: suspending the movement cost Gandhi more personal prestige than anyone—he absorbed that cost before asking cadres to follow, which is exactly why they could. Compare the standard revolutionary grammar, where violence is blamed on provocateurs and discipline is enforced by tribunal; Gandhi inverts both: no external scapegoats, no punitive machinery—only the movement's own willingness to stop itself. Signature: better to stand down than to run sick—the credit of a movement is grown not by its victories but by its readiness to punish itself.

流程: 标准立宪：把非暴力纪律写为运动的存在条件而非策略选项——违纪即触发停机，而非扣分 → 追责向内：群众暴力发生，先检讨训练、组织与期望管理，禁止'外部煽动'叙事 → 公开自罚：领导者公开承担并说明纠错代价（中止、绝食、自责），把纠错变成示范行为 → 复训重启：停摆期间完成纪律复训与组织净化后，方可重启行动——重启以'合格'而非'平静'为准

金句: 群众尚未配得上非暴力——我宁可中止运动，也不让方法在失守的纪律上继续。

## M-GAN-006 道德规范统一战线法 / Moral-Norm United Front

黏合一个被宗教、种姓、阶级、语言切碎的社会，靠的不是利益交换与封官许愿，而是一组所有人都无法拒绝的道德规范：印穆团结、废不可触性、禁酒、khadi 自足——'最不可团结者'（穆斯林、贱民）被设为统一战线的前提而非战利品。同时把'对手'精确切分：英帝国政府是被反对的对象，英国人民与良知不是——'不合作的是制度，不是民族'，这为国际舆论与对手内部的良知力量留出通道。要害有三：其一，黏合剂必须高于各方私利——真理与非暴力是印度教徒、穆斯林、无神论者都能进入的公约数；其二，把道德试金石摆在阵线入口——拒绝与贱民同桌者不得成为运动的领导者，统一战线的纯度由入口把关；其三，'分化对手'以瓦解其合法性叙事为主：把'帝国对臣民'改写为'良知对不义'，使镇压在道义上自我贬值。方法论签名：用利益粘起来的阵线利尽则散，用规范粘起来的阵线利尽不散——把最不可团结者放在第一位，阵线才有资格谈团结。

To hold together a society fragmented by religion, caste, class, and language, do not rely on patronage and bargains; rely on a set of moral norms no party can refuse: Hindu-Muslim unity, the abolition of untouchability, temperance, khadi self-reliance—with the 'least unitable' (Muslims, the Depressed Classes) placed as the front's premise, not its spoils. Meanwhile, split the opponent with surgical precision: the British imperial government is the object of opposition, the British people and conscience are not—'we non-cooperate with the system, not the nation,' leaving a channel open for international opinion and for conscience inside the adversary's own camp. Three disciplines carry the mode. First, the adhesive must stand above every faction's private interest: truth and non-violence are the common denominator into which Hindus, Muslims, and atheists can all walk. Second, the moral touchstone sits at the front's entrance: a leader who refuses to dine with an untouchable is disqualified—the front's purity is gated at admission, not audited after victory. Third, 'divide the opponent' means dismantling his legitimacy narrative: reframe 'empire versus subjects' as 'conscience versus injustice,' so every act of repression devalues the repressor morally even when it wins tactically. In practice the front held landlords (Patel), modernists (Nehru), industrialists (Birla), and village masses inside one discipline, and its 1932 defense of Depressed Classes' interests even forced a constitutional revision. Signature: fronts glued by interest scatter when interest ends; fronts glued by norm do not—put the least unitable first, and only then does the front earn the word 'unity.'

流程: 立公约数：提炼高于各派私利的共享规范（真理、非暴力、最低尊严），作为阵线的宪法条款 → 入口把关：把'善待最边缘者'（贱民、少数教派）设为参与与领导资格的门槛，纯度在入口而非事后 → 切分对手：把对手政府与其人民、良知切开，对前者不合作、对后者始终留门 → 维护优先级：教派或种姓冲突爆发时，统一战线的维护优先于战役推进——宁缓十战，不裂阵线

金句: 检验统一战线的不是我们能联合多少人，而是我们把最不可团结者放在了第几位。

## M-GAN-007 纺车群众路线法 / Spinning-Wheel Mass Line

把最大的政治动作分装进最小的日常动作：手纺 khadi 不是经济政策而是参与制度——人人有纺车，则人人每天都能'为独立纺半小时纱'，政治参与不再需要识字、资产与胆量，只需要一双手。这同时解决了运动的三大瓶颈：动员规模（百万文盲农妇也能上场）、经济造血（洋布抵制需要国产替代）、纪律养成（日复一日的纺纱是耐性与集体的日常操练）。要害有三：其一，参与零门槛——把'为国效力'从士大夫专利改成每个身体的日常动作；其二，劳作即宣誓——穿上土布就是穿上了立场，消费行为变成政治表态且无需发言；其三，从群众痛点反推方案——khadi、村社自治、禁酒皆来自纺妇、农户、贱民的真实处境而非会议室推演，'方案在村庄里'。方法论签名：不要问群众能否理解纲领——把纲领裁成一只只纺锤，让他们在手上的节奏里理解它；参与即教育，纺车即讲台。

Package the largest political act into the smallest daily one: hand-spinning khadi is not an economic policy but a participation mechanism—give everyone a spinning wheel and everyone can 'spin half an hour for independence' every day; political participation no longer requires literacy, property, or daring, only a pair of hands. This dissolves the movement's three hardest bottlenecks at once: mobilization scale (millions of illiterate village women can join), economic viability (boycotting foreign cloth demands a domestic substitute), and discipline formation (daily spinning is a standing drill in patience and collective rhythm). Three disciplines make it work. First, zero-threshold participation: 'serving the nation' is converted from a literati privilege into a bodily routine anyone can perform—when participation is frictionless, mobilization ceases to be the scarce resource. Second, labor is the oath: wearing homespun is wearing a position; consumption becomes political statement without a single speech, and because the act is repeated daily, commitment is renewed daily rather than spent once at a rally. Third, programs are reverse-engineered from the people's pain points: khadi, village self-rule, and temperance all grew out of the actual condition of spinners, peasants, and the Depressed Classes—not out of conference-room deduction; by 1940 Gandhi's own accounting recorded 275,000 villagers earning over Rs. 34 lakh through the wheel, a working audit that the 'program was found in the village.' Compare the demagogue's mass rally, where participation is emotional and evaporates by morning; the wheel's participation is manual, verifiable, and cumulative—it leaves cloth on the table. Signature: never ask whether the masses can understand the program—cut the program into spindles and let them understand it in the rhythm of their hands; participation is education, and the wheel is the podium.

流程: 痛点反推：深入最底层（纺妇、农户、贱民）收集真实处境，从痛点反推可参与的方案而非从理念推演 → 裁成日常：把宏大目标拆成零门槛、可每日重复的小动作（纺纱、织布、戒酒、教一个字） → 劳作即表态：让消费与穿戴承担政治表态功能——无需发言即可立场，无需集会即可累积 → 账目回验：定期公布参与与产出数据（人数、纱量、收入），使'群众路线'可审计而非口号

金句: 把独立裁成半小时的纺纱——让一亿双手先于任何条约完成它。

## M-GAN-008 建设性纲领自治法 / Constructive Programme Swaraj

把'独立'定义为可提前交付的现实而非等待批准的未来：swaraj（自治）不是接管德里，而是七十万村庄此刻即可开始的自足——khadi 生产、村社自治机构、基础教育、卫生、禁酒、废不可触性、印穆团结……《建设性纲领》列出十余项'不等宗主国批准就能做'的建设项，使独立从谈判桌上的悬案变成村庄里的既成事实。要害有三：其一，建设先行于抗争——抗争瘫痪旧秩序，建设准备新秩序，只抗不建则胜利之日即真空之始；其二，自治的单位最小化——村社共和国'以一村为体，全自足'，去中心化使统治者的撤退不留下权力真空给新的集权者；其三，纲领就是教材——每项建设都在训练自治能力，独立日的政府不是从天而降而是从十几年村社实践里长出来的。方法论签名：别只拆旧房子——边拆边在原址砌新屋的地基；自由不是被授予的，是被每日建设出来的。

Define independence as a deliverable present rather than a pending future: swaraj is not the capture of Delhi but a self-sufficiency that seventy myriads of villages can begin this very day—khadi production, village self-government bodies, basic education, sanitation, temperance, abolition of untouchability, Hindu-Muslim unity. Gandhi's Constructive Programme enumerated more than a dozen undertakings that require no imperial permission, converting independence from a hanging question on the negotiating table into an accomplished fact inside the villages. Three disciplines carry the mode. First, building precedes breaking: resistance paralyzes the old order while construction prefigures the new one—resist without building, and the day of victory is the first day of vacuum. Second, the unit of self-rule is minimized: the village republic, self-contained at the scale of one village, decentralizes so thoroughly that when the ruler withdraws, no vacuum is left for a new centralizer to inherit—power grows back into a lattice, not a pyramid. Third, the programme is the curriculum: every constructive item trains a capacity of self-rule, so the government of independence day is not parachuted in but grown out of a decade of village practice. Hind Swaraj (1909) supplies the deep critique beneath the programme—against machine civilization and the modern state as such, on the ground that 'it is not the British who have hold of India; it is modern civilization'—which is why liberation must be constructive: expelling the ruler while keeping his machine merely changes the operator. Signature: do not merely demolish the old house—lay the new house's foundation on the same ground while it falls; freedom is not conferred, it is constructed daily.

流程: 列出可即行清单：凡不须经统治者批准、村民今日即可开始的建设项一律列出并排定优先级 → 最小单位自治：以村为单位建自治机构与自足经济，使自治能力在统治存续期间即已成型 → 抗建配对：每场抗争战役配对一项建设任务（抵制洋布配 khadi，反抗税配村社基金），保证破坏性时刻同步生产替代物 → 交接即验收：独立/政权更替之日，以村庄自治机构的成熟度验收独立成色——权力真空留给谁，独立就是谁

金句: 独立不是接管旧机器，而是让机器停转之前，村庄已学会自己发电。

## M-GAN-009 梯度升级符号战法 / Graded Escalation and Symbolic Warfare

把升级从情绪的洪水改造成刻度的阶梯：请愿→不合作→公民抗命→拒税，每一级都有明确触发条件、训练门槛与可撤回设计；同时每级战役都要选一个'符号当量'极高的靶子——盐（人人可制却被垄断）、布（人人可织却被禁）、登记证（人格的侮辱性标签）——让一次行动同时是战役、教科书与头条。要害有三：其一，符号选靶先于军事逻辑——靶子不必最致命，但必须最可叙事：一把海盐使全球读者三秒读懂'凭什么我制盐犯法'；其二，升级刻度公开——对手与群众都知道下一级是什么、由什么触发，这既约束己方情绪、也向对手发出可控性证明；其三，演的是'不义之法的荒谬'——盐长征385公里不攻击任何设施，全程只是步行与制盐，荒谬感（政府对步行者镇压）本身就是弹药。方法论签名：弱者没有兵力，但有刻度与符号——刻度使升级可控，符号使一次行动顶一万次广播。

Convert escalation from a flood of emotion into a ruled ladder: petition, then non-cooperation, then civil disobedience, then tax refusal—each rung with explicit trigger conditions, training thresholds, and retractable design; and at every rung choose a target of maximal symbolic equivalence—salt (anyone can make it, yet it is a monopoly), cloth (anyone can weave it, yet it is banned), the registration certificate (a label of degraded personhood)—so a single action is simultaneously a campaign, a textbook, and a headline. Three disciplines govern the ladder. First, symbolic selection precedes military logic: the target need not be the most damaging but must be the most narratable—one pinch of salt let every reader on earth grasp in three seconds 'why is it a crime to make my own salt.' Second, the scale is public: opponent and public alike know what the next rung is and what triggers it—this disciplines one's own base against emotional surges and proves to the adversary that the movement is a controlled instrument, which paradoxically strengthens the demand for settlement. Third, what is staged is the absurdity of the unjust law: the 385-kilometer march attacked no installation—walking and salt-making were the whole of it—and the absurdity of the state marching police against walkers was itself the ammunition, converting repression into publicity with every blow. Compare the insurrectionary playbook, where escalation is a secret and targets are chosen for damage; Gandhi publishes the escalation and chooses targets for meaning. Signature: the weak have no troops, but they have a scale and a symbol—the scale keeps escalation controllable, and the symbol makes one action worth ten thousand broadcasts.

流程: 立阶梯：写明请愿→不合作→公民抗命→拒税的完整刻度、各级触发条件与训练门槛，并公开 → 选符号靶：在各级选'人人可做却被禁止/人人可懂却被垄断'的靶子，使行动自带可传播性 → 演荒谬：行动设计以'展示法律之荒谬'为中心（步行制盐、公开焚证），让镇压成为对我方叙事的免费广告 → 控节奏：以对手反应与群众训练度为节奏器，升级只沿刻度推进，可上亦可撤——情绪不进入决策链

金句: 升级要有刻度，行动要有符号——刻度管住自己人，符号撬动全世界。

## M-GAN-010 真理实验迭代法 / Experiments with Truth (Iterative Verification)

把'真理'从占有物改写为渐近线：甘地自题其自传为'我体验真理的故事（My Experiments with Truth）'——他不宣称掌握真理，只宣称在持续实验；每一场运动、每一次绝食、每一条戒律（不食、不语日、缄默祈祷）都是一次可复盘的实验，结论写进公开档案，失败照录。这为道德行动者提供了科学家的迭代纪律：假设（此法能唤醒良知）→实验（盐长征/绝食）→观测（对手与群众反应）→修正（中止、改期、改法）。要害有三：其一，实验者不豁免于实验——甘地以自己为第一实验对象（饮食、节欲、沉默），先在最小系统验证再放大到社会；其二，公开档案使迭代可审计——《印度舆论》《青年印度》连续刊载实验记录，成功与失败同权入档；其三，手段-目的同一性是实验的约束项——任何'好结果可以不择手段'的捷径都被禁入，因为手段会毒化样本。方法论签名：真理不是答辩时才拿出的一劳永逸的答案，而是像盐一样每日重新制取的必需品——我唯一宣称的是：我仍在实验。

Rewrite truth from a possession into an asymptote: Gandhi titled his autobiography The Story of My Experiments with Truth—he claims no mastery of truth, only a continuing experimental practice; every campaign, every fast, every vow (dietary discipline, day of silence, prayer) is a reviewable experiment whose results are written into the public record, failures included with equal rights. This gives the moral actor the scientist's iterative discipline: hypothesis (this method can awaken conscience), experiment (the Salt March, a fast), observation (how opponent and masses respond), correction (suspension, postponement, redesign). Three disciplines define the practice. First, the experimenter is never exempt from the experiment: Gandhi ran the protocol on himself first—diet, celibacy, silence—validating at the scale of one body before scaling to society, so the movement's methods arrive pre-tested rather than preached. Second, the public archive makes iteration auditable: Indian Opinion and Young India carried running logs in which success and failure were filed with the same weight—readers could watch the method learn, which is precisely what made the method transferable to King, Mandela, and beyond. Third, means-ends unity is the experiment's constraint set: no shortcut of 'good ends may use any means' is admissible, because tainted means poison the sample—the method is scientific precisely because it refuses contaminated data. Where dogma freezes a movement around fixed conclusions, the experimental mode keeps it revisable: Gandhi changed positions (on empire after Amritsar, on caste practice, on machinery) and said so openly, taking the reputational cost of revision in exchange for staying truthful. Signature: truth is not a final answer produced for the defense; it is a necessity re-made daily, like salt—the only thing I claim is that I am still experimenting.

流程: 最小系统先行：任何方法先在自己身体与生活上实验（饮食、沉默、戒律），验证后再放大到运动 → 档案化迭代：连续公开记录每次实验的目的、过程、结果——成功失败同权入档，供他者复制与批评 → 定期复盘修正：以既定节奏（日记、周刊、年终总结）复盘假设偏差，公开宣布修正而不掩饰改口 → 约束项常设：手段-目的同一性作为实验红线——凡靠污染手段得到的'成功'一律不计入证据

金句: 我不过是在实验真理——结论永远待修正，而修正本身必须公开。
