## 一、历史定位

程颐（1033-1107），字正叔，世称伊川先生，河南洛阳人。北宋哲学家、教育家，理学奠基人之一，与其兄程颢世称二程，开创洛学。

**代表著作**: 《河南程氏遗书》《周易程氏传》《礼记解》《语录》

## 二、核心思想

- 性即理法（性即理也，天道也）
- 主敬涵养法（涵养须用敬，进学则在致知）
- 格物穷理法（今日格一件，明日格一件）
- 体用一源法（体用一源，显微无间）
- 理一分殊法（天下只有一个理）
- 渐进贯通法（积习既多，然后脱然有贯通处）
- 存理灭欲法（存天理，灭人欲）
- 先知后行法（学莫先于致知）
- 经学入德法（《大学》，初学入德之门也）
- 内外一理法（敬以直内，义以方外）

**名言**: 性即理也；涵养须用敬，进学则在致知；体用一源，显微无间；天下只有一个理；饿死事极小，失节事极大
**出处**: 《河南程氏遗书》《周易程氏传》

**历史影响**: 程颐与兄程颢同为北宋理学奠基人，世称二程。其学说后为朱熹继承发展，形成程朱理学正统。

## 三、十大思维模式 (M-CHI-001 ~ M-CHI-010)

### M-CHI-001 性即理法

- **英文名**: Nature-as-Principle Method
- **类别**: 本体论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 1
- **领域**: 人性论/形上学 / Theory of Human Nature / Metaphysics
- **出处**: 《河南程氏遗书》卷二上
- **定义**: 人性即天理，天命之性纯善。 程颐首次系统论证'性即理也'命题：天理下贯于人即为性，性善根源于理之本然。此法将人性的形上根据锚定于宇宙本体，使道德本体论获得超越经验的支撑。气禀之性的引入解释了恶的来源——理虽本善，但气有清浊，禀受不同故有善恶之分。此命题成为朱熹'天命之性'与'气质之性'二元结构的直接来源，是宋明人性论的基石。
- **定义(EN)**: Nature is principle: the heavenly principle descends into humanity as nature, and nature is good because principle is originally good. Cheng Yi systematically established 'nature is principle' as the metaphysical foundation of ethics. The introduction of 'temperamental nature' explains evil — principle is pure but qi is turbid, so endowments vary and good/evil emerge. This proposition became the direct source for Zhu Xi's dual structure of 'invested nature' and 'physical nature', forming the cornerstone of Song-Ming theory of human nature.
- **关键概念**: 性即理、天命之性、气质之性、理本善、气禀说
- **原文依据**: 性即理也，何谓性？曰：天道焉而行乎人者也，谓之道。在天为命，在义为理，在人为性，主于身为心，其实一也。
  > Nature is principle. What is nature? It is the heavenly way functioning in humanity. In heaven it is called mandate, in righteousness it is called principle, in humanity it is called nature, governing the body it is called heart-mind — in essence they are one.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 确认人性根源于天理而非气质
  2. 区分天命之性与气质之性
  3. 理解恶的来源在于气禀之偏而非本性之恶
  4. 通过变化气质恢复本然之善
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Confirm that human nature derives from principle, not from temperamental endowment
  2. Distinguish invested nature from physical nature
  3. Understand that evil originates from turbid qi, not from original nature
  4. Transform temperamental endowment to restore original goodness
- **代表性案例**:
  - 程颐'性即理也'命题成为理学人性论的基石：天理下贯于人即为性，性本善根源于理之本然
  - 引入'气禀'解释恶的来源：理虽本善，但气有清浊，禀受不同故有善恶之分
  - 朱熹继承并发展为'天命之性'与'气质之性'的二元结构，成为此后八百年人性论的标准范式
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Cheng Yi's 'nature is principle' became the cornerstone of Neo-Confucian theory of human nature: heavenly principle descends into humanity as nature, goodness rooted in principle's original purity
  - The 'temperamental endowment' explanation for evil: principle is pure but qi varies in clarity and turbidity, so endowments differ and good/evil emerge
  - Zhu Xi inherited and developed this into the dual structure of 'invested nature' and 'physical nature', becoming the standard paradigm for eight centuries
- **当代应用**:
  - 道德自律的形上论证
  - 个体差异的哲学解释
  - 教育本性的理论基础
  - 人格完善的终极依据
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Metaphysical grounding of moral autonomy
  - Philosophical explanation of individual differences
  - Theoretical foundation for education's nature
  - Ultimate basis for personality perfection
- **关联模式**: M-CHI-002、M-CHI-005、M-CHI-008

### M-CHI-002 主敬涵养法

- **英文名**: Reverence-Nurturing Method
- **类别**: 工夫论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 2
- **领域**: 修养方法论/内在训练 / Cultivation Methodology / Inner Training
- **出处**: 《河南程氏遗书》卷二上、卷十八
- **定义**: 以'敬'为入道之门，通过庄敬存养收束身心。'敬'不是静态的虚无，而是'主一无适'的专注状态——心有所主，不旁骛他物。'入道莫如敬'，'学者莫若且先理会得敬'。敬是对治心散、气浮的根本工夫，通过'居敬'使内心常存主宰，喜怒哀乐未发时保持'中'的状态，已发时保持'和'的状态。此法是程颐工夫论的起点，与'格物穷理'互为表里。
- **定义(EN)**: Reverence as the gate to the Way: through solemn nurturing, one gathers body and mind. 'Reverence' is not static emptiness but 'single-minded focus' — the heart has its master and does not stray. 'Nothing leads into the Way like reverence.' It is the fundamental practice against scattered mind and floating qi. Through 'dwelling in reverence,' the heart maintains its mastery constantly; before emotional arousal one preserves the 'mean,' and after arousal the 'harmony.' This method is Cheng Yi's starting point in cultivation, paired with 'investigating things to extend knowledge.'
- **关键概念**: 敬、主一无适、居敬、庄敬日强、未发之中
- **原文依据**: 涵养须用敬，进学则在致知。敬则自虚静，不可把虚静唤做敬。君子庄敬日强，安肆日偷。
  > Nurturing must use reverence; advancing learning lies in extending knowledge. Reverence brings natural stillness — one must not mistake stillness for reverence. The gentleman grows strong daily through solemn reverence; the lax and careless grow mean.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 确立'敬'为入道之门——收束身心、不旁骛物
  2. 在未发时保持庄敬存养，使心体清明
  3. 在已发时以敬节制情感，使发而中节
  4. 持之以恒使庄敬成为日常习惯
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Establish reverence as the gate to the Way — gathering body and mind without straying
  2. Maintain solemn nurturing when emotions are unaroused, keeping the heart clear
  3. Apply reverence to moderate emotions when aroused, ensuring appropriate expression
  4. Sustain this consistently until reverence becomes daily habit
- **代表性案例**:
  - 程颐强调'敬'是'圣门第一义'，朱熹称'程先生所以有功于后学者，最是敬之一字有力'
  - 程颐批评只守一个敬不知集义则是都无事——敬需配合穷理方为完整工夫
  - 朱熹终身持守'主敬'工夫：'敬字工夫，乃圣门第一义，彻头彻尾，不可顷刻间断'
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Cheng Yi emphasized 'reverence' as 'the first principle in the sage's school'; Zhu Xi said 'Cheng's greatest contribution is the character reverence alone'
  - Cheng Yi warned that merely holding reverence without gathering righteousness is empty — reverence must be paired with investigating things
  - Zhu Xi held 'reverence' as lifelong practice: 'Reverence is the first principle, from beginning to end, never to be interrupted for a moment'
- **当代应用**:
  - 专注力训练
  - 情绪管理
  - 冥想与正念的哲学基础
  - 职业素养培养
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Focus training
  - Emotion management
  - Philosophical foundation for meditation and mindfulness
  - Professional competence development
- **关联模式**: M-CHI-001、M-CHI-003、M-CHI-006

### M-CHI-003 格物穷理法

- **英文名**: Investigating Things to Extend Knowledge Method
- **类别**: 认识论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 3
- **领域**: 知识论/学习方法论 / Epistemology / Learning Methodology
- **出处**: 《河南程氏遗书》卷十八、卷二十五
- **定义**: 格物即穷理——通过观察事物、读书明理来扩充知识，最终达到'豁然贯通'。'今日格一件，明日格一件，积习既多，然后脱然有贯通处'。此法强调渐进积累的认知路径：不要求穷尽天下万物，只需每日格一件，积累多了自然贯通。它同时承认'闻见之知'的价值，主张通过多读书、多学习来辅助德性之知的显现。这是程颐对《大学》'格物致知'的系统阐释，也是程朱理学认识论的核心。
- **定义(EN)**: Investigating things is exhausting principle — through observing phenomena and studying classics to extend knowledge, ultimately achieving 'sudden thorough comprehension.' 'Today investigate one thing, tomorrow another; after accumulation grows abundant, then suddenly there is thorough comprehension.' This method emphasizes gradual cognitive accumulation: one need not exhaust all things under heaven, only investigate one thing daily, and after sufficient accumulation, thorough comprehension occurs naturally. It also acknowledges the value of 'empirical knowledge,' advocating reading and study to assist the manifestation of 'moral knowledge.' This is Cheng Yi's systematic interpretation of 'investigating things to extend knowledge' from the Great Learning, and the core of Cheng-Zhu epistemology.
- **关键概念**: 格物、穷理、豁然贯通、积习、闻见之知
- **原文依据**: 须是今日格一件，明日格一件，积习既多，然后脱然有贯通处。
  > One must investigate one thing today, another tomorrow; after accumulated practice becomes abundant, then suddenly there is thorough comprehension.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 确立'格物即穷理'的认知目标
  2. 每日观察一事物、研读一经书，积累物理
  3. 通过持续积累达到'脱然贯通'
  4. 贯通之后，未见者亦能推知
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Establish 'investigating things is exhausting principle' as the cognitive goal
  2. Observe one thing daily, study one classic, accumulating knowledge of principles
  3. Through sustained accumulation reach 'sudden thorough comprehension'
  4. After thorough comprehension, even unobserved things can be inferred
- **代表性案例**:
  - 程颐'今日格一件，明日格一件'——渐进积累的认知方法论，区别于顿悟路径
  - 区分'闻见之知'与'德性之知'：闻见之知来自感官接触，德性之知乃内心固有
  - 朱熹继承发展为'即物穷理'的方法论：格物不止一事，穷理不止一理
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Cheng Yi's 'today one thing, tomorrow another' — gradual accumulative cognitive methodology, distinct from sudden enlightenment paths
  - Distinction between 'empirical knowledge' and 'moral knowledge': empirical knowledge comes from sensory contact, moral knowledge is innately present
  - Zhu Xi inherited and developed this into 'investigating things to exhaust principle' methodology
- **当代应用**:
  - 系统性学习
  - 知识积累方法
  - 渐进式问题解决
  - 深度学习策略
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Systematic learning
  - Knowledge accumulation methods
  - Gradual problem-solving
  - Deep learning strategies
- **关联模式**: M-CHI-002、M-CHI-004、M-CHI-007

### M-CHI-004 体用一源法

- **英文名**: Body-Function-One-Origin Method
- **类别**: 本体论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 4
- **领域**: 易学/形上学方法论 / Yijing Hermeneutics / Metaphysical Methodology
- **出处**: 《周易程氏传·序》
- **定义**: '体用一源，显微无间'——理（体/微）与象（用/显）同源同体，不可分离。程颐在《周易程氏传序》中提出此命题，作为易学诠释的根本原则：通过有形之象把握无形之理，理在象中而不离象。这不仅是易学方法论，更是整个理学的形上学架构：现象与本质不二，形式与内容统一，理论与实践一致。'有理而后有象，有象而后有数'——理为本，象为数皆依理而立。
- **定义(EN)**: 'Body and function share one origin, subtle and manifest have no interval' — principle (body/subtle) and image (function/manifest) share one source and cannot be separated. Cheng Yi proposed this in the Preface to his Commentary on the Yijing as the fundamental principle of Yijing interpretation: through visible images grasp invisible principle, principle exists within images without departing from them. This is not merely Yijing methodology but the entire Neo-Confucian metaphysical architecture: phenomenon and essence are non-dual, form and content unified, theory and practice consistent.
- **关键概念**: 体用一源、显微无间、理在象中、有理而后有象
- **原文依据**: 至微者理也，至著者象也。体用一源，显微无间。
  > The most subtle is principle; the most manifest is image. Body and function share one origin; subtle and manifest have no interval.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 理解体用一源：理与象同源不可分
  2. 掌握'因象以明理'的诠释路径
  3. 在具体情境中把握理与象的统一
  4. 以'一源'思维统摄理论与实践
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Understand body-function one origin: principle and image share source and are inseparable
  2. Master the hermeneutic path of 'illuminating principle through images'
  3. Grasp the unity of principle and image in concrete situations
  4. Unify theory and practice through 'one origin' thinking
- **代表性案例**:
  - 程颐《周易程氏传》以'体用一源'统摄全篇：通过卦象阐释天理，以理释象、以象显理
  - 此命题成为程朱易学的核心方法论，直接影响朱熹'即物穷理'的认识论
  - 反例警示：若将体用割裂为二，则陷入形而上学与经验主义的二元对立——程颐正是以'一源'突破这一困境
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Cheng Yi's Commentary uses 'body-function one origin' to unify the whole: through hexagram images elucidate heavenly principle, using principle to explain images and images to reveal principle
  - This proposition became the core methodology of Cheng-Zhu Yijing studies, directly influencing Zhu Xi's epistemology
  - Counterexample: severing body and function into two leads to the dualism of metaphysics and empiricism — Cheng Yi broke through this predicament with 'one origin'
- **当代应用**:
  - 理论与实践统一
  - 本质与现象的统一思维
  - 系统思考
  - 跨学科整合
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Theory-practice unity
  - Essence-phenomenon unified thinking
  - Systems thinking
  - Interdisciplinary integration
- **关联模式**: M-CHI-001、M-CHI-005、M-CHI-009

### M-CHI-005 理一分殊法

- **英文名**: One-Principle-Many-Manifestations Method
- **类别**: 本体论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 5
- **领域**: 普遍性与特殊性/形上学 / Universality and Particularity / Metaphysics
- **出处**: 《河南程氏遗书》卷二上
- **定义**: '天下只有一个理'，但此理在不同事物中有不同表现。'一物之理即万物之理'——普遍之理在具体事物中各有分殊。此法解决'一'与'多'的关系：理是一，但万物各得其理；万物各异，但理无二致。这一原理后来被朱熹发展为'月印万川'的经典比喻：一月普现一切水，一切水月一月摄。
- **定义(EN)**: 'The whole world has only one principle,' but this principle manifests differently in different things. 'The principle of one thing is the principle of all things' — universal principle has particular manifestations in each entity. This method resolves the relationship between 'one' and 'many': principle is one, yet all things each receive their own principle; all things differ, yet principle is undivided. This principle was later developed by Zhu Xi into the classic metaphor of 'the moon reflected in ten thousand rivers': one moon appears in all waters, all waters' moons are but one moon.
- **关键概念**: 理一、分殊、万物一理、月印万川
- **原文依据**: 散之在万殊，万殊原于一理。理一分殊。
  > Scattered among myriad particularities, all particularities originate from one principle. One principle, many manifestations.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 确认万物共具一理
  2. 理解理在不同事物中的分殊表现
  3. 在具体情境中把握理的特定表现形式
  4. 避免将'一'抽象化为脱离具体的空泛概念
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Confirm that all things share one principle
  2. Understand how principle manifests differently across entities
  3. Grasp the particular form of principle in concrete situations
  4. Avoid reducing 'one' to an empty abstraction detached from particulars
- **代表性案例**:
  - 程颐'天下只有一个理'——普遍之理在不同事物中各有分殊
  - 朱熹以'月印万川'比喻理一分殊：一月普现一切水，一切水月一月摄
  - 反例警示：若只执'理一'忽略'分殊'，则会陷入抽象普遍主义；若只执'分殊'忽略'理一'，则会陷入相对主义
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Cheng Yi's 'the world has only one principle' — universal principle manifests differently across entities
  - Zhu Xi's 'moon reflected in ten thousand rivers' metaphor: one moon appears in all waters, all waters' moons are but one
  - Counterexample: fixating on 'one principle' while ignoring 'many manifestations' leads to abstract universalism; fixating on 'manifestations' while ignoring 'one principle' leads to relativism
- **当代应用**:
  - 原则与灵活的平衡
  - 统一标准下的个性尊重
  - 系统整合思维
  - 多元一体的组织管理
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Balance of principle and flexibility
  - Respecting individuality under unified standards
  - System integration thinking
  - Diversity-in-unity organizational management
- **关联模式**: M-CHI-001、M-CHI-004、M-CHI-008

### M-CHI-006 渐进贯通法

- **英文名**: Gradual Accumulation to Sudden Insight Method
- **类别**: 认识论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 6
- **领域**: 学习论/认知方法论 / Learning Theory / Cognitive Methodology
- **出处**: 《河南程氏遗书》卷十八
- **定义**: 认知的渐进路径：'今日格一件，明日格一件，积习既多，然后脱然有贯通处'。程颐强调从量的积累到质的飞跃：不需要穷尽天下万物，只需每日格一件，积累多了自然'贯通'。此法既不同于顿悟论（不要求一次性彻悟），也不同于经验主义的机械积累（强调最终的质变飞跃）。'贯通'不是归纳推理，而是直觉性的'豁然开朗'，是长期积累后的认知跃迁。
- **定义(EN)**: The gradual path of cognition: 'Today investigate one thing, tomorrow another; after accumulation grows abundant, then suddenly there is thorough comprehension.' Cheng Yi emphasizes moving from quantitative accumulation to qualitative transformation. One need not exhaust all things under heaven — only investigate one thing daily, and after sufficient accumulation, 'thorough comprehension' occurs naturally. This method differs from both sudden enlightenment (no single moment of total awakening required) and mechanical empiricism (emphasizing the qualitative leap at the end). 'thorough comprehension' is not inductive reasoning but intuitive 'sudden clarity' — a cognitive leap after prolonged accumulation.
- **关键概念**: 积习贯通、今日格一件、脱然贯通、量变到质变
- **原文依据**: 须是今日格一件，明日格一件，积习既多，然后脱然有贯通处。
  > One must investigate one thing today, another tomorrow; after accumulated practice becomes abundant, then suddenly there is thorough comprehension.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 确立渐进积累的认知态度
  2. 每日格一件，不急于求成
  3. 在持续积累中等待'贯通'时刻
  4. 贯通后，未见者亦能推知
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Establish gradual accumulative cognitive attitude
  2. Investigate one thing daily, do not rush for results
  3. Wait for the moment of 'thorough comprehension' amid sustained accumulation
  4. After thorough comprehension, even unobserved things can be inferred
- **代表性案例**:
  - 程颐'今日格一件，明日格一件'——渐进积累的认知方法论
  - 朱熹继承发展为'今日格一物，明日格一物'的日用工夫
  - 反例警示：若把渐进理解为无限延长，则会陷入'终日格物'却无贯通的空转——程颐同时强调'贯通处'的质变时刻
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Cheng Yi's 'today one thing, tomorrow another' — gradual accumulative cognitive methodology
  - Zhu Xi inherited and developed this into daily practice of 'investigating one thing today, another tomorrow'
  - Counterexample: if gradual accumulation is understood as infinite prolongation, one enters idle spinning without thorough comprehension — Cheng Yi simultaneously emphasizes the qualitative leap moment of 'thorough comprehension'
- **当代应用**:
  - 持续性学习
  - 习惯养成

### M-CHI-007 存理灭欲法

- **英文名**: Preserve-Principle Eliminate-Desire Method
- **类别**: 伦理学 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 7
- **领域**: 道德修养/价值判断 / Moral Cultivation / Value Judgment
- **出处**: 《河南程氏遗书》卷三十
- **定义**: '存天理，灭人欲'——通过去除私欲恢复天理本性。人欲是天理的遮蔽，'无人欲即皆天理'。此法强调道德修养的目标是去除私欲、恢复本然之善。'饿死事极小，失节事极大'——在极端情境下，保存天理比保全生命更重要。程颐的这一命题成为程朱理学的核心道德规范，但也因其严苛性而备受争议。
- **定义(EN)**: 'Preserve heavenly principle, eliminate human desires' — through removing selfish desires, restore original heavenly principle nature. Human desires obscure heavenly principle; 'where there are no desires, all is principle.' This method emphasizes that the goal of moral cultivation is removing selfish desires to restore original goodness. 'To starve to death is a small matter; to lose one's moral integrity is a great matter' — in extreme situations, preserving principle matters more than preserving life. Cheng Yi's proposition became the core moral norm of Cheng-Zhu Neo-Confucianism, but has also been heavily controversial due to its severity.
- **关键概念**: 存天理、灭人欲、私欲、天理、节操
- **原文依据**: 人心私欲也，道心天理也。……无人欲即皆天理矣。饿死事极小，失节事极大。
  > Human mind is selfish desire; dao mind is heavenly principle. ... Where there are no desires, all is heavenly principle. To starve to death is a small matter; to lose moral integrity is a great matter.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 识别天理与人欲的区别
  2. 觉察内心私欲的萌动
  3. 通过主敬涵养去除私欲
  4. 恢复天理本然之善
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Identify the distinction between heavenly principle and human desires
  2. Detect the stirring of selfish desires in the heart
  3. Remove desires through reverence and nurturing
  4. Restore original heavenly principle goodness
- **代表性案例**:
  - 程颐'饿死事极小，失节事极大'——将道德节操置于生命之上
  - '无人欲即皆天理'——欲望是理之遮蔽，去除欲望即是恢复天理
  - 反例警示：若把'灭欲'推向极端，会陷入禁欲主义——程颐同时强调'理'本身包含正当的人之常情，灭的是'私欲'而非'人欲'本身
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Cheng Yi's 'to starve to death is small, to lose integrity is great' — placing moral integrity above life itself
  - 'Where there are no desires, all is heavenly principle' — desires obscure principle; removing desires is restoring heavenly principle
  - Counterexample: pushing 'eliminating desires' to the extreme leads to asceticism — Cheng Yi simultaneously emphasized that 'principle' itself contains legitimate human feelings; what is eliminated is 'selfish desire' not 'human desire' itself
- **当代应用**:
  - 自我约束与自律
  - 价值观澄清
  - 欲望管理
  - 道德勇气培养
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Self-discipline and self-restraint
  - Value clarification
  - Desire management
  - Moral courage development
- **关联模式**: M-CHI-001、M-CHI-002、M-CHI-008

### M-CHI-008 先知后行法

- **英文名**: Knowledge-Before-Action Method
- **类别**: 知行论 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 8
- **领域**: 实践哲学/认识论 / Philosophy of Practice / Epistemology
- **出处**: 《河南程氏遗书》卷九
- **定义**: 以知为本、先知后行——'能知则能行，行是知的结果'。程颐在知行关系上主张知优先于行：必须先明理，然后才能正确行动。这与陆王心学'知行合一'的路径不同。程颐强调'学莫先于致知'，认为没有正确的认知指导，行动就会迷失方向。但'知'不是外在的经验知识，而是对天理的内在本性的体认。
- **定义(EN)**: Knowledge precedes action: 'to know is to be able to act; action is the result of knowing.' Cheng Yi holds that knowledge takes priority over action in the knowledge-action relationship: one must first understand principle, then act correctly. This differs from Lu-Wang 'unity of knowledge and action.' Cheng Yi emphasizes 'learning has no priority over extending knowledge,' believing that without correct cognitive guidance, action goes astray. But 'knowledge' here is not external empirical knowledge, but the inner apprehension of heavenly principle's original nature.
- **关键概念**: 先知后行、知行为本、明理先行、真知必行
- **原文依据**: 学莫先于致知。致知在格物。……能知则能行，行是知的结果。
  > Learning has no priority over extending knowledge. Extending knowledge lies in investigating things. ... To know is to be able to act; action is the result of knowledge.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 确立知的优先地位
  2. 通过格物穷理获得真知
  3. 以真知指导行动
  4. 在实践中检验和深化认知
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Establish the priority of knowledge
  2. Obtain true knowledge through investigating things and exhausting principle
  3. Guide action through true knowledge
  4. Test and deepen cognition through practice
- **代表性案例**:
  - 程颐'学莫先于致知'——知识的优先性
  - '真知'与'闻见之知'的区分：真知乃德性之知，不假见闻
  - 朱熹继承发展为'知轻重心重'的知行观，影响后世数百年
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Cheng Yi's 'learning has no priority over extending knowledge' — the priority of knowledge
  - Distinction between 'true knowledge' and 'empirical knowledge': true knowledge is moral knowledge, not dependent on sensory experience
  - Zhu Xi inherited and developed this into 'knowledge is light, action is heavy' view of knowledge-action, influencing later centuries
- **当代应用**:
  - 理论与实践的关系
  - 决策前的充分准备
  - 知识先导的行动策略
  - 反思性实践
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Theory-practice relationship
  - Thorough preparation before action
  - Knowledge-first action strategy
  - Reflective practice
- **关联模式**: M-CHI-002、M-CHI-003、M-CHI-006

### M-CHI-009 经学入德法

- **英文名**: Classics-as-Gateway Method
- **类别**: 教育哲学 | **层级**: 核心 | **优先级**: 9
- **领域**: 经典诠释/教育方法论 / Classical Hermeneutics / Educational Methodology
- **出处**: 《河南程氏遗书》卷十八
- **定义**: 以《大学》为'初学入德之门'，通过经典学习进入道德修养。程颐特别重视《大学》，认为它是儒家入门的经典。'《大学》，孔氏之遗书，而初学入德之门也。' 他主张读书须'晓其文义，然后求其意'，反对脱离经文的空谈。此法强调经典学习与实践体认的结合：读书明理不是终点，而是通向道德实践的起点。
- **定义(EN)**: Using the Great Learning as the 'gateway to virtue for beginners,' one enters moral cultivation through classical study. Cheng Yi particularly valued the Great Learning, regarding it as the gateway classic of Confucianism. 'The Great Learning is the legacy book of Confucius and the gateway to virtue for beginners.' He insisted that reading must first 'understand the literal meaning, then seek its import,' opposing empty talk divorced from classics. This method emphasizes the combination of classical study and practical apprehension: studying classics to understand principle is not the end but the starting point toward moral practice.
- **关键概念**: 入德之门、大学、读书明理、文义与意
- **原文依据**: 《大学》，孔氏之遗书，而初学入德之门也。……凡看文字，先须晓其文义，然后可求其意。
  > The Great Learning is Confucius's legacy book and the gateway to virtue for beginners. ... When reading any text, one must first understand its literal meaning, then one can seek its import.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 以《大学》为入门经典
  2. 读书须先晓文义，再求其意
  3. 将经典学习与道德实践结合
  4. 以经学为根基确保理论的正当性
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Take the Great Learning as the gateway classic
  2. Reading must first grasp literal meaning, then seek its import
  3. Combine classical study with moral practice
  4. Use classical learning as foundation to ensure theoretical legitimacy
- **代表性案例**:
  - 程颐将《大学》列为'入德之门'，确立其为初学者必读经典
  - 程颐强调'晓文义然后求其意'——反对脱离文本的空谈和臆测
  - 朱熹继承发展为'四书'体系，《大学》始终居于核心地位
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Cheng Yi placed the Great Learning as the 'gateway to virtue,' establishing it as the essential gateway classic for beginners
  - Cheng Yi emphasized 'first understand literal meaning, then seek its import' — opposing empty talk and speculation divorced from texts
  - Zhu Xi inherited and developed this into the 'Four Books' system, with the Great Learning remaining central
- **当代应用**:
  - 经典教育
  - 文本细读方法
  - 学习次第设计
  - 理论与实践结合
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Classical education
  - Close reading methodology
  - Learning sequence design
  - Theory-practice integration
- **关联模式**: M-CHI-003、M-CHI-006、M-CHI-010

### M-CHI-010 内外一理法

- **英文名**: Inner-Outer-One-Principle Method
- **类别**: 修养论 | **层级**: 辅助 | **优先级**: 10
- **领域**: 身心修养/内外统一 / Body-Mind Cultivation / Inner-Outer Unity
- **出处**: 《河南程氏遗书》卷一
- **定义**: '敬以直内，义以方外'——内在的敬与外在的义统一于同一理。程颐强调内外本不二：敬是内在的涵养工夫，义是外在的处事准则，二者皆本于同一'理'。'合内外之道也'——内在修养与外在行为必须一致，不可割裂。此法反对'内敬外义'的割裂理解，强调内外一贯。
- **定义(EN)**: 'Reverence makes the interior straight, righteousness squares the exterior' — inner reverence and outer righteousness are unified in the same principle. Cheng Yi emphasized inner and outer are fundamentally not two: reverence is inner nurturing practice, righteousness is outer conduct standard, both rooted in the same 'principle.' 'This is the way of unifying inner and outer' — inner cultivation and outer behavior must be consistent, cannot be severed. This method opposes the severed understanding of 'inner reverence, outer righteousness,' emphasizing inner-outer consistency.
- **关键概念**: 敬义双修、内外一贯、合内外之道、理一
- **原文依据**: 敬以直内，义以方外。敬义夹持，上下立矣。……合内外之道也。
  > Reverence makes the interior straight, righteousness squares the exterior. Reverence and righteousness supporting each other — the upper and lower are established. ... This is the way of unifying inner and outer.
- **操作步骤**:
  1. 确立敬为内在修养的根本
  2. 确立义为外在行为的准则
  3. 理解敬义本是一理的两面
  4. 在实践中保持内外一致
- **操作步骤(EN)**:
  1. Establish reverence as the root of inner cultivation
  2. Establish righteousness as the standard of outer conduct
  3. Understand that reverence and righteousness are two aspects of one principle
  4. Maintain inner-outer consistency in practice
- **代表性案例**:
  - 程颐'敬以直内，义以方外'——敬义本是一事的两面
  - 与张载'太虚即气'对比：程颐以'理'统摄内外，张载以'气'统摄内外
  - 反例警示：若把敬义理解为先后工序（先敬后义或先义后敬），则会错失其同时性——敬义本是一理的两面
- **代表性案例(EN)**:
  - Cheng Yi's 'reverence makes the interior straight, righteousness squares the exterior' — reverence and righteousness are two aspects of one thing
  - Contrast with Zhang Zai's 'Tai Xu is qi': Cheng Yi unifies inner-outer through 'principle,' Zhang Zai through 'qi'
  - Counterexample: reading reverence and righteousness as sequential steps (first reverence then righteousness, or vice versa) misses their simultaneity — they are two aspects of one principle
- **当代应用**:
  - 内外一致的人格修养
  - 职业道德与个人品格的统一
  - 领导力的内外兼修
  - 知行合一的实践
- **当代应用(EN)**:
  - Inner-outer consistent personality cultivation
  - Unity of professional ethics and personal character
  - Inner-outer cultivation of leadership
  - Practice of unity of knowledge and action
- **关联模式**: M-CHI-002、M-CHI-004、M-CHI-008
