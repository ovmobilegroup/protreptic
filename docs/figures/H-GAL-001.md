# 盖伦 (H-GAL-001) Galen

- 年代 Era: 罗马帝国（帕加马—亚历山大里亚—罗马）：从希腊医学诸派争鸣到医学体系的集大成与千年正统 / Roman Empire (Pergamum-Alexandria-Rome): from the warring schools of Greek medicine to the grand synthesis and a millennium of medical orthodoxy
- 文明 Civilization: 希腊/罗马（西方） / Greece / Rome (Western)
- 角色 Roles: 内科医生、角斗士医师、御医（马可·奥勒留等四朝皇帝）、解剖学家、实验生理学先驱、医学哲学家、百科全书式著述家
- 模式数 Modes: 10 (M-GAL-001, M-GAL-002, M-GAL-003, M-GAL-004, M-GAL-005, M-GAL-006, M-GAL-007, M-GAL-008, M-GAL-009, M-GAL-010)

## 概述 Summary

古代医学的集大成者，其体系统治西方与伊斯兰医学一千四百年。核心方法论：以『最好的医生也是哲学家』为纲领，在经验派、理性派、方法派三派之争中不做党徒而做裁断者——主张唯一正确的学派是把理性推理与可检验经验结合起来的学派；以角斗士医师的千余具『打开的身体』把解剖学变成医学的认识论地基，在无人体解剖的禁令下用猪、猴、羊做替身并让结构替功能作证（《论身体各部分的功能》的目的一体化推理）；以公开活体解剖的戏剧性干预实验让机制当场现形——切断喉返神经让尖叫的猪当场失声、结扎输尿管让肾脏的秘密当场揭晓、剪开心包让胸腔负压的秘密被听见；以脉搏与体温的精细分类把『观察』升级为可命名、可传递的分类学（脉搏二十七种属性的诊断体系）；以文本考据辨伪法在伪作横行、抄本讹误的时代为希波克拉底做语文学清理，把『读对文本』变成医学的第一素养；以自我实验与药物 trials 把治疗学从配方传统变成可检验的实践；以罗马公开论辩的竞技场把学术竞争变成裁决机制——在群医环伺的现场演示中让对手沉默；以庞大自注性正典（《论我自己的书》）把个人产出变成可传承的体系工程。他是把哲学、解剖、实验、临床、文本五条线拧成一根医学主轴的转换器式人物。

The great synthesizer of ancient medicine whose system ruled Western and Islamic medicine for fourteen centuries. Core methodology: taking 'the best physician is also a philosopher' as his program, he refused partisanship in the war among Empiricists, Rationalists, and Methodists and made himself the arbiter—insisting the only sound school is the one that joins rational inference to testable experience; he turned anatomy into medicine's epistemological foundation through the thousand-plus 'opened bodies' of his gladiator years, using pigs, monkeys, and goats as surrogates under the ban on human dissection and letting structure testify for function (the unified teleological reasoning of On the Usefulness of the Parts); he made mechanisms reveal themselves on the spot through dramatic interventional demonstrations in public vivisection—severing the recurrent laryngeal nerve to silence a squealing pig mid-scream, ligating the ureters to expose the kidney's secret, cutting the pleura to let the chest's negative pressure be heard; he upgraded 'observation' into a nameable, transmissible taxonomy with his fine classification of pulses and fevers (twenty-seven pulse attributes); in an age of forgeries and corrupted manuscripts he applied philological hygiene to the Hippocratic corpus, making 'reading the text correctly' medicine's first discipline; he turned therapeutics from recipe tradition into testable practice through self-experimentation and drug trials; and he converted scholarly competition into an adjudication mechanism in the combative arena of Roman public disputation, silencing rivals before assembled crowds. With his massive self-annotating canon (On My Own Books) he turned personal output into a transmissible system-engineering project. He was the converter-figure who twisted philosophy, anatomy, experiment, clinic, and text into one spine of medicine.

## 思维模式 Thinking Modes

### M-GAL-001 学说溯源自验法 / Doctrine-Sourcing and Self-Verification Method

- 领域: 追溯每个学说的来源并亲手验证/拒收未经检验的二手真理 (认识论/知识检验)
- 定义: 盖伦所处的罗马医学世界被两大教条学派割裂：教条派（理性主义者）只在书房里从公理演绎医学，经验派（赫罗菲卢斯的后学）只认三百年积累的病例记录、拒绝一切理论推理。盖伦两边都不接受。他在《论最好的医生也是哲学家》《论自己的著作》与《论我本人的见解》中反复申明：医学的知识必须通过三重检验——追溯来源（这个学说是谁、在什么观察基础上提出的）、检验证据（他的观察本身是否可靠、是否可重复）、亲手验证（我在自己的经验中能否见到同样的东西）。他以解剖学与药学的巨量亲手工作做示范：凡是前人的说法，他先弄清其根据，再到解剖台、诊所、药房里复核；希波克拉底之所以被他奉为唯一权威，不是因为古老，而是因为希波克拉底的结论能在他自己的观察中被一次次重新看见。方法上这意味着：对任何传入的知识，先问『它的证据是什么』，再问『那个证据我今天还能重新获得吗』——凡是两条答案都是空白的断言，暂列为待验证项而非公理；权威的合法性来自其结论的可复验性，而非其年龄或名气。
- Definition: Galen's Roman medical world was split between two dogmatic schools: the Rationalists, who derived medicine from axioms in their studies, and the Empiricists, heirs of Herophilus, who trusted only three centuries of accumulated case records and refused all theoretical inference. Galen accepted neither. In On the Examination of the Best Physician, On My Own Books, and On My Own Opinions he insisted that medical knowledge pass a triple test—trace the source (who proposed this doctrine, on what observations), weigh the evidence (were those observations sound and repeatable), verify by one's own hand (can I see the same thing in my own experience). His enormous firsthand work in anatomy and pharmacy served as demonstration: every inherited claim he first sourced, then re-checked at the anatomical table, in the clinic, and in the apothecary's shop; Hippocrates was his sole authority not for being ancient but because Hippocrates' conclusions could be seen again and again in Galen's own observations. Methodologically: for any incoming knowledge, first ask 'what is its evidence,' then 'can that evidence be re-obtained today'—assertions blank on both counts are filed as to-be-verified, not as axioms; an authority's legitimacy comes from the re-verifiability of its conclusions, not from its age or fame.
- 金句: 最好的医生也必须是哲学家——因为医术的判断需要逻辑，而逻辑要求每一条断言都拿出自己的证据。
- Quote: The best physician is also a philosopher—for the art's judgments need logic, and logic demands that every assertion produce its own evidence.
- 四步过程: 对接受的每条学说标注来源：谁提出、依据何种观察或推理、原初证据是否留存 → 评估原初证据的质量：观察条件是否可控、样本是否充分、结论是否超出证据范围 → 设计自己的复验：在当前条件下重新获得同类证据（解剖、病例、试验），记录所见与所传是否一致 → 按复验结果分级：复验通过者入公理库，无法复验者降为待定，与证据相悖者公开标记存疑并给出冲突细节
- Process: Tag every inherited doctrine with its source: who proposed it, on what observation or reasoning, and whether the original evidence survives → Grade the original evidence: were the conditions controlled, the sample adequate, the conclusion within the reach of the evidence → Devise your own re-verification: re-obtain similar evidence under current conditions (dissection, cases, trials) and record whether what you see matches what was transmitted → Rank by outcome: re-verified claims enter the axiom shelf; unverifiable ones drop to pending; contradicted ones are publicly flagged with the conflicting details
- 关键概念: 溯源、亲手复验、理性与经验合一、可复验性、权威的降格
- 关联模式: M-GAL-002, M-GAL-003, M-HAR-001

### M-GAL-002 公开解剖演示法 / Public Anatomical Demonstration Method

- 领域: 当众亲手解剖、让眼睛取代文本/知识以可复现的表演交付 (科学方法论/实证示范)
- 定义: 盖伦在《解剖程序》(De Anatomicis Administrationibus) 中把解剖学从书斋搬到公共剧场：他多次在罗马当众解剖活体与尸体，在场观众包括哲学家、执政官与同行医生；他甚至邀请怀疑者自己拿刀操作，因为他宣称『要相信自己的眼睛而不是我的话』。这在方法上是双重的武器化。其一，对抗书本研究：当时的解剖教学由奴隶或理发匠操刀、教师高声朗读盖伦前辈的文本，盖伦要求主讲人必须亲手执刀——文本与实物在同一个动作中当场对质，谁错谁对，观众的眼睛当场裁决。其二，对抗私人主张：他公开解剖等于把结论连同其证据链一起交付，任何在场者都能重复同一操作检验同一结论。他对对手的攻击也从不停留在论战文字，而是要求『带个尸体来，我们当众看』。方法上这意味着：当知识以文本形态传递时，文本错误会自我繁殖；最可靠的交付形态是可重复的公开操作——把主张变成一个别人可以亲手重做的演示，让证据的可靠性以『任意第三方可复现』而非『作者的名望』作担保。
- Definition: In On Anatomical Procedures Galen moved anatomy from the study to the public theater: he repeatedly dissected living bodies and corpses in Rome before audiences of philosophers, consuls, and fellow physicians, and even invited skeptics to take the knife themselves, declaring that one should 'trust one's own eyes, not my words.' This was a two-edged weapon. First, against book-based study: anatomical teaching of his day had slaves or barbers wield the blade while a lecturer read Galen's predecessors aloud; Galen demanded that the lecturer hold the knife himself—text and object confronted in a single act, adjudicated on the spot by the audience's eyes. Second, against private claims: public dissection delivered a conclusion together with its evidence chain, so anyone present could repeat the same operation and test the same result. Even his attacks on rivals never stayed in polemical prose—he demanded 'bring a cadaver, and let us see in public.' Methodologically: when knowledge travels as text, textual errors self-replicate; the most reliable delivery is the repeatable public operation—turn a claim into a demonstration anyone can redo, so reliability is guaranteed by 'reproducible by any third party' rather than by the author's fame.
- 金句: 不要相信我的话——自己拿起刀，让你们自己的眼睛作证。
- Quote: Do not trust my words—take up the knife yourselves, and let your own eyes bear witness.
- 四步过程: 把争议主张翻译为一次可在公共场合完成的操作：指定对象、操作步骤、预期所见 → 邀请质疑者到场甚至亲自操作，事先声明裁决标准——所见与预测一致则主张成立 → 全程使用在场者可核验的素材（当场取得的动物或标本），杜绝『事先准备好的』可疑道具 → 演示后立即公开步骤记录（后来化为《解剖程序》的操作手册），使不在场者也能按册重演
- Process: Translate the disputed claim into one operation completable in public: specify the subject, the steps, the expected sight → Invite doubters to attend—even to wield the knife—and declare the criterion in advance: if the sight matches the prediction, the claim stands → Use material verifiable by all present (animals or specimens obtained on the spot), leaving no room for suspicious 'prepared' props → Immediately after the demonstration publish the procedure (later codified in On Anatomical Procedures), so absent parties can replay it from the manual
- 关键概念: 公开演示、亲手执刀、可复现性、文本对质实物、眼见裁决
- 关联模式: M-GAL-001, M-GAL-003, M-HAR-002

### M-GAL-003 切断-功能归因法 / Section-and-Function-Attribution Method

- 领域: 以切断、结扎、毁损定位功能/功能的存在靠其消失来证明 (科学方法论/干预实验)
- 定义: 盖伦把解剖学从『看结构』推进到『问功能』，用的手法是系统化的干预：怀疑某结构承担某功能，就切断、结扎或毁损它，看什么能力随之消失、什么能力安然无恙。在《解剖程序》与《论身体各部分的用途》中记录了成套此类实验：切断喉返神经则嘶鸣消失（发声功能定位）；结扎输尿管则同侧肾仍肿胀而膀胱不充盈（尿液通路定位）；在不同水平横断脊髓产生不同节段的瘫痪与感觉丧失（脊髓传导的分节定位）；切断肋间神经与相关肌肉逐条验证呼吸动作的分工（他精确区分了肋间肌、膈肌与辅助呼吸肌各自的贡献）。这里的方法论骨架是一个严格的双侧对照：干预之前先记录基线行为，干预之后立即再记录，功能的变化与未变的部分共同把『这个结构做什么』逼出来——用排除法在整体中为每个零件归位。方法上这意味着：『某组件承担某功能』的命题只有两种证据来源——组件完好时功能在场、组件被特异性移除后功能特异性消失；二者齐备才允许归因，只看在场不叫证据。
- Definition: Galen pushed anatomy from 'looking at structure' to 'interrogating function' by systematic intervention: suspecting that a structure carries a function, he cut, ligated, or destroyed it and watched which capacity vanished and which survived. On Anatomical Procedures and On the Usefulness of the Parts record whole sets of such experiments: sever the recurrent laryngeal nerves and the squeal is gone (localizing voice); ligate a ureter and that kidney swells while the bladder does not fill (mapping the urine path); transect the spinal cord at different levels for paralysis and sensory loss in different segments; cut the intercostal nerves and muscles one by one to verify the division of labor in breathing. The methodological skeleton is strict before-and-after comparison: record baseline behavior, intervene with minimal collateral damage, record again—both the change and the non-change force out 'what this structure does,' assigning each part its post by elimination. Methodologically: 'this component carries this function' has only two sources of evidence—the function present while the component is intact, and the function specifically gone once the component is specifically removed; attribution is licensed only when both hold; presence alone is not evidence.
- 金句: 想知道一个部分有什么用，就毁掉它，然后看身体失去了什么。
- Quote: To learn what a part is for, destroy it, and see what the body loses.
- 四步过程: 提出结构-功能假设并找出可观测的行为指标（声音、运动、排泄、感觉），先行记录完整基线 → 设计特异性最小干预：只切断/结扎/毁损目标结构，尽量不波及邻近组织，保留对照侧或对照功能 → 干预后立即重测全部指标：记录特异性消失项与未变项，排除间接损伤的混淆 → 反向修复或双侧干预做收尾确认（另一侧再切、或松开结扎），变化随操作可逆即归因成立
- Process: Pose the structure-function hypothesis with observable behavioral indices (voice, motion, excretion, sensation); record a full baseline first → Design the minimally specific intervention: cut, ligate, or destroy only the target structure, sparing neighbors, keeping a control side or control function → Re-test all indices immediately after: note the specifically vanished items and the unchanged ones, ruling out confounding from collateral damage → Close with reversal or bilateral intervention (cut the other side, or release the ligature); attribution stands when the change tracks the operation reversibly
- 关键概念: 干预实验、功能定位、双侧对照、特异性缺失、排除归因
- 关联模式: M-GAL-002, M-GAL-004, M-HAR-001

### M-GAL-004 目的论结构追问法 / Teleological Structure-Interrogation Method

- 领域: 对每个结构追问『它为何是这般构造』/以用途反推设计原因 (认识论/自然哲学)
- 定义: 盖伦在《论身体各部分的用途》(De Usu Partium) 中立下他一生最宏大的方法纲领：解剖学的终点不是描述结构，而是论证『自然不做徒劳之事』——每一块骨、每一条肌肉、每一条血管、每一个瓣膜般的小结构，都必须回答『它为什么长成这样、这个形状服务于什么用途』。他逐一示范：手为什么是五指、拇指为什么能对掌；静脉壁为什么薄而含瓣、动脉壁为什么厚而弹；肝脏为什么分叶、肾为什么有乳头；甚至解释 spleen 这类『看不清用途』的器官时也努力给出功能性假说而非宣布无用。这种追问不是神学装饰，而是当时的生成性研究程序：从用途反推结构解释了无数此前只被罗列的细节（例如他为解释动脉壁弹性而提出动脉与心脏协同输血的论证方向），也为后世打开了『功能分析』这条至今仍在生理学与演化生物学中运转的提问方式。方法上这意味着：面对一个结构，除了问『它是什么』还必须问『为什么是这个形状而不是别的』——把形状的差异当作证据，去推断约束与功能；即便时代提供的因果工具不完善，这个追问本身也在源源不断地产出可检验的假说。
- Definition: In On the Usefulness of the Parts Galen laid down his grandest program: the end of anatomy is not to describe structure but to demonstrate that 'nature does nothing in vain'—every bone, muscle, vessel, and valve-like detail must answer 'why is it built this way, what use does this shape serve.' He demonstrated one by one: why the hand has five digits and the thumb opposes; why veins are thin and valved while arteries are thick and elastic; why the liver is lobed, why the kidney has papillae; even for organs like the spleen whose use was obscure he strove to give functional hypotheses rather than declare them useless. This questioning was no theological ornament but the generative research program of his day: reasoning back from use explained countless previously merely catalogued details (his argument for arterial elasticity led him toward the arteries cooperating with the heart in driving blood), and it opened the line of 'functional analysis' still running in physiology and evolutionary biology. Methodologically: facing a structure, one must ask not only 'what is it' but 'why this shape and not another'—treating differences in form as evidence for inferring constraints and functions; even when an era's causal toolkit is imperfect, the question itself keeps yielding testable hypotheses.
- 金句: 自然不做徒劳之事——因此解剖学的任务，是为身体的每一个部分找出它的用途。
- Quote: Nature does nothing in vain—therefore the task of anatomy is to find the use of every part of the body.
- 四步过程: 完整描述目标结构后，列出它的形状参数与其近缘对照（同功能不同形、同形不同功能的结构） → 追问每个形状差异的代价与收益：这个形状使哪种运作成为可能、排除了哪种运作 → 把『为何不是别的形状』也当作证据：与设计不良的对照结构比较，提炼真正的约束条件 → 产出可检验的假说并回归观察或干预（见切断法）：用途推测若与实际运作相悖即修正
- Process: After fully describing the target structure, list its shape parameters against near relatives (same function, different form; same form, different function) → Interrogate each shape difference for cost and benefit: what operation this shape makes possible, what it forecloses → Treat 'why not some other shape' as evidence too: compare with poorly designed counterparts to distill the real constraints → Yield testable hypotheses and return to observation or intervention (see the sectioning method): revise the conjectured use when actual operation contradicts it
- 关键概念: 目的论、自然不做徒劳之事、功能分析、形态反推、约束推断
- 关联模式: M-GAL-003, M-GAL-005, M-DAR-001

### M-GAL-005 床边征候判读法 / Bedside Sign-Reading Method

- 领域: 以脉象、症状与病史的细微差异反推体内病变/观察代替猜测 (科学方法论/临床观察)
- 定义: 盖伦不仅是解剖学家，更是古代最出色的诊断家。《论诊断》《论脉象以其为医术之用》《论患部》(De Locis Affectis) 记录了他的核心方法：身体内部的病变不可直视，但会以可观察的征候泄漏出来——脉的快慢、强弱、节律与每一下搏动的形状；尿的沉渣与颜色；发热曲线的走势；疼痛的部位、放射与诱发条件；病史中症状出现的时间次序。他的工作是把这些零散征候组织成一套反推内部状态的读法：脉搏细而弱指向深层消耗性疾病，脉管本身的搏动差异被他细分为二十七种以对应不同部位的病变；对《论患部》中的瘫痪病例，他通过哪块肌肉瘫痪、哪种感觉保留来反推损伤位于脑还是脊髓、在传导路径的哪一段——在没有影像学的时代把『定位诊断』做到了惊人的深度。他要求医生『成为病人的观察者而非传闻的记录者』，每个诊断都应指明所依据的征候与排除的备选。方法上这意味着：对不可直视的内部状态，系统化地枚举可观测的泄漏信号、为每个信号建立与内部状态的对应表、再交叉验证多个信号以压低误判——观察的精度决定推断的天花板。
- Definition: Galen was not only an anatomist but the finest diagnostician of antiquity. On Diagnosis, On the Pulse, and On Affected Parts record his core method: internal lesions cannot be seen directly, but they leak into observable signs—the speed, force, rhythm, and shape of each pulse beat; the sediment and color of urine; the course of fever curves; the site, radiation, and triggers of pain; the temporal order of symptoms in the history. His work organized these scattered signs into a reading system for back-inferring internal states: a thin weak pulse pointing to deep wasting disease, pulse differences subdivided into some twenty-seven kinds mapped to lesions at different sites; for the paralytic cases of On Affected Parts he back-inferred whether the lesion lay in brain or cord and at which segment, from which muscles were paralyzed and which sensations preserved—astonishing depth in 'localizing diagnosis' without imaging. He demanded the physician 'be an observer of the patient, not a recorder of hearsay,' every diagnosis stating the signs it rests on and the alternatives excluded. Methodologically: for internal states that cannot be seen directly, systematically enumerate the observable leakage signals, build a correspondence table between signals and states, and cross-validate several signals to suppress misreading—the precision of observation sets the ceiling of inference.
- 金句: 医生要做病人的观察者，而不是别人叙述的记录者——病变藏在体内，但每一处病变都会在外面留下自己的签名。
- Quote: The physician should be an observer of the patient, not a recorder of hearsay—the lesion hides within, but every lesion leaves its signature outside.
- 四步过程: 系统采集多通道泄漏信号：脉搏（速率、力度、节律、搏形）、排泄物、体温曲线、疼痛特征、症状时间序 → 为每类信号建立『信号-内部状态』对应表，标明每个对应的可靠度与已知例外 → 多信号交叉定位：用信号组合而非单一信号推断内部状态，逐个排除备选病因 → 以结局回填对应表：治疗反应与病程走向作为对推断的检验，错判的信号对应关系随之修订
- Process: Systematically collect multi-channel leakage signals: pulse (rate, force, rhythm, beat-shape), excreta, fever curves, pain features, temporal order of symptoms → Build a signal-to-state correspondence table per channel, marking each mapping's reliability and known exceptions → Cross-localize with signal combinations: infer internal states from combinations, excluding candidate causes one by one → Backfill the table with outcomes: treatment response and disease course test the inference, and misjudged mappings are revised accordingly
- 关键概念: 征候学、定位诊断、脉象判读、信号反推、交叉验证
- 关联模式: M-GAL-004, M-GAL-001, M-NEW-001

### M-GAL-006 脉搏分类诊断法 / Pulse Taxonomy Diagnostics

- 领域: 诊断学/脉搏与体温分类学 (核心方法)
- 定义: 把指尖下那一下搏动从'医生的个人感觉'升级为'可命名、可比较、可传递的分类学对象'。盖伦以《论脉搏》四卷建立起一套前所未有的精细分类：按速度、大小、力度、节律、规律性等约二十七种属性逐维定义脉搏——每种属性再分等级、每级配上诊断意义与对应疾病状态，使'脉洪而缓、按之复起'这样的描述成为跨医生、跨地域、跨世代可复现的知识单元。要害有三：其一，多维拆解——拒绝把脉搏当作浑然一体的'好坏'，而拆成可独立判定的属性轴，任何一次触诊都是沿二十七根轴同时取值；其二，与发热、炎症状态交叉编目——脉搏不是孤立征象，而是身体内部状态的窗口，读脉即读病理过程；其三，分类必须服务于治疗决策——分类学不是描述的癖好，而是'此刻该放血还是该用收敛药'的行动指南。方法论签名：观察若不能被命名与分级，就只能停留在这个医生的指尖；一旦被命名，它就进入了所有人的指尖。
- Definition: Upgrade the beat under the fingertip from a physician's private sensation into a nameable, comparable, transmissible taxonomy. In the four books On the Pulse, Galen built an unprecedentedly fine classification: the pulse is broken down along some twenty-seven attributes—speed, magnitude, strength, rhythm, regularity, and more—each attribute graded, each grade assigned diagnostic meaning and its corresponding disease state, so that 'large, slow, and rising again under pressure' becomes a knowledge unit reproducible across physicians, regions, and generations. Three essentials: first, multidimensional decomposition—refuse to treat the pulse as an undifferentiated good-or-bad impression and split it into independently judgeable axes, so every palpation takes a reading along twenty-seven axes at once; second, cross-cataloguing with fevers and inflammations—the pulse is not an isolated sign but a window onto the body's inner state, and reading the pulse means reading the pathological process; third, taxonomy must serve therapeutic decision—it exists not as a descriptive pastime but as the guide for whether to bleed now or give an astringent. Signature: an observation that cannot be named and graded remains trapped in one physician's fingertip; once named, it enters every physician's fingertips.
- 金句: 脉搏的每一次跳动都携带着身体的证词——医生的任务是把它读成可命名的语言。
- Quote: Every beat of the pulse carries the body's testimony—the physician's task is to read it into nameable language.
- 四步过程: 属性拆轴：把被观察对象沿多个独立维度拆解，每一维定义清楚、分出等级——不给'整体印象'留位置 → 分级编目：每一维的每个取值配上典型病象与鉴别要点，形成可查的对照表 → 交叉印证：单一征象不独立下判断，须与发热、疼痛、排泄物等其他征象交叉编目后合诊 → 指向处置：分类的终点是治疗决策——每一次'读数'都必须落到'此刻做什么'的处方上
- Process: Decompose into axes: split the observed object along independent dimensions, each clearly defined and graded—no room for holistic impressions → Grade and catalogue: assign each value on each axis its typical disease picture and discriminating points, forming a look-up table → Cross-verify: no single sign decides alone—cross-catalogue the pulse with fever, pain, and excretions before judging → Point to action: classification ends in therapeutic decision—every reading must land on 'what to do now'
- 关键概念: 脉搏二十七属性、脉搏分类学、征象与病理的交叉编目、可传递的临床观察、脉象描述术语体系
- 关联模式: M-GAL-001, M-GAL-004, M-GAL-002

### M-GAL-007 文本考据辨伪法 / Philological Text Criticism

- 领域: 语文学/文本校勘与辨伪 (支撑方法)
- 定义: 在伪作横行、抄本讹误、市场书贩随意改题的时代，为知识传统做语文学的清理：读对文本是学问的第一素养。盖伦发现罗马书市上署着'希波克拉底'之名出售的著作近半为伪托或讹变，于是用语法分析、文体比较、术语史与医学内容四重证据逐篇鉴定真伪——《论伪书》把'这本书真的是那个人写的吗'变成一个可以给出论证的技术问题而非趣闻。要害有三：其一，以术语定年代——一个概念若在作者生前尚未出现，书中用它即为伪托的铁证；其二，以文体与语法指纹比对——同一作者的行文习惯如同笔迹，伪作在虚词与句式上必然走样；其三，为正典做注释工程——辨伪不是拆毁传统而是修复传统：剔除伪篇、校正讹文、写出批注，让真正属于希波克拉底的文本重新站得住。方法论签名：传统不是祖先传下来的一切，而是经过鉴定后仍能站住的那部分；读错文本的人不可能正确地继承传统。
- Definition: In an age of forgeries, corrupted copies, and booksellers freely rebranding scrolls, perform philological hygiene on a knowledge tradition: reading the text correctly is scholarship's first discipline. Finding that nearly half the books sold in Rome under Hippocrates' name were spurious or corrupted, Galen authenticated them piece by piece with fourfold evidence—grammar, stylistic comparison, history of technical terms, and medical content—On the Habits of Mind for a Hippocratic treating 'was this really written by that man?' as a technical question admitting argument, not an anecdote. Three essentials: first, dating by terminology—if a concept did not yet exist in the alleged author's lifetime, its presence is proof of forgery; second, matching stylistic and grammatical fingerprints—an author's habits of phrasing are like handwriting, and a forger inevitably slips in particles and syntax; third, an annotation project for the canon—authentication is not demolition but repair: strike the spurious, correct the corrupt, write the commentary, and make the genuinely Hippocratic stand firm again. Signature: a tradition is not everything the ancestors handed down but what still stands after examination; whoever misreads the text cannot rightly inherit the tradition.
- 金句: 市面上以先贤之名出售的书中，过半是伪托——不辨真伪而引用，等于替骗子背书。
- Quote: More than half the books sold under the ancients' names are spurious—to cite without authentication is to vouch for a swindler.
- 四步过程: 建正典底本：先确立'真本'清单与标准文本，凡入列者必经鉴定——不鉴定则不入典 → 四重取证：语法、文体、术语史、专业内容四条证据线交叉定真伪，单一证据不足以下结论 → 以讹勘讹：对抄本链条做校勘，用最古与最一致的抄本纠正讹文，标注而非抹去异文 → 批注传世：以注释本而非原典孤本传承——注释本身就是对'如何读'的示范与强制
- Process: Establish a canonical base text: an authenticated list and standard text—nothing enters the canon unexamined → Gather fourfold evidence: grammar, style, terminology history, and content cross-determine authenticity; one line alone never decides → Collate corruption with corruption: correct readings using the oldest and most concordant manuscripts, recording variants rather than erasing them → Hand down by commentary: transmit through annotated editions—the commentary itself models and enforces how the text must be read
- 关键概念: 辨伪、术语定年代、文体指纹比对、正典注释工程、抄本校勘
- 关联模式: M-GAL-001, M-GAL-006, M-GAL-002

### M-GAL-008 自我实验与药物试验法 / Self-Experimentation and Drug Trials

- 领域: 治疗学/药理验证 (支撑方法)
- 定义: 把治疗学从'祖传配方'变成'可检验的实践'：凡药必试，凡试必有对照性的推理。盖伦在《论简单药物的功效》中系统地以'自身先服'为第一道检验——医生开出任何药物之前，先在自己身上体验其效力与毒性；再以动物与不同病情的患者比较验证；并以'简药'（单一成分）与'复药'（配方）分开试验的方法论，拒绝在多变量搅在一起的配方传统里下结论——先弄清单味药各自做什么，再谈组合。要害有三：其一，以自己为最可靠的仪器——自我实验不仅是勇气，更是把观察者与观察对象合一从而排除'听说'误差；其二，功效必须与病因学推理对得上——药物不是碰运气的工具，它起效的路径必须能被'四体液'病理框架解释，解释不通则存疑；其三，剂量、时序、剂型都是变量而非常识——同样的药，给药时机与炮制方式不同则效验不同，须逐一记录。方法论签名：未经检验的药方只是传说；医生的信誉建立在'这药我自己吃过'之上。
- Definition: Turn therapeutics from inherited recipes into testable practice: every drug must be tried, and every trial must carry controlled reasoning. In On the Powers of Simple Drugs, Galen systematized 'first taste it yourself' as the first test—a physician experiences any drug's efficacy and toxicity in his own body before prescribing; then cross-verifies in animals and patients with differing conditions; and, by testing 'simple' (single-ingredient) drugs separately from 'compound' prescriptions, refuses to conclude inside a recipe tradition where variables are confounded—first establish what each simple drug does, then discuss combinations. Three essentials: first, use yourself as the most reliable instrument—self-experimentation is not bravado but the fusion of observer and observed, eliminating hearsay error; second, efficacy must square with pathological reasoning—a drug is not a lottery ticket, its mode of action must be explainable within the humoral framework, and what cannot be explained stays suspect; third, dose, timing, and preparation are variables, not common sense—the same drug works differently by timing and processing, and each must be recorded. Signature: an untested prescription is only a legend; a physician's credibility rests on 'I have taken this myself.'
- 金句: 医生的处方权建立在自我试验之上——我未曾亲验的药，不开给任何人。
- Quote: A physician's right to prescribe is founded on self-trial—a drug I have not tested on myself, I prescribe to no one.
- 四步过程: 先服后开：任何药物进入处方前，医生先在自己身上做第一轮效力与毒性观察 → 简复分试：先单独测清每种简单成分的功效，再测组合——拒绝在多变量混合中笼统下结论 → 推理对勘：起效方式必须能被病理框架解释，解释不通的效验标记为存疑而非采纳 → 全程记录：剂量、给药时机、炮制方式、患者体质逐项记录，使一次试验可被他人复制
- Process: Taste before you prescribe: the physician's own body is the first trial for every drug's efficacy and toxicity → Test simples before compounds: establish each ingredient's power alone, then combinations—never conclude inside confounded mixtures → Reconcile with reasoning: a drug's mode of action must be explainable in the pathological framework; what cannot be explained is flagged, not adopted → Record everything: dose, timing, preparation, and patient constitution itemized so any trial can be repeated by another hand
- 关键概念: 自我实验、简药与复药分试、功效与病因学对勘、剂量-时序-剂型变量、单变量试药次序
- 关联模式: M-GAL-003, M-GAL-005, M-GAL-001

### M-GAL-009 公开论辩裁决法 / Public Disputation Adjudication

- 领域: 学术竞争/公共演示与论辩 (核心方法)
- 定义: 把罗马的学术竞技场变成裁决机制：真理的主张不由权威任命，而在群贤环伺的公开演示中当场决胜。盖伦主动走进论辩场——在听众面前做解剖演示、当场解读陌生病例、与对手同台论战，以'让对手沉默'而非'让听众喝彩'为获胜标准。要害有三：其一，可重复的现场证据——每一次公开演示都是可被在场任何人核查的实验，论证不是修辞而是可指认的结构（切断的神经、结扎的血管就摆在眼前）；其二，以对手的最强形式应战——他研读并复述各派学说后再攻其要害（对经验派、理性派、方法派皆是），拒绝打稻草人；其三，接受现场检验的暴露风险——公开论辩意味着自己的错误同样当场暴露，胜出的前提是敢于把方法论放进敌意审查。方法论签名：在无人监督时说的是意见，在众目环伺的解剖台前说的是知识；学术竞争若无公开裁决，就退化为宗派的地盘。
- Definition: Turn Rome's scholarly arena into an adjudication mechanism: claims to truth are not appointed by authority but decided on the spot in public demonstration before assembled experts. Galen walked into the arena deliberately—performing dissections before audiences, reading unfamiliar cases extempore, debating rivals face to face—taking 'the rival falls silent' rather than 'the crowd applauds' as the standard of victory. Three essentials: first, repeatable on-site evidence—every public demonstration is an experiment checkable by anyone present; the argument is not rhetoric but a pointable structure, the severed nerve and ligated vessel lying before the eyes; second, engage the rival's strongest form—he studied and restated each school's doctrine before striking its weak point, against Empiricists, Rationalists, and Methodists alike, refusing to fight straw men; third, accept the exposure risk of public test—open disputation exposes one's own errors on the same spot, and winning presupposes the nerve to put one's own method under hostile review. Signature: spoken without supervision, it is opinion; spoken before the eyes at the dissection table, it is knowledge. Scholarly competition without public adjudication decays into sectarian turf.
- 金句: 让对手当场沉默，胜过让听者当场喝彩——裁决属于证据，不属于掌声。
- Quote: Better to silence the rival on the spot than to please the crowd—the verdict belongs to evidence, not applause.
- 四步过程: 设公开场合：主动选择有专家与对手在场的演示场合——裁决的效力来自在场者的质量而非人数 → 做可核查的演示：以活体解剖、当场触诊、当场断症呈现证据，每一步指向在场的可验证对象 → 复述对手最强论：先以对手认可的术语准确重述其立场并获其默认，再展开攻击 → 以沉默为胜：不以喝彩论输赢——对手无法再反驳、无法再演示，方为裁决完成
- Process: Choose the public venue: deliberately demonstrate where experts and rivals are present—adjudication's force comes from the quality of witnesses, not their number → Demonstrate checkably: present evidence by vivisection, extempore palpation, and on-the-spot diagnosis, each step pointing to a verifiable object in the room → State the rival's strongest case: restate the position in the rival's own terms until he concedes the restatement, then attack → Take silence as victory: not applause but the rival's inability to reply or demonstrate again marks adjudication complete
- 关键概念: 公开解剖演示、当场病例解读、让对手沉默、最强形式应战、敌意审查
- 关联模式: M-GAL-003, M-GAL-008, M-GAL-001

### M-GAL-010 自注性正典工程法 / Self-Annotating Canon Engineering

- 领域: 知识工程/著述体系与自我校勘 (核心方法)
- 定义: 把个人毕生产出变成一个可传承的体系工程：为浩繁著作编目、分类、写导论、定次序，并亲自标注哪些是伪托、哪些是初稿、哪些已佚——《论我自己的书》是史上最早的作者自编全集导读之一，以作者本人之手建立'真盖伦'的边界。要害有三：其一，体系自足——各书分工明确（解剖归于《论身体各部分的功能》、治疗归于《论治疗方法》、哲学纲领单列），读者按图索骥即可走完整条医学主轴，无需依赖口传；其二，自我校勘——亲自指出自己早期著作的疏漏与后来的修正，把'作者的成长史'写进正典本身，使体系带有可修订的接口而非凝固的教条；其三，为传承而设计——每部书预设明确的读者与用途，篇幅、语言（为罗马听众的希腊文）、案例选取都服务于'在我死后仍可被正确学会'。方法论签名：著作若不能被正确地继承，等于没有写过；体系的完成不在于写完最后一部书，而在于把全部书写进一张后人拿得动的地图。
- Definition: Turn a lifetime's output into a transmissible system-engineering project: catalogue, classify, introduce, and sequence the vast corpus, personally marking which works are spurious, which are drafts, which are lost—On My Own Books is among history's earliest author-compiled guides to a complete works, drawing the boundary of 'the true Galen' with the author's own hand. Three essentials: first, systemic self-sufficiency—each book has a defined post (anatomy in On the Usefulness of the Parts, therapy in On the Method of Healing, philosophical program separate), so a reader can walk the whole spine of medicine by the map without oral transmission; second, self-collation—he personally flags the lapses of his early works and his later corrections, writing the author's growth history into the canon itself so the system carries a revisable interface rather than a frozen dogma; third, designed for transmission—every book presupposes a defined reader and use, with length, language (Greek for Roman audiences), and case selection all serving 'learnable correctly after my death.' Signature: writings that cannot be rightly inherited were never written; a system is complete not when the last book is finished but when all the books fit onto a map posterity can lift.
- 金句: 我不仅为我的病人写作，也为四百年后仍想正确学会医学的人写作。
- Quote: I write not only for my patients but for those four hundred years hence who still wish to learn medicine rightly.
- 四步过程: 编目定界：亲自为全部著作编目，标明真伪、初稿与佚失，划出'真本'的边界 → 分工成图：为每部著作规定它回答的问题与它在体系中的位置，使各书拼成一张完整地图 → 自校修订：在后期著作中公开标注早期著作的疏漏与修正，把成长史写进正典 → 预设传承：为每部书预设读者与用途，确保没有作者在场时体系仍可被正确学会
- Process: Catalogue and bound: personally catalogue all works, marking spurious, draft, and lost—drawing the boundary of the genuine → Map by division of labor: assign each book its question and its place in the system so the works assemble into one complete map → Self-collate: publicly flag early works' lapses and later corrections in later books, writing the growth history into the canon → Design for succession: presuppose each book's reader and use so the system remains correctly learnable with no author present
- 关键概念: 自编全集导读、著作分工体系、自我校勘与修订接口、为传承设计、可教学的课程结构
