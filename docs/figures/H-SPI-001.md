# 斯宾诺莎 (Spinoza) — H-SPI-001

- Era: 1632 年生于阿姆斯特丹一个葡萄牙系塞法迪犹太商人家庭——父亲是marginado（归化葡萄牙的'新基督徒'）后代，逃回荷兰重归犹太教并在交易所立足——斯宾诺莎早年入社区学校习希伯来文、圣经与迈蒙尼德、以撒·阿布拉瓦内尔的注释传统，兼从凡·登·恩德学拉丁文并由此进入笛卡尔-文艺复兴的人文世界——1656 年 23 岁时以'异端邪说'被阿姆斯特丹葡萄牙犹太公会处以赫尔姆（cherem，绝罚）：诅咒被当众宣读，'无人可与他同住、同读、同行'——被绝罚后拒绝悔改，以磨制光学镜片为生，先后居莱茵斯堡、福尔堡、海牙——1663 年出版《笛卡尔哲学原理》（依几何方式证明），是生前唯一以本名发表的著作——1670 年匿名出版《神学政治论》，以圣经批判与政教分离论震动欧洲，被斥为'无神论者'——1673 年收到海德堡大学教席邀请，以'讲授自由恐受限制'婉拒——生前完成《伦理学》（依几何次序证明），因畏惧迫害未付梓，1677 年 44 岁死于肺病（磨镜尘埃伤肺），身后由友人出版《伦理学》《政治论》《书信集》——莱布尼茨、休谟、康德、黑格尔均受其塑造，黑格尔说'要开始做哲学，必先做斯宾诺莎主义者'
- Era (EN): Born 1632 in Amsterdam to a Portuguese Sephardic merchant family—his father descended from marranos (forced converts to Catholicism in Portugal) who had returned to the Netherlands, reverted to Judaism and thrived in the exchange—the boy entered the community school to learn Hebrew, Scripture and the commentarial tradition of Maimonides and Isaac Abravanel, and learned Latin from Franciscus van den Enden, entering the humanist world of Descartes and the Renaissance—in 1656, at twenty-three, he was placed under cherem (ban) by Amsterdam's Portuguese-Jewish congregation for 'heresies': the curse read aloud, 'let no one speak to him, read with him, walk with him'—refusing to recant, he ground optical lenses for a living, living in Rijnsburg, Voorburg and The Hague—in 1663 he published the Principles of Cartesian Philosophy demonstrated in the geometric manner, the only book under his own name in his lifetime—in 1670 the Theological-Political Treatise appeared anonymously, its biblical criticism and argument for separating church and state convulsing Europe as the work of 'an atheist'—in 1673 he declined a professorship at Heidelberg, fearing his freedom to teach would be limited—he had completed the Ethics (demonstrated in geometrical order) but, fearing persecution, left it unpublished; he died of lung disease (glass dust from lens-grinding) in 1677 at forty-four—friends published the Ethics, the Political Treatise and the Letters posthumously—Leibniz, Hume, Kant and Hegel were all shaped by him; Hegel: 'to begin philosophizing, you must first be a Spinozist'

- School: 几何理性的最高实践者（把哲学写成欧几里得式证明的唯一的体系性人物）/ 实体一元论的开创者（神即自然 Deu sive Natura）/ 情感论的第一人（以因果几何处理情感的近代奠基者）/ 圣经批判之父（历史语法解经法）/ 政教分离与思想自由的最早系统论证者 / 自足者的伦理学（以理解代替祈祷的拯救论）

## Historical significance
斯宾诺莎（Baruch/Benedictus de Spinoza, 1632-1677），十七世纪荷兰共和国的哲学家，近代理性主义的三大代表之一，也是哲学史上被自己共同体处以绝罚、又被后世哲学奉为必经之路的唯一人物。他的历史地位由五件事锚定。其一，实体一元论：以'实体不能共享属性'的纯概念推论把神与世界归一为唯一实体，'神即自然'（Deus sive Natura）从此成为泛神论与近代自然主义的枢纽公式。其二，《伦理学》的几何体例：唯一把全部哲学——本体论、认识论、情感论、伦理学——写成欧几里得式证明的体系性人物，形式本身成为一种哲学宣言。其三，情感论：第一次把爱、恨、忧惧当作可推导的自然事件系统处理，'被动的情感一旦形成清晰观念即不再是激情'成为三百年后认知行为疗法的哲学骨架。其四，圣经批判与思想自由：《神学政治论》以历史语法读经，把启示之书还原为历史之书，并以'思想自由不可让渡'论证政教分离——现代圣经批判学与世俗主义的共同源头。其五，人格与体系的合一：被绝罚不悔改、以磨镜自养、拒海德堡教席、拒继遗产——每一项哲学主张都以生活方式兑现，黑格尔因此断言'要么是斯宾诺莎主义者，要么不是哲学家'。代价与极限：几何形式把论证的艰难前置为定义的艰难，凡不接受定义者被整体排除在体系之外；一元论使个体的实在性变得可疑，人格自由在必然的总体中近乎消失；而'以理解代替祈祷'的拯救论对不幸者代价极高——它要求人成为近乎无情绪的智者，一个普通人无法以之为日常依托。他的体系因此更像一座无法居住的完美建筑：被所有后来者丈量，却少有人搬进去住。

## Historical significance (EN)
Spinoza (Baruch/Benedictus de Spinoza, 1632-1677), philosopher of the seventeenth-century Dutch Republic, one of the three great rationalists of early modernity—and the only figure in the history of philosophy excommunicated by his own community and afterwards made a obligatory passage by later philosophy. Five achievements anchor his standing. First, substance monism: by pure conceptual reasoning—'substances cannot share attributes'—God and the world are folded into one substance, and 'Deus sive Natura' becomes the hinge formula of pantheism and modern naturalism. Second, the geometric form of the Ethics: the only systematic thinker ever to cast his whole philosophy—ontology, epistemology, the theory of the affects, ethics—as Euclidean demonstration, the form itself a philosophical manifesto. Third, the theory of the affects: love, hate, grief and fear treated for the first time as deducible natural events—'a passion ceases to be a passion once we form a clear and distinct idea of it' becoming, three centuries later, the philosophical skeleton of cognitive-behavioral therapy. Fourth, biblical criticism and freedom of thought: the Theological-Political Treatise reading Scripture by historical grammar, reducing the book of revelation to a book of history and arguing the separation of church and state from the inalienability of thought—common source of modern criticism and of secularism. Fifth, the unity of person and system: ban without recantation, self-support by lens-grinding, the chair declined, the inheritance refused—every philosophical claim cashed out in a way of life, earning Hegel's verdict that 'to be a philosopher one must first be a Spinozist.' Costs and limits: the geometric form front-loads the difficulty into the definitions, and whoever refuses them is excluded from the system wholesale; monism casts doubt on the reality of individuals, personal freedom all but vanishing within the totality of necessity; and a salvation that substitutes understanding for prayer costs the unfortunate dearly—it demands an almost passionless sage no ordinary person can live by. His system remains an uninhabitable perfect building: measured by every later thinker, lived in by few.

## M-SPI-001 几何收束法 / Geometric Closure Method (more geometrico)

- Definition: 论证工程方法论/以几何学形式承载哲学内容：定义—公理—命题—证明—绎理的链条使每一步只依赖其前件，任何异议被定位为'请指出哪一步错了'——形式的不动性把争论从修辞拉回结构
- Definition (EN): Argument-engineering methodology / casting philosophy in the form of geometry: the chain of definitions—axioms—propositions—demonstrations—corollaries makes each step depend only on its predecessors, and any objection is localized to 'show me which step fails'—the immobility of form pulls the dispute from rhetoric back to structure
- Source: 《伦理学》全书的'依几何次序证明'体例；《笛卡尔哲学原理》的几何化转写；《知性改进论》对方法的自白
- Key quote: 我要以几何的方式证明一切——因为几何不劝人，几何不允诺，几何只使人看见。 / I would demonstrate everything in the geometrical manner—for geometry does not persuade, geometry does not promise; geometry only makes one see.
- Process:
  - 先把对象冷处理：把最烫手的题目（神、情感、救赎）翻译成可定义的术语——定义先行，歧义没收
  - 立最少的公理：只承认极少数自明命题，其余全部必须从公理与已证命题推出——禁止从权威、从多数、从利益出发
  - 一命题一证明：每个命题附一个证明，争议被定位为'请指出哪一步错了'——争论从态度之争变成结构检查
  - 以附录补以情动人：在几何之外附上对常识形而上学的清算（如《伦理学》卷一附录）——形式冷处理与情感批判并行
- Process (EN):
  - Cool the object first: translate the hottest topics (God, the passions, salvation) into definable terms—definitions come first, ambiguity is confiscated
  - Lay down the fewest axioms: admit only a handful of self-evident propositions; everything else must be derived from axioms and proven propositions—no appeals to authority, majority or interest
  - One proposition, one proof: attach a demonstration to each proposition, so any dispute is localized to 'show me which step fails'—the quarrel becomes a structural inspection
  - Add an appendix to move the heart: outside the geometry append a settling of accounts with common metaphysics (as in Part I's appendix of the Ethics)—cold form and warm critique run in parallel
- Representative cases:
  - 《伦理学》卷一：从'实体即自因'的定义出发以二十余个命题推出'神即自然'——关于神的最易起争端的结论被写成最难反驳的形式
  - 《伦理学》卷三：把爱与恨写成命题与绎理（'爱是为外因所引起的观念伴随的快乐'）——最主观的领域获得最客观的体例
  - 生前只署名出版《笛卡尔哲学原理》：以几何方式重写他人哲学——先以最冷的体例证明自己能写最标准的书
- Representative cases (EN):
  - Ethics Part I: from the definition of substance as self-cause, some twenty propositions derive 'God or Nature'—the most disputable conclusion about God cast in the hardest form to refute
  - Ethics Part III: love and hate written as propositions and corollaries ('love is joy accompanied by the idea of an external cause')—the most subjective domain given the most objective form
  - The only signed book of his life, the Principles of Cartesian Philosophy: rewriting another's philosophy in the geometrical manner—first proving, in the coldest genre, that he can write the most standard of books
- Modern applications:
  - 产品决策的证明化：把'为什么做这个功能'写成定义-假设-推论链，评审不再是说服比赛而是逐步检查——异议必须落到具体某一步
  - 法律意见书的结构：事实认定（公理）—法律适用（证明）—结论（命题），使不同立场的分歧点显式化
  - 技术方案评审的'证伪定位法'：用编号命题拆解方案，反对意见只许指向具体编号——把'我不喜欢'变成'第3步不成立'
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Demonstrated product decisions: write 'why this feature' as a definitions—hypotheses—derivations chain, so review is not a persuasion contest but a stepwise inspection—every objection must land on a specific step
  - The structure of a legal opinion: findings of fact (axioms)—application of law (demonstrations)—conclusion (proposition), making the point of disagreement between positions explicit
  - Falsification-localized design review: decompose a proposal into numbered propositions and permit objections only against a numbered step—turning 'I don't like it' into 'step 3 fails'

## M-SPI-002 实体归一法 / Substance-Unification Method

- Definition: 本体论方法论/以'实体不能共享属性'的纯概念推理把万物归入唯一实体：凡独立存在者必有全部属性，两实体不能同属一类，故实体唯一——神即自然，一元不是立场而是推论
- Definition (EN): Ontological methodology / folding all things into one substance by pure conceptual reasoning: whatever exists independently must possess all attributes, and two substances cannot belong to the same class—hence substance is unique: God or Nature, monism not as a stance but as a deduction
- Source: 《伦理学》卷一命题1-15（实体、属性与样式的定义及推演）；《书信》第50封对'神即自然'的说明
- Key quote: 神即自然——凡是存在的，都在神之内；没有神，任何东西既不能存在，也不能被理解。 / God, or Nature—whatever is, is in God; and without God nothing can be, or be conceived.
- Process:
  - 先立资格条件：定义'实体=在自身内并通过自身而被理解'，'属性=知性看来构成实体本质的东西'——把争论前移到定义
  - 推排他性：两实体不能共享属性，凡实体必有无限属性——任何有限物因属性有限而不能是实体
  - 归一：唯一有无限属性的实体即神，而万物皆是该实体的样式——'多'只是唯一者在不同维度与状态下的呈现
  - 重释后果：创造变为自我展现（内因因），奇迹变为自然规律的无知，目的论变为人类中心的想象
- Process (EN):
  - Set the qualifying condition first: define 'substance = what is in itself and conceived through itself,' 'attribute = what the intellect perceives as constituting a substance's essence'—move the dispute forward into the definitions
  - Derive exclusivity: two substances cannot share an attribute, and every substance must possess infinite attributes—no finite thing, its attributes being finite, can be a substance
  - Unify: the one substance with infinite attributes is God, and all things are its modes—the 'many' are the one presented under different aspects and states
  - Redraw the consequences: creation becomes self-expression (immanent cause), miracle becomes ignorance of natural law, teleology becomes human-centered imagination
- Representative cases:
  - 《伦理学》卷一命题14：'除神以外没有任何实体'——用半页纸的概念推理终结'神与世界谁先谁后'的千年问题
  - '赫尔姆'之后的自辩：被绝罚的理由正是把神等同于自然——以推论承受共同体最重的惩罚，且不收回一步
  - 与莱布尼茨的通信：莱布尼茨要'最佳的 possible world'，斯宾诺莎只接受'必然的唯一世界'——两种本体论气质的分水岭
- Representative cases (EN):
  - Ethics Part I, Proposition 14: 'Besides God no substance can be'—half a page of conceptual reasoning closing the millennium-old question of whether God or the world comes first
  - The self-defense after the cherem: the very reason for his ban was equating God with Nature—bearing the community's heaviest penalty for a deduction, retracting not a step
  - The letters with Leibniz: Leibniz wants 'the best of all possible worlds'; Spinoza accepts only 'the one necessary world'—the watershed between two ontological temperaments
- Modern applications:
  - 系统架构的'唯一事实源'论证：凡可独立变更者必有独立数据模型，两模型不能共享主键语义——故凡共享同一事实者必须归一存储
  - 组织设计中的'本体论审计'：先问'这个概念能不能独立存在'，把依附性概念（指标、角色）降级为样式而非实体，消除重复建制
  - 科学理论的归一化压力：把电与磁、空间与时间统一进一个方程体系的思路，与'凡自存者必含全部属性故唯一'同构
- Modern applications (EN):
  - The single-source-of-truth argument in systems architecture: whatever can change independently needs its own data model, and two models cannot share key semantics—hence what shares one fact must be unified in storage
  - Ontological auditing in organization design: first ask 'can this concept exist on its own,' demoting dependent concepts (metrics, roles) to modes rather than substances, eliminating duplicate institutions
  - The unifying pressure in science: folding electricity with magnetism, space with time into one system of equations is isomorphic to 'whatever exists of itself must contain all attributes, hence is unique'
- Related modes: M-SPI-001（几何收束法——归一法的容器：'实体唯一'正是以几何形式写出的结论）; M-SPI-003（必然安顿法——归一法的伦理后果：万物皆样式则一切皆必然）; M-ZZ-001（张载·太虚即气法——跨文明平行：同以唯一实体（气/神-自然）消解'有无'与'神俗'的二分）

## M-SPI-003 必然安顿法 / Necessity-Settling Method (amor fati intellectualis)

- Definition: 伦理方法论/以重定义代替安慰：把'自由'重定义为'依自身本性的必然性而存在并行动'，把'命运'重定义为'可被理解的原因链条'——抱怨必然是没有理解必然，理解即自由
- Definition (EN): Ethical methodology / redefinition in place of consolation: redefine 'freedom' as 'existing and acting by the necessity of one's own nature,' and 'fate' as 'a chain of causes that can be understood'—to complain of necessity is not to have understood it; to understand is to be free
- Source: 《伦理学》卷一附录（对目的论的清算）、卷五命题6与对命运的论述；《神学政治论》论预言与必然
- Key quote: 自由的人绝少想到死；他的智慧不是对死的沉思，而是对生的沉思。——不要嘲笑，不要悲叹，也不要诅咒，而要理解。 / A free man thinks of death least of all things; his wisdom is a meditation not of death but of life. — Not to laugh, not to lament, not to curse, but to understand.
- Process:
  - 先清算想象：指出'目的''偶然''天意'是人从自身处境出发的想象性概念——把抱怨的对象先取消掉
  - 重定义自由：自由不是'可以不做'而是'由自己的本性必然地做'——石头落地的必然与智者行动的必然不同在原因是否内在于本性
  - 把遭遇转写为认知：对某个情绪形成清晰而明确的观念，该情绪即由激情变为行动——理解不是消除情感而是改变其来源
  - 在永恒形式下收束：把个别事件放回唯一实体的必然秩序中观看，由理解生出对必然的喜悦（对神的爱）
- Process (EN):
  - Settle accounts with imagination first: 'purpose,' 'chance' and 'providence' are imaginary notions projected from the human situation—cancel the very object of complaint
  - Redefine freedom: freedom is not 'being able to do otherwise' but 'doing by the necessity of one's own nature'—the necessity of a falling stone and of a wise agent differ only in whether the cause lies within the nature
  - Rewrite suffering as cognition: when one forms a clear and distinct idea of an affect, it ceases to be a passion and becomes an action—understanding does not remove the emotion but changes its source
  - Close under the form of eternity: place the individual event back within the necessary order of the one substance, and let understanding give birth to joy in necessity (the intellectual love of God)
- Representative cases:
  - 绝罚后的余生：被共同体放逐、赖磨镜为生、拒海德堡教席——每一项'不幸'都被他当作自己本性之必然而承受，无一抱怨流传
  - 《伦理学》卷五论对死亡的平静：自由的人绝少想到死——把必死性从恐惧对象转为被理解的事实
  - 对《神学政治论》遭禁毁的态度：不逃亡、不收回、继续写——'不嘲笑、不悲叹、不诅咒，而要理解'成为他处理自身厄运的方法
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The life after the ban: exiled by the community, living by lens-grinding, declining the Heidelberg chair—each 'misfortune' borne as the necessity of his own nature, not one complaint recorded
  - Ethics Part V on equanimity toward death: the free man thinks of death least of all—mortality converted from an object of fear into an understood fact
  - His attitude to the banning of the Theological-Political Treatise: no flight, no retraction, more writing—'not to laugh, not to lament, not to curse, but to understand' as his method for his own misfortune
- Modern applications:
  - 决策后的'必然复盘法'：区分'当时可得的因果链条'与'事后偏见'——对已发生的结果只提取因果认知，不提取悔恨，减少内耗
  - 慢病与衰老的心理管理：把不可逆事件重定义为'可理解的原因网络'，患者从'为什么是我'转向'机制是什么'——理解降低被动情绪强度
  - 组织危机沟通：领导人以'这是我们系统的必然输出'代替'运气不好'，把团队从寻找责任人转向理解结构——情绪预算节约给改进
- Modern applications (EN):
  - The necessity-review after a decision: separate 'the causal chain available at the time' from 'hindsight bias'—extract causal understanding from what happened, not remorse, cutting internal friction
  - Psychological management of chronic illness and aging: reframe irreversible events as 'an intelligible network of causes,' turning the patient from 'why me' to 'what is the mechanism'—understanding lowers the intensity of passive emotion
  - Crisis communication in organizations: a leader replacing 'bad luck' with 'this is our system's necessary output,' moving the team from hunting the culprit to understanding the structure—saving the emotional budget for improvement
- Related modes: M-SPI-002（实体归一法——本体论前提：万物皆样式则一切皆必然，自由必须重新定义）; M-SPI-004（情感几何法——方法承载体：理解中和情感的具体操作就在情感几何之内）; M-AUR-001（奥罗宾多·内卷演化双程法——对照：同以'超越视角'安顿命运，奥罗宾多许诺演化超越，斯宾诺莎只许理解与喜悦）

## M-SPI-004 情感几何法 / Geometry-of-the-Passions Method

- Definition: 心理-哲学方法论/把情感当作自然事件来推演：情感=观念+身体状态的变化，爱与恨、希望与恐惧都可以从'快乐/痛苦×对象'的组合中推导——情感第一次成为可证明的科学对象
- Definition (EN): Psycho-philosophical methodology / deducing the passions as natural events: an affect = an idea plus a change of bodily state; love and hate, hope and fear are derivable from combinations of 'joy/pain × object'—the passions becoming for the first time a demonstrable, scientific object
- Source: 《伦理学》卷三（情感的起源与定义）、卷四（情感的奴役与力量）、卷五（情感的控制）
- Key quote: 情感是一种观念，通过这种观念，心灵肯定身体的全部或部分力量大于从前。——被动的情感一旦形成清晰的观念，就不再是激情。 / An affect is an idea by which the mind affirms a greater or lesser power of its body than before. — A passion ceases to be a passion as soon as we form a clear and distinct idea of it.
- Process:
  - 先取消'情感的特殊地位'：情感不是心灵的另一个世界，而是'观念+身体状态'的同一事件——心智与身体一体地动
  - 立两大基调：快乐=向更大完满的过渡，痛苦=向更小完满的过渡——所有情感都是这两基调与不同对象的组合
  - 推导谱系：爱=对致快乐对象的依恋，恨=对致痛苦对象的疏离，嫉妒=爱与想象中的排他叠加——情感族谱被写成一页定义
  - 找杠杆：情感的力量与其对象观念的联接数成正比——用更强的观念联接（对必然的理解）替换被动的联接，即是治愈
- Process (EN):
  - Cancel the passions' special status first: an affect is not another world of the mind but one event of 'idea + bodily state'—mind and body move as one
  - Lay down the two base tones: joy = the passage to greater perfection, pain = the passage to lesser—all affects are combinations of these with objects
  - Derive the genealogy: love = attachment to a joy-causing object, hate = aversion from a pain-causing one, envy = love overlaid with imagined exclusivity—a whole family tree of the passions written as one page of definitions
  - Find the lever: an affect's power grows with the number of associations of its object's idea—replacing passive associations by the stronger one (understanding of necessity) is the cure
- Representative cases:
  - 《伦理学》卷三用四十八个命题推导爱与恨的生成、竞争与模仿的机制—— jealousy 被定义为'爱与恨同时指向同一个对象且力图排除他人'——情感世界第一次获得类似元素周期表的推导结构
  - 对'悔恨'的分析：悔恨是痛苦而非德性，因为它起于被动观念——斯宾诺莎反对以懊悔为道德引擎，主张以理解替代
  - 个人生活印证：绝罚、破产式的遗产诉讼（拒继父业）、磨镜的孤独——他没有留下激情文字，情感全部转入几何命题
- Representative cases (EN):
  - Ethics Part III, forty-eight propositions deriving the genesis of love and hate and the mechanics of emulation and imitation—jealousy defined as 'love and hate toward one and the same object with the endeavor to exclude others'—the world of the passions gaining, for the first time, something like a periodic table
  - On remorse: remorse is pain, not virtue, because it arises from passive ideas—Spinoza refuses remorse as a moral engine and proposes understanding in its place
  - Confirmation in his life: the ban, the lawsuit renouncing his father's estate, the solitude of lens-grinding—he left no passionate writings; all passion passed into geometric propositions
- Modern applications:
  - 认知行为疗法（CBT）的哲学骨架：'对情绪的观念决定情绪的强度与性质'——贝克与埃利斯的疗法可视为情感几何法的临床化
  - 产品与营销的情绪设计：把用户情绪拆为'基调（得/失）×对象（自我/他人）×联接强度'三因子，预测行为反应——情绪第一次可被建模
  - 亲密关系的'情感谱系'复盘：把冲突情绪还原为'依恋+想象+排他'的组合，避免把情绪当作对方的人格指控——吵架变成成分分析
- Modern applications (EN):
  - The philosophical skeleton of cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT): 'the idea held of an emotion determines its intensity and quality'—Beck's and Ellis's therapies read as the clinical form of the geometry of the passions
  - Emotion design in product and marketing: decompose user emotion into 'tone (gain/loss) × object (self/other) × associative strength,' predicting behavioral reactions—emotion becoming, at last, modelable
  - A genealogy review for intimate conflict: reduce conflict emotions to combinations of 'attachment + imagination + exclusivity,' so emotion is no longer an accusation of character—an argument becomes a compositional analysis
- Related modes: M-SPI-003（必然安顿法——情感几何法的伦理应用：理解如何中和被动情感）; M-SPI-005（历史语法解经法——同一自然化原则在文本领域的应用：把神圣文本当作自然对象）; M-DAR-001（达尔文·自然选择法——方法平行：同把人类看似特殊领域（情感/物种）还原为可推导的自然机制）

## M-SPI-005 历史语法解经法 / Historical-Grammatical Exegesis Method

- Definition: 文本批判方法论/像读自然那样读圣经：只问语言、语境、作者与成书史，不问教义与神启——把'解经'从神学的授权下收回，交给历史与语言学的法官
- Definition (EN): Textual-critical methodology / read Scripture the way one reads nature: ask only of language, context, authorship and composition history, never of doctrine or revelation—recalling 'interpretation' from the custody of theology and remanding it to the judges of history and philology
- Source: 《神学政治论》第7章（论圣经的解释）、第8-10章（成书与作者考证）；《书信》对奇迹的批注
- Key quote: 解释圣经与解释自然的方法并无不同——都只是从历史中引出可靠的东西，而非把我们的想象投射进去。 / The method of interpreting Scripture is no different from the method of interpreting nature—both extract the certain from history rather than projecting our imagination into it.
- Process:
  - 先悬置神学前提：不预设圣经为神启——把它当作一件'以希伯来语写成、在历史中成书'的文本
  - 建立语言-历史底账：考察原文语词的用法、各卷的成书年代、作者身份、编辑与删改的痕迹
  - 以文本解文本：圣经中含义模糊之处只能由圣经自身的用法裁决——禁止从外部教义倒灌意义
  - 推论后果：先知是想象力卓越者而非哲学家，奇迹是无知者的名目，圣经的权威在'正义与博爱'的实践教训而非字句——神学之书还原为历史之书
- Process (EN):
  - Suspend the theological premises first: assume no divine inspiration—treat Scripture as a text 'written in Hebrew and composed in history'
  - Build the linguistic-historical ledger: examine the usage of the original words, the dating of each book, the identity of authors, the traces of editing and revision
  - Let the text interpret the text: where Scripture is ambiguous, only Scripture's own usage may decide—no pouring in of meaning from external doctrine
  - Draw the consequences: prophets excel in imagination, not philosophy; miracle is a name for ignorance; Scripture's authority lies in the practical lesson of justice and charity, not the letter—the book of revelation reduced to a book of history
- Representative cases:
  - 《神学政治论》对摩西五经作者的考证：以文体转换、重复记载与摩西不可能记载自己之死，论证五经非摩西一人所著——现代圣经批判学的开山论文
  - 对'太阳绕地'经文（约书亚记）的处理：以'神为适应俗人理解而说话'解释——文本权威与科学事实的接口设计
  - 该书的遭遇：1674 年被荷兰三级会议查禁、斯宾诺莎被同侪视为'无神论者'——方法本身的代价由方法承担者支付
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The Treatise's examination of the Pentateuch's authorship: shifts of style, duplicated narratives, and Moses' impossibility of recording his own death argue the five books are not one man's—founding paper of modern biblical criticism
  - The handling of the 'sun stood still' verse (Joshua): explained by 'God speaking to the understanding of the common people'—an interface designed between textual authority and scientific fact
  - The book's fate: banned by the Dutch States General in 1674, its author branded 'atheist'—the cost of the method paid by the one who bore the method
- Modern applications:
  - 文献尽调的'悬置权威法'：审一份合同或财报，先不预设'对方诚实'——只从文本内部的一致性、语言的时效性、版本流变建立底账
  - 组织制度史考证：一套'从来如此'的流程，以文件签发时间、措辞更替与引用关系重建其真实起源——把神话还原为决策序列
  - 产品文档的文本考古：用户误读频发的条款，只考察该词在全部文档中的实际用法，不从'设计意图'倒灌解释——以语料裁决语义
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Suspension-of-authority due diligence: reviewing a contract or financial report, first assume no good faith—build the ledger only from internal consistency, dating of language, and version history
  - Textual archaeology of institutional history: a process that 'has always been so' is reconstructed from issuance dates, shifts of wording and cross-references—myth reduced to a sequence of decisions
  - Textual archaeology of product documents: for clauses users misread constantly, examine only the term's actual usage across all documents, never importing the 'design intent'—corpus, not intent, decides meaning
- Related modes: M-SPI-001（几何收束法——同一'冷处理'精神：几何冷处理概念，语法冷处理文本）; M-SPI-006（自由政体法——政治后果：解经权收回之后，思想自由成为国家的必需）; M-HER-002（赫茨尔·演说造国法——反向对照：赫茨尔以共同体叙事造国，斯宾诺莎以历史批判拆解共同体叙事的文本基础）

## M-SPI-006 自由政体法 / Free-State Method (the argument for liberty of thought)

- Definition: 政治工程方法论/以自保逻辑论证思想自由：国家（契约化的群体权力）比个人更强，但无法强夺内在判断——压制思想只会制造伪善与反噬，故'思想自由是民主政体的目的与荣耀'，政权只应管辖行动
- Definition (EN): Political-engineering methodology / arguing liberty of thought from self-preservation: the state (contracted collective power) is stronger than the individual but cannot seize inner judgment—suppressing thought breeds hypocrisy and backlash; hence 'freedom of thought is the purpose and glory of the democratic state,' and sovereignty should govern actions only
- Source: 《神学政治论》第16章（国家的基础：自然权利与契约）、第20章（思想自由不可让渡）；《政治论》对政体的制度设计
- Key quote: 让言论自由存在于政府与法律之内，思想自由则无法被剥夺——思想自由本身即是德性与国家和平的来源。 / Let freedom of speech be granted within government and law; freedom of thought cannot be taken away—freedom of thought is itself the source of virtue and of the peace of the state.
- Process:
  - 先立国家的本质：国家是个体让渡部分强制权所构成的'人的权力'——它强大但在物理上不能进入人的头脑
  - 划管辖边界：政权只能管辖外显行动，内在判断（信仰、推理、情感）从逻辑上不可让渡——禁止思想只会得到口是心非
  - 论证自由的收益：被允许的异议被纳入和平的框架，被禁止的思想获得殉道光环并走向密谋——宽容是自保而非施舍
  - 给神学定位：教义无强制手段则必须依附政权或社会——政教分离既是政权自保也是宗教自身的净化条件
- Process (EN):
  - Establish the state's essence first: the state is 'human power' constituted by individuals' transfer of part of their force—powerful, yet physically unable to enter the mind
  - Draw the jurisdictional boundary: sovereignty governs outward action only; inner judgment (belief, reasoning, feeling) is inalienable by logic—forbidding thought yields only hypocrisy
  - Argue liberty's dividend: permitted dissent is folded into a peaceful frame; forbidden thought gains a martyr's halo and drifts to conspiracy—tolerance is self-preservation, not charity
  - Place theology: doctrine without coercive means must attach itself to the state or to society—the separation of church and state is at once the state's self-preservation and religion's own purification
- Representative cases:
  - 《神学政治论》第20章的最后论证：'目的与荣耀'——荷兰联省共和国的现实宽容被提升为政治原则，反过来也成为对荷兰的规劝
  - 1673 年拒海德堡教席：'我不知哲学自由会限制到何种程度'——用行动为'思想自由'划出个人底线
  - 与德·维特兄弟时代的关系：共和派执政的宽容政策与本书互为印证——1672 年德·维特遇害后该书遭禁，斯宾诺莎欲上街贴'野蛮人'抗议，被房东锁在屋里
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The closing argument of Treatise ch. 20, 'the purpose and the glory': the real toleration of the Dutch Republic elevated into a principle, which in turn rebukes the Dutch themselves
  - Declining the Heidelberg chair in 1673: 'I do not know to what limits the freedom of philosophizing would be confined'—drawing a personal line for freedom of thought in action
  - The link with the De Witt era: the republican regents' toleration and the Treatise corroborate each other—after De Witt's murder in 1672 the book was banned, and Spinoza, wanting to post a placard 'ultimi barbarorum,' was locked in by his landlord
- Modern applications:
  - 平台治理的'行动/言论'双层规则：行为可以封禁，观点不可删除（除非违法）——把'管辖外显行动'写进社区公约，减少价值观执法的任意性
  - 公司内部异见的'免罪机制'：允许匿名与公开的反对意见进入正式流程——压制的反对意见会转入私下与离职，宽容是组织自保
  - 现代政教分离的制度设计：任何宗教组织不得掌握国家强制力——斯宾诺莎三百年前的技术论证至今仍是世俗主义的标准论据
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Two-layer rules for platform governance: conduct can be banned, viewpoints not deleted (unless unlawful)—writing 'govern outward action' into the charter reduces arbitrary values-based enforcement
  - An immunity mechanism for dissent inside firms: anonymous and open objections admitted into formal process—suppressed dissent migrates to the private and to resignations; tolerance is organizational self-preservation
  - Institutional design of modern secularism: no religious body may hold the coercive power of the state—Spinoza's three-century-old technical argument remains the standard case for secularism
- Related modes: M-SPI-005（历史语法解经法——政治前提：解经权从教权收回，思想自由才成为国家必需）; M-SPI-009（自足人格法——个人层面的同构：国家管辖不了头脑，任何权威也管辖不了自足者）; M-SUL-001（苏莱曼·律法并轨法——结构对照：同以'两轨分界'治理多元，苏莱曼以君主权威担保边界，斯宾诺莎以不可让渡的个体权利担保边界）

## M-SPI-007 德性即力量法 / Virtue-as-Power Method (conatus ethics)

- Definition: 伦理-行动方法论/把'应然'换成'力量'：每个存在者都在努力（conatus）保持自身存在，德性不是服从外在规范而是'依本性的力量行动'——自我保存不是自私而是德性的全部基础
- Definition (EN): Ethico-practical methodology / swapping 'ought' for 'power': every being strives (conatus) to persevere in its existence, and virtue is not obedience to an external norm but 'acting from the power of one's own nature'—self-preservation is not selfishness but the whole foundation of virtue
- Source: 《伦理学》卷三命题6-7（conatus 原理）、卷四（'德性即力量'与利他的推导）；《政治论》对人性的现实主义前提
- Key quote: 德性不是别的，就是依自己本性的法则而行动；而努力的最高目标，就是理解——因为理解最能增进存在的力量。 / Virtue is nothing but acting from the laws of one's own nature; and the highest object of striving is understanding—for understanding most increases the power of existing.
- Process:
  - 立最小原理：凡存在者皆努力保持其存在——把'为什么要行动'的终极答案放在存在论而非道德论
  - 重铸德性：德性=力量=依本性而行动的能力，服从外在规范只是他律——'应当'被翻译为'何种行动真正增进你的力量'
  - 从自利推出利他：独居的人力量有限，'对人的联合'与公正最增进自身力量——利他被推导为高级的自利而非义务
  - 降级痛苦型道德：谦卑、悔恨、怜悯的痛苦不增进力量，只有以理解为基础的情感才配为德性的燃料
- Process (EN):
  - Lay down the minimal principle: whatever exists strives to persevere in existence—the ultimate answer to 'why act' placed in ontology, not morality
  - Recast virtue: virtue = power = the capacity to act from one's own nature; obedience to external norms is mere heteronomy—'ought' is translated into 'which action truly increases your power'
  - Derive altruism from self-interest: one alone is weak; 'the union of men with men' and justice most increase one's own power—altruism deduced as higher self-interest, not duty
  - Demote pain-based morality: the pain of humility, remorse and pity does not increase power; only understanding-based emotion deserves to fuel virtue
- Representative cases:
  - 拒海德堡教席与拒遗产：两个决定都基于'哪一种生活更能依我的本性存在'——德性即力量法的个人执行样本
  - 《政治论》开头的方法声明：'不嘲笑人类的行为，不悲叹，不诅咒，而要理解'——把人当作'有情感的力学对象'研究制度
  - 与霍布斯的分岔：同以自我保存为起点，霍布斯推出'人人反对人人'，斯宾诺莎推出'理性导出正义与友谊'——同一原理的两种温度
- Representative cases (EN):
  - Declining the Heidelberg chair and renouncing the inheritance: both decided by 'which life lets me exist according to my nature'—a personal execution sample of virtue-as-power
  - The methodological declaration opening the Political Treatise—'not to mock, not to lament, not to curse human actions, but to understand'—studying institutions treating men as affective-mechanical objects
  - The parting from Hobbes: both start from self-preservation; Hobbes derives 'war of all against all,' Spinoza derives 'reason leads to justice and friendship'—one principle, two temperatures
- Modern applications:
  - 个人决策的'力量审计'：评估一个选择的标准不是'是否符合他人期待'而是'它长期增减我行动的自主性与能力'——把 conatus 变成决策指标
  - 组织激励的现实主义设计：先承认员工按自身利益努力（conatus），制度只做'把个体努力引导向合作'的通道——不指望道德说教改造人性
  - 教练与辅导中的'反悔恨法'：以'这次失败暴露了哪个可改进的结构'替代自责——痛苦型情感不产生力量，结构认知才产生
- Modern applications (EN):
  - A power audit for personal decisions: judge a choice not by others' expectations but by 'whether it grows my long-run capacity and autonomy of action'—conatus as a decision metric
  - Realistic incentive design in organizations: admit first that employees strive by their own interest (conatus); institutions only build channels steering individual striving toward cooperation—not expecting moralizing to remake human nature
  - The anti-remorse method in coaching: replace self-blame with 'which improvable structure did this failure expose'—pain-based emotion generates no power; structural cognition does

## M-SPI-008 第三种知识法 / Third-Kind-Knowledge Method (scientia intuitiva)

- Definition: 认知升级方法论/把知识分为三层并指明最高层：想象（符号与经验）、理性（共同概念的推理）、直觉（从神-自然的本质一次性把握个别事物的本质）——认知训练的目标是把日常经验升级到第三层
- Definition (EN): Cognitive-upgrade methodology / grading knowledge into three kinds and naming the highest: imagination (signs and experience), reason (inference from common notions), intuitive knowledge (grasping the essence of a particular thing at once from the essence of God-or-Nature)—the aim of cognitive training being to raise ordinary experience to the third kind
- Source: 《伦理学》卷二命题40附释二（三种知识）、卷五（第三种知识与对神的理智之爱）；《知性改进论》对方法的早期表述
- Key quote: 第一种知识是想象，第二种是理性，第三种是直觉——第三种知识从神的本质直接推进到事物的本质，它本身就是最高的德性。 / The first kind is imagination, the second reason, the third intuitive knowledge—from the essence of God it proceeds directly to the essence of things; it is itself the highest virtue.
- Process:
  - 先诊断来源：检查一个信念是从符号与传闻来（第一层）、从推理来（第二层）、还是从本质的直接把握来（第三层）
  - 清退第一层的权力：想象不因流行而正确——数字、典故、口碑都只是'想象的秩序'而非'理性的秩序'
  - 以共同概念重建：把问题还原到各方共有的真概念上推理——争论从立场之争降维到概念共享
  - 朝直觉训练：反复从最普遍的原理观看个别事物，直到'个别物在整体中的位置'变成一次直接的把握——知识由此自带情感力量
- Process (EN):
  - Diagnose the source first: check whether a belief comes from signs and hearsay (first kind), from inference (second), or from direct grasping of essence (third)
  - Withdraw imagination's authority: popularity makes imagination no truer—numbers, anecdotes and reputation are only 'the order of imagination,' not of reason
  - Rebuild from common notions: reduce the question to true concepts shared by all parties and reason from there—the dispute is downgraded from a clash of positions to shared concepts
  - Train toward intuition: view the particular repeatedly under the most universal principles until 'its place in the whole' becomes a single direct grasp—knowledge then carries its own emotional force
- Representative cases:
  - 《伦理学》卷二对'第四比例数'的著名例题：同求数值，商人用算术（第一层）、数学家用推理（第二层）、而被数字本质浸透者一眼看出（第三层）——三种认知质量的同题示范
  - 他自身的知识层级轨迹：从希伯来学校的经文传统（第一层）到笛卡尔式推理（第二层）到《伦理学》的体系性直觉（第三层）
  - 对'错误'的定义：错误不是缺乏观念，而是'观念与被剥夺的相应确信'——把认识论问题翻译成认知结构问题
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The famous 'fourth proportional' example of Ethics II: seeking the number, the merchant multiplies (first kind), the mathematician infers (second), and one steeped in the nature of number sees at once (third)—one problem demonstrating three grades of cognition
  - The trajectory of his own knowledge: from the scriptural tradition of the Hebrew school (first) through Cartesian inference (second) to the systemic intuition of the Ethics (third)
  - His definition of error: error is not the lack of an idea but 'an idea deprived of the corresponding certainty'—an epistemological problem translated into a problem of cognitive structure
- Modern applications:
  - 学习路径设计：识别自己对新领域的知识层级——背结论（想象层）、会推导（理性层）、见结构即答（直觉层），并刻意把第二层压缩为第三层的练习
  - 专家判断的制度化：重大决策要求'直觉判断+可写出的推理链'双轨提交——第三种知识没有第二层的备份不准进入决策记录
  - 团队沟通的'共同概念法'：争论先列举双方都接受的真概念，再从共享处推理——避免在想象的层次上辩论
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Designing a learning path: identify your grade of knowledge in a new field—memorized conclusions (imagination), derivable (reason), structure-seen-at-a-glance (intuition)—then deliberately compress the second grade into the third by practice
  - Institutionalizing expert judgment: for major decisions require 'intuitive judgment + a writable chain of reasoning' as twin submissions—an intuition without its second-grade backup may not enter the decision record
  - The common-notions method for team argument: first list the true concepts both sides accept, then reason from the shared base—avoid debating at the level of imagination
- Related modes: M-SPI-001（几何收束法——形式装置：证明的链条正是把读者从第一层运送到第二层的交通工具）; M-SPI-003（必然安顿法——伦理终点：第三种知识生产的喜悦就是拯救本身）; M-AUR-005（奥罗宾多·降入检验法——跨传统平行：同设认知层级并以最高层为归宿，但奥罗宾多的通道是瑜伽演化，斯宾诺莎是几何理解）

## M-SPI-009 自足人格法 / Self-Sufficiency-of-Person Method

- Definition: 存在方式方法论/以手艺、理解与极简需求构成不依赖任何共同体的自足人格：把'被放逐'转写为'不被奴役'——宁可正直地贫穷，不可依附地富足
- Definition (EN): A methodology of existence / composing a self-sufficient person from craft, understanding and minimal needs, independent of any community: rewriting 'exile' as 'freedom from bondage'—better poor in integrity than rich in dependence
- Source: 《伦理学》卷四附录（'论怎样的人才能最好地生活'的五十条箴言）；《书信》第6封（拒遗产）、第44封（论安逸与道德）；同时代人的传记（卢卡斯、科勒鲁斯）
- Key quote: 不要追求荣誉的幻觉：能使人真正快乐的只有三件事——理解的增长、心灵的满足与身体的健康；其余皆为手段。 / Do not chase the phantom of honor: only three things yield true joy—the growth of understanding, the satisfaction of the mind, and the health of the body; all the rest are means.
- Process:
  - 建立经济自足：一项与人格不冲突的手艺（磨镜）——收入不依赖任何共同体或赞助人的好恶
  - 压缩需求清单：把欲望分为'为存在所必需'与'为想象所驱动'，只保留前者——需求越小，受制于人的表面越小
  - 重估社会货币：公众赞许被诊断为'最不稳定的力量来源'，从人格账户中降级——不追求学位、教席与上流身份
  - 以思想质量重组共同体：绝罚不是孤独判决——以书信网络结交奥尔登堡、拉茨等异教友人，把'共同体'从出身单位换成精神单位
- Process (EN):
  - Build economic self-sufficiency: a craft compatible with the person (lens-grinding)—income depending on no community's or patron's favor
  - Compress the list of wants: divide desire into 'necessary for existence' and 'driven by imagination,' keeping only the former—the smaller the needs, the smaller the surface exposed to others' power
  - Revalue social currency: public praise is diagnosed as the least stable source of power and demoted from the account of the person—no degree, no chair, no polite society pursued
  - Reconstitute community by quality of thought: the ban was no sentence of solitude—a correspondence network with Oldenburg, Leibniz and other unchurched friends replaces the community of birth with one of mind
- Representative cases:
  - 1673 年海德堡选帝侯的教席邀请被拒：'我不知哲学自由会限制到何种程度'——附带的俸禄被明确放弃，人格自足的教科书时刻
  - 拒绝继承父亲遗产并诉诸法庭让妹妹独得：以一次诉讼把'家族财产'从人生账户中注销
  - 海牙的斗室与一桌一床：去世时的遗产是一副镜片工具与几册书——科勒鲁斯记其'生前简朴，死后只余思想的利息'
- Representative cases (EN):
  - Refusing the Heidelberg chair offered by the Elector Palatine in 1673: 'I do not know to what limits the freedom of philosophizing would be confined'—the stipend explicitly declined, the textbook moment of personal self-sufficiency
  - Renouncing his father's estate and letting his sister take it by lawsuit: one proceeding striking 'family property' from the ledger of his life
  - A small room in The Hague, a table and a bed: at death his estate was a set of lens tools and a few books—Colerus records a man simple in life, leaving after him only the interest of thought
- Modern applications:
  - 职业选择的自足审计：接一份工作前先问'这份收入是否要求我改造自己的判断'——把收入来源与人格独立的关系显式化
  - 数字时代的'需求最小化'：减少订阅、赞许与社交表演的账户——每一项被取消的需求都是一处不被攻击的暴露面
  - 共同体重组的实践：以共同工作的质量（而非出身、教籍或平台身份）挑选合作者——斯宾诺莎的书信网络是分布式共同体的原型
- Modern applications (EN):
  - A self-sufficiency audit for career choices: before taking a job ask 'does this income require me to remodel my judgment'—making explicit the relation between income source and independence of person
  - Minimizing wants in the digital age: cut subscriptions, likes and social performance—each want cancelled is one attack surface closed
  - The practice of reconstituting community: choose collaborators by the quality of shared work, not by origin, confession or platform identity—Spinoza's correspondence network as the prototype of the distributed community
- Related modes: M-SPI-006（自由政体法——宏观同构：个人不依附共同体如同国家不依附教权）; M-SPI-007（德性即力量法——原理基础：自足是'力量自养'的人格形态）; M-DAV-001（大卫·休谟·温和怀疑法——对照：同以'理解'立身，休谟在社交中养怀疑，斯宾诺莎在独处中养体系）

## M-SPI-010 体系人格收束法 / System-as-Person Closure Method

- Definition: 收束方法论/使体系与人格互相质押：哲学体系的每一个结论都被作者以生活方式兑现（拒教席、拒遗产、被绝罚不悔改），同时人格的每一次选择都被体系证明为必然——'这个人就是他的体系'
- Definition (EN): A methodology of closure / making system and person pledge each other: every conclusion of the philosophy is cashed out by its author's way of life (declining the chair, renouncing the estate, refusing recantation), while every choice of the life is demonstrated as necessary by the system—'the man is his system'
- Source: 《伦理学》卷五命题42（'至福不是德性的报偿，而是德性本身'）与其一生的对照；《书信》贯穿的行为记录；卢卡斯与科勒鲁斯的传记
- Key quote: 至福不是德性的报偿，而是德性本身；我们也不是因为克制情欲才享有至福，恰恰相反，因为享有至福才能克制情欲。 / Blessedness is not the reward of virtue but virtue itself; nor do we enjoy it because we restrain our lusts—on the contrary, because we enjoy it we are able to restrain them.
- Process:
  - 先把体系写完整：以几何体例把全部结论（本体论、情感论、政治论）收进一本书，生前不出版也不删改——体系先于人身安全
  - 让每个结论过生活检验：拒教席对应'思想自由不可让渡'，拒遗产对应'需求最小化'，不悔改对应'必然安顿'——每条箴言在生平中有对应事件
  - 以体系消化代价：绝罚、贫病与书禁都被写为可理解的自然事件——痛苦不再有体系外的申诉渠道，而成为体系力量的证明
  - 接受收束的极限：体系的自洽担保不了外在真理性——后人（黑格尔到分析哲学）持续在其内部与外部双重检验，人格的利息由此增值
- Process (EN):
  - Complete the system first: gather all conclusions (ontology, theory of the affects, politics) into one book in geometric form, publishing nothing before death and retracting nothing—the system precedes personal safety
  - Subject every conclusion to the test of life: declining the chair enacts 'liberty of thought is inalienable'; renouncing the estate enacts 'minimal wants'; no recantation enacts 'necessity settled'—each maxim has its biographical counterpart
  - Digest the costs within the system: the ban, poverty, illness and the book's banning are all written as intelligible natural events—pain has no channel of appeal outside the system, becoming instead evidence of its strength
  - Accept the limit of closure: internal coherence cannot guarantee external truth—successors from Hegel to analytic philosophy kept testing it from within and without, and the interest of the person thereby grew
- Representative cases:
  - 一生一件大事记：23 岁被绝罚不悔改、31 岁拒遗产、41 岁拒教席、44 岁死于磨镜肺病——每一节点都是体系条文的兑现，《伦理学》因此被读作'用生命做的证明'
  - 《伦理学》的出版策略：完成多年而不出版、只在友人圈传抄——生前名声为零，身后体系完整，收束优先于曝光
  - 黑格尔的判词：'要么是斯宾诺莎主义者，要么不是哲学家'——体系与人格合一的收束使后世哲学无法绕开他
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The ledger of one life: ban without recantation at twenty-three, renounced estate at thirty-one, declined chair at forty-one, death from lens dust at forty-four—every node the cashing of a proposition; the Ethics reads as 'a demonstration performed with a life'
  - The publication strategy of the Ethics: finished years before, unpublished, circulated only among friends—zero fame while alive, a complete system after death; closure before exposure
  - Hegel's verdict: 'to be a philosopher one must first be a Spinozist'—a closure uniting system and person that later philosophy could not bypass
- Modern applications:
  - 知识产品的'体系-人格'同构发布：把方法论写成可检验的完整体系并先以自身实践兑现（吃自己的狗粮）——主张的每个条款在作者生活中有可指认的事件
  - 创始人声誉的工程化管理：把核心价值观写成少量可证伪的条款并公开兑现记录——人格的利息来自兑现而非叙事
  - 写作与出版的收束策略：作品完成优先于陆续曝光——把'体系的完整'置于'身份的可见'之上，避免半成品损害主张
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Publishing knowledge products as system-person isomorphism: write the method as a testable whole and cash it out in one's own practice first (dogfooding)—every clause of the claim has an identifiable event in the author's life
  - Engineering the reputation of a founder: write core values as a few falsifiable clauses with a public record of cashing them—the interest of the person comes from fulfillment, not narrative
  - A closure strategy for writing and publication: completion before exposure—placing 'the integrity of the system' above 'the visibility of the persona,' so half-finished work never damages the claim
- Related modes: M-SPI-001（几何收束法——形式起点：体系完整性的技术保障）; M-SPI-009（自足人格法——人格起点：不依附使兑现成为可能）; M-SUL-010（苏莱曼·法度人格收束法——结构对照：同以人格收束体系，苏莱曼以王权与头衔收束帝国制度，斯宾诺莎以生活兑现哲学体系——权力的收束与理解的收束之分）

- zh: 一家老牌出版社要修订一部'内部通行三十年'的编辑规范。新主编参照历史语法解经法：先悬置'规范是权威'的前提，把规范当作一件历史文本——建立签发时间底账、比对不同版本的措辞更替、梳理各条之间的引用关系。考证发现：被奉为'创社原则'的三条其实是九十年代一次事故后的临时补丁，而真正的创社文件从无人引用。修订按'以文本解文本'进行：模糊条款只由全部文档中该词的实际用法裁决，不从'当年意图'倒灌。反对者质问'你凭什么否定传统'，他回答'我没有否定传统，我考证了它'。代价：考证耗了半年，比拍脑袋重写慢得多——历史方法的成本是时间。
- en: A venerable publisher is to revise an editorial standard 'observed for thirty years.' The cost: half a year of examination, far slower than rewriting by intuition—the historical method's price is time.

### C-SPI-006 (M-SPI-006)

- zh: 一个万人开源社区的治理组面临'政治言论是否清退'的争议：一派要求全面禁止，一派要求全面放任。参照自由政体法，他们先立国家（社区）的本质：社区权力来自成员让渡的部分强制权，它管得了仓库与行为，管不了头脑。据此划界：管辖外显行动（骚扰、破坏构建），不管辖内在判断（观点、立场）——被允许的异议留在议题流程里，被删除的观点会带着殉道光环出走并另立门户。新公约运行一年，'出走党'没有出现，但出现了新问题：某些观点让'行动'外溢（拒绝评审某些人的代码）——边界之争从'说不说'变成'做不做'，治理组发现自由政体是一个需要持续仲裁的接口，而非一次性判决。
- en: A ten-thousand-member open-source community's governance group disputes whether 'political speech' warrants expulsion—one faction demands a total ban, another total license. ### C-SPI-007 (M-SPI-007)

- zh: 一位产品负责人要给一个'永远做不出爆款'的老产品线定去留。她参照德性即力量法先立最小原理：这条线存在的努力方向是什么——保持自身的存在还是借尸还魂？然后把'应当'翻译成'力量'：逐一评估每个选项'长期增减团队行动的自主性与能力'。数据揭示反直觉的结论：关停该线并让团队并入新业务，人员的技能迁移率反而最高——留恋'曾经爆款'是痛苦型情感（悔恨与骄傲的组合），不产生力量。她给出决策：关停，同时公开记录全部推导。代价：一位老工程师离职时说'你们把情怀做成了算术'——力量审计能提升决策质量，却可能损伤意义感。
- en: A product lead must decide the fate of an 'eternally hitless' aging product line. ### C-SPI-008 (M-SPI-008)

- en: An engineer finds he 'knows many microservice terms but cannot design,' and three courses bring no improvement. The cost: in a new field (ML systems) his intuition is zero—the third kind cannot be rushed past the second.

### C-SPI-010 (M-SPI-010)
