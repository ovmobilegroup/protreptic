## 经典案例
Confucius (551-479 BC), from Zouyi in the State of Lu. Orphaned and poor in youth, he devoted himself to study at fifteen, stood firm at thirty, was free from doubts at forty, understood the Mandate of Heaven at fifty, obeyed the orders of heaven at sixty, and at seventy could follow the desire of his heart without overstepping the bounds. Early in his career he served in Lu, serving as magistrate of Zhongdu, Minister of Works, and Grand Sakoi, implementing governance by ritual. In the tenth year of Duke Ding of Lu, he presided over the suburban drinking ritual, using rectification of names to rectify the bureaucracy. Later, due to the dominance of the Three Huan clans, he traveled among the states for fourteen years, encountering the hardship at Chen and Cai where he ran out of food, yet continued singing and playing the lute without cessation. In his later years he returned to Lu, edited the Book of Songs and Book of Documents, regulated the music of Ritual and Music, compiled the Spring and Autumn Annals, and used the Spring and Autumn brush method to judge history with subtle words and great significance. He had three thousand disciples, seventy-two of whom were worthy, compiled the Analects as a record of sayings, and formed the core classics of Confucianism. His four-dimensional engineering of Ren-Li-Zhongyong educational engineering, rectification of names protocol, teacher-student transmission, became the kernel of Chinese civilization's operating system for two millennia.
