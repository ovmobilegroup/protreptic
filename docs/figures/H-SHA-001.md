# 商羯罗 (Adi Shankara) — H-SHA-001

- Era: 约 788 年生于印度喀拉拉马拉巴尔海岸的迦底村（婆罗门家庭，幼年丧父）——少年师从乔荼波陀一系的导师牧尊（Govinda），在纳尔马达河畔出家为游方僧——确立'不二论吠檀多'（Advaita Vedanta）纲领：梵（Brahman）是唯一实在，'我'（Atman）即梵，现象世界的多性是无明（ avidya）所生的幻现——约 788-820 年间徒步遍行次大陆：从喜马拉雅的巴德林纳特到南印度的斯林格里，以'立敌对论'的公开辩论（śāstrārtha）降服弥曼差、佛教与耆那教学者，传说中与曼陀罗弥湿罗的论辩使贝纳雷斯改宗吠檀多——为传法建立四座根本道场（阿默尔沃蒂、德瓦拉卡、普里、乔提尔/斯林格里，一说还有迦西），立'十名'僧团传承——完成'三经疏'（对《梵经》《薄伽梵歌》、主要《奥义书》的注疏，即 prasthāna-traya）以及《千部解脱论》（Upadeśasāhasrī）、《分辨宝鬘》（Vivekacūḍāmaṇi）等独立著作——约 32 岁在喜马拉雅迦西（一说凯达纳特）去世，传说以'入定离去'收束一生。他生前被部分正统派视为'假佛教徒'（pracchanna-bauddha），身后却成为印度教复兴最重要的哲学奠基人，被尊为'商羯罗阿阇梨'（Shankaracharya）
- Era (EN): Born c. 788 in Kaladi on the Malabar coast of Kerala, into a Brahmin family, fatherless in early childhood—as a boy he took renunciation under Govinda of the lineage of Gaudapada on the banks of the Narmada, becoming a wandering monk—and gave Advaita Vedanta its program: Brahman alone is real, the Self (Atman) is Brahman, the manifold world is appearance born of ignorance (avidya)—between c. 788 and 820 he walked the length of the subcontinent, from Badrinath in the Himalaya to Sringeri in the south, defeating Mimamsa, Buddhist and Jaina scholars in open disputation (sastratha)—legendarily converting Banaras to Vedanta in the debate with Mandana Misra—and founded four paramount monasteries (Sringeri, Dwarka, Puri, Joshi/Badrinath) with the Dashanami order of renouncers—and wrote the 'commentaries on the three foundations' (prasthanatraya: the Brahma-sutras, the Bhagavad Gita, the principal Upanishads) together with independent works, the Upadesasahasri ('A Thousand Teachings'), the Vivekacudamani ('Crest-Jewel of Discrimination')—dying at about thirty-two at Kashi (or Kedarnath) in the Himalaya, said to have departed in meditation. Some orthodox critics in his lifetime called him a 'crypto-Buddhist'; after his death he became the most important philosophical founder of the Hindu revival, honored as 'Shankaracharya'

- School: 不二论吠檀多的立法者（梵我不二，'唯一实在'的形上学总纲）/ 遮诠辩证法的大师（以'不是、不是'（neti neti）界定绝对）/ 立敌对论的辩论工程师（先把对手观点立到最强再驳）/ 注疏传统的重塑者（以注'三经'的方式为新学派立法）/ 亲证优先的修行方法论者（经典指向、体验裁决）

## Historical significance
商羯罗（约788-820），印度哲学家、吠檀多不二论的集大成者，被广泛视为印度教哲学传统的最大塑造者之一。他的历史地位由五件事锚定。其一，'不二论'总纲的确立：梵是唯一不落的实在，'我'（阿特曼）即梵——他把《奥义书》'梵我同一'的散见命题锻造成一座完整的形上学体系，并以'幻'（maya）与'无明'解释多样世界的现起，'不二而不一，幻现而非无'。其二，'三经疏'的立法工程：他选择为《梵经》、十部主要《奥义书》与《薄伽梵歌》作注——借注疏的外衣行立法之实，从此吠檀多取代弥曼差成为婆罗门教义学的中心舞台，其后所有吠檀多学派（罗摩奴阇的限定不二论、摩陀伐的二元论）都必须先回应商羯罗。其三，辩论方法论：'立敌对论'（purvapaksha 先立敌论）——把对手立场表述到比对手自己更强，再加以驳斥——这套论辩工艺使他的论著至今是印度哲学论辩范式的教科书。其四，制度性遗产：四大根本道场与'十名'游方僧团，把哲学变成可持续八百年的组织系统；至今四位商羯罗阿阇梨仍被视为印度教的最高教阶之一。其五，修行方法论：'四备'（分辨、离欲、六德、求解脱）与'亲证'（anubhava）优先——经典只是地图，解脱必须由直接的自我认知达成。代价与极限：为保'吠陀权威'他不得不承认吠陀仪式在日常层面的有效性，使体系带上'两谛'的分层妥协；对幻的界定引出'个体如何无明、梵为何生幻'的诘难（后世'无明归属难题'）；三十二岁早逝使体系多有赖后世弟子（如筏遮塞波底）补全。他至今仍是'印度哲学'在国际语境中的默认面孔。

## Historical significance (EN)
Adi Shankara (c. 788-820), Indian philosopher and the great systematizer of Advaita Vedanta, is widely regarded as one of the most influential shapers of Hindu philosophical tradition. Five achievements anchor his standing. First, the program of non-dualism: Brahman alone is the unfailing reality and the Self (Atman) is Brahman—he forged the scattered Upanishadic sayings of 'Brahman-Atman identity' into a complete metaphysical system, explaining the manifold world through maya (appearance) and ignorance (avidya): 'non-dual yet not one, apparent yet not nothing.' Second, the legislative project of the prasthanatraya: he chose to comment on the Brahma-sutras, the ten principal Upanishads and the Bhagavad Gita—legislation in the dress of commentary—so that Vedanta displaced Mimamsa at the center of Brahminical doctrine, and every later Vedanta school (Ramanuja's qualified non-dualism, Madhva's dualism) had to answer Shankara first. Third, dialectical method: purvapaksha—the opponent's view first stated in its strongest form, then refuted—a craft that keeps his treatises the textbook of Indian philosophical disputation. Fourth, the institutional legacy: four paramount monasteries and the Dashanami order, turning philosophy into an organization eight centuries durable; the four Shankaracharyas remain among the highest dignities of Hinduism. Fifth, soteriological method: the fourfold qualifications (discrimination, dispassion, the six virtues, the desire for liberation) with the priority of direct experience (anubhava)—scripture is only a map; liberation must be achieved by direct self-knowledge. Costs and limits: to preserve Vedic authority he had to concede the validity of ritual at the everyday level, stratifying his system into 'two truths'; his account of maya invites the classic objection—whose is the ignorance, and why does Brahman generate appearance (the problem of the locus of avidya); his death at thirty-two left the system to be completed by successors such as Vacaspati Misra. He remains the default face of 'Indian philosophy' in the international context.

## Unique thinking
他思维方式的底色是'分辨为刃、遮诠为路：绝对不可定义，只能通过系统否定逼近；一切对立都是无明的投影'。六个标志性动作。其一，'不二还原法'——对任何经验对象连续追问'它是独立的实在吗'，把现象一层层还原到唯一不落的基础（梵），还原的终点不是'一个最大的东西'而是'一切分别的消融'。其二，'遮诠显真法'——对绝对只作否定性定义（'不是这，不是那'），凡可指认、可言说的都被否定，留下的沉默即是答案——避免把绝对降格为概念。其三，'幻相层级法'——把'实在'分为三级（错觉层/日常层/究竟层），同一物在不同层级有不同真值，冲突由'层级错位'消解，而非由一方压倒另一方。其四，'立敌对论法'——辩论前先把对手立场重构为最强形态（purvapaksha），再从其内部引出自毁——驳倒的必须是'最强的对手'，否则胜利无效。其五，'亲证检验法'——经典与推理都只是'指向的手指'，最终裁决在直接体验（anubhava）：'梵我同一'必须被亲证，而非被相信。其六，'目证自明法'——意识是自明自证的'见证'（sakshi）：一切认识需要认识者，而认识者本身不需要被认识，如同灯不自需照。代价与极限：遮诠方法的极致使积极主张稀缺，体系在'说了什么'上显得沉默；幻相层级在实践上易滑向'轻视世界'（他本人因此被质疑反伦理、反行动）；为吠陀权威保留的仪式层与非二元核心之间的张力从未彻底解决。

## Unique thinking (EN)
The bedrock of his thinking: discrimination as the blade, apophasis as the road—the absolute cannot be defined, only approached through systematic negation, and every opposition is a projection of ignorance. Six signature moves. First, non-dual reduction—of any experienced object, ask in succession 'is it an independent reality,' dissolving phenomena layer by layer into the one unfailing ground (Brahman); the terminus is not 'the largest thing' but the dissolution of all distinction. Second, apophatic definition—the absolute receives only negative predication ('not this, not that'); whatever can be pointed at or spoken is denied, and the silence left standing is the answer—keeping the absolute from degradation into a concept. Third, the hierarchy of reality—'real' comes in three grades (illusion, the everyday, the ultimate); the same object holds different truth-values at different levels, and conflict is dissolved as level-mismatch rather than settled by one side crushing the other. Fourth, the strongest-opponent method—before debate, reconstruct the adversary's position into its strongest form (purvapaksha), then draw self-destruction from within it; only the strongest opponent is worth refuting, else the victory is void. Fifth, experience-verification—scripture and inference are both 'fingers pointing'; final judgment lies in direct experience (anubhava): 'Brahman is the Self' must be realized, not believed. Sixth, witness-awareness—consciousness is the self-luminous witness (sakshi): every cognition requires a cognizer, and the cognizer itself needs no cognizing, as a lamp needs no lighting. Costs and limits: apophasis at its extreme leaves positive doctrine scarce, the system famously silent on 'what it affirms'; the hierarchy of reality in practice slides toward contempt for the world (he was charged with anti-ethics, anti-action); the tension between the ritual layer kept for Vedic authority and the non-dual core was never fully resolved.

## M-SHA-001 不二还原法 / Non-Dual Reduction Method

- Definition: 形上学追问方法论/对任何经验对象连续追问'它是独立的实在吗'，把现象一层层还原到唯一不落的基础——还原的终点不是'一个最大的东西'，而是一切分别的消融
- Definition (EN): Metaphysical-inquiry methodology/ask of any experienced object in succession 'is it an independent reality,' dissolving phenomena layer by layer into the one unfailing ground—the terminus is not 'the largest thing' but the dissolution of all distinction
- Source: 《梵经注》（Brahmasūtrabhāṣya）1.1 论梵之本性；《分辨宝鬘》'我不是身体'阶梯；《千部解脱论》第十八篇论意识不可还原
- Key quote: 凡是可见者皆非观者；一切还原止于那不能被还原的觉知。 / Whatever is seen is not the seer; all reduction ends at the awareness that cannot be reduced.
- Process:
  - 对对象提出还原问句：'它依什么而立？'——每答出一层依存，就把该层降格为'派生的'
  - 沿依存链继续下问，直到抵达一个'它依他而立'的回答不再成立的项——那项即候补的基础
  - 检验候补基础：它是否也是'被经验的对象'？若是，还原继续——直到只剩'经验本身/意识本身'
  - 区分'最高者'与'消融'：若结论仍是诸存在中的一个存在者，说明还原未完成——真正的终点是分别本身的消融
- Process (EN):
  - Put the reduction question to the object: 'on what does it stand?'—each answer names a dependence, demoting that layer to the derivative
  - Continue down the chain of dependence until reaching a term for which no answer of the form 'it stands on something else' holds—that term is the candidate ground
  - Test the candidate: is it also 'an experienced object'? If so, continue—until only experience itself, consciousness itself, remains
  - Distinguish 'highest being' from 'dissolution': if the conclusion is still one being among beings, the reduction is incomplete—the true terminus is the dissolution of distinction itself
- Representative cases:
  - 《梵经注》：对身体→感官→心识→空的连续还原，止于不可还原的意识——'多样是唯一基础上的表相'的总纲示范
  - 《分辨宝鬘》：'我不是身体/感官/心'的阶梯教学——把还原法教成可操作的日常修行程序
  - 与'第一因'论证的分岔：亚里士多德止于'不动的推动者'（最高存在者），商羯罗坚持还原到'存在本身'——同一方法、两种终点的对照
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The Brahma-sutra commentary: the chain body-senses-mind-space reduced to irreducible consciousness—the founding demonstration that the manifold is appearance on one ground
  - The Vivekacudamani: the ladder 'I am not the body/senses/mind'—the method taught as a workable daily procedure
  - The fork from the first-cause argument: Aristotle stops at the unmoved mover (a highest being), Shankara insists on reduction to Being itself—same method, two termini
- Modern applications:
  - 根本原因分析：对问题连续追问'它依什么而立'，不满足于找到'最大的原因'，而是找到'一切原因共同预设的那一项'（常是未言明的框架）
  - 产品定位：连续追问'这个卖点依什么成立'，把功能层层还原到用户无法再还原的那个'体验本身'——差异化不在功能堆叠而在基础层
  - 哲学/科学史阅读训练：读任何体系先问'它的还原止于何处、为何止于此'——终点选择往往决定整个体系的形状
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Root-cause analysis: keep asking 'on what does this stand?'—do not settle for the largest cause; find the item all causes jointly presuppose (often an unstated framework)
  - Product positioning: ask in succession 'on what does this selling point stand,' reducing features to the 'experience itself' the user cannot reduce further—differentiation lives at the ground layer, not the feature stack
  - Training in reading philosophy and history of science: of any system first ask 'where does its reduction stop, and why'—the choice of terminus usually decides the shape of the whole system

## M-SHA-002 梵我同一法 / Brahman-Atman Identity Method

- Definition: 终极命题方法论/'汝即彼'——把宇宙论的最高项（梵）与自我认知的最深处（阿特曼）判定为同一：不是隶属或相似，而是'我即梵'——一切救赎论与认识论的杠杆系于此等式
- Definition (EN): Ultimate-proposition methodology/'thou art that'—the judgment that the highest term of cosmology (Brahman) and the deepest stratum of self-knowledge (Atman) are identical: not subordination or resemblance but literal identity—every lever of soteriology and epistemology hangs on this equation
- Source: 《梵经注》1.1.4 论圣传句之义；《千部解脱论》第一、十八篇；《唱赞奥义书》6.8-16 '汝即彼'原本语境
- Key quote: 汝即彼——不是相似，不是隶属，是同一。 / Thou art that—not resemblance, not subordination, but identity.
- Process:
  - 找出待考察的最高命题（如'汝即彼'），先写下它的字面义并设问：若作弱化解读（相似/隶属），命题还有没有信息量？
  - 为命题两端各做语义分层：表层义（可描述、可指认的）与深层义（不可描述、只可指示的）
  - 检验等式只在深层成立：表层等式有反例即弃，深层等式须有经验样品（如深眠中的纯觉）佐证
  - 推演等式的后果：一旦同一成立，原目标（解脱/解决）从'获得/抵达'改写为'取消误认'——所有下游策略随之改变
- Process (EN):
  - Identify the highest proposition under review ('thou art that'); write down its literal sense and ask: under weakened readings (resemblance/subordination), does the proposition still carry information?
  - Stratify the semantics of both terms: a surface sense (describable, pointable-at) and a deep sense (indescribable, only indicated)
  - Test the equation at the depth alone: discard it if surface counterexamples exist; support the deep equation with an experiential specimen (such as the pure awareness of deep sleep)
  - Derive the consequences: once identity holds, the original goal (liberation/the solution) is rewritten from 'acquisition/arrival' to 'cancelling a misidentification'—all downstream strategy changes with it
- Representative cases:
  - 《梵经注》1.1.4：为使'汝即彼'有信息量，以 lakshana 间接指示义分层两词——语义分层法的经典立法
  - 《千部解脱论》：以深眠中的'觉知到无'为'我即梵'的日常样品——体验裁决的示范
  - 解脱观的改写：从不二论看，'解脱'不是死后抵达梵界（那是表层最高），而是当下取消'我是有身之我'的误认——同一命题重写全部救赎策略
- Representative cases (EN):
  - Brahma-sutra commentary 1.1.4: to make 'thou art that' informative, both terms are stratified by lakshana, the indicated meaning—the founding legislation of semantic stratification
  - The Upadesasahasri: the 'awareness of nothing' in deep sleep as the daily specimen of 'I am Brahman'—the demonstration of experience-arbitration
  - The rewrite of liberation: on non-dualism, liberation is not arriving at the world of Brahman after death (the surface maximum) but cancelling here and now the misidentification 'I am this embodied self'—one proposition rewriting the whole soteriological strategy
- Modern applications:
  - 谈判/整合中的'等式检验'：当双方提出各自最高命题（'我们的方案就是答案'），先做语义分层——表层冲突是否掩盖深层同一（共同的真实利益）
  - 战略定位：把组织的最高使命与成员的最深动机做同一性检验——'你做的事就是你这个人'的等式成立时，招聘与留存策略全部改写
  - 概念辨析训练：遇到'X即Y'式最高命题（'数据即资产''用户即上帝'），先问'作弱化解读还有无信息量'，再做两端分层检验
- Modern applications (EN):
  - The equation test in negotiation and integration: when each side offers its highest proposition ('our plan is the answer'), stratify the semantics first—does surface conflict mask deep identity (the shared real interest)?
  - Strategic positioning: test the identity between an organization's highest mission and its members' deepest motivation—when 'what you do is who you are' holds, recruiting and retention strategy rewrite entirely
  - Training in conceptual analysis: on 'X is Y' ultimate propositions ('data is the asset,' 'the user is god'), first ask whether the weakened reading still carries information, then stratify both terms
- Related modes: M-SHA-001（不二还原法——同一命题的论证引擎：还原到意识不可还原处，'我'与'梵'的等式才有支点）; M-SHA-003（幻相层级法——等式的适用层控制：'我即梵'只在究竟谛成立，在世俗谛须按俗谛规则行动）; M-AUG-001（奥古斯丁·内在光照法——远缘对照：奥古斯丁向内还原找到'光照之上帝'，商羯罗向内还原找到'即梵之我'——同一方向、两种终点命名）; M-ZZ-001（张载·气本论——远缘对照：张载以'太虚即气'立一元，商羯罗以'梵即我'立一元——同为'最高项与日常项同一'的形上学立法）
- Representative figures:
  - 商羯罗 / Adi Shankara: 本模式的原型——把'汝即彼'从圣传句锻造成论辩与救赎的总杠杆 / the prototype—who forged 'thou art that' from a scriptural saying into the master lever of dialectic and soteriology
  - 乔荼波陀 / Gaudapada: 先师——以'醒梦眠三相'分析先行为同一命题提供了现象学材料 / the precursor—whose analysis of waking, dream and deep sleep supplied the phenomenological material for the identity thesis
  - 耶若婆佉 / Yajnavalkya: 文本源头——《大森林奥义书》中'不见者见一切'的论主，'梵我同一'最早的论证者 / the textual source—the disputant of the Brihadaranyaka Upanishad ('the unseen seer of all'), the earliest arguer for Brahman-Atman identity

## M-SHA-003 幻相层级法 / Hierarchy-of-Reality Method

- Definition: 真理分级方法论/把'实在'分为错觉层、日常层、究竟层三级——同一物在不同层级有不同真值，一切表面冲突先检查'层级错位'再裁决，不由一方压倒另一方
- Definition (EN): Graded-truth methodology/'real' in three grades—illusory, everyday, ultimate—the same object holds different truth-values at different levels; every surface conflict is first checked for level-mismatch before adjudication
- Source: 《梵经注》2.1 论幻与两谛；《薄伽梵歌注》论'知究竟者仍履行职分'；绳蛇喻见《分辨宝鬘》及诸注
- Key quote: 蛇从未存在，绳从未消失——错在层级，不在对象。 / The snake never existed, the rope never vanished—the error is in the level, not the object.
- Process:
  - 遇到冲突命题时，先为每一方标注'它在本层级是否有效'——多数冲突是层级错位而非事实错误
  - 建立层级清单：本问题的错觉层（误认）、日常层（共同有效规则）、究竟层（不落的基础）各是什么
  - 执行层级纪律：高层真理不取消低层义务，低层经验不越级否证高层——跨界陈述一律标记为无效
  - 按层级分别处理：错觉层做纠正（点灯），日常层做优化（守规则），究竟层做认出——三种操作不可混用
- Process (EN):
  - On conflicting propositions, first mark for each 'is it valid at its level'—most conflicts are level-mismatch, not factual error
  - Build the level inventory: what are the illusory layer (misidentification), the everyday layer (shared working rules), the ultimate layer (the unfailing ground) of this question
  - Enforce level-discipline: higher truth does not cancel lower obligation; lower experience does not overstep to refute the higher—mark all cross-level statements void
  - Handle by level: correction at the illusory (light the lamp), optimization at the everyday (keep the rules), recognition at the ultimate—never mixing the three operations
- Representative cases:
  - 绳蛇喻：蛇在错觉层真实（逃跑合理）、在证知后从未存在、绳在两层皆在——三级实在的完整标本
  - 《薄伽梵歌注》：商羯罗坚持'知究竟者仍须履行种姓职分'——层级纪律防止'一切皆幻故无须行动'的虚无主义出口
  - 对'我看见多样'的抗辩：'看见'的效力只限日常层，故不能越级证伪'梵是唯一'——不越级否证的论辩示范
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The rope-snake: the snake real at the illusory level (flight justified), never existent after knowledge, the rope present at both levels—the complete specimen of graded reality
  - The Gita commentary: Shankara insists the knower of the ultimate still performs his station's duty—level-discipline closing the nihilist exit 'all is illusion, no action needed'
  - The defense against 'I see multiplicity': 'seeing' is valid only at the everyday level, hence cannot overstep to falsify 'Brahman alone'—the dialectical demonstration of no cross-level refutation
- Modern applications:
  - 工程/科学的模型分层：把'地图不是疆域'操作化——物理模型、工程近似、日常直觉各有真值域，冲突先查层级再裁决（牛顿力学在低速层不被相对论'证伪'而是被'定位'）
  - 组织沟通：把'愿景层真理'与'运营层规则'分级管理——愿景不取消流程义务，季度数据不越级否证使命
  - 心理辅导：把'想法'与'事实'与'元认知'分三级——错觉层的灾难化想法做纠正，不做真伪辩论
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Model layering in engineering and science: operationalizing 'the map is not the territory'—physical models, engineering approximations and everyday intuitions each hold a truth-domain; on conflict, check the level before adjudicating (Newtonian mechanics at low speed is 'located' by relativity, not 'falsified')
  - Organizational communication: manage 'vision-level truth' and 'operations-level rules' as graded—vision does not cancel process obligations; quarterly data does not overstep to refute the mission
  - Psychotherapy: grade 'thoughts,' 'facts' and 'metacognition'—catastrophizing at the illusory level is corrected, not debated for truth
- Related modes: M-SHA-001（不二还原法——层级体系的究竟层正是还原的终点：唯一不落的基础）; M-SHA-002（梵我同一法——同一等式只在究竟层成立，层级法为它划定适用域）; M-KD-001（康德·二律背反法——远缘对照：康德把矛盾定位为'理性越界'，商羯罗定位为'层级错位'——同为'以层次消解矛盾'的结构）; M-HAY-003（哈耶克·两种知识法——远缘对照：哈耶克分'建构理性'与'演化规则'两个知识层，层级互不僭越——世俗版的层级纪律）
- Representative figures:
  - 商羯罗 / Adi Shankara: 本模式的原型——把三级实在与层级纪律立法为不二论的'和平条款' / the prototype—who legislated graded reality and level-discipline as the peace terms of non-dualism
  - 龙树 / Nagarjuna: 方法的先师——'二谛'（世俗谛/胜义谛）之分是层级法的佛教原型，商羯罗将其细化为三级 / the precursor of the method—the Buddhist 'two truths' (conventional/ultimate) is the prototype Shankara refined into three grades
  - 康德 / Immanuel Kant: 平行对照——以现象/物自体分层消解理性矛盾，与绳蛇喻共享'错在层级不在对象'的结构 / the parallel—dissolving rational antinomies by the phenomenon/thing-in-itself division, sharing the rope-snake structure 'the error is in the level, not the object'

## M-SHA-004 立敌对论法 / Strongest-Opponent Method

- Definition: 论辩方法论/辩论前先把对手立场重构为最强形态（purvapaksha）——以对手可能给出的最佳论证陈述其立场，再从其内部引出自毁；驳倒的必须是'最强的对手'，否则胜利无效
- Definition (EN): Dialectical methodology/before debate, reconstruct the adversary's position into its strongest form (purvapaksha)—stating it with the opponent's best arguments, then drawing self-destruction from within; only the strongest opponent is worth refuting
- Source: 《梵经注》1.1 起对弥曼差与佛教诸派的 purvapaksha 章节设计；《千部解脱论》论论辩次第；印度经院论辩（śāstrārtha）传统规范
- Key quote: 未曾把敌论立到最强者，不配自称驳倒了它。 / Whoever has not stated the opponent's view at its strongest may not claim to have refuted it.
- Process:
  - 写下对手立场的'最强版本'：前提、论证链、对已知反驳的预答——以'对手派读者会点头'为完成标准
  - 在最强版本内部找张力：该立场为解释 A 现象付出的代价，是否恰好使它无法解释 B 现象
  - 从内部构造二难：沿对手自己的前提推演，终点 either 自毁 either 须引入与其核心矛盾的新前提
  - 应答（siddhanta）只做两件事：指认自毁点 + 陈述自己的立场如何吸收对手所解释的一切——不额外嘲讽
- Process (EN):
  - Write the strongest version of the opponent's position: premises, argument chain, anticipated answers to known objections—complete when the opponent's own readers would nod
  - Find the tension inside the strongest version: does the price the position pays to explain phenomenon A exactly disable it from explaining phenomenon B?
  - Build the dilemma from within: following the opponent's own premises to a terminus that either self-destructs or must import a premise contradicting its core
  - The siddhanta does two things only: point at the self-destruction, and state how one's own position absorbs everything the opponent explained—no added mockery
- Representative cases:
  - 《梵经注》对唯识的 purvapaksha：把'唯识无境'立到能解释梦与幻觉，再以'记忆锚点''识之对象结构'从内部击穿——最强对手法的教科书示范
  - 对弥曼差：先把'仪式自足、无须梵'立成完整体系（弥曼差确是当时主流），再以'有效性从何而来'追击——从承认对手主流地位开始
  - 传说中的曼陀罗弥湿罗之辩：商羯罗先与对手之妻辩'家政法'以补齐自身知识的短板，再进入义理主战场——'知彼'纪律的极端示范
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The purvapaksha of Yogacara in the Brahma-sutra commentary: 'consciousness-only' stated strong enough to explain dream and illusion, then pierced from within by the anchors of memory and the object-structure of cognition—the textbook demonstration
  - Against Mimamsa: 'ritual suffices, no Brahman needed' first stated as a complete system (Mimamsa being the then-dominant school), then pursued with 'whence the validity?'—beginning by granting the opponent's dominance
  - The legendary debate with Mandana Misra: Shankara first debated the opponent's wife on 'household conduct' to close the gaps in his own knowledge before entering the doctrinal main contest—the extreme discipline of 'knowing the other'
- Modern applications:
  - 评审/辩论准备：写'对方最强辩护词'并请对方阵营的人审校后再设计反驳——驳稻草人不产生知识
  - 竞争分析：把竞品战略重构为'若我是对方，这是我会做的最优解'，再找其结构性代价——低估对手的战略必然失败
  - 学术综述写作：每一批评前先以对手的最佳形态复述其观点，'钢人'标准替代'稻草人'标准
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Review and debate preparation: draft the opponent's strongest defense, have their camp audit it, then design the rebuttal—refuting a straw man produces no knowledge
  - Competitive analysis: reconstruct the rival's strategy as 'the optimal move were I them,' then locate its structural cost—strategy built on underestimating opponents fails
  - Academic review writing: restate each view in its best form before criticizing—the steel-man standard replacing the straw man
- Related modes: M-SHA-001（不二还原法——自宗义的内核：驳倒众说后立起的'唯一不落之基'）; M-SHA-005（遮诠显真法——自宗义对绝对只作否定陈述，与立敌对论形成'强破弱立'的组合）; M-KIE-007（克尔凯郭尔·反讽清场法——远缘对照：克氏以反讽'清场'旧立场再立新，商羯罗以最强重构引出自毁——同为'先穷尽对手再翻盘'的论辩工艺）; M-VLL-003（略萨·对抗引擎法——跨域对照：略萨要求叙事先找到'敌手'，商羯罗要求论辩先造出'最强敌手'——同为'以对手定义自身'）
- Representative figures:
  - 商羯罗 / Adi Shankara: 本模式的原型——把 purvapaksha 从论辩礼节提升为'重构即信誉'的写作纪律 / the prototype—who elevated purvapaksha from a courtesy of debate into the writing discipline 'reconstruction is credit'
  - 陈那 / Dignaga: 技术环境——佛教因明学为整个论辩传统提供了'立敌共许'的形式规范 / the technical milieu—Buddhist logic supplied the formal norms of 'shared premises' for the whole disputation tradition
  - 穆勒 / John Stuart Mill: 平行对照——'任何反驳若非对对手论证最强形态的反驳即无价值'，与 purvapaksha 纪律几乎逐字相同 / the parallel—'a refutation of the weakest form of an argument is worthless' matches the purvapaksha discipline almost verbatim

## M-SHA-005 遮诠显真法 / Apophatic-Definition Method

- Definition: 极限概念方法论/对不可定义者只作否定性定义——凡可指认、可言说、可对象化的都被系统否定，留下的沉默即是答案：以排除法逼近绝对，避免把绝对降格为诸概念中的一个
- Definition (EN): Methodology for limit-concepts/the undefinable receives only negative definition—whatever can be pointed at or spoken is systematically denied, the silence left standing is the answer: approaching the absolute by elimination, keeping it from becoming one concept among concepts
- Source: 《大森林奥义书》3.9.26 'neti neti' 及商羯罗注；《梵经注》1.1.14-20 论梵之不可描述性；《千部解脱论》论名言与真实之分界
- Key quote: 不是这，不是那——凡被指出的都不是；未指出的，即是。 / Not this, not that—whatever is pointed at is not it; what is never pointed at, that it is.
- Process:
  - 确认对象属于'极限概念'（不可定义、不可指认、一经定义即失真）——此类对象禁用肯定定义
  - 开列'误认候选清单'：一切可能被当作答案的常见概念（在此传统：创世神、能量、空、时间……）
  - 逐项否定并说明'为何它不是'：每项否定须给出理由，而非空喊——排除法是论证不是修辞
  - 指向残余：排除完毕后以'那不可排除者'指路，并声明一切言说到此为止——沉默是方法的完成而非失败
- Process (EN):
  - Confirm the object is a limit-concept (undefinable, unpointable-at, falsified by any definition)—affirmative definition is banned for it
  - Draw up the list of candidates for misidentification: every common concept that might be mistaken for the answer (in this tradition: creator-god, energy, space, time...)
  - Negate item by item with reasons: each negation must argue why it is not—elimination is argument, not rhetoric
  - Point at the remainder: with exclusions complete, indicate 'what cannot be excluded' and declare all speech ended—the silence is the method's completion, not its failure
- Representative cases:
  - 《梵经注》：对'梵'逐项否定（不粗不细不短不长……）——遮诠法的总纲示范，每一次否定排除一个误认候选
  - 《大森林奥义书》'neti neti' 注：把'不是这，不是那'从神秘短语提升为认识路径——排除本身即'显影'
  - 与否定神学的平行：迈蒙尼德规定'上帝只有否定属性'，与商羯罗'梵只可否定地言说'构成跨文明方法族——各自独立立法、同一逻辑
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The Brahma-sutra commentary: Brahman denied item by item (not gross, not subtle, not short, not long...)—the master demonstration, each negation excluding one candidate for misidentification
  - The commentary on 'neti neti': a mystical phrase raised into a path of knowing—exclusion itself as 'developing' the real
  - The parallel with negative theology: Maimonides' 'God has only negative attributes' beside Shankara's 'Brahman is spoken only negatively'—an independent, cross-civilizational legislation of the same logic
- Modern applications:
  - 核心概念提炼：对'我们的核心价值观是什么'先做排除法（列出一切'不是'），剩下的空间往往比正面口号更精确
  - 复杂系统描述：对不可直接观测的变量（如'组织文化''市场情绪'），用'排除了所有可测解释后残余的方向'来指示——负空间法
  - 产品差异化：不说'我们是什么'，逐项说'我们不是什么'——排除法定位在嘈杂市场中辨识度更高
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Core-concept extraction: for 'what are our values,' run elimination first (list everything we are not)—the remaining space is often more precise than any positive slogan
  - Describing complex systems: for unobservable variables ('organizational culture,' 'market sentiment'), indicate by 'the direction remaining after all measurable explanations are excluded'—the negative-space method
  - Product differentiation: instead of saying what you are, say item by item what you are not—eliminative positioning cuts through noisy markets
- Related modes: M-SHA-001（不二还原法——遮诠是还原最后一公里的言说方式：终点不可肯定，只可否定地逼近）; M-SHA-006（亲证检验法——否定的终点须由体验裁决，否则排除法悬空）; M-MON-002（孟德斯鸠·否证定义法——远缘对照：孟德斯鸠以'自由不是什么'定义自由，与遮诠共享'以排除显义'的结构）; M-LZ-001（老子·道不可道法——远缘对照：'道可道非常道'与 neti neti 同为'极限概念禁用肯定言说'的最早立法，一东一西）
- Representative figures:
  - 商羯罗 / Adi Shankara: 本模式的原型——把 neti neti 从神秘短语立法为'排除即显影'的认识路径 / the prototype—who legislated neti neti from a mystical phrase into the epistemic path 'exclusion as developing'
  - 托名狄奥尼修斯 / Pseudo-Dionysius: 平行立法者——否定神学的西方源头：上帝只能以否定谓词言说 / the parallel legislator—the Western source of negative theology: God spoken only through denied predicates
  - 迈蒙尼德 / Maimonides: 平行立法者——'否定的属性'：肯定谓词加于上帝即歪曲，与商羯罗逻辑逐点相同 / the parallel legislator—'negative attributes': affirmative predicates distort God, point-for-point the logic of Shankara

## M-SHA-006 亲证检验法 / Experience-Verification Method

- Definition: 认识裁决方法论/经典与推理都只是'指向的手指'，最终裁决在直接体验——'梵我同一'必须被亲证而非被相信；未经体验核验的结论暂记为假设
- Definition (EN): Epistemic-adjudication methodology/scripture and inference are 'fingers pointing'; final judgment lies in direct experience—'Brahman is the Self' must be realized, not believed; conclusions unverified by experience are held as hypotheses
- Source: 《千部解脱论》闻思修三阶；本集第一篇论导师与亲证；《梵经注》论'亲证为最终裁决'
- Key quote: 经典指出方向，推理清除疑云，唯有体验完成认识。 / Scripture points the way, inference clears the doubts, experience alone completes the knowing.
- Process:
  - 为待检验命题安排三阶：先闻（吸收最权威的表述），再思（以推理正面与反面检验、清除全部疑点），后修（设体验程序使其成为直接认识）
  - 把一切未亲证的结论标记为'假设'——包括自己最珍视的信念；假设不驱动最终行动
  - 设计'可重复的体验程序'（冥想、自省、特定情境下的观察），使结论可以在自身经验中被再次检验
  - 以'与既有可靠证词不冲突'过滤体验报告：新体验刷新旧证词的资格，以其与整体验证链的相容性为条件
- Process (EN):
  - Arrange three stages for the proposition under test: hearing (absorb the most authoritative statement), reflection (test it by inference both ways, clear all doubts), meditation (design a practice making it direct knowing)
  - Mark every unrealized conclusion as 'hypothesis'—including one's most cherished beliefs; hypotheses do not drive final action
  - Design repeatable experiential procedures (meditation, self-examination, observation in specified situations) so the conclusion can be re-tested within one's own experience
  - Filter experience-reports by non-contradiction with the reliable testimony already held: new experience earns the right to refresh old testimony only through coherence with the whole chain of verification
- Representative cases:
  - 《千部解脱论》：闻-思-修三阶的完整立法——'推理不通就信是迷信，推理通了不修是空谈'的次序纪律
  - 《梵经注》的认识论：圣传句被定性为'最初亲证者的证词'——权威来源于体验，故体验有权刷新权威
  - 师徒传承制度：导师提供'实验设计'、弟子'重做实验'——亲证检验法被制度化为八百年不辍的传承系统
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The Upadesasahasri: the full legislation of hearing-reflection-meditation—'believing without clarity is superstition; clarity without practice is empty talk'
  - The epistemology of the Brahma-sutra commentary: the great sayings defined as 'testimony of the first realizers'—authority sourced in experience, hence refreshable by experience
  - The guru-disciple institution: the teacher supplies the experimental design, the disciple redoes the experiment—experience-verification institutionalized as an eight-century lineage
- Modern applications:
  - 个人学习系统：对任何重要主张执行'闻思修'——吸收最强表述、正反推理清疑、设计可重复的个人验证程序，未完成三阶前标记为假设
  - 科学素养：把'权威文献=证人证词'——尊重但可刷新；自己的重复实验是认识的完成动作，不是可选项
  - 管理决策：先高层听（吸收内外部最强观点）、再推理（红队检验）、再试点（组织层面的'亲证'），三阶不齐不全员推开
- Modern applications (EN):
  - A personal learning system: run hearing-reflection-practice on any major claim—absorb the strongest statement, test by inference both ways, design a repeatable personal verification; before all three, mark it hypothesis
  - Scientific literacy: treat authoritative literature as witness-testimony—respected but refreshable; one's own replication is the completing act of knowing, not optional
  - Management decisions: first absorb the strongest internal and external views, then red-team the reasoning, then pilot (the organization's 'realization'); never roll out before all three stages
- Related modes: M-SHA-001（不二还原法——亲证的对象正是还原的终点：意识自身）; M-SHA-004（立敌对论法——推理阶（思）的执行工艺：最强对手法属于'思'的阶段）; M-HZX-005（黄宗羲·史学方法论——远缘对照：黄宗羲'文献互证+实地证'与商羯罗'经典+亲证'共享'证据分阶、亲历裁决'的结构）; M-DAR-001（达尔文·观察累积法——远缘对照：达尔文以长期观察裁决假说，商羯罗以长期修证裁决圣传句——同为'假设必须过体验关'）
- Representative figures:
  - 商羯罗 / Adi Shankara: 本模式的原型——把'亲证'立法为认识链的最终裁决者，使哲学具有'可重复实验'的结构 / the prototype—who legislated realization as final adjudicator, giving philosophy the structure of repeatable experiment
  - 乔荼波陀 / Gaudapada: 先师——'醒梦眠'三相分析为亲证提供了可自检的现象学清单 / the precursor—whose three states (waking, dream, sleep) supplied a self-checkable phenomenological checklist for realization
  - 威廉·詹姆斯 / William James: 平行对照——'宗教经验之种类'以个人体验为第一手数据，与亲证裁决共享'体验是认识的原始数据'立场 / the parallel—The Varieties of Religious Experience treating personal experience as primary data, sharing the stance 'experience is the raw material of knowing'

## M-SHA-007 四备阶梯法 / Fourfold-Qualification Method

- Definition: 修行/训练设计方法论/把'觉悟'拆成四道前置资格与三阶训练——任何高阶转化先核查'备件齐不齐'，备件不齐者不接受高阶训练；'顿悟'的即时性建立在备件的长期完备之上
- Definition (EN): Methodology of training design/'awakening' decomposed into four prior qualifications and three stages—any higher transformation first checks prerequisite completeness; the unqualified are not admitted to higher training; the instantaneity of 'sudden awakening' rests on long-completed readiness
- Source: 《分辨宝鬘》18-30 颂（四备清单）；《千部解脱论》闻思修三阶；四大道场的师徒授受制度
- Key quote: 最后一根火柴是即时的，但柴必须早已干燥。 / The final match is instant, but the wood must long since be dry.
- Process:
  - 把目标拆解为'不可跳级的高阶转化'，明确它的真正前置条件（不是名义门槛，而是缺了必失败的备件）
  - 为每道备件设计可自查的判据：分辨（能否说清常与无常）、离欲（对结果是否仍有贪着）……逐项核验而非总体印象
  - 建立分层授受：备件齐者进入高阶，不齐者回底层训练并指定补件任务——高阶教导不发给未备者
  - 把'突破'重新归因：任何'顿悟'发生时，先盘点是哪些长期备件使它可能——把奇迹改写为工程
- Process (EN):
  - Decompose the goal into an unskippable higher transformation and identify its true prerequisites—not nominal gates but components without which failure is certain
  - Design self-auditable criteria per qualification: discrimination (can one state the permanent vs impermanent), dispassion (does craving for outcomes remain)—verify item by item, not by overall impression
  - Institute stratified transmission: the qualified enter higher training; the unqualified return to foundational work with assigned remediation—higher instruction is not issued to the unprepared
  - Reattribute breakthroughs: whenever a 'sudden awakening' occurs, first inventory which long-built components made it possible—rewriting miracle as engineering
- Representative cases:
  - 《分辨宝鬘》：四备清单作为'招生标准'逐项可查——求道者先自查再申请，导师先核验再授法
  - 闻思修的次序纪律：思未通过不授修，修未成不称悟——三级之间同样执行备件核查
  - 柴火论对'顿悟'的重释：传说中商羯罗本人早年即悟，但其师承仍要求完整训练——'即时性以完备性为前提'的立法样本
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The Vivekacudamani: the fourfold list as an auditable admissions standard—the seeker self-audits before applying, the teacher verifies before instructing
  - The order-discipline of hearing-reflection-meditation: no meditation instruction before reflection passes, no claim of awakening before meditation matures—prerequisite checks between every stage
  - The dry-wood rereading of 'sudden awakening': the tradition holds Shankara realized early, yet his lineage still required full training—'instantaneity premised on completeness' legislated
- Modern applications:
  - 人才培养：把关键岗位能力拆成可核查的备件清单（基础技能/判断力/心理稳定性），备件不齐不授高阶职责——晋升标准从'年限'改为'备件'
  - 运动员训练：力量与技术分层——专项技术（高阶转化）只发给基础力量达标者，避免'用技术代偿力量'的伤病
  - 变革管理：组织'顿悟式转型'前先盘点四备（认知分辨力/对现状的离欲/协作纪律/求变渴求），备件不齐先补件再转型
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Talent development: decompose critical-role capability into auditable components (foundational skill, judgment, psychological stability); no higher responsibility until the components clear—promotion by 'prerequisites,' not tenure
  - Athletic training: strength and technique layered—sport-specific skill (the higher transformation) issued only to those whose strength base clears the bar, preventing injury from 'technique compensating for strength'
  - Change management: before an organization's 'epiphany transformation,' audit the fourfold readiness (cognitive discrimination, dispassion about the status quo, collaborative discipline, thirst for change); remediate first, transform second
- Related modes: M-SHA-006（亲证检验法——三阶训练的内容：四备是三阶的准入，三阶是亲证的通道）; M-SHA-002（梵我同一法——阶梯的终点：'备件工程'服务的唯一目标是那一等式的亲证）; M-ZZJ-003（张载/诸家·工夫论类模式——远缘对照：宋明理学'变化气质先于穷理'与四备阶梯同构——'先修人再授学'的东亚平行）; M-MIY-001（宫崎骏·积累精进法——跨域对照：宫崎'画一万张才谈风格'与'柴必须早已干燥'——同为'即时性以完备性为前提'）
- Representative figures:
  - 商羯罗 / Adi Shankara: 本模式的原型——把'觉悟'从奇迹改写为'备件工程+最后一环' / the prototype—who rewrote awakening from miracle into 'prerequisite engineering plus the final link'
  - 牧尊 / Govinda Bhagavatpada: 制度对手方——作为导师对少年商羯罗执行了完整的备件核验与授受，是阶梯的'执行侧'示范 / the institutional counterparty—as teacher he ran the full qualification audit and transmission on the young Shankara, the 'execution side' of the ladder
  - 朱熹 / Zhu Xi: 平行立法者——'格物致知、循序渐进'的为学次第与四备阶梯同为'不可跳级'的东亚立法 / the parallel legislator—the sequence of 'investigating things, extending knowledge, advancing step by step' as the East Asian legislation of the same unskippable ladder

## M-SHA-008 注疏立法法 / Commentary-as-Legislation Method

- Definition: 知识创业方法论/借注疏权威文本的外衣行新学派立法之实——注解的选择性强调（字面义/指示义、入选/落选、字面/训导）重塑传统本身——经典被'继承'的时刻正是被'改写'的时刻
- Definition (EN): Knowledge-venture methodology/legislating a new school under the dress of commentary—the selective emphasis of the gloss (literal vs indicated meaning, inclusion vs exclusion, doctrine vs pedagogy) reshapes the tradition itself—the moment a classic is 'inherited' is the moment it is 'rewritten'
- Source: '三基'（prasthana-traya）的选定与注疏体例；《奥义书》'主要名单'的建构；《薄伽梵歌注》对'训导性段落'的处理
- Key quote: 我不立新经——我为旧经作注；而传统在我注后已换了一副骨骼。 / I write no new scripture—I comment on the old; yet the tradition after my gloss has a different skeleton.
- Process:
  - 盘点领域内的'权威文本'：确认哪些文本有无需自证的合法性，以及主流解释目前归于哪个阵营
  - 选定'注疏根据地'（对应三基）：数目克制、层级分明（经/疏/论），并为每一部设计它在新体系中承担的功能
  - 以注解行使解释权：区分字面义与指示义、正典与训导、核心与边缘——每一次区分都朝新总纲倾斜
  - 让'继承'产生'改写'：注成之后，旧文本的引用权重、教学顺序、学派光谱都已被重新排列——新学派以注家身份出生
- Process (EN):
  - Inventory the field's authoritative texts: which carry legitimacy requiring no proof, and which camp the mainstream interpretation currently serves
  - Select the commentarial base camps (the analog of the three foundations): few in number, clearly ranked (root text/gloss/treatise), each assigned its function in the new system
  - Exercise interpretive right through the gloss: separate literal from indicated meaning, canon from pedagogy, core from margin—each separation tilting toward the new program
  - Let inheritance produce rewriting: once the gloss is done, citation weights, teaching order and the spectrum of schools are rearranged—the new school born wearing the commentator's gown
- Representative cases:
  - 三基的选定与注疏：《梵经》《薄伽梵歌》、十部主要《奥义书》——'主要名单'本身就是立法（落选的奥义书从此边缘化）
  - 对'仪式段落'的处理：将吠陀仪式性经文解释为'为初级者设的训导'——以注解把弥曼差的核心降格为'入门教材'，完成义学中心的转移
  - 历史上反复出现的同型工程：孔子'述而不作'以删述六经立儒学；海德格尔以康德书重建现象学——注疏是变革者最安全的战场
- Representative cases (EN):
  - Selecting and glossing the three foundations: the Brahma-sutras, the Gita, the ten 'principal' Upanishads—the very list of the principal is legislation (the excluded Upanishads marginalized ever after)
  - Handling the ritual passages: Vedic ritual texts glossed as 'instruction for beginners'—the Mimamsa core demoted to introductory material by commentary alone, the doctrinal center displaced
  - The recurring type across history: Confucius 'transmitting, not creating' founding Confucianism through editing the classics; Heidegger rebuilding phenomenology through a book on Kant—commentary as the reformer's safest battlefield
- Modern applications:
  - 行业标准策略：新公司不为空白领域另立标准，而是'深度参与'现有权威标准的修订——以'解释与补充'实现事实立法
  - 组织变革：改革者以'重新解读创始人文本/公司宪法'推行新战略——托古改制比另起炉灶的阻力低一个量级
  - 学术切入点选择：博士论文选主流经典的'再解释'而非无人问津的新题目——权威文本的注疏自带读者与合法性
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Standards strategy: rather than founding a standard in a blank field, a newcomer 'participates deeply' in revising the authoritative existing one—de facto legislation through 'interpretation and supplement'
  - Organizational change: reformers push new strategy by reinterpreting the founder's texts or the company constitution—reform under the ancients meets an order of magnitude less resistance than building anew
  - Choosing an academic entry point: a dissertation reinterpreting a canonical text rather than a neglected novelty—commentary on an authority brings readers and legitimacy with it
- Related modes: M-SHA-003（幻相层级法——'训导性段落'的解释技术正是层级法在文本层的应用：把低阶内容安放进低阶层）; M-SHA-004（立敌对论法——注疏的内部结构：purvapaksha 章节本身是注疏体的论辩骨架）; M-KIE-009（克尔凯郭尔·假名分层法——同型对照：克氏以假名分层出版立'主观真理'新学，商羯罗以注疏分层立'不二'新学——同为'借文体外衣行立法'）; M-BORG-005（博尔赫斯·伪造考据法——反向对照：博尔赫斯伪造'注'托起虚构，商羯罗真注权威托起新学——同为'注体'的战略使用，一虚一实）
- Representative figures:
  - 商羯罗 / Adi Shankara: 本模式的原型——以注家身份成为新传统的创始人，'注疏立法'的最高完成度 / the prototype—founder of a new tradition wearing the commentator's gown, the highest completion of commentary-as-legislation
  - 孔子 / Confucius: 同型立法者——'述而不作'：以删述六经完成儒学立法，与商羯罗共享托古战略 / the same-type legislator—'transmitting, not creating': Confucianism legislated through editing the classics, sharing Shankara's strategy of reform under the ancients
  - 罗摩奴阇 / Ramanuja: 方法的对手方——同样以注疏还注疏，逐条'再注解'商羯罗的强调——注疏立法法的自我反击样本 / the method's adversary—commentary answered with commentary, re-glossing Shankara's emphases line by line—the craft's self-counterattack

## M-SHA-009 目证自明法 / Witness-Awareness Method

- Definition: 意识哲学方法论/把意识定位为自明自证的'见证'——一切认识需要认识者，而认识者本身不需要被认识（灯不自需照）；凡被观察者皆非观察者，沿'见证线'从任何经验回溯到不可被经验的不落之点
- Definition (EN): Philosophy-of-consciousness methodology/consciousness as the self-luminous 'witness'—every cognition requires a cognizer, and the cognizer itself needs no cognizing (the lamp needs no lighting); whatever is observed is not the observer; along the witness line any experience traces back to the unfailing point that cannot be experienced
- Source: 《梵经注》1.1.4 及 2.3 论'见证'（sakshi）；《千部解脱论》论意识自明（svaprakasha）；《大森林奥义书》'不见者见一切'之注
- Key quote: 灯照明万物而不需另一盏灯照亮自己；觉知亦然。 / The lamp lights all things without another lamp to light it; so too awareness.
- Process:
  - 对当前任一经验内容（念头、情绪、感受）执行'分离律'：把它放入'被观察'一侧，追问'谁在观察'——它不是观察者
  - 沿'见证线'回撤：新出现的'观察者'（如'我在觉察'的念头）同样落入被观察侧——继续回撤，直到一个不再出现在清单上的项
  - 以'灯喻'检验自明性：该最终项是否还需被认识？若需，则继续倒退；不需，则它是自明者——认识链的截止点
  - 做三相检验（醒/梦/深眠）：检查'觉知'是否在三种状态报告中都预设地出现——贯通者即见证，见证即'我'的深层
- Process (EN):
  - Run the split on any current content (thought, emotion, sensation): place it on the 'observed' side and ask 'who observes'—it is not the observer
  - Withdraw along the witness line: the newly surfaced 'observer' (say, the thought 'I am aware') likewise falls on the observed side—continue until a term no longer appears on the list
  - Test self-luminosity with the lamp image: does this final term itself need to be known? If yes, the regress continues; if no, it is the self-luminous—where the chain of knowing stops
  - Run the three-state test (waking/dream/deep sleep): check whether 'awareness' is presupposed in all three state-reports—what runs through them is the witness, and the witness is the deep 'I'
- Representative cases:
  - 《梵经注》'见证'章：把意识从诸经验中'提走'——凡被知觉者皆非知觉本身——见证定位法的总纲
  - 深眠论证：'我睡得很香'的报告预设深眠中仍有'觉知到无'者——以日常经验为三相贯通提供样品
  - 二十世纪的平行物：胡塞尔'一切被对象化的都不是先验自我'——现象学反思剩余与 sakshi 定位的同构
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The 'witness' chapters of the Brahma-sutra commentary: consciousness lifted out from among experiences—whatever is perceived is not perception itself—the master statement of witness-location
  - The deep-sleep argument: the report 'I slept soundly' presupposes one who 'knew the nothing' through sleep—daily experience supplying the specimen of witness through three states
  - The twentieth-century parallel: Husserl's 'whatever is objectified is not the transcendental ego'—the phenomenological remainder isomorphic with the sakshi location
- Modern applications:
  - 元认知训练：对'我在焦虑'执行分离律——'焦虑'是被观察的内容，'觉知到焦虑'者是另一项；回撤训练降低内容与主体的融合
  - 产品设计中的'观察者盲区'：用户测试记录'用户做了什么'时，同步追问'记录者的视角预设是什么'——凡被记录者皆非记录框架
  - AI 与意识讨论：'系统输出可被观察'不等于'系统的观察者可被观察'——分离律为意识归因提供纪律
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Metacognitive training: run the split on 'I am anxious'—anxiety is observed content; the one aware of anxiety is another term; withdrawal training reduces fusion of content with subject
  - The observer's blind spot in product research: alongside recording 'what the user did,' ask 'what the recorder's perspective presupposes'—whatever is recorded is not the recording frame
  - AI and consciousness debates: 'the system's output is observable' does not entail 'the system's observer is observable'—the split disciplines attribution of consciousness

## M-SHA-010 生解收束法 / Generation-and-Release Method

- Definition: 组织传承方法论/一生的体系必须转化为不依赖本人存续的制度：地理冗余（四方道场）、谱系冗余（僧团传承）、教学法冗余（可教学阶梯）——人死去，生成机制活着
- Definition (EN): Methodology of institutional succession/a lifetime's system must be converted into institutions surviving the founder: geographic redundancy (monasteries in four quarters), genealogical redundancy (the order's lineages), pedagogical redundancy (a teachable ladder)—the man dies, the generative mechanism lives
- Source: 四大根本道场（amnaya-matha）的建立传统；'十名'（Dashanami）僧团制度；《分辨宝鬘》与《千部解脱论》的可教学化设计
- Key quote: 让体系不再需要我——这是我最后也是最重要的著作。 / To make the system no longer need me—that was my last and greatest work.
- Process:
  - 把体系的'生命线'列成清单：哪些环节当前只系于创始人一人（知识、判断、网络、权威）
  - 为每个单点设计冗余：地理冗余（多点分镇）、谱系冗余（多支传承、明定授受规则）、教学法冗余（把隐性知识拆成可教阶梯）
  - 执行'替换自己'：亲自授权各支继承人、亲自编写/审定可教学的教材与准入标准——传承设计必须由创始人完成，不可代劳
  - 以退出检验制度：创始人离场（或早逝）后审计体系是否仍自行生成新导师、新学生、新解答——衰减即补冗余
- Process (EN):
  - Inventory the system's lifelines: which links currently rest on the founder alone (knowledge, judgment, network, authority)
  - Design redundancy per single point: geographic (anchored at multiple sites), genealogical (multiple lineages with explicit transmission rules), pedagogical (tacit knowledge decomposed into a teachable ladder)
  - Execute self-replacement: personally authorize successors in each lineage, personally write or ratify the teachable materials and admission standards—succession design must be the founder's own work, not delegated
  - Test the institutions by exit: audit after the founder leaves (or dies early) whether the system still generates new teachers, students and answers on its own—decay signals missing redundancy
- Representative cases:
  - 四大根本道场：分镇次大陆四角——任何一地被毁教法不绝，单点失败被地理设计预先排除
  - '十名'僧团与师徒授受：亲证检验法被做成可代代重做的'实验传统'——体系不依赖任何个体的不可复制性
  - 早逝的最终检验：商羯罗约三十二岁去世，而体系在其死后八百年仍扩张——'人死去，生成机制活着'的完整样本
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The four paramount monasteries anchoring the four quarters—any one destroyed, the teaching survives; single points of failure excluded by geographic design
  - The Dashanami order and guru-disciple transmission: experience-verification turned into an experiment-tradition redoable every generation—the system depending on no individual's irreplaceability
  - The final test of early death: Shankara died at about thirty-two, and eight centuries on the system had expanded—'the man dies, the generative mechanism lives' in full
- Modern applications:
  - 创始人型企业传承：退休前完成三冗余——多点分设（地理）、多支接班与明定授权规则（谱系）、把创始人隐性判断写成可教课程（教学法），以'离场后仍能自我生成'为验收标准
  - 开源项目可持续性：总线因子（bus factor）治理——文档、测试、多人维护权分镇，'任何一人离开项目不死'
  - 知识产品（方法论/学派）设计：出书之外必须建'可教学的阶梯+可认证的传承谱系'，否则体系随个人热度衰减

  - Open-source sustainability: bus-factor governance—documentation, tests and maintainer rights anchored at multiple points; 'any one person leaving, the project does not die'
  - Designing knowledge products (methodologies, schools): beyond the book, build 'a teachable ladder plus a certifiable lineage'—else the system decays with personal fame
- Related modes: M-SHA-006（亲证检验法——谱系冗余的内容核心：师徒授受制度使亲证可代代重做）; M-SHA-007（四备阶梯法——教学法冗余的实体：把高阶知识拆成可教学的准入阶梯）; M-JEF-003（杰斐逊·制度设计类模式——远缘对照：杰斐逊以成文宪法与大学设计为'理念'续命，与道场-僧团设计同为'生成机制转移'的立法）; M-SUK-010（苏加诺·失衡警示法——反向对照：苏加诺依赖个人魅力而制度承接失败——'缺冗余'的二十世纪政治样本，与商羯罗构成成败对照）
