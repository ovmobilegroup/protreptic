## 模式全文

### M-ASM-001 看不见的手 / Invisible Hand: Emergent Order from Self-Interest

**domain**: 政治经济学/自发秩序 / Political Economy / Spontaneous Order

**definition_zh**: 把'公共善如何产生'从'善良设计者的工程问题'改写为'分散自利如何被价格信号聚合的系统问题'：屠夫、酿酒师、面包师并非出于恩惠供给晚餐，而是出于自利；但正是这场逐利竞争，在没有人打算为公共利益服务的情况下，把资源持续推向公众最需要的地方。要害有三：其一，信息是分散且不可集中的——任何计划者的头脑都不可能装下一个社会的全部局部知识，价格以一个数字浓缩了供给、需求与稀缺的全部信息，让陌生人之间无需对话即可协作；其二，秩序可以是涌现的产物——市场秩序不是自上而下设计的成果，而是无数个体决策的副产物，判断制度好坏的标准因此不是设计者的意图而是涌现结果；其三，机制有效有前提——看不见的手只在产权明晰、竞争充分、无欺诈强制的制度框架内起作用，斯密同时是严厉的批评垄断与商人操纵立法者的人，'看不见的手'从不为纵容贪婪作辩护。方法论签名：不要问谁在为公共利益操盘——去检查那些让私人利益在追求自身时不得不服务他人的制度条件，条件成立时秩序自动涌现，条件缺失时再多善意的操盘手也堆不出一个市场。

**definition_en**: The invisible hand rewrites the question of how public good arises: not as an engineering problem for a benevolent designer, but as a systems problem of how dispersed self-interest is aggregated into general welfare. Three disciplines give the mode its force. First, knowledge is dispersed and cannot be centralized: no planner's mind can hold a society's local knowledge of wants, resources, and opportunities; the price system condenses all of it into a single number, so strangers cooperate across continents without ever meeting or sharing a purpose—coordination replaces command. Second, order can be an emergent product: the market order is not the outcome of top-down design but a byproduct of countless individual decisions; the standard for judging an institution therefore shifts from the designer's intention to the emergent result, which explains why well-intentioned schemes fail and unintended arrangements succeed. Third, the mechanism is conditional, not magical: the invisible hand operates only inside a framework of secure property rights, genuine competition, and honest dealing; Smith himself was a fierce critic of monopoly, collusion, and merchants who capture legislation—'people of the same trade seldom meet together, even for merriment, but the conversation ends in a conspiracy against the public.' Self-interest left unchecked conspires against competition, producing rent-seeking rather than public good, and externalities are exactly where prices fall silent. The method is to audit the institutional conditions, not to worship the outcome. Signature: do not ask who is managing the public interest—examine the institutional conditions under which private advantage must serve others to serve itself; when the conditions hold, order emerges unbidden, and when they fail, no committee of benevolent managers can conjure a market into being.

**key_quote_zh**: 我们的晚餐并非来自屠夫、酿酒师或面包师的恩惠，而是来自他们对自身利益的关切。

**process_zh**: 识别分散信息：列出决策所需的局部知识（偏好、成本、稀缺），评估任何单一头脑能否集中掌握——通常不能；寻找聚合机制：确认该场景是否存在让分散自利相互碰撞的交换与竞争机制（价格、投标、匹配），没有就先搭建；审查制度前提：核对产权、竞争、诚实交易三项条件是否成立，排除垄断、欺诈与外部性对信号的扭曲；让结果涌现：停止逐项指挥，观察涌现结果是否改善公共福利，再据此微调规则而非直接干预价格

**related_modes**: M-ASM-002, M-ASM-004

### M-ASM-002 分工与生产力跃迁 / Division of Labour and the Productivity Leap

**domain**: 经济学/组织生产 / Economics / Organization of Production

**definition_zh**: 把'产出增长'从'加人加机器的线性问题'改写为'工序分解与专用化的跃迁问题'：斯密的制针厂把抽丝、拉直、切截、磨尖、装头等十八道工序分给十八个专门工人，日产量从人均一二十枚跃至四千八百枚，跃升来源被拆解为三项——技能熟练度的提升、工序切换时间的消除、以及机器发明（因工人专注于单一环节而更易被诱发）。要害有三：其一，分解先于增效——生产力跃迁的起点是把复合任务拆到'单一动作可被反复优化'的粒度，未经分解的岗位无论多努力都停在旧均衡；其二，分工受市场范围限制——只有当交换规模大到足以吸收专门化的全部产出时，分工才划算；萨伊与阿林·杨据此推出累积因果：分工扩大产出，产出扩大市场，更大的市场又催生更细分工，增长因此是内生自我加速的；其三，分工的代价是'心智的钝化'——斯密本人警告终日重复简单操作会使人'成为最愚钝无知的人'，这为教育与轮岗设计埋下伏笔。方法论签名：先分解任务，再谈努力——生产力的杠杆不在让人干得更多，而在把任务切到能让专业、机器与市场范围相互喂养的粒度。

**definition_en**: The division of labour rewrites output growth: not a linear problem of adding labour and machines, but a leap achieved by decomposing the task and specializing each step. Three disciplines give the mode its force. First, decomposition precedes improvement: the starting point of any productivity leap is breaking a compound task down to the grain where a single motion can be repeatedly optimized; an undecomposed role, however hard-working, stays trapped at the old equilibrium. Second, the division of labour is limited by the extent of the market: specialization pays only when the scale of exchange is large enough to absorb the whole dedicated output; Say and Allyn Young turned this into cumulative causation—division of labour enlarges output, output enlarges the market, and the larger market induces still finer specialization, making growth endogenous and self-accelerating, with Young's famous 1928 paper declaring this 'the most illuminating' proposition in economics. Third, Smith himself named the cost: a man who performs a few simple operations his whole life 'becomes as stupid and ignorant as it is possible for a human creature to become'—the mind is dulled by routine, which is why mature division-of-labour design must budget for rotation, education, and re-skilling, never treating workers as permanent fixtures of a single operation. The method is to manage the coupling between task granularity, market size, and human capacity, never to maximize specialization in isolation. Signature: decompose the task before praising the effort—the lever of productivity lies not in making people work more, but in cutting tasks to the grain where specialization, machinery, and the extent of the market begin to feed one another.

**key_quote_zh**: 分工是受市场范围限制的——这是经济学中最富启发的命题之一。

**process_zh**: 任务分解：把复合流程拆为单一动作粒度的工序，逐项计时并标出切换损耗；评估市场承载力：估算交换规模能否吸收专门化后的全部产出，不达阈值则维持较低分工度或先扩市场；配置专用资产：按工序配置专用工具、设备与培训，诱发环节级机器与工艺创新；轮换与再训练：以轮岗与持续教育对冲专业化带来的心智钝化，保持工序弹性

**related_modes**: M-ASM-001, M-ASM-010

### M-ASM-003 价格-价值三重结构 / Natural Price, Market Price and the Gravitation Mechanism

**domain**: 经济学/价值理论 / Economics / Theory of Value

**definition_zh**: 把'价格为何波动、又为何不散架'理解为三重结构：自然价格是恰好覆盖地租、工资与利润（按自然率）的'中心价格'，即商品'与其真实所值、真实成本相当'的锚；市场价格是实际成交价，受供给量与'有效需求'（愿付自然价格的需求）的对比而围绕中心波动；引力机制则是资本家追逐利润率的套利——市价高于自然价时超额利润吸引资本流入、供给增加、价格回落；低于时资本撤出、供给收缩、价格回升。要害有三：其一，区分锚与波动——把长期成本结构（自然价）与短期供需噪声（市价）分层，才能判断哪个价格信号可信、哪个是暂时偏离；其二，引力机制的主体是套利者——价格回归不是道德自律的结果，而是每个资本家'从不把资本用在不称心用途上'的持续逐利，市场出清的执行人正是逐利者本人；其三，引力会被阻尼——垄断、行业秘密、资本与劳动力的自由流动受阻都会放缓甚至冻结回归，斯密明言这些条件下市价可以长期背离自然价。方法论签名：任何一个价格都同时是三层信息的叠加——先问它偏离自然价有多远、套利通道是否畅通，再决定把它当作信号还是噪声，被垄断冻结的价格既不是锚也不是波，而是谎言。

**definition_en**: The triple structure of price and value explains both why prices fluctuate and why they do not come apart. The gravitation mechanism is profit-rate arbitrage: when market price exceeds natural price, the excess profit draws capital and labour into the trade, supply swells, and price falls back; when it falls below, capital withdraws, supply contracts, and price recovers—the quantity brought to market 'naturally suits itself to the effectual demand,' in Smith's metaphor borrowed from the physical sciences. Three disciplines give the mode its force. First, separate the anchor from the noise: stratify the long-run cost structure (natural price) from short-term supply-demand fluctuations (market price), so one can judge which price signal is trustworthy and which is a temporary deviation—not every price movement carries information about value. Second, the executor of gravitation is the arbitrageur: price convergence is not the outcome of moral discipline but of every capitalist's ceaseless pursuit of advantage—he never employs his capital where he expects it to yield less; the market is cleared by the profit-seekers themselves, which means the mechanism needs free entry, not altruism. Third, gravitation can be damped or frozen: monopoly, trade secrets, blocked mobility of capital and labour, and regulation-induced scarcity all slow or suspend the return; Smith explicitly warns that under such conditions market price can be held above natural price 'for many years together.' A frozen price is therefore not an anchor and not a wave—it is a distortion wearing the costume of information. Signature: every price is a superposition of three layers—first ask how far it sits from its natural price and whether the arbitrage channel is open, and only then decide whether to read it as signal or as noise; a price frozen by monopoly is neither anchor nor fluctuation but a lie.

**key_quote_zh**: 自然价格仿佛是中心价格，一切商品的价格都不断受其引力而被拉向它。

**process_zh**: 核算自然价：按土地地租、劳动工资、资本利润的自然率分解商品长期成本，确定中心价格带；测量市价偏离：比较实际成交价与自然价带的偏离方向与幅度，区分有效需求变化与临时扰动；检查套利通道：核对进入壁垒、资本与劳动流动性、信息透明度，判断引力机制是否畅通；按分层定价决策：把畅通引力下的市价当真实信号，把被冻结的偏离价识别为扭曲而非价值

**related_modes**: M-ASM-002, M-ASM-005

### M-ASM-004 同情共感与公正旁观者 / Sympathy and the Impartial Spectator (TMS)

**domain**: 道德哲学/道德判断机制 / Moral Philosophy / Mechanism of Moral Judgment

**definition_zh**: 把'道德判断从何而来'从'神授戒律或先天理性'改写为'想象中换位的情感校准机制'：人在想象中进入他人处境，核对彼此情感是否合拍（sympathy，同情共感）——合拍则赞许，不合拍则谴责；《道德情操论》开篇即言人的同情共感源于想象性的换位，'人若终其一生从未借他人之眼映照过自己的情感，将如生来失明者'。由于当事人的激情总强于旁观者所能共感的程度，人学会在想象中召唤一个'公正旁观者'，以第三方之眼审视自己的情感与行为，由此生成良心。要害有三：其一，道德是镜像而非戒律——自我判断依赖先被他人判断，'我以他人之眼审视自己'，良心的原材料是社会性映照；其二，公正旁观者提供升维标准——他剥离利益、地位与私谊，要求'以真正无偏旁观者的眼光看我'，这使道德判断可以独立于舆论与权力（多数人的喝彩不等于旁观者的首肯）；其三，同情共感有其失败模式——旁观者天然更同情富人与成功者（'钦佩富人与大人物、忽视穷人与小人物'的倾向）会系统性扭曲道德校准，斯密称之为道德情操腐败的最大而最普遍的原因。方法论签名：判断自己行为的唯一可靠席位在观众席上——先找到那个既非你的受益者、也非你敌人的旁观者席位，把他的眼睛借来照自己的激情，凡经不起公正旁观者注视的欲望，都不是你该服从的声音。

**definition_en**: Sympathy and the impartial spectator rewrite where moral judgment comes from: not from divine command or innate rational law, but from an imaginative position-swap that calibrates feeling against feeling. Three disciplines give the mode its force. First, morality is a mirror, not a code: self-judgment presupposes having been judged by others—the self that condemns is constructed from the gaze of the world; a person who had never seen himself reflected in the sentiments of others would be, Smith argues, as blind to his own conduct as one born blind is to colour. Second, the impartial spectator supplies an upgrade path above public opinion: he strips out interest, rank, and friendship, demanding that we judge 'as another and impartial spectator would examine it'; this makes moral judgment independent of applause and power—the loudest crowd is not the same thing as the spectator's assent, and a corrupt age's applause is precisely what the internal spectator exists to refuse. Third, sympathy has systematic failure modes: spectators naturally admire the rich and the great and are cold toward the poor—'the disposition to admire the rich and the great, and to neglect persons of poor and mean condition'—which bends moral calibration toward status; Smith treats this deference as 'the great and most universal cause of the corruption of our moral sentiments,' which is why the method insists on auditing whose eye you are borrowing. Signature: the only reliable seat for judging your own conduct is in the audience—find the spectator who is neither your beneficiary nor your enemy, borrow his eyes to look at your passions, and whatever desire cannot survive his gaze is not a voice you should obey.

**key_quote_zh**: 我们以想象中公正旁观者审视自己行为的方式检视自己的行为——人心中那个人（the man within the breast），是良心的真正居所。

**process_zh**: 换位想象：进入他人处境，逐项核对自己的情感反应与其情感是否合拍，标出不合拍点；检视旁观者席位：确认当前借用的眼光来自谁——利益相关者、权力者还是真正中立者，排除被地位光环污染的镜子；召唤公正旁观者：以完全无利益、无关系的第三方视角重新审视自己的激情与拟议行为；依据旁观者裁决行动：凡经不起旁观者注视的欲望降权，经得起者升格为可执行的决定

**related_modes**: M-ASM-001, M-ASM-009

### M-ASM-005 有限政府与税赋四原则 / Limited Government and the Four Canons of Taxation

**domain**: 政治经济学/公共财政 / Political Economy / Public Finance

**definition_zh**: 把'国家该做什么'从'权力范围之争'改写为'职能清单加税收约束的双重设计'：斯密把主权者义务限定为三项——保护社会不受暴力侵犯（国防）、保护每个成员不受其他成员压迫（司法）、建设并维护私人无力或不愿承办的公共工程与公共机构（公共品），即后世所称'守夜人国家'的原始版本；同时以税收四原则约束取财之道——公平（按能力比例纳税）、确定（税额、方式与时间必须明确不可恣意）、便利（在纳税人最方便的时间与方式征收）、经济（征管成本最小，'不得使人民荷包在税之外再多掏'）。要害有三：其一，职能与税制是一枚硬币——政府的边界由公共品的'私人无力承办'标准划出，而征税四原则保证国家取财不侵蚀市场赖以运行的激励与确定性，二者缺一即滑向重商主义式的掠夺性财政；其二，确定性是第一美德——斯密断言哪怕税负不轻，只要'确定'，其不平等尚可忍受，而'不确定的税收'使每个纳税人都任凭征税官吏恣意摆布，不确定本身就是暴政；其三，征管成本是隐形税负——繁琐稽查、迁延与刁难都让人民的实际负担远超国库所得，四原则把国家自己放进被审计的位置。方法论签名：先给国家开一张封闭的职能清单，再给国库装上四把锁——公平、确定、便利、经济，任何越过清单的野心或绕开锁的征课，都必须由主张者举证，而不是由纳税者忍受。

**definition_en**: Limited government is designed as a double structure: a closed list of state functions plus a tax discipline that keeps the state's taking from destroying the market it protects. Against this boundary stands the taxation side, the four canons: equity (subjects contribute in proportion to their respective abilities, 'in proportion to the revenue which they respectively enjoy under the protection of the state'); certainty (the tax, its amount, the manner and time of payment, must be clear and plain, 'not arbitrary'); convenience (levied in the time and manner most likely to be convenient for the contributor); and economy (every tax ought to take out of people's pockets as little as possible over and above what it brings into the public treasury). Three disciplines give the mode its force. First, function and fiscal rule are one coin: the state's edge is drawn by the standard of what private capital cannot profitably provide, while the four canons guarantee that raising revenue does not corrode the incentives and certainty on which the market runs—drop either half and the state slides toward the predatory, mercantilist finance Smith spent Book V attacking. Second, certainty is the first virtue: Smith insists that 'a very considerable degree of inequality' may be endured, but 'arbitrariness is always great and intolerable'—an uncertain tax exposes every subject to the caprice of the tax-gatherer, and uncertainty is itself a form of tyranny, chilling investment more surely than a high but known rate. Third, collection cost is a hidden tax: vexatious visits, delays, and inspection apparatus make the people's real burden exceed the treasury's gain; the fourth canon is a demand that the state audit itself. Signature: hand the state a closed list of duties first, then lock the treasury with four locks—equity, certainty, convenience, economy—and let anyone who oversteps the list or picks a lock carry the burden of proof, not the taxed.

**key_quote_zh**: 不确定的税收纵非苛暴之极，也使每个纳税人任凭征税官吏的恣意摆布——税收的不确定本身就是一种暴政。

**process_zh**: 划定职能清单：以'私人资本是否愿意且能够承办'为筛，把国家职责封闭在国防、司法、公共工程三类之内；逐项设计筹资：对每项职能选择税种，核对其符合公平、确定、便利、经济四原则的程度；优先保证确定性：公布税基、税率、缴纳时间与方式并写入法典，压缩征管自由裁量；审计征管成本：测算纳税人实际负担与国库所得的差额，凡差额过大即重构征管流程

**related_modes**: M-ASM-003, M-ASM-008

### M-ASM-006 自利与利他双层人性论 / Self-Interest and Benevolence: The Two-Layer Theory of Human Nature

**domain**: 人性论与经济社会学 / Theory of Human Nature and Economic Sociology

**definition_zh**: 斯密人性论的双层结构：在《国富论》中，经济行为以自利为动力——'我们的晚餐并非来自屠夫、酿酒师和面包师的恩惠，而是来自他们对自身利益的关切'；在《道德情操论》中，人又天然具有同情共感（sympathy），以'公正旁观者'为道德裁判。二者并非矛盾，而是同一人性在交易领域与道德领域的分层呈现：市场把自利聚合为公共善，道德把同情聚合为品格。三个要害：一、自利是分析交易秩序的起点而非人的全部——斯密从未说人是纯粹自利的动物，恩惠在家庭与友谊中真实运作，只是不能作为大规模协作的组织原则；二、'看不见的手'的聚合效果依赖制度前提，自利若无法治与竞争约束，会退化为掠夺而非互利；三、同情心随社会距离衰减，因此大社会的秩序不能托付于仁爱，必须托付于以自利为轮齿的分工交换系统。方法论签名句：先分清人性在哪一层运作，再选择用道德劝谕还是用制度设计去动员它——自利归市场，仁爱归私域，正义归法律。

**definition_en**: Smith's theory of human nature is two-layered, not contradictory. In The Wealth of Nations, the moving spring of economic life is self-interest: 'It is not from the benevolence of the butcher, the brewer, or the baker, that we expect our dinner, but from their regard to their own interest' — address yourself to their self-love, never to your necessities but to their advantages. In The Theory of Moral Sentiments, however, the first fact of human psychology is sympathy: however selfish man may be supposed, 'there are evidently some principles in his nature, which interest him in the fortune of others.' The two books describe one human being in two registers. Second key: the aggregation of self-interest into public benefit is conditional, not automatic; the 'invisible hand' works because the merchant prefers domestic industry with face-to-face accountability, enforceable contracts, and competitive pressure; strip these away and self-interest degrades into monopoly, fraud, and plunder. Smith is no apologist for greed: TMS diagnoses the 'disposition to admire, and almost to worship, the rich and the powerful' as a deception of the imagination, policed by the 'impartial spectator.' Third key: institutional design must match the layer of human nature it mobilizes — asking the butcher for charity produces poverty; asking strangers for exchange produces abundance; relying on universal benevolence produces an empire of flies and gnats. Hence: first identify which layer of human nature operates in the domain, then choose whether to mobilize it by moral exhortation, market incentive, or legal justice — self-interest to the market, benevolence to the private sphere, justice to the law.

**key_quote_zh**: 我们的晚餐并非来自屠夫、酿酒师或面包师的恩惠，而是来自他们对自身利益的关切。

**process_zh**: 识别行为领域的性质：是陌生人间的重复交易，还是熟人社会的互助网络；在交易域默认自利前提设计规则，在私域承认并保护仁爱与情感纽带；检验自利聚合是否有制度前提（法治、竞争、对称信息），缺失则干预或修法；用公正旁观者检验：该安排在无利害的旁观者眼中是否可辩护

**related_modes**: M-ASM-009, M-ASM-001

### M-ASM-007 寻租批判与利益集团警示 / Critique of Rent-Seeking and the Merchants' Conspiracy Warning

**domain**: 政治经济学与公共政策 / Political Economy and Public Policy

**definition_zh**: 斯密对利益集团的批判是《国富论》的隐伏主线：同业者聚会必成对付公众的密谋，重商主义的特许公司、关税、行会与殖民地垄断，都是商人与制造商借立法之手把私利固化为租金。三个要害：一、利益集团捕获政策的过程常以'公共福祉'为修辞外衣——宣称雇用不起本国劳工、须以高关税保护，实为抬价牟利；二、政治分配权力本身就是可竞租的资源，立法越能干预经济，寻租的期望收益越高，聪明才智就从生产转向游说；三、反制之道不是消灭商业，而是拆租：贸易自由化、废除特许垄断、控制政治对经济租金的分配权。方法论签名句：每当一项'保护性'政策出台，先问租金流向谁的口袋，再问消费者与公众为此付出了什么。

**definition_en**: The critique of organized producer interests is the hidden spine of The Wealth of Nations. Smith's warning is explicit: 'People of the same trade seldom meet together, even for merriment and diversion, but the conversation ends in a conspiracy against the public, or in some contrivance to raise prices.' He neither prohibits such meetings — no law can prevent people from sitting together — nor trusts the merchants' word; any proposal from this order 'ought always to be listened to with great precaution... with the most scrupulous, and with the most suspicious attention.' First key: rent-seeking travels in the costume of public welfare. Merchants claim they cannot employ national labour unless protected by duties and bounties, when their real interest is higher prices and restricted competition; Smith tolerates the Navigation Act as security policy but condemns the colonies-trade monopoly as pure privilege that 'deranges the distribution of the stock of society.' Second key: political power over economic allocation is itself a contestable prize — the more the legislature can redirect trade, grant charters, or license guilds, the higher the return to lobbying relative to production, diverting capital and intelligence from employing productive hands to courting privilege. Stigler's capture theory and Tullock's and Krueger's rent-seeking formalize this chapter directly. Third key: the remedy is not to abolish commerce but to dismantle rent — free trade, abolition of exclusive companies, and narrowing the government's power to confer privilege, which lowers the payoff to conspiracy rather than merely criminalizing it. Signature: whenever a 'protective' policy is proposed, first ask into whose pocket the rent will flow, and only then what consumers and the public must pay for it.

**key_quote_zh**: 同行业的人即便为了消遣娱乐而聚会，交谈也常以对付公众的密谋或抬高价格的诡计告终。

**process_zh**: 识别政策提案的真实受益人：追踪租金最终流向哪些生产者集团；审查提案的修辞：'国家安全''扶持幼稚产业''保护劳工'背后的利益结构；比较寻租成本与生产成本：游说、牌照、配额消耗的资源是否本可创造价值；拆租优先于管制：扩大竞争与自由贸易，收紧政府授予权力的通道

**related_modes**: M-ASM-003, M-ASM-006

### M-ASM-008 系统构建与思想实验法 / Systems Construction and Thought-Experiment Method

**domain**: 科学哲学与理论建构 / Philosophy of Science and Theory Construction

**definition_zh**: 斯密在《天文学史》中把哲学定义为'关于连接原则的科学之史'：科学不是事实的堆叠，而是想象力的系统——用一套内在连贯的原理，把原本分散、异质、令人不安的现象连成一幅有秩序的图景，从而平息好奇心与惊奇。他自称沿用牛顿方法：从最简明的单因假设出发（分工、同情），推演出复杂现象，再以观测校准。三个要害：一、系统的价值在于连接力而非穷尽性——一个把万有引力推到木星卫星的原理，优于十万个观测记录；二、思想实验是系统构建的廉价引擎——'灯塔国''被注视的岩石'等假想情境让人分离出单一机制；三、系统必须保持可修订性：斯密明言每个系统都是想象力的产物，终将被更简明的系统取代，因此对自家体系也应保持批评距离。方法论签名句：先把分散的现象连成一个想象系统，再用观测苛待它——能被修正的系统才配称为科学。

**definition_en**: Smith's essay 'The History of Astronomy' contains his explicit philosophy of science: philosophy is 'the science of the connecting principles of nature,' born of wonder at disjointed, anomalous phenomena that unsettle the imagination. A scientific system is 'an imaginary machine' — like a watch — whose parts are so adjusted that once set in motion they reproduce the observed regularities; its ideal is the Newtonian method, 'to introduce order and coherence' by tracing all phenomena from a single, simple, familiar principle, as Newton derived planets, comets, and tides from universal gravitation. First key: a system's value lies in its connecting power, not its exhaustiveness — one principle that carries from the apple to the moons of Jupiter beats ten thousand unconnected observations; Smith applied this to his own work, making the division of labour in WN and sympathy in TMS each a single spring from which heterogeneous phenomena move together. Third key: every system is provisional and must remain criticizable. Smith warns that admiration for a system's ingenuity can degenerate into the 'spirit of system' — the man of system 'so enamoured of the supposed beauty of his own ideal plan' that he forgets the real people on the chessboard — and each age's best system, Ptolemy's, Copernicus's, or Newton's, will be superseded; his own letters urging revision of WN's America chapters show the discipline applied to himself. Signature: first connect scattered phenomena into an imaginary system, then torment that system with observation — only a system that can be corrected deserves to be called science.

**key_quote_zh**: 哲学是对自然连接原则的探究——是关于连接原则的科学之史。

**process_zh**: 从惊奇出发：找出令想象力不安的分散现象或反常；构造最简明的单一连接原理，用思想实验隔离单一机制做思想推演；把原理推演至远端领域，检验其连接力（从一个苹果到木星卫星）；以观测校准并保持系统可修订性，警惕'系统精神'凌驾真实的人

**related_modes**: M-ASM-002, M-ASM-009

### M-ASM-009 生产力-资本积累-制度演进框架 / Productivity-Capital Accumulation-Institutional Evolution

**domain**: 增长理论与制度经济学 / Growth Theory and Institutional Economics

**definition_zh**: 斯密是第一个系统化增长模型的经济学家：分工提高劳动生产率，生产率剩余转化为储蓄，储蓄积累为资本，资本扩大可雇用的生产性劳动者，进而深化分工——一个自我强化的增长回路；但回路能否运转取决于制度环境：'和平、简便的赋税、过得去的司法'（peace, easy taxes, tolerable justice），加上自由港与自由贸易，其余交给自然进程。三个要害：一、增长的引擎是生产性劳动与储蓄，不是贵金属——重商主义把财富等同于金银，斯密把财富等同于年产出流；二、资本积累受'生产性/非生产性劳动'比例约束，政府若大规模雇用非生产性劳动，即抽走积累燃料；三、制度不是外生背景而是增长的第五要素——司法不公使商业萎缩，赋税苛扰使资本外逃，肯尼迪法令与《杰富法》之类反复重申：一国从最野蛮到最富足，只需和平、低税与过得去的司法，其余皆自然而至。方法论签名句：把增长拆为生产率、积累、制度三层后，先修制度地基，再谈引擎轰鸣。

**definition_en**: Smith built the first systematic growth model in economics. Its engine is a self-reinforcing loop: the division of labour raises productivity; the surplus becomes saving; saving accumulates as capital; capital hires more productive labourers; a larger working population deepens the division of labour — and the circle turns again. 'Capitals are increased by parsimony': frugality, not prodigality, feeds the fund that employs productive hands. But the loop runs only inside an institutional envelope, stated as a maxim in his 1755 lectures and echoed in WN IV.9: 'Little else is requisite to carry a state to the highest degree of opulence from the lowest barbarism, but peace, easy taxes, and a tolerable administration of justice; all the rest being brought about by the natural course of things... any government which endeavours to go further is at once a most oppressive and a most intolerable tyranny.' First key: the engine of growth is productive labour and saving, not precious metals; against mercantilism, wealth is the flow of annual produce, not bullion — gold and silver are merely the wheel of circulation, and a country rich in mines can remain poor in real opulence. Third key: institutions are the fifth factor, not exogenous backdrop. Injustice 'pars down' commerce: insecure property makes capital flee, arbitrary or vexatious taxes discourage industry more than their amount, partial justice drives merchants into conspiracy with power. Smith's institutional minimalism is a growth-accounting claim, not laissez-faire romanticism: secure property, light and predictable taxes, and tolerable courts are preconditions without which parsimony is irrational and the accumulation engine stalls. Signature: decompose growth into productivity, accumulation, and institutions — repair the institutional foundation first, then speak of the engine's roar.

**key_quote_zh**: 使一国从最野蛮状态升至最富足境地的，几乎只需和平、简便的赋税与过得去的司法，其余一切皆由事物的自然进程带来。

**process_zh**: 核算增长的来源：区分生产率提升、要素积累与制度环境三层贡献；追踪储蓄-投资链条：剩余是否流向雇用生产性劳动的资本；审计制度地基：产权安全、税负简便、司法可预期性是否齐备；按'最薄弱环节'排序改革：先修司法与税制，再谈刺激与补贴

**related_modes**: M-ASM-001, M-ASM-010

### M-ASM-010 自利聚合的边界条件与政府职能 / Boundary Conditions of Aggregated Self-Interest and the Role of Government

**domain**: 公共经济学与国家理论 / Public Economics and the Theory of the State

**definition_zh**: 斯密不是放任主义者：他明确划定'自然自由体系'的边界，在边界内自利聚合近乎自动，边界之外必须由主权者补位——国防、司法、公共工程与公共机构（含教育）、以及维持君主尊严的收入。三个要害：一、市场失灵的原始清单由斯密开出——商人利益天然反对公共利益并使垄断倾向化、灯塔与道路等私利无法融资的公共品、儿童教育因父母短视与外部性而投资不足；二、 Progress of Opulence 本身有限度：分工受市场广度限制，殖民地垄断'使社会资本的分配偏离自然轨道'，增长路径可以被政治扭曲乃至锁定在次优轨道；三、政府职能的划定是边际问题而非原则问题——'看得见的手'该在何处介入，取决于哪项制度失灵、何种干预成本低、以及干预本身不被捕获的概率。方法论签名句：自利聚合的边界在哪里，政府的职责清单就从哪里开始——先证明市场失灵，再证明干预有效，两证俱全方可伸手。

**definition_en**: Smith is not a doctrinaire laissez-faire thinker. The system of natural liberty has explicit boundaries, and where they end the sovereign's duties begin: protecting society from violence and invasion; protecting every member from injustice or oppression through 'an exact administration of justice'; and 'the duty of erecting and maintaining certain public works and certain public institutions, which it can never be for the interest of any individual, or small number of individuals, to erect and maintain; because the profit could never repay the expense to any individual or small number of individuals, though it may frequently do much more than repay it to a great society.' First key: Smith drew the original catalogue of market failure. Merchants' interest is 'always in some respects different from, and even opposite to, that of the public,' so their proposals must be heard with suspicious attention — an early statement of monopoly tendency and capture; roads, bridges, and canals 'cannot be maintained by any private person,' and though Smith prefers user-fee financing (turnpikes, light dues), the light of a lighthouse cannot reasonably be charged to individual ships, so general revenue must fund it. Third key: the Progress of Opulence has limits, and politics can distort or lock in inferior paths — the colony trade monopoly 'deranges the distribution of the stock of society,' pushing capital where it would not naturally flow, 'evidently hurtful to the great society.' The boundary of government is a marginal question, not a principle question. Signature: the catalogue of government begins exactly where the boundary of aggregated self-interest ends — first prove market failure, then prove effective intervention, and only with both proofs in hand may the state act.

**key_quote_zh**: 一切特惠或限制的制度一经完全废止，最明白最单纯的自然自由体系便会自行确立……君主从此摆脱监督私人产业的义务，只尽三重职责。

**process_zh**: 界定市场边界：逐项检验交易是否满足竞争、对称信息、无外部性前提；证明失灵：给出该领域自利聚合不足的具体机制（公共品、垄断、外部性、信息不对称）；证明干预有效且不被捕获：比较干预成本、政府能力与被利益集团劫持的风险；优先选择市场近似方案：使用者付费、可执行的产权、竞争性供给先于直接政府供给

**related_modes**: M-ASM-007, M-ASM-008
