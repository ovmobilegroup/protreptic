# 莲花生 (Padmasambhava) — H-PAD-001

- Era: 传说约 717 年生于邬金国（今巴基斯坦斯瓦特河谷，一说出生即无父无母、由莲花中生）——入印度那烂陀等大寺学显教，后师从八大持明者遍学密续——应赤松德赞之邀于约 776-780 年间入藏：沿路调伏苯教神灵为护法，助建桑耶寺并开显密双轨之制——为君臣二十五弟子等分层灌顶传法——传大圆满与八大法行，留椎击三要——预见教法超出现众消化力，将大量密法封存为伏藏并留授记——约八世纪末离藏赴西南罗刹国（传说），留下系列预记与'我还会再来'的承诺——身后被宁玛派尊为'第二佛'，莲师信仰遍布藏地与喜马拉雅诸国：《中阴闻教得度》等伏藏数百年间次第掘出，宁玛派以'三根本'（上师莲师、本尊、空行）立修法之中枢
- Era (EN): By legend born c. 717 in Uddiyana (the Swat valley of today's Pakistan; some say wombless, born of a lotus)—studied exoteric doctrine at Nalanda and other great Indian monasteries, then mastered the tantras under eight vidyadhara masters—invited by Trisong Detsen, entered Tibet c. 776-780: subduing Bon deities into protectors along the way, aiding the building of Samye and instituting the dual sutra-tantra track—gave tiered empowerment to the twenty-five royal disciples and others—transmitted Dzogchen and the Eight Great Sadhana Cycles, leaving the three statements that strike the vital point—foreseeing that the teaching would exceed its audience's capacity, he sealed much of the esoteric corpus as terma with prophecies—departed for the rakshasa lands of the southwest c. the late eighth century (by legend), leaving a series of prophecies and the promise 'I shall return'—after his death honored by the Nyingma as 'the second Buddha,' his cult spreading across Tibet and the Himalayas: terma such as the Bardo Thodol were revealed one after another over centuries, and the Nyingma school made him the hub of the 'three roots' (guru Padmasambhava, yidam, dakini)

- School: 藏传佛教宁玛派的奠基祖师（'宁玛'即'旧译'，尊莲师为教法之源）/ 伏藏（terma）制度的立法者（时间分层传承的设计者）/ 密续灌顶与三昧耶制度在西藏的系统化者 / 大圆满教法的主要传入者（椎击三要的口传者）/ 被尊为'咕噜仁波切'与'第二佛'的喜马拉雅信仰中枢

## Historical significance
莲花生（约717-800），邬金国出身（传说莲花化生），藏传佛教宁玛派奠基者，被尊为'第二佛'。他的历史地位由五件事锚定。其一，佛教在藏地的立足：应赤松德赞之邀入藏，沿路调伏苯教神灵为佛教护法——新教法借旧神灵的地方威望落地，避免了你死我活的宗教替换战。其二，显密双轨的制度设计：与寂护分工并立于桑耶寺——戒律与学问为地基、灌顶与实修为顶层，晋升门槛（灌顶）居中——藏传佛教千年的教会结构由此定型。其三，伏藏制度：预见教法超出现众消化力，把大量密法封存于岩、湖、心识并留授记指明未来掘藏者——宁玛派由此获得'自我更新机制'，《西藏度亡经》即伏藏产出。其四，大圆满与中阴教法的传入：'椎击三要'的直指方法论与中阴导引的阶段化手册，构成藏地心性学的双柱。其五，信仰制度的后续影响：莲师'八变'形象、上师相应法与伏藏掘出循环，使宁玛派在无中央权威下延续千年——一个以'封存-掘出'为节律的活体系。代价与极限：生平几乎全部被传说包裹（莲花化生、降魔神变、长寿数百），史实层仅能锚定赤松德赞时期入藏与桑耶寺的角色；'调伏'被苯教一方记为文化殖民；伏藏真伪之辨成为教团千年的认证难题。

## Historical significance (EN)
Padmasambhava (c. 717-800), by legend born of a lotus in Uddiyana, founded the Nyingma school of Tibetan Buddhism and is honored as the 'second Buddha.' Five achievements anchor his standing. First, planting Buddhism in Tibet: invited by Trisong Detsen, he subdued Bon deities into Buddhist protectors along the way—the new teaching landed on the old gods' local prestige, avoiding a war of mutual annihilation. Second, the dual-track institution: working alongside Shantarakshita at Samye—discipline and scholarship as foundation, empowerment and practice as summit, the promotion gate (empowerment) between—the millennium-stable ecclesial structure of Tibetan Buddhism took shape. Third, the terma system: foreseeing the teaching would exceed its audience, he sealed esoteric texts in rock, lakes and minds with prophecies naming future revealers—giving Nyingma a self-renewal mechanism; the Tibetan Book of the Dead is terma output. Fourth, transmitting Dzogchen and the bardo teachings: the direct-pointing methodology of the 'three statements' and the staged navigation of the Bardo Thodol form the twin pillars of Tibetan mind-nature doctrine. Fifth, the downstream institution of belief: the Eight Manifestations, guru yoga and the concealment-revelation cycle let Nyingma endure a millennium without central authority—a living system pulsing to the rhythm of sealing and revelation. Costs and limits: his life is almost wholly wrapped in legend (lotus birth, demon subjugation, centuries of life); the historical layer anchors only his arrival under Trisong Detsen and his Samye role; 'subjugation' is recorded by Bon as cultural colonization; and authenticating terma became the school's millennium-long verification problem.

## Unique thinking
他思维方式的底色是'对时间与人心双层设防的传承工程：教法会超出现在的人，也会超出在场的人'。六个标志性动作。其一，'伏藏封存法'——预见接受者未备时把成果封存于体系自身的土壤（岩、湖、心识），预设解锁者与解锁条件：知识在时间中自我保管。其二，'顺缘调伏法'——内核不变而形态随受众改变：收编对方的神灵、按根器分层说法——适配而非说服。其三，'本尊相应法'——在受控想象中直接生成理想心智状态并暂时认同，再以次第拆除：先成为，再做出。其四，'双轨并立法'——戒律与密续、显与密不裁决而并行，共享门户与终点、以灌顶为晋升门槛。其五，'忿怒镇守法'——以更强的形态接管对方能量并收编为护法：被镇伏者成为承重结构而非边界外威胁。其六，'预记收束法'——离场时以预言完成最后部署：把风险、继任者画像与转机条件封入对未来的一揽子投递。代价与极限：伏藏真伪无法当场区分（认证依赖事后追认）；'按根器说法'若无诚实诊断即滑向操纵；生平传说化使史实层薄——他的方法论只能从其制度后果（伏藏体系、双轨结构、中阴文本）反向重构。

## Unique thinking (EN)
The bedrock of his thinking: a transmission engineering doubly fortified against time and human hearts—the teaching will exceed the people of now, and the people on the spot. Six signature moves. First, terma concealment—foreseeing receivers not yet ready, seal the findings in the system's own soil (rock, lakes, minds), pre-designating unlocker and unlocking conditions: knowledge keeps itself across time. Second, affinity adaptation—unchanged core, form fitted to audience: enlisting the other's gods, teaching in layers by capacity—fitting, not persuading. Third, deity yoga—generating the ideal mental state directly in controlled imagination and identifying with it provisionally, then dismantling in stages: become first, act second. Fourth, the dual-track institution—discipline and tantra, sutra and esoteric, running in parallel without adjudication, sharing gate and destination with empowerment as the promotion gate. Fifth, wrathful subjugation—taking over the opponent's energy in a stronger form and conscripting it as protector: the subdued become load-bearing structure, not a threat beyond the border. Sixth, the prophetic exit—finishing the final deployment with prophecy: risks, successor profiles and renewal conditions sealed into one delivery to the future. Costs and limits: genuine and forged terma cannot be told apart on the spot (authentication is retroactive); 'teaching by capacity' without honest diagnosis slides into manipulation; and the legend-wrapped biography leaves a thin historical layer—his methodology can only be reconstructed from its institutional aftermath (the terma system, the dual-track structure, the bardo texts).

## M-PAD-001 伏藏封存法 / Terma-Concealment Method

- Definition: 传承设计方法论/成果超出当代接受能力时，把成果封存于系统自身的载体中并预设解锁者与解锁条件——让知识在时间中自我保管，而非强行交付
- Definition (EN): Transmission-design methodology/when findings exceed the present capacity to receive them, seal them inside the system's own carriers with a pre-designated unlocker and unlocking conditions—knowledge keeps itself across time instead of being force-delivered
- Source: 《大宝伏藏》（Rinchen Terdzö，一百一十一卷伏藏总集，蒋贡康楚与秋吉林巴汇编）；娘热·尼玛沃色、古如却旺、多吉林巴、噶玛林巴各家伏藏文献；《五部遗教》
- Key quote: 今时未至，吾埋宝藏于岩、湖、心中；后世之人，依吾授记，当掘而出之。 / The time is not yet: I bury the treasure in rock, lake and mind; a person of later ages, following my prophecy, shall dig it out.
- Process:
  - 评估接受时点：判断目标受众当下能否消化成果的完整形态——若不能，强行交付的损耗是什么？（失真、反弹、误用）
  - 选择封存载体：把成果写入不易朽坏且与体系共生的介质——文本、器物、地理、制度惯例或成员心智，并让封存动作本身不留破坏性痕迹
  - 预设解锁协议：写明'由谁、何时、凭何征兆'可开启——解锁者须与载体有可验证的绑定关系，且解锁条件不依赖封存者在场
  - 授权自我保管：封存后不追问、不遥控——体系的当下形态接受能力边界，把完成品的命运交给已写好的未来
- Process (EN):
  - assess the moment of reception: can the intended audience digest the finding in full form now?—if not, what is the cost of force-delivery (distortion, backlash, misuse)?
  - choose the sealing carrier: write the finding into a durable, system-symbiotic medium—texts, objects, geography, institutional custom or members' minds—so the sealing itself leaves no destructive trace
  - pre-designate the unlocking protocol: specify by whom, when and upon what signs it may be opened—the unlocker must be verifiably bound to the carrier, and the conditions must not depend on the sealer's presence
  - authorize self-keeping: after sealing, do not micro-manage—the system's present form accepts its capacity boundary and hands the finished work to a future already written

## M-PAD-002 顺缘调伏法 / Affinity-Adaptation Method

- Definition: 传播沟通方法论/内核不变而形态随受众改变：先诊断对方的根器、语言与恐惧，再以对方能接受的形态重述同一内核——收编而非替换
- Definition (EN): Dissemination methodology/unchanged core, form adapted to audience: first diagnose capacity, language and fears, then restate the same core in an acceptable form—enlist rather than replace
- Source: 《莲花生大师本生传》（邬金岭巴掘藏）；《巴协》（sba bzhed，吐蕃宫廷史料）；宁玛派'咕噜八变'仪轨文献；《如意宝树史》
- Key quote: 我不与雪山之神战，我请他为法护法；我不对牧人说空性，我先教他转经。 / I do not war with the gods of the snows; I ask them to guard the dharma. I do not speak emptiness to herders; I first teach them to turn the prayer wheel.
- Process:
  - 诊断受众：列出对方的既有体系（信仰、惯例、恐惧、语言）——找出其中不可拆的情感基座与可再利用的结构
  - 决定收编或绕行：对方体系中的元素，凡可重新委以角色者收编（保留外形、替换功能），凡不可收编者绕行而非正面对抗
  - 按根器分层：把同一内核写成不同密度的形态——从最小可接受版本到完整版本，每层都能独立成立且指向同一内核
  - 验证内核不失：任何形态重述后，检查最小集（核心主张）是否仍在——适配是换衣不是换人
- Process (EN):
  - diagnose the audience: map their existing system (beliefs, customs, fears, language)—locate the untouchable emotional base and the reusable structures
  - choose enlistment or bypass: for each element, either enlist (keep the form, reassign the function) or bypass rather than confront head-on
  - stratify by capacity: write the one core at different densities—from the minimal acceptable version to the full version, each layer independently valid and pointing to the same core
  - verify the core survives: after restating in any form, check the minimal set (the core claim) still holds—adaptation changes clothes, not the person

  - 莲花生入藏沿路'调伏'苯教神灵为护法：念青唐拉、十二丹玛女神等被纳入佛教护法体系——新教法借旧神灵的地方威望落地
  - 对赤松德赞讲王者之道、对桑耶寺译师讲译经规范、对弟子授密续灌顶——同一教法在宫廷、译场、密室三密度并行

## M-PAD-003 本尊相应法 / Deity-Yoga Identification Method

- Definition: 能力习得方法论/以受控想象直接生成理想心智状态并暂时认同，再于次第中拆除认同——'先成为再做出'取代'先模仿后接近'
- Definition (EN): Capability-acquisition methodology/directly generate the ideal mental state in controlled imagination and identify with it provisionally, then dismantle the identification in stages—'become first, act second' replaces 'imitate first, approach later'
- Source: 《秘密集会续》（Guhyasamaja Tantra）；《八大法行》仪轨（八epadma修部）；《中阴闻教得度》生起圆满次第章节；宁玛派《大圆满前行引导文》观想章节
- Key quote: 凡夫见我为外，行者观己为本尊；生起已，当知能观所观俱寂。 / The ordinary person sees the deity as outside; the practitioner beholds themselves as the deity. Having generated it, know that both the beholder and the beheld subside.
- Process:
  - 选定本尊：按目标心智状态选原型（要勇气选忿怒尊、要慈悲选寂静尊）——原型是能力的拟人化索引
  - 受控进入：以仪式设定认同的边界（空间、时间、姿态），在边界内逐项生成本尊的身、语、意——直到能以其心续运作
  - 在认同中行动：以本尊的心理状态处理真实事务（决策、对话、危机）——行为先于性格改变，因为状态已经切换
  - 次第退出：仪式化地把认同消融于空性，记录'哪些状态带出来了、哪些留在了角色里'——防止自我被角色吞并
- Process (EN):
  - select the deity: choose the archetype by target mental state (wrathful for courage, peaceful for compassion)—the archetype is a personified index of the capability
  - controlled entry: use ritual to set identification's boundary (space, time, posture), generating the deity's body, speech and mind item by item until you can operate from its mind-stream
  - act within identification: handle real affairs (decisions, dialogues, crises) from the deity's mental state—behavior changes before character, because the state has switched
  - staged exit: ritually dissolve the identification into emptiness and record which states were brought out and which stayed in the role—guarding the self from being swallowed by the role
- Representative cases:
  - 宁玛派'八大法行'修法：修者依次认同文殊身、莲花语、真实意等八位本尊——把八类能力（智慧、语德、禅定等）拟人化后逐一习得

  - 莲花生传法仪轨中'咕噜八变'观想：弟子依次观想莲师八种化身——同一人格的八个面向被用作八种心理状态的切换练习
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The Nyingma Eight Great Sadhana Cycles: practitioners sequentially identify with the eight heruka deities—wisdom, speech, samadhi and other capacities personified and acquired one by one
  - The Bardo Thodol's deathbed guidance: the dying are taught to 'recognize the light you see as the display of your own mind'—deity yoga applied at death: identify with what most frightens you rather than flee it

- Modern applications:

## M-PAD-004 双轨并立法 / Dual-Track Institution Method

- Definition: 制度设计方法论/两种必要但互斥的要素不裁决而并行：共享门户与终点、以门槛分层、各轨互为对方的保险——地基与顶层同寺而立
- Definition (EN): Institution-design methodology/two necessary but conflicting elements run in parallel rather than one being adjudicated: shared gate and destination, threshold stratification, each track insuring the other—foundation and summit in one house
- Source: 《拔协》（sba bzhed，桑耶寺建寺与顿渐之诤史料）；《娘氏教法源流》；宁玛派教法史中的'显密判教'章节
- Key quote: 戒为地，咒为顶；无地者堕，无顶者滞——一寺之内，两轨俱行。 / Discipline is the ground, mantra the summit; without the ground one falls, without the summit one stalls—within one temple, both tracks run.
- Process:
  - 识别不可合并的要素对：列出体系内'都必要但互相掣肘'的两种要素（规范与体验、质与量、长期与速效）——确认裁决会杀死其中之一
  - 设计共享底座：两条轨道必须共享的门户（准入、价值、术语、终点）先固定下来——差异放在方法层而非目标层
  - 设置晋升门槛：明确从一轨进入另一轨的资格条件与遴选程序——门槛是双轨不被滥用的唯一保险
  - 定期对轨校准：设立双轨代表参与的校准机制，防止两条轨道演化为两个教派——并立的目的是互补而非分家
- Process (EN):
  - identify the unmergeable pair: list the two elements both necessary yet mutually constraining (norms and experience, quality and quantity, long-term and fast)—confirm that adjudication would kill one
  - design the shared base: fix first what both tracks must share (admission, values, terminology, destination)—differences live at the method level, not the goal level
  - install the promotion gate: state the qualification and selection procedure for moving from one track into the other—the gate is the only insurance against dual-track misuse
  - calibrate the tracks periodically: create a calibration mechanism with representatives of both, preventing two tracks from becoming two sects—parallelism aims at complementarity, not schism
- Representative cases:
  - 桑耶寺的功能分区：显教经院（译场、戒坛、学院）与密咒殿（灌顶、实修）同寺并立——'地基与顶层'的建筑化制度设计

- Representative cases (EN):
  - Samye's functional zoning: exoteric academy (translation hall, ordination platform, college) and tantric hall (empowerment, practice) standing in one monastery—architecturalized institution design of 'foundation and summit'
  - Shantarakshita and Padmasambhava invited to Tibet in succession: one established discipline and scholarship, the other subdued obstacles and taught tantra—the court institutionalized the dual track with 'two invitations'

## M-PAD-005 忿怒镇守法 / Wrathful-Subjugation Method

- Definition: 防御威慑方法论/以更强的忿怒形态接管对方能量并导入受控轨道，收编而非消灭——被镇伏者成为体系的承重结构而非边界外威胁
- Definition (EN): Deterrence methodology/take over the opponent's energy in a more powerful wrathful form and channel it into a controlled track—conscription, not annihilation; the subdued become the system's load-bearing structure, not a threat beyond its border
- Source: 《莲花遗教》镇伏章节；宁玛派'多杰佐烈'（忿怒莲师）仪轨；《巴协》桑耶寺建寺传说；'十二丹玛'护法谱系文献
- Key quote: 以慈悲为骨，以忿怒为面；不灭其力，而改其向。 / Compassion for bone, wrath for face; not destroying the force but redirecting it.
- Process:
  - 评估对方能量：确认破坏性力量的规模、来源与动员逻辑——镇伏的前提是承认它有真实能量，而非贬低其为'无知'
  - 切换至更强形态：在不越出自身价值内核的前提下，启用对方能感知的最高威慑形态——形态的烈度以'令其停止'为度而非'令其毁灭'为度
  - 导入受控轨道：在对方停止后立即给出新岗位（规则内的角色、可参与的收益）——威慑的收尾是收编而非惩罚
  - 固定为承重结构：把被收编者的利益与新体系的存续绑定——让'反对你'的成本长期高于'为你工作'
- Process (EN):
  - assess the opponent's energy: gauge the scale, source and mobilization logic of the destructive force—subjugation presumes it has real energy, rather than dismissing it as 'ignorance'
  - switch to the stronger form: without leaving your value core, activate the most deterrent form the opponent can perceive—intensity calibrated to 'make them stop,' not 'make them perish'
  - channel into a controlled track: the moment they stop, offer a new post (a role within the rules, a share of gains)—deterrence ends in conscription, not punishment
  - fix as load-bearing structure: bind the conscripted party's interests to the system's survival—make 'opposing you' permanently costlier than 'working for you'

  - 忿怒莲师（多杰佐烈）仪轨：修者观修忿怒相以降伏内外障碍——威慑形态先用于自身心理治理，后用于对外威慑

## M-PAD-006 灌顶授权法 / Empowerment-Gatekeeping Method

- Definition: 权限管理方法论/危险能力以分层仪式授权：能力、许可、责任、边界四件同授——既不全封也不全开，门禁与安全带一体
- Definition (EN): Permission-management methodology/dangerous capability authorized through tiered rites: capability, license, responsibility and boundary conferred as one—neither total lockdown nor total openness; gate and seatbelt in one
- Source: 《大圆满龙钦心髓前行》灌顶章节；宁玛派'四灌顶'仪轨文献；《秘密集会续》灌顶品；莲师《誓言尊与智慧尊》开示
- Key quote: 未授之钥不开未备之门；授钥之时，誓与钥俱下。 / An unbestowed key opens no unready door; when the key is given, the vow descends with it.
- Process:
  - 给能力定危险级：评估目标能力的伤害半径与可逆性——危险级决定授权的层级数与门禁强度
  - 设计分层钥匙：把能力拆解为可逐级授予的层级，每级配对应的训练、许可与操作范围——不授完整钥匙给未备之人
  - 捆绑责任签署：每次授权同时签下使用边界与违规后果——让获得能力者在获得时就了解责任的全部重量
  - 设置持续合规：授权不是一次性事件——建立定期复检、吊销机制与同伴监督，让许可处于'持续有效'而非'永久持有'状态
- Process (EN):
  - rate the capability's danger: assess harm radius and reversibility—danger class determines the number of tiers and gate strength
  - design tiered keys: decompose the capability into grantable levels, each with its training, license and operating range—never a full key to the unready
  - bind signed responsibility: every grant carries its usage boundary and consequences—so the newly capable learn the full weight of responsibility at the moment of gaining capability
  - install continuous compliance: authorization is not one-off—periodic re-checks, revocation and peer oversight keep the license 'currently valid' rather than 'permanently held'
- Representative cases:
  - 莲花生为赤松德赞等'君臣二十五弟子'分层灌顶：按各自根器授予不同层级的修法许可——同一师门、不同权限
  - 四灌顶次第制度：宝瓶（身）→秘密（语）→智慧（意）→词义（心性直指）——最危险的'心性直指'放在最高层级并配最重誓言
  - 《前行引导文》要求灌顶前完成出离心、菩提心等前行训练——授权前的强制训练是门禁的一部分而非可选项
- Representative cases (EN):
  - Padmasambhava gave tiered empowerment to the 'twenty-five disciples' of the king and court: practice licenses granted by each one's capacity—one master, different permissions
  - The four-empowerment sequence: vase (body) → secret (speech) → wisdom (mind) → word-meaning (direct pointing at mind-nature)—the most dangerous 'direct pointing' sits at the top tier with the heaviest vows
  - The Ngondro preliminaries require renunciation and bodhicitta training before empowerment—mandatory pre-authorization training as part of the gate, not an option
- Modern applications:
  - 安全行业的分级权限体系：root 权限、生产环境变更权、密钥访问权分层授予并绑定审计与吊销——'能力与责任同授'的工程实现

  - AI 系统的能力分级部署：模型能力按危险级分层开放（API 审核、红队测试、部署配额）——灌顶逻辑在通用技术上的翻版
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Tiered privilege in security engineering: root, production-change and key-access rights granted in tiers, bound to audit and revocation—the engineering realization of 'capability and responsibility conferred together'
  - Prescription authority in medicine: credentials for anesthetics and chemotherapy require training, examination and periodic recertification—tiered authorization with continuous compliance
  - Capability-tiered AI deployment: model capabilities opened by danger class (API vetting, red-teaming, deployment quotas)—the abhisheka logic transposed to general technology
- Related modes: M-PAD-003（本尊相应法——灌顶授权的对象正是认同练习：没有边界设定的认同是心理危险品，灌顶是它的安全带）; M-PAD-004（双轨并立法——灌顶是显密双轨之间的晋升门槛：双轨制度的安全阀）; M-PAD-007（中阴导引法——中阴导引本身是'词义灌顶'层级的知识：最深的导引只授予完成全部层级者）; M-AQU-001（阿奎那·信仰与理性分层法——远缘对照：阿奎那给教义分'理性可证/须启示'两层并各配方法，灌顶给密法分四级并各配许可——都以分层管理知识的危险度）; M-HAY-001（哈耶克·自发秩序法——反向对照：哈耶克警惕一切人为门禁对扩散的阻碍，灌顶制度相信无门禁的扩散必致灾难——知识扩散的放任与门禁之争）

## M-PAD-007 中阴导引法 / Bardo-Navigation Method

- Definition: 危机过渡方法论/把剧烈过渡预先分解为带路标的阶段序列并逐段预写导引——把'不认识发生了什么'的恐惧替换为'认出'的行动
- Definition (EN): Crisis-transition methodology/decompose violent transitions in advance into stages with landmarks, pre-writing guidance for each—replacing the fear of 'not knowing what is happening' with the act of 'recognizing'
- Source: 《中阴闻教得度》（Bardo Thodol，噶玛林巴伏藏）；《六中阴教导》（莲师传措嘉伏藏文献）；《大圆满中阴愿文》
- Key quote: 尊贵的某某，谛听：你所见的焰光，非自外来——是你自心之光明；认出它，即是解脱。 / O noble one, listen: the radiance you see comes not from outside—it is the light of your own mind; to recognize it is liberation.
- Process:
  - 列全过渡的阶段：把目标过渡（死亡、重组、失业）分解为依次出现的阶段，为每阶段写下可识别的征兆——没有路标就没有导航
  - 预写逐段导引：对每阶段预写三行——'你会见到什么'、'它是什么'、'此刻做什么'；导引须短促可直接执行（当事人在过渡态认知资源极低）
  - 在清醒时完整预演：当事人在状态稳定时通读全程剧本并演练关键动作——预演的价值在于让'剧本'进入肌肉记忆
  - 指定在场导引人：过渡发生时由导引人依剧本逐段提示——导引人须熟记全部路标，且提前获得当事人对每个动作的预授权
- Process (EN):
  - enumerate the transition's stages: decompose the target transition (death, reorganization, job loss) into successive stages with recognizable signs for each—no landmarks, no navigation
  - pre-write per-stage guidance: for each stage write three lines—'what you will see,' 'what it is,' 'what to do now'; guidance must be brief and executable (cognitive resources are minimal in transition)
  - rehearse in full while lucid: the person reads the whole script and drills the key moves while stable—rehearsal works by putting the script into procedural memory
  - appoint the on-scene guide: in transition a guide prompts stage by stage per the script—the guide must know every landmark and hold pre-authorization for each action
- Representative cases:
  - 《中阴闻教得度》的完整结构：临终→实相→投生三中阴，每段配'你会见到什么/那是什么/如何应对'的口传导引——死亡导航手册的完整样本
  - 宁玛派'六中阴'教法：把生处、梦境、禅定、临终、实相、投生全部纳入中阴框架——过渡态被系统性扩张为'人生的主要地形'
  - 现代安宁疗护对《度亡经》的借鉴：预告性谈话（anticipatory guidance）与临终导引的实践——中阴方法在世俗临终关怀中的转译
- Representative cases (EN):
  - The Bardo Thodol's full structure: three bardos—dying, reality, becoming—each with oral guidance of 'what you will see / what it is / how to act,' the complete sample of a death navigation manual
  - The Nyingma 'six bardos' teaching: birth, dream, meditation, dying, reality and becoming all brought under the bardo frame—transition states systematized as 'the main terrain of life'
  - Modern hospice's borrowing from the Bardo Thodol: anticipatory guidance and bedside death-navigation practice—the bardo method translated into secular end-of-life care
- Modern applications:
  - 企业并购的'整合中阴手册'：把重组期分解为宣布→震荡→重组→固化四阶段，每阶段预写'你会看到什么（流言、离职潮、指标波动）与该做什么'——把未知的恐慌预演为已知
  - 个人职业过渡的'离职剧本'：辞职前写好'第一个月你会感到什么/什么时候最危险/何时联系谁'——为自己预写导引
  - 灾难应急的'阶段卡片'：地震、火灾的求生指南按'最初10秒/1分钟/10分钟'分段并配明确动作——过渡态认知极低时的预写导引
- Modern applications (EN):
  - The 'integration bardo manual' in M&A: decompose the reorganization into announce → shock → restructure → consolidate, pre-writing 'what you will see (rumors, departures, metric wobble) and what to do'—rehearsing the unknown into the known
  - The personal 'exit script' for career transitions: before resigning, write 'what month one will feel like / when it is most dangerous / whom to call when'—pre-writing your own guidance
  - Staged cards in disaster response: survival guides for earthquake and fire broken into 'first 10 seconds / 1 minute / 10 minutes' with explicit actions—pre-written guidance for when cognition is minimal
- Related modes: M-PAD-001（伏藏封存法——中阴导引正是伏藏体系的旗舰产出：为'未来的每个人'预写的导引，由伏藏师在四百年后掘出）; M-PAD-003（本尊相应法——中阴导引的'认出本尊'环节以本尊认同的日常训练为前提：生前未练，死后认不出）; M-PAD-006（灌顶授权法——中阴导引属于最高层级授权的知识：它的效力依赖当事人对导引者的预先信任授权）; M-SHA-006（商羯罗·亲证检验法——对照：商羯罗要求亲证作为真理标准，中阴导引要求生前预演作为死亡通行证——都拒绝把关键检验推迟到无法补考的时刻）; M-ST-001（斯多亚·消极想象法——同构对照：斯多亚人每天预演失去（premeditatio malorum）以消解恐惧，中阴导引把预演推向极限情境——预告未知是共同的解恐惧机制）

## M-PAD-008 大圆满直指法 / Direct-Pointing-of-Dzogchen Method

- Definition: 根本认知方法论/觉性本自具足、修行只是认出：师的工作不是传授而是制造让弟子看见'我本具足'的契机——获得先于努力，守护重于获取
- Definition (EN): Foundational-epistemology methodology/awareness is innate and practice merely recognizes it: the master's work is not transmission but creating the occasion for the disciple to see 'I already have it'—acquisition precedes effort, guarding outweighs gaining
- Source: 《大圆满椎击三要》（措嘉记录莲师开示）；《无垢光尊者七宝藏》（龙钦巴）；《且却与妥噶》宁玛派口传文献；《声应成续》
- Key quote: 云散时方见晴空，然晴空非云散所造——觉性亦然：非修所成，唯为认出。 / Only when clouds part do you see the clear sky, yet the sky was not made by their parting—so with awareness: not made by practice, only recognized.
- Process:
  - 确信'已在'：先审查目标（觉性、天赋、领导力）是否真的本自具足而非可制造——若非本具，此法不适用，改用渐修法
  - 制造契机而非讲课：师不铺陈理论，而设计让对象'亲眼看见自己已具备'的瞬间（高压后的松弛、意外的惊愕、直指的提问）——契机指向'看'，不指向'学'
  - 当场确认：在契机发生的当下，请对象确认'就是此刻这个'——确认要具体（那能听、能知、不随念头走的），防止错认念头为觉性
  - 转入守护：认出之后切换模式——不再追加新知识，而是设计让认出不丢失的日常（短而频的回认、情境触发器、同伴互查）
- Process (EN):
  - confirm it is innate: first examine whether the target (awareness, talent, leadership) is truly already present rather than manufacturable—if not innate, this method does not apply; switch to gradual cultivation
  - create occasions, not lectures: the master lays out no theory but designs moments for the object to 'see for themselves they already have it' (release after pressure, sudden shock, a pointing question)—the occasion points at seeing, not studying
  - confirm on the spot: at the moment of the occasion, have the object confirm 'just this, now'—confirmation must be specific (that which hears, knows, and does not follow thoughts), preventing mistaking a thought for awareness
  - shift to guarding: after recognition, change modes—no new knowledge is added; instead design daily routines that keep the recognition (brief frequent re-recognitions, situational triggers, peer checks)
- Representative cases:
  - 莲师'椎击三要'：以'直指本觉→于此决断→解脱自信'三句完成从认出到守护的完整路线——大圆满方法论的浓缩立法
  - 传统公案中的'直指'时刻：弟子问'什么是佛'，师答'你问话的那个是谁'——把寻求者当场转向寻求者自身，认出'能问者即所要找的'
  - 《七宝藏》对'错认'的系统排查：龙钦巴列出把念头、空感、乐受误认为觉性的十余种歧途——直指法的纠错清单
- Representative cases (EN):
  - Padmasambhava's 'three statements that strike the vital point': 'direct introduction to awareness, decisive confidence therein, confidence in liberation therein'—the condensed legislation of the route from recognition to guarding
  - The 'direct pointing' moment in classic koans: asked 'what is Buddha,' the master answers 'who is the one asking?'—turning the seeker on the spot toward the seeker, recognizing 'the one asking is what is sought'
  - The Seven Treasures' systematic survey of misrecognition: Longchenpa lists over a dozen cul-de-sacs of mistaking thoughts, blankness or bliss for awareness—the pointing method's error checklist
- Modern applications:
  - 导师制的'直指式反馈'：不铺陈理论，而是在学员真实工作的高压后松弛一刻直接指出'你刚才的处理就是这种能力本身'——让学员确认而非学习
  - 产品设计的'功能已存在'原则：先确认用户'本自具足'的工作流，再做减法移除遮蔽它的环节（而非叠加新功能）——大圆满减法在界面上的应用
  - 组织人才盘点中的'已具备优先'：把高潜人才的问题从'缺什么培训'改为'其已有的哪项能力未被组织看见与使用'——先认出再配置
- Modern applications (EN):
  - Direct-pointing feedback in mentoring: no theory-laying; instead, at the release right after a stretch assignment, point straight: 'what you just did is the capability itself'—having the learner confirm rather than learn
  - The 'capability already exists' principle in product design: first confirm the workflow users innately have, then subtract the steps obscuring it (rather than adding features)—Dzogchen subtraction applied to interfaces
  - 'Already-have-first' in talent review: change the question for high potentials from 'what training is missing' to 'which existing capability does the organization fail to see and use'—recognize first, then deploy
- Related modes: M-PAD-003（本尊相应法——生起次第的'造'与直指的'认'构成大圆满的前后两翼：先在想象中生成本尊，最终认出观者本身不需生成）; M-PAD-007（中阴导引法——中阴导引的'认出光明即自心'正是直指法的临终应用：生前认得熟，死后才认得出）; M-PAD-006（灌顶授权法——直指是最高层级的'词义灌顶'内容：最直接的教导配最重的门槛与誓言）; M-SHA-001（商羯罗·不二还原法——深层对照：商羯罗的'你即梵'（tat tvam asi）与大圆满直指结构同源——都是'终极认识是被指出的而非修成的'；分野在于商羯罗以经典为权威源头，大圆满以师者的当下直指为源头）; M-ZZ-001（张载·大心体物法——远缘对照：张载'大其心则能体天下之物'以扩展心量达于万物一体，大圆满以认出本具觉性达于显空不二——两个'心本来够大'的传统）
- Representative figures:
  - 莲花生 / Padmasambhava: 本模式的原型——把大圆满教法传入西藏并留下'椎击三要'的直指立法者 / the prototype—the introducer of Dzogchen into Tibet and legislator of the 'three statements that strike the vital point'
  - 龙钦巴 / Longchenpa: 直指法的系统整理者（十四世纪）——《七宝藏》把直指、错认排查与守护次第编为完整学理 / the systematizer of direct pointing (fourteenth century)—the Seven Treasures codified introduction, error-survey and guarding into a full doctrine
  - 苏格拉底 / Socrates: 跨文明同构——'知识已在、师者接生'的产婆术与直指法共享'先在性+指出者'结构 / the cross-civilizational isomorph—'knowledge is already there, the teacher midwifes' shares the innate-plus-pointer structure with direct introduction

## M-PAD-010 感恩托付法 / Gratitude-Entrustment Method

- Definition: 传承收束方法论/以感恩把成果归还源头传承链、以托付把体系交给作为'行使者'的后人——体系的完成者自认'取宝人'而非立法者
- Definition (EN): Legacy-closure methodology/return the fruits to the source lineage with gratitude and hand the system to successors as 'executors'—the completer claims 'treasure-fetcher,' not legislator
- Source: 《莲花遗教》发愿与回向章节；伏藏文献通例之'顶礼上师'开篇；《益西措嘉佛母传》所载莲师临别嘱咐；宁玛派'上师相应法'文献
- Key quote: 法非我造，乃自师承；藏非我有，乃待后人——我只是雪域的取宝人。 / The dharma is not mine but my lineage's; the treasure is not mine but awaits posterity's hand—I am only Tibet's treasure-fetcher.
- Process:
  - 在完成时点做'贡献归属审计'：逐项列出体系成果中属于传承、属于协作、属于时代机缘、属于本人的份额——审计本身即防神化的第一步
  - 以归源叙述重写成果文本：把'我发明了X'改写为'依师传之X，于此地新境中显发'——保留贡献事实、变更权威结构
  - 设计托付契约：明确后人以什么身份接手（行使者而非继承人）、接手时获得什么义务（守护条件、再传责任）——托付是双向契约而非单向遗赠
  - 以感恩仪式收束：为体系开篇与结尾安置礼敬文本，使'注意力从作者导向链'制度化——让每一次被阅读都是一次归源
- Process (EN):
  - run a contribution-attribution audit at completion: itemize which shares of the system's fruits belong to lineage, collaboration, circumstance and oneself—the audit itself is step one against deification
  - rewrite the fruits' texts in source-returning narrative: 'I invented X' becomes 'following my masters' transmission of X, unfolded in this new terrain'—facts kept, authority structure changed
  - design the entrustment contract: define in what capacity successors take over (executor, not heir) and what obligations come with it (keeping conditions, re-transmission duty)—entrustment is a two-way contract, not a one-way bequest
  - close with gratitude rites: install homage texts at the system's opening and end, institutionalizing 'attention directed from author to chain'—every future reading becomes an act of source-return
- Representative cases:
