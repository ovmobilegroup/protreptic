**Core Contribution (English)**: An Zijie participated in Shenzhen SEZ construction. Gradual Reform (Mode 8): pilot-first. Crossing River by Feeling Stones (Mode 38): Three Favorables evaluation of SEZ effects. Birdcage Economy/Boundary Thinking (Mode 39): export-oriented, attract foreign investment, technology import. United Front (Mode 10): unite overseas Chinese, Hong Kong/Macau compatriots, foreign investors.  

**执行步骤 (中文)**:
1. 第1步：找切入点——出口创汇、吸引外资、技术引进

3. 第3步：红绿灯评估——三个有利于评估特区成效
4. 第4步：经验固化——总结特区经验、形成可复制政策
5. 第5步：逐步扩大——沿海开放城市、长江三角洲、珠江三角洲、闽三角
6. 第6步：制度化——特区立法权、外资法规、海关监管体制

**Execution Steps (English)**:
1. Step 1: Find entry point - export foreign exchange, attract foreign investment, technology import
2. Step 2: Small-scale pilots - Shenzhen, Zhuhai, Shantou, Xiamen four SEZs
3. Step 3: Red-green light evaluation - Three Favorables evaluate SEZ effectiveness
4. Step 4: Experience solidification - summarize SEZ experience, form replicable policies
5. Step 5: Gradual expansion - coastal open cities, Yangtze Delta, Pearl River Delta, Min Delta
6. Step 6: Institutionalization - SEZ legislative power, foreign investment laws, customs supervision system

**预期成果 (中文)**: ['经济特区建设', '对外开放政策', '渐进式改革']  
**Expected Outcome (English)**: ["Build China's first generation SEZs", 'Attract foreign investment and technology', 'Form opening up gradual methodology']  

**经典案例 (中文)**: 安子介的核心实践：经济特区建设；对外开放政策；渐进式改革
