# 胡适 (H-HS-001) 胡适 Hu Shih

- 年代 Era: 清末至台湾时期 / Late Qing to Taiwan era (1891-1962)
- 文明 Civilization: 中国（东亚） / China (East Asian)
- 角色 Roles: 学者、白话文运动领袖、新文化运动旗手、教育家、外交官、思想方法论的提倡者
- 模式数 Modes: 10 (M-HS-001, M-HS-002, M-HS-003, M-HS-004, M-HS-005, M-HS-006, M-HS-007, M-HS-008, M-HS-009, M-HS-010)

## 概述 Summary

新文化运动的核心旗手，白话文运动的首倡者，中国现代学术范式的奠基人之一。核心方法论：以『大胆的假设，小心的求证』十字诀重写治学程序、以历史演化眼光（『历史的态度』）把一切制度与价值看作可变的演进序列、以『研究问题，输入学理，整理国故，再造文明』为新文明的建设性纲领、以白话文革命把认知工具从文言贵族手里交给大众、以『多研究些问题，少谈些主义』把论战拉回可验证的具体问题层面、以容忍与自由互为条件化解教条冲突、以『功不唐捐』的长期主义对冲一时成败。他是把怀疑精神制度化、把改良主义操作化的转换器式人物。

The central standard-bearer of the New Culture Movement, initiator of the vernacular language revolution, and a founder of modern Chinese scholarship. Core methodology: the ten-character formula 'bold hypotheses, careful verification' rewriting the procedure of inquiry, the historical attitude (evolutionary vision) that treats every institution and value as a mutable evolving series, the constructive program of 'study problems, import theories, reorganize the national heritage, recreate civilization,' the vernacular revolution that handed the instrument of cognition from the classical-literati elite to the masses, 'study more problems, talk less of isms' to pull polemics back to the level of verifiable specific questions, tolerance and liberty as mutual conditions for dissolving dogmatic conflict, and the long-termism of 'no effort is ever wasted' to hedge short-term outcomes. He was a converter-figure who institutionalized the spirit of doubt and operationalized reformism.

## 思维模式 Thinking Modes

### M-HS-001 假设求证十字法 / Bold-Hypothesis-Careful-Verification Method

- 领域: 科学方法论的本土化/学术程序设计 (认识论/治学方法)
- 定义: 胡适面对的根本问题是：中国有考据传统但没有系统的科学方法，杜威的实验主义又太哲学化，普通人拿不动。他的解法是把实验主义压缩成十字诀——『大胆的假设，小心的求证』。假设要大胆，因为不大胆就提不出有信息量的命题；求证要小心，因为证据是唯一能处死错误假设的刑具。两者缺一即废：只有大胆没有小心是清谈，只有小心没有大胆是抄卡片。方法上这意味着：把一套复杂方法论压缩为可执行、可传诵的最小程序对（生成假设的自由度 + 检验假设的严格度），让非专业人士也能照做。
- Definition: Hu Shih faced a fundamental problem: China had a philological tradition but no systematic scientific method, while Dewey's experimentalism was too philosophical for ordinary hands. His solution was to compress experimentalism into a ten-character formula—'bold hypotheses, careful verification.' Hypotheses must be bold, for without boldness no informative proposition gets proposed; verification must be careful, for evidence is the only executioner of wrong hypotheses. Either half alone fails: boldness without care is empty talk; care without boldness is card-copying. Methodologically: compress a complex methodology into a minimal executable, transmissible procedure-pair (freedom for generating hypotheses + rigor for testing them), so non-specialists can follow it too.
- 金句: 大胆的假设，小心的求证——假设不大胆，不能有新发明；证据不充分，不能使人信仰。
- Quote: Bold hypotheses, careful verification—without bold hypotheses no new discoveries; without sufficient evidence, no conviction.
- 四步过程: 对悬而未决的问题，先故意提出反直觉、有信息量的假设（宁可大胆，不可平庸） → 把假设翻译成『若为真，则应能找到何类证据』的可检验形式 → 以最严格的证据标准去搜求证伪材料——主动去找能杀死自己假设的证据 → 证据不足时悬而不断，把结论的置信度与证据强度对齐
- Process: For an open problem, deliberately propose counter-intuitive, informative hypotheses (prefer bold over bland) → Translate the hypothesis into testable form: 'if true, what kind of evidence should exist?' → Search for disconfirming material under the strictest standards—actively hunt evidence that could kill your own hypothesis → Suspend judgment when evidence is insufficient; align the confidence of conclusions with the strength of evidence
- 关键概念: 大胆假设、小心求证、实验主义、证据主义、方法论压缩
- 关联模式: M-HS-002, M-HS-003, M-HS-005

### M-HS-002 历史演化态度法 / Historical-Evolutionary Attitude Method

- 领域: 把一切制度与观念看作可变的演进序列 (史学方法论/认识论)
- 定义: 胡适说的『历史的态度』（historical attitude）是一种元方法：任何制度、文本、观念都不是天生如此，而是从某个源头开始一层层演化出来的。一旦看清它是『怎么变成这样的』，它的神圣性与必然性就自动瓦解——文言文不是汉语的天然形态，只是两千年书写制度的沉积；贞节不是永恒道德，只是某一时代的规范。这套眼光既用于解放（去魅），也用于研究（追问演化链条上的每一次变异及其原因）。方法上这意味着：面对任何『理所当然』之物，先追问它的发生史；神圣感往往是演化过程被遗忘的症状。
- Definition: Hu Shih's 'historical attitude' is a meta-method: no institution, text, or idea is naturally what it is—it evolved, layer by layer, from some origin. Once you see how it 'came to be this way,' its sacredness and inevitability dissolve on their own—classical Chinese is not the natural form of the language but the sediment of two millennia of writing institutions; chastity is not eternal morality but the norm of one era. This vision serves both liberation (demystification) and research (asking after each mutation in the evolutionary chain and its causes). Methodologically: for anything that seems self-evident, first ask for its genesis; a sense of the sacred is often the symptom of a forgotten evolution.
- 金句: 研究问题要下工夫，须要有历史的眼光——一切制度都是历史的产物，知道了它的来历，便不觉得它神圣不可侵犯。
- Quote: Studying a problem takes a historical vision—every institution is a product of history; once you know where it came from, it no longer seems too sacred to touch.
- 四步过程: 锁定被视为当然的对象（制度、文类、道德律），拒绝从其当下形态出发 → 向上追溯演化链条：找出最早可考的形态与每一次重大变异的节点 → 对每次变异追问动力：什么条件使旧形态无法维持、新形态胜出 → 以演化史重新给对象定位：它此刻的形态只是链条上的暂时均衡，可以继续演化
- Process: Lock onto the object taken for granted (institution, genre, moral rule) and refuse to start from its present form → Trace the evolutionary chain upward: find the earliest attested form and the nodes of each major mutation → Ask the driver of each mutation: what conditions made the old form untenable and the new form win → Relocate the object via its history: its present shape is only a temporary equilibrium in the chain, capable of further evolution
- 关键概念: 历史的态度、明变求因、去魅、演化序列、评判的态度
- 关联模式: M-HS-001, M-HS-004, M-HS-006

### M-HS-003 问题主义过滤法 / Problem-over-Ism Filtering Method

- 领域: 把主义话语还原为具体问题 (社会思想/论辩方法)
- 定义: 1919年『问题与主义』论战中，胡适面对的现象是：抽象主义话语正在吞噬公共讨论——人人谈根本解决，个个谈论主义，具体问题（人力车夫的生计、大总统的权限、加入何种联盟）反而无人研究。他的解法是设立一道过滤程序：任何宏大主张必须先分解为若干可研究、可验证、可逐步解决的具体问题，否则不予讨论。这不反主义本身，而是拒绝主义作为免检通行证。方法上这意味着：当讨论被抽象名词绑架时，用『这个主张具体要求改变什么？证据何在？第一步做什么？』把对话拉回可操作层面。
- Definition: In the 1919 debate on 'Problems and Isms,' Hu Shih faced this phenomenon: abstract ideological discourse was devouring public discussion—everyone preached total solutions and talked isms, while concrete problems (rickshaw pullers' livelihoods, the president's powers, which alliance to join) went unstudied. His solution was a filtering procedure: any grand claim must first be decomposed into specific problems that can be studied, verified, and solved stepwise, or it is refused discussion. He did not oppose isms as such; he refused to let an ism serve as an inspection-free pass. Methodologically: when discussion is hijacked by abstract nouns, pull it back to the operable level with 'what does this claim concretely propose to change? where is the evidence? what is the first step?'
- 金句: 主义起于问题……空谈好听的『主义』是极容易的事，是阿猫阿狗都能做的事，研究问题才是极困难的事。
- Quote: Isms arise from problems... To prate of a pleasing 'ism' is the easiest thing in the world—any cat or dog can do it; studying problems is what is truly hard.
- 四步过程: 识别讨论中的抽象名词与一揽子承诺（『根本解决』『彻底改造』） → 对每个大主张追问三件事：具体要改变什么？依据什么证据？第一步行动是什么？ → 把通过过滤的主张登记为问题清单，按可解决性与紧迫性排序 → 以逐个问题的解决进度作为评判标准的唯一来源，拒绝用主义站队替代验证
- Process: Identify abstract nouns and package promises in a discussion ('total solution,' 'thorough transformation') → Ask three things of every grand claim: what concretely to change? on what evidence? what is the first step? → Register claims that pass the filter as a problem list, ranked by solvability and urgency → Judge by the solving progress on problems alone; refuse to substitute ism-alignment for verification
- 关键概念: 问题与主义、一揽子解决的迷思、逐步改良、具体性过滤、主义的假设化
- 关联模式: M-HS-001, M-HS-005, M-HS-008

### M-HS-004 白话工具革命法 / Vernacular-Tool Revolution Method

- 领域: 认知工具的下沉/传播介质革命 (文化传播/语言哲学)
- 定义: 胡适观察到：中国的知识垄断不在学校而在文字——文言是一门需要十年苦学才能掌握的排他性工具，掌握它的人构成一个靠工具壁垒维系的士大夫阶级。白话文运动的本质不是文学风格之争，而是工具革命：把表达与阅读的成本降低一个数量级，让识字与思考脱钩于阶级。他的策略耐人寻味——不写理论檄文而写《文学改良刍议》这样温和的『刍议』，用『历史演化的文学史论』证明白话自古是活文学，让革命以『复归正统』的面目出现，降低反对面。方法上这意味着：当瓶颈是一套排他性工具时，最深的革命是替换工具本身，且要为革命设计一条阻力最小的合法性叙事。
- Definition: Hu Shih observed that China's monopoly on knowledge lay not in schools but in script—classical Chinese was an exclusionary tool requiring a decade of bitter study, and its masters formed a literati class sustained by the tool barrier itself. The vernacular movement was thus not a quarrel over literary style but a tool revolution: cut the cost of expression and reading by an order of magnitude, decoupling literacy and thinking from class. His strategy is telling—not a thunderous manifesto but the mild 'Modest Proposals for Literary Reform,' and a literary history proving the vernacular had always been living literature, so that the revolution arrived in the guise of 'returning to orthodoxy,' minimizing resistance. Methodologically: when the bottleneck is an exclusionary tool, the deepest revolution is replacing the tool itself—and design the least-resistant legitimacy narrative for the revolution.
- 金句: 要有活的文学，先要有活的语言；白话是文学的正宗，是几千年来活人用活的语言写出的活文学。
- Quote: For living literature you first need a living language; the vernacular is the true vein of literature—the living literature written by living people in living language for millennia.
- 四步过程: 诊断瓶颈是否为『工具本身』而非工具的使用者或制度 → 为替代工具建立合法性叙事：证明新工具并非外来嫁接，而是被遗忘的正统 → 以最小清单发布改革纲领（八不主义），使其可传诵、可执行、可模仿 → 用示范作品（自己的白话诗集、剧本、学术著作）证明新工具的上限，堵住『白话不能表达高深思想』的质疑
- Process: Diagnose whether the bottleneck is the tool itself, not its users or institutions → Build a legitimacy narrative for the replacement tool: show the new tool is not a foreign graft but a forgotten orthodoxy → Publish the program as a minimal checklist (the Eight Don'ts), making it transmissible, executable, imitable → Prove the new tool's ceiling with demonstration works (your own vernacular poems, plays, scholarship), sealing off the objection that the vernacular cannot carry deep thought
- 关键概念: 白话文、工具革命、八不主义、国语的文学、降低表达成本
- 关联模式: M-HS-002, M-HS-006, M-HS-010

### M-HS-005 证据悬判法 / Suspension-of-Judgment-by-Evidence Method

- 领域: 结论置信度与证据强度对齐 (认识论/学术伦理)
- 定义: 胡适治学的标志性纪律是：证据到哪里，结论就到哪里；证据不够，宁可悬而不判。他考据《红楼梦》作者问题时明确区分『已证实的』与『暂定的』，把曹雪芹的自传说标为可修正的假说而非定论。这套纪律的背景是对中国旧学两种病的诊断：一是武断（证据不足强下结论），二是盲从（古人说了就算）。悬判不是懦弱，而是把『不知』公开展示为学术坐标——让后续研究者知道哪块地基是实的、哪块是悬空的。方法上这意味着：结论必须携带自己的证据标签（已证/暂定/悬判），并预留被推翻的接口。
- Definition: The signature discipline of Hu Shih's scholarship: conclusions go only as far as the evidence; where evidence is wanting, suspend judgment. In tracing the authorship of Dream of the Red Chamber he explicitly separated 'the proven' from 'the tentative,' marking the self-fiction hypothesis of Cao Xueqin as a revisable hypothesis, not a verdict. The backdrop is his diagnosis of two diseases of old scholarship: arbitrariness (conclusions outrunning evidence) and blind deference (what the ancients said settles it). Suspension is not timidity but a public display of 'not knowing' as an academic coordinate—letting later researchers know which part of the foundation is solid and which hangs in air. Methodologically: every conclusion must carry its own evidence label (proven / tentative / suspended) and leave an interface for refutation.
- 金句: 有一分证据，只可说一分话；有三分证据，然后可说三分话——治国学必须处处存一个搜求证据的目的。
- Quote: With one measure of evidence, say one measure; with three measures, say three—in studying the heritage, everywhere hold the purpose of hunting evidence.
- 四步过程: 为每个候选结论盘点证据：直接证据、间接证据、无证据各占多少 → 按证据强度给结论贴标签：已证实/暂定/存疑，标签与结论绑定发布 → 对证据不足的部分显式宣布悬判，并说明补齐证据需要什么材料 → 新的反证出现时，按承诺的接口自动降级或推翻旧结论，不为面子护短
- Process: Inventory evidence for each candidate conclusion: direct, indirect, none—how much of each → Label conclusions by evidence strength: proven / tentative / open, label bound to the conclusion on publication → Explicitly declare suspension where evidence falls short, and state what material would complete it → When counter-evidence arrives, downgrade or overturn per the promised interface—never defend for face
- 关键概念: 有一分证据说一分话、悬而不判、证据等级、可修正假说、反武断
- 关联模式: M-HS-001, M-HS-003, M-HS-009

### M-HS-006 再造文明纲领法 / Recreating-Civilization Program Method

- 领域: 新文明的建设性纲领设计 (文化战略/文明论)
- 定义: 胡适批评新文化运动中的两种失败姿态：一是国粹派的『保存』（把文明当标本），二是全盘摧毁派的『破坏』（只有拆没有建）。他给出的纲领是四件事——『研究问题，输入学理，整理国故，再造文明』：问题提供现实动力，学理提供工具库存，整理国故使旧资源在新框架下可再生，最后的落点是再造而非守成或清空。这套纲领的关键在『整理』二字：不是判旧学死刑，而是把它送进现代学术程序重新登记。方法上这意味着：文明转型的建设性路线是四步并举的流水线——没有问题导向会空转，没有学理会低效，没有整理国故会断根，没有再造落点会沦为整理癖。
- Definition: Hu Shih criticized two failed postures in the New Culture Movement: the classicists' 'preservation' (treating civilization as a specimen) and the total-destroyers' 'demolition' (all teardown, no build). His program had four items—'study problems, import theories, reorganize the national heritage, recreate civilization': problems supply the live drive, theories the tool inventory, reorganizing the heritage makes old resources renewable under a new framework, and the terminus is recreation, not preservation or clearing. The pivot is 'reorganize': not sentencing old learning to death but enrolling it in modern scholarly procedure for re-registration. Methodologically: the constructive route of civilizational transition is a four-step pipeline run in parallel—without problem orientation it idles; without imported theory it is inefficient; without reorganizing the heritage it severs roots; without recreation as terminus it degenerates into a reorganizing mania.
- 金句: 新思潮的唯一目的是再造文明——文明不是笼统造成的，是一点一滴造成的。
- Quote: The sole aim of the new intellectual current is to recreate civilization—civilization is not made wholesale, but drop by drop.
- 四步过程: 开问题清单：从当下生活中列出真实痛感的问题序列，作为运动的问题库 → 建学理库存：系统翻译与引进方法论工具，使每个问题有可用的分析武器 → 设整理程序：把旧学术按现代标准重新分类、注释、编目，使其可被新框架调用 → 立再造落点：定期检查四步的产出是否汇聚为新的建设性成果，防止运动退化为批判表演
- Process: Open a problem list: derive from present life a sequence of genuinely felt problems as the movement's problem bank → Build a theory inventory: systematically translate and import methodological tools so every problem has usable analytical weapons → Set a reorganizing procedure: reclassify, annotate, and catalog old scholarship to modern standards so the new framework can call on it → Fix the recreation terminus: periodically check whether the four steps' outputs converge into constructive results, lest the movement decay into a performance of critique
- 关键概念: 再造文明、整理国故、输入学理、评判的态度、建设性改良
- 关联模式: M-HS-002, M-HS-004, M-HS-008

### M-HS-007 容忍自由互生法 / Tolerance-as-Condition-of-Liberty Method

- 领域: 自由的条件结构/异见的伦理基础 (政治哲学/伦理学)
- 定义: 胡适晚年提出他自称最重要的洞见：容忍比自由更重要，容忍是一切自由的根本——没有容忍异己的雅量，任何人都可能在掌权后用自己的『自由』去压别人的自由。他给这个命题一个认识论地基：一切关于『绝对正确』的确信（他自己青年时代的『必以吾辈所主张者为绝对之是』就曾如此）都是不容忍的心理根源；而人是易错的，谁也没有资格自认终审。方法上这意味着：自由不是靠宣告获得，而是靠培养一种对自己确信的怀疑习惯来维护；你为异己保留的空间，正是你自己的自由在下一个政权手里的唯一担保。
- Definition: In his later years Hu Shih offered what he called his most important insight: tolerance matters more than liberty, and tolerance is the root of all freedom—without the magnanimity to tolerate dissenters, anyone in power may use his own 'liberty' to suppress the liberty of others. He gave the thesis an epistemic base: all conviction of 'absolute rightness' (his own youthful belief that 'what we hold must be absolutely right' was one such) is the psychological source of intolerance; and since humans are fallible, no one is entitled to sit as final judge. Methodologically: liberty is won not by proclamation but by cultivating the habit of doubting one's own convictions; the space you reserve for dissenters is the only guarantee your own freedom will have in the next regime's hands.
- 金句: 容忍是一切自由的根本——没有容忍，就没有自由。
- Quote: Tolerance is the root of all freedom—without tolerance there is no freedom.
- 四步过程: 审计自己的『绝对之是』清单：哪些信念被我视作不容讨论的终审结论 → 给每个终审信念补上易错性标签：我可能在哪些条件下错了 → 主动为异己观点设立受保护空间（发表、辩论、免于报复），并公开承诺不行使终审权 → 以制度而非人格担保：把容忍写成规则（如辩论程序、议事规则），使其不依赖掌权者的雅量
- Process: Audit your list of 'absolute rights': which beliefs do I hold as beyond discussion, as final verdicts → Attach a fallibility label to each terminal belief: under what conditions might I be wrong → Actively create protected space for dissent (publication, debate, immunity from retaliation) and publicly pledge never to exercise final judgment → Guarantee by institution, not personality: write tolerance into rules (debate procedures, rules of order) so it does not depend on the magnanimity of whoever holds power
- 关键概念: 容忍与自由、易错性、绝对之是的迷思、异见伦理、自我怀疑的习惯
- 关联模式: M-HS-003, M-HS-008, M-HS-009

### M-HS-008 点滴改良渐进法 / Drop-by-Drop Incremental Reform Method

- 领域: 改良主义的具体化/对革命万能论的替代 (社会变革/行动哲学)
- 定义: 胡适的社会变革观建立在一个判断上：『文明不是笼统造成的，是一点一滴造成的』；『解放不是笼统解放，改造也不是笼统改造』。革命话语承诺一次性重置，但重置后的制度仍要靠逐日运转，而运转的具体能力无法靠宣言获得。他的替代方案是把变革单位从『制度』降为『问题』：一个一个问题研究、一项一项改造，用可核查的增量累积代替不可核查的总承诺。这不是胆怯，而是一种对复杂性的尊重——社会是耦合系统， wholesale 重置的成本总会在细节处清算。方法上这意味着：以问题清单为变革的单位，以每项的可验证改善为进度条，拒绝以最终蓝图的正当性豁免中间过程的错误。
- Definition: Hu Shih's view of social change rests on one judgment: 'civilization is not made wholesale but drop by drop'; 'liberation is not wholesale, nor is transformation.' Revolutionary discourse promises a one-shot reset, but the institutions after the reset must still run day by day, and the concrete capacity to run them cannot come from a manifesto. His alternative lowers the unit of change from 'the system' to 'the problem': study problems one by one, transform item by item, accumulate verifiable increments instead of unverifiable total promises. This is not timidity but respect for complexity—society is a coupled system, and the cost of a wholesale reset is always settled in the details. Methodologically: take the problem list as the unit of change and each item's verifiable improvement as the progress bar, refusing to exempt mid-process errors on the strength of a blueprint's ultimate legitimacy.
- 金句: 文明是一点一滴的进化，解放是一点一滴的解放——改造也必须是一点一滴的改造。
- Quote: Civilization evolves drop by drop, liberation comes drop by drop—and transformation must likewise be drop by drop.
- 四步过程: 把总目标拆成问题清单，每项标明可验证的改善标准 → 优先解决『可动』的问题：从现有条件就能启动的最小改造单元入手 → 每项改造做完即验收并公示，用已完成清单替代宏大叙事作为合法性来源 → 对总蓝图保持工具化态度：蓝图随问题清单的进展而修订，不反过来为蓝图牺牲清单上的具体改善
- Process: Split the total goal into a problem list, each item marked with a verifiable improvement standard → Solve the 'movable' problems first: start from the minimal transformation units launchable under current conditions → Accept and publicize each completed item; let the completed list, not grand narrative, be the source of legitimacy → Hold the total blueprint instrumentally: revise it as the list progresses; never sacrifice concrete improvements on the list for the blueprint's sake
- 关键概念: 点滴进化、逐步改良、问题的变革单位、反一揽子承诺、可核查增量
- 关联模式: M-HS-003, M-HS-006, M-HS-010

### M-HS-009 功不唐捐长期法 / No-Effort-Ever-Wasted Long-Termism Method

- 领域: 长期主义的心理结构/对无用感的对冲 (人生战略/时间哲学)
- 定义: 『功不唐捐』（佛典语：功德不会白费）是胡适对抗虚无的时间哲学：他一生多次被打成过时人物——白话赢了之后被新派讥为保守，被旧派骂为名教罪人，政权更迭间两头挨打——但其回应不是转向投机，而是把每一件认真做过的功课视为不会消失的沉积：『没有一点努力是会白白丢了的』。这套方法的前提是把成果的计量单位从『一时的胜败』换为『沉积的总量』：一篇考据、一次讲演、一个被时代冷落的立场，都可能在几十年后成为别人的地基。方法上这意味着：在无法预测哪份努力会被兑现的环境里，以『只做经得起时间的工作』替代『预测什么会走红』。
- Definition: 'Gong bu tang juan'—no merit is ever wasted (from the Buddhist canon)—is Hu Shih's temporal philosophy against nihilism: repeatedly branded obsolete across his life (mocked as conservative by the new camp once the vernacular won, cursed as an enemy of the moral order by the old camp, beaten from both sides amid regime change), his response was not to speculate on trends but to treat every earnest piece of work as permanent sediment: 'no effort is ever wholly lost.' The premise: switch the unit of account from 'momentary wins and losses' to 'total sediment'—a textual study, a lecture, a position cold-shouldered by the age may become someone's foundation decades later. Methodologically: in an environment where you cannot predict which effort will pay off, replace 'predict what will trend' with 'only do work that withstands time.'
- 金句: 没有一点努力是会白白地丢了的——功不唐捐！
- Quote: No effort is ever wholly wasted—no merit is ever lost in vain!
- 四步过程: 把计量单位从事件胜败改为沉积总量：定期盘点『我留下了什么经得起十年检验的东西』 → 做事时附加时间检验标准：这项工作十年后还有人需要吗 → 冷遇期不转向：区分『被时代冷落』与『经不起检验』——前者继续做，后者才修正 → 相信复利的不可见期：公开承诺长期标准，使自己在无人喝彩时仍有执行依据
- Process: Switch the unit of account from event wins/losses to total sediment: periodically inventory 'what have I left that withstands a ten-year test' → Attach a time test to every task: will this still be needed in ten years? → Do not pivot in a cold season: distinguish 'cold-shouldered by the age' from 'fails the test'—continue the former, fix only the latter → Trust the invisible phase of compounding: commit publicly to long-term standards so you retain grounds to execute when no one applauds
- 关键概念: 功不唐捐、时间沉积、反投机、长期主义、无用之用
- 关联模式: M-HS-001, M-HS-007, M-HS-010

### M-HS-010 终身学童示范法 / Lifelong-Apprentice Exemplification Method

- 领域: 以自身为方法示范物/权威的去魅式领导 (人格养成/领导力)
- 定义: 胡适的领导方式极为特殊：他倡议的每一套方法，都先在自己身上公开做示范——倡白话则自己先写白话诗（并允许写坏）、倡怀疑则先写自我忏悔文章、倡证据则公开修正自己已出版的结论。他把『我可能错』做成了领导力资产而非负债：一个肯当众当学童的人，其倡导的方法才可信。这与传统师道的『师严而后道尊』相反——他不经营无瑕的权威，而经营可核查的诚实。方法上这意味着：推广任何新方法时，最高效的路径不是论证其正确，而是以自己的失败与修正作为公开演示数据；领导者保留被质疑的接口，方法才获得被采纳的接口。
- Definition: Hu Shih's mode of leadership was singular: for every method he advocated, he first ran the demonstration on himself in public—advocating the vernacular, he wrote vernacular poems first (allowing them to be bad); advocating doubt, he wrote self-reproach essays first; advocating evidence, he publicly revised his own published conclusions. He made 'I may be wrong' a leadership asset, not a liability: only when the advocate is willing to play apprentice in public does the method he preaches become credible. This inverts the traditional Confucian teacher's 'the master is stern so the way is honored'—he managed not an unblemished authority but a checkable honesty. Methodologically: in promoting any new method, the most efficient path is not to argue its correctness but to use your own failures and revisions as open demonstration data; a leader who keeps an interface open to being questioned gives his method an interface to be adopted.
- 金句: 我做白话诗，是要用自己来做试验品——试验失败也可以给别人做参考。
- Quote: I write vernacular poems using myself as the test subject—my failures too can serve as reference for others.
- 四步过程: 倡导一项新方法前，先设计一个自己身上可公开执行的示范实验 → 公开承诺修正接口：预先声明何种证据出现时我将收回何种结论 → 把失败写进示范记录（《尝试集》自序即失败报告），使追随者获得真实的成本预期 → 定期做公开的自我批判，维持『学童身份』的可持续性，防止示范者重新变成无瑕权威
- Process: Before advocating a new method, design a demonstration experiment executable on yourself and in public → Commit openly to a revision interface: declare in advance under what evidence I shall retract what conclusion → Write failures into the demonstration record (the preface to Changshi is a failure report), giving followers a realistic cost expectation → Perform public self-critique periodically to keep the apprentice identity sustainable, lest the demonstrator congeal back into unblemished authority
- 关键概念: 终身学童、示范数据、自我修正公开化、权威去魅、领导力的诚实资产
- 关联模式: M-HS-001, M-HS-005, M-HS-007
