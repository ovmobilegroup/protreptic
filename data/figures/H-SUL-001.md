# 苏莱曼大帝 (Suleiman) — H-SUL-001

- Era: 1494 年生于特拉布宗，父亲塞利姆一世时为总督——其母哈芙莎苏丹出自克里米亚汗室，使他从少年起就兼有突厥-鞑靼两支血脉——1510 年代先后任卡法与马尼萨省督，在塞利姆一世東征西讨时留镇帝国本土——1520 年 26 岁继位，史家记录其'不曾经历宫廷阴谋的成长'——在位 46 年，御极之初连下贝尔格莱德（1521）与罗得岛（1522），1526 年莫哈奇一役击溃匈牙利王国、1529 年兵临维也纳城下——东方对萨非波斯三度亲征（1534/1548/1553），西线对哈布斯堡维持匈牙利缓冲附庸——1533 年把北非海盗海雷丁·巴巴罗萨招为帝国海军元帅，1538 年普雷韦扎海战大败基督教联合舰队，同年与法兰西王国缔结正式同盟（基督教大国与奥斯曼的首次结盟）——内政上以四十六年工程颁行一系列 kanunname（敕法集），与沙里亚并行而不悖，博得'Kanuni'（立法者）的称号——1534 年打破百年定制与许蕾姆（罗克塞拉娜）正式成婚，后宫影响力自此有法理身份，开'苏丹女权时期'之先声——以 waqf（宗教捐产）供养苏莱曼尼耶清真寺及帝国公共设施体系，建筑师希南的穹顶把'正义之环'写成空间几何，苏丹本人以笔名 Muhibbi 写下传世情诗——1566 年第七次远征中病逝于锡盖特瓦尔军中，帝国在其治下达于极盛；死后'立法者'后继无人，制度失去人格质押而渐次官僚化
- Era (EN): Born 1494 in Trabzon, the son of Selim I, then a provincial governor—his mother Hafsa Sultan of Crimean khanly descent gave him from boyhood a mixed Turkic-Tatar inheritance—served as governor of Kaffa and Manisa in the 1510s, holding the imperial core while his father campaigned—in 1520 he acceded at twenty-six, chroniclers noting a youth 'unscarred by palace intrigue'—across a 46-year reign he took Belgrade (1521) and Rhodes (1522) at the outset, crushed the kingdom of Hungary at Mohács (1526) and stood beneath Vienna's walls (1529)—thrice campaigned in person against Safavid Persia (1534/1548/1553) while holding the Hungarian buffer against the Habsburgs—in 1533 he made the North African corsair Hayreddin Barbarossa grand admiral; in 1538 the fleet crushed the Christian coalition at Preveza, and in the same year the formal alliance with France was concluded (the first league of a great Christian power with the Ottomans)—at home, across four decades he promulgated a series of kanunnames (statutory codes) running parallel to the sharia without collision, earning the title 'Kanuni' (the Lawgiver)—in 1534 he broke a century of custom by formally wedding Hürrem (Roxelana), giving harem influence a legal identity and heralding the 'Sultanate of Women'—waqf (pious endowment) funded the Süleymaniye mosque and the imperial public-works system; the architect Sinan wrote the 'circle of justice' as dome geometry, while the sultan himself, under the pen name Muhibbi, wrote love lyrics that endure—died in camp at Szigetvár in 1566 on his seventh campaign, the empire at its zenith; after him no heir to 'the Lawgiver' arose, and the institutions, unpledged by a person, slowly bureaucratized

## Historical significance
苏莱曼一世（Suleiman I, 1494-1566），奥斯曼帝国第十位苏丹，在位 46 年（1520-1566），帝国军事、法制与文化极盛期的缔造者，奥斯曼人尊称 Kanuni（立法者），欧洲人称 the Magnificent（壮丽者）。他的历史地位由五件事锚定。其一，双轨法典体系：以敕法集（kanunname）在神启法之外另立世俗法典，税目、刑等、封地逐项数字化，使'法律'在伊斯兰世界内部第一次获得神启之外的第二来源——欧洲人因此称他 the Magnificent，他的臣民则称他 Kanuni。其二，多元治理结构的完成：米勒特叠层（宗教共同体自治+头人质押）、奴官擢升（devshirme bond 精英通道）、蒂玛尔封地（税即饷的战争机器自我供血）三件套在他的治下同时臻于完备，帝国以极薄的官僚层治理横跨三洲的多元人口。其三，海权转轨：招安巴巴罗萨为海军元帅、营建加拉塔与苏埃兹船坞、1538 年普雷韦扎大败联合舰队——陆权帝国在地中海取得十六世纪的支配性海权。其四，均势外交的开创：1536 年法土同盟把'欧洲 vs 奥斯曼'的意识形态框架撬开为均势算术，缓冲附庸、周期战争与楔子同盟构成大国竞争的降维管理范式。其五，文化工程与正当性双账户：waqf 捐产体系使公共品世代免于财政紧缩，希南的穹顶把'正义之环'写成空间几何，Muhibbi 诗篇使'立法者'同时是'诗人'——制度账户与文化账户同步累积正当性。代价与极限：制度的效力高度依赖他个人权威的持续输血——死后'立法者'后继无人，kanun 被乌列玛逐步收编、蒂玛尔渐次世袭化、近卫军从'王之仆'变成'王之王'；许蕾姆与鲁斯坦的宫廷斗争以其子穆斯塔法之死收场，'公正立法者'的圣像在民间叙事中被永久刺破；且制度的财政地基（蒂玛尔自我循环、waqf 死手产权）在扩张停止后反过来侵蚀帝国税基——黄金时代的制度设计里已预埋了衰落的解体线。

## Historical significance (EN)
Suleiman I (1494-1566), tenth sultan of the Ottoman empire, reigned 46 years (1520-1566), builder of the empire's military, legal and cultural zenith—Kanuni (the Lawgiver) to his own subjects, the Magnificent to Europe. Five achievements anchor his standing. First, the two-track code: the kanunnames laid a secular law beside the divine law, fixing taxes, penal grades and fiefs item by item as numbers—'law' acquiring in the heart of the Islamic world a second source beside revelation, so that Europe called him the Magnificent while his subjects called him Kanuni. Second, the completion of plural governance: the millet layering (communal self-rule plus headman hostage), the bonded-elite channel (devshirme), and the timar fief (tax-as-pay, the war machine self-funding) all matured together under him, a thin bureaucracy governing three continents. Third, the pivot to sea: Barbarossa made grand admiral, dockyards at Galata and Suez, Preveza (1538) crushing the Christian fleet—the land empire winning the Mediterranean's sixteenth century. Fourth, the invention of balance-of-power diplomacy: the 1536 Franco-Ottoman alliance prying the ideological frame 'Europe versus the Ottomans' open into arithmetic, with buffers, cyclical war and wedge alliances forming a paradigm of dimension-lowered great-power management. Fifth, culture as a second account of legitimacy: waqf endowments freeing public goods from fiscal cycles, Sinan's domes writing the 'circle of justice' as geometry, the Muhibbi verses making the Lawgiver at once a poet—the institutional and cultural accounts accumulating together. Costs and limits: the institutions lived on his personal authority—after him no heir to 'the Lawgiver,' the kanun reabsorbed by the ulema, the timar turning hereditary, the janissaries from 'the king's servants' into 'the king of kings'; the court struggle of Hürrem and Rüstem ended with his son Mustafa's execution, permanently piercing the icon of 'the just lawgiver' in folk narrative; and the fiscal foundations (the timar's self-circulation, the waqf's dead hand) eroded the tax base once expansion stopped—the golden age's institutional design had the seams of decline pre-sewn.

- Process:
  - 按教籍划单元：以宗教共同体（而非语言、地域或阶层）为治理单元，每个单元配一个苏丹确认的法人领袖——单元划分先于一切治理设计

  - 只卖两项服务：帝国的存在感压缩为军事保护与跨共同体仲裁——凡共同体能自理的，帝国官吏一概不出场，治理成本随外包面同步收缩
- Process (EN):
  - Cut the units by registry: take the religious community (not language, territory or class) as the unit of governance, each with one sultan-confirmed legal head—the cut precedes all governance design
  - Set the umbrella ceiling: list what each community may decide alone (marriage, burial, schooling, charity, internal justice) and what escalates (sedition, treason, inter-communal cases)—both lists written into decree, not left to tacit understanding
  - Hostage the headman: appointment, salary and honor of communal leaders rest with the sultan—translating 'communal loyalty' into the operable variable of one man's interests
  - Sell only two services: compress the empire's presence to military protection and inter-communal arbitration—wherever a community can govern itself, imperial officials never appear, and governance cost shrinks with the outsourced surface
