## M-INM-001 会计学经营检验法 / Accounting-based Management Verification

把会计从'事后记账的文科'改造成'经营的中枢神经'：经营的一切动作必须在会计数字上即时显形，数字与现场一一对应——'会计是经营的中枢，一旦中枢失灵，公司就会瘫倒'。稻盛在创立京瓷后自学会计，发现传统财务报表把经营真相滞后、抽象化，于是立下'筋 attaching骨的经营'原则：每条数字背后必须有实物的对应，每件实物必须有数字的对应——销售最大化与费用最小化之间的差，就是经营者可以直接触摸的经营本身。要害有三：其一，'一一对应'原则（One-to-One Correspondence）杜绝一切'数字与实物脱节'的粉饰空间——票据与货物同行，虚构在复式记账里无处藏身；其二，'完美主义'原则要求100%而非99%——会计上一个百分点的宽容会滚成现场十个百分点的松弛；其三，'玻璃般透明的经营'把账目向全员开放，使数字成为组织共同的认知语言而非管理层的权力工具。方法论签名：凡经营判断，先回到会计的筋与肉——看不见数字的现场是谣言，看不见现场的数字是谎言。

Rebuild accounting from a retrospective clerk's art into the central nervous system of management. Inamori taught himself bookkeeping after founding Kyocera and found that conventional financial statements delay and abstract the operating truth, so he decreed 'muscle management': every number must have a real thing behind it, and every real thing must have its number—what remains between maximized revenue and minimized expense is the very body of management that a leader can touch. Three disciplines carry the mode. First, One-to-One Correspondence closes every gap where figures and things drift apart: invoices travel with goods, so fabrication finds nowhere to hide in the double-entry frame. Second, perfectionism demands one hundred percent, not ninety-nine—tolerating one sloppy point on paper loosens ten points on the shop floor, because the field reads the ledger's tolerance as license. Third, glass-wall transparency opens the books to every employee, converting accounting from a management privilege into the organization's shared cognitive language: when everyone reads the same numbers, padding becomes visible to the very people it would exploit. Inamori's signature test: any operating judgment must return to the muscle and bone of accounting—a site you cannot see in numbers is rumor, and a number you cannot trace to a site is a lie. At JAL he began the turnaround not with layoffs or financing but by teaching this operating accounting to executives and building per-route profit statements, which made every route's earnings visible monthly and carried a bankrupt airline to record profit within two years.

流程: 对照建档：为每类经营物建立'实物—票据—账目'的三向对照档案，凡三者对不上的环节即是管理黑洞 → 现场核数：经营会议不念报表而念现场数字——单位时间核算表的每一格都能指认到具体工序与责任阿米巴 → 双限挤压：把'销售最大化'与'费用最小化'立为并行动词而非被动结果，任何费率上升必须先回答'它带来了多少销售' → 透明公示：账目按月向全员公开，让每个阿米巴看见自己与全局——数字透明化即责任分散化

金句: 会计乃经营之中枢——数字与实物一一对应之处，粉饰无处遁形。

## M-INM-002 阿米巴单元核算法 / Amoeba Unit Accounting

把公司拆成数十到数千个能独立核算的微小单元（阿米巴），每个单元像一家小公司一样经营：独立核算收入、扣除成本、再除以总工时得出'单位时间附加值'——让一线每秒钟都能看见'自己这每小时为组织创造了多少价值'。单元切分遵循三条判据：能独立核算、能独立完成一个业务循环、不破坏公司整体性与使命。要害有三：其一，内部交易按'市场买卖'定价，上一道工序是'卖方'下一道是'买方'，市场的摩擦力被引入组织内部，浪费无处遁形；其二，阿米巴不考核'利润绝对额'而考核'单位时间附加值'——大小单元公平可比，人多的巴不靠人海取胜；其三，核算表的目的是'让员工当主角'而非监控工具：数字每周张贴，人人知道自己每小时值多少钱、浪费了多少钱。方法论签名：组织大到看不见经营真相时，就把组织切成能被一双眼睛看住的单元——每个巴都是一间透明的玻璃房。

Split the company into dozens, eventually thousands, of tiny self-accounting units called amoebas, each run like a small firm: revenue minus costs, divided by total labor hours, yields the unit-time added value that lets frontline members see hourly what they create for the organization. Segmentation obeys three tests: the unit must be able to account for itself independently, complete a full business cycle on its own, and never tear the integrity of the corporate whole—amoebas are amoebas, not pushpins pinned to an org chart. Three mechanisms make it work. First, internal handoffs are priced as market trades: the upstream process is the seller, the downstream is the buyer, so the friction of the market is imported inside the walls and waste becomes expensive to keep. Second, amoebas are not ranked on absolute profit but on unit-time added value, so a large unit cannot win by headcount and a three-person workshop can outshine a factory—fairness is built into the denominator. Third, the accounting sheet exists to make employees the protagonists, not to surveil them: figures are posted weekly, everyone knows what their hour earns and what their hour wastes, and comparison itself becomes the improvement engine. Boundaries are reviewed quarterly and re-divided as markets and technologies shift. When an organization grows too large for any one pair of eyes to see its truth, cut it until each piece fits inside one pair of eyes—every amoeba is a glass house.

流程: 切分判据：按'能独立核算、能跑完业务闭环、不撕裂整体使命'三要件把组织切到最小可经营单元 → 定价内部市场：为每道工序间的交付物定内部买卖价格，让下一巴像顾客一样挑剔上一巴的品质与交期 → 核算公开：每周公布各巴的单位时间附加值与工时，让比较、浪费与改进机会同时显形 → 轮转重组：巴的边界随市场与技术变动每季复审——该合并合并、该拆分拆分，阿米巴是变形虫而非图钉

金句: 让第一线的每一位员工都能看见自己每小时创造的价值——全员经营，从数字可见开始。

## M-INM-003 作为人何谓正确判断法 / What Is Right as a Human Being

把一切经营判断收拢到一句最朴素的基准语上：'作为人，何谓正确？'——不诉诸行业惯例、股东利益或胜负得失，而是回到幼儿园就学过的做人常识：不撒谎、不给人添麻烦、要正直、要公平、要努力。每当京瓷、KDDI或日航面临'合法但不义''有利但有愧'的抉择时，稻盛用这条基准把判断从利益计算拉回原理原则。要害有三：其一，基准必须抽象到普适、朴素到不可再问——'作为人'三个字让任何身份辩解（作为总裁、作为竞争者）失效；其二，原理原则不是道德装饰而是决策提速器：复杂局面里大多数选项被一句常识直接淘汰，剩下的才需要计算；其三，基准要由领导者率先垂范：稻盛在日航不取一分薪酬、乘经济舱，把'领导者以私利私欲为敌'变成可见事实。方法论签名：凡抉择，先剥离一切身份与利益，只以'作为人'的身份过一遍常识的筛——筛剩下的再算账。

Collapse every business judgment onto one plain question: 'As a human being, what is the right thing to do?'—not industry practice, shareholder value, or the win-loss ledger, but the kindergarten honesty everyone already owns: do not lie, do not burden others, be fair, be diligent, be grateful. Whenever Kyocera, DDI, or JAL faced a choice that was legal but unjust, or profitable but shameful, Inamori used this baseline to pull the decision back from interest calculation to principle. Three disciplines give it teeth. First, the baseline must be abstract enough to be universal and plain enough to be unanswerable: the phrase 'as a human being' voids every identity-based excuse—'as a CEO', 'as a competitor'—before the argument starts. Second, principle is a decision accelerator, not a decoration: in complex situations most options die instantly under one stroke of common sense, and only the surviving few deserve arithmetic—ethics is the first gate, not the last coat of paint. Third, the leader must live it first and visibly: at JAL Inamori took zero salary and flew economy, converting 'the leader treats private gain as the enemy' from a slogan into a public fact. The working sequence: strip every title and stake until only the human being remains, sift options through kindergarten honesty, run cool cost-benefit analysis only on the survivors, then publish the leader's own choice and reasons so the maxim hardens into organizational muscle memory.

流程: 剥离身份：把决策者的一切头衔与利益关系先放一边，只保留'作为人'这一重身份 → 常识过筛：以不撒谎、不贪、公平、利他等幼儿园级常识把明显不义的选项直接淘汰 → 计算残局：对筛剩下的少数选项再做利弊与可行性的冷静计算——伦理是第一道闸而非最后一块遮羞布 → 垂范公示：领导者自己的选择与理由向全员公开，使基准从格言变成组织的肌肉记忆

金句: 判断的基准只有一个：不问得失，先问作为人何谓正确——答案错了，账算得再精也是错。

## M-INM-004 成功方程式法 / The Success Equation (Way of Thinking × Enthusiasm × Ability)

把'人生·工作的结果'写成乘法而非加法：结果 = 思维方式 × 热情 × 能力，取值区间各为负一百到正一百（思维方式）、零到一百（热情与能力）。乘法的要害在于思维方式可为负：能力平平但思维正面者可以做出可观结果；能力超群而思维为负者（聪明、热情，方向反了）结果反而最惨——天才破产，庸才成事。要害有三：其一，'思维方式'居首且权重最大，它回答'为谁而做'，一票否决全部其余；其二，热情（努力）比能力更可自控：稻盛自认平庸，以'付出不亚于任何人的努力'这一唯一可控变量对冲天赋差距；其三，乘法解释了为什么'恃才者败'——能力是存量，思维是符号位，热情是放大器；符号为负时放大器越强崩塌越快。方法论签名：评估任何人（包括自己）时，先看符号位再看放大器——负数乘以一万还是负数。

Write the outcome of life and work as a multiplication, not a sum: Result = Way of Thinking × Enthusiasm × Ability, with the way of thinking scaled from minus one hundred to plus one hundred and enthusiasm and ability from zero to one hundred. Multiplication makes the mindset a sign bit: a mediocre talent with a right mind can compound a respectable result, while a brilliant, passionate person with a negative mindset—the cleverest engine driving the wrong way—produces the worst outcomes of all; genius bankrupts while the plodder builds. Three disciplines follow. First, the way of thinking leads and holds veto power because it answers 'for whom is this work done?': any engine running on private interest or vanity zeroes the entire product, however able its operator. Second, enthusiasm is the only fully controllable variable—ability is largely given, but effort can always be raised—so Inamori, rating himself mediocre, staked everything on 'strive harder than anyone else' as the floor, not the aspiration, and let that single lever offset every talent gap. Third, the multiplicative form explains why the gifted fail: ability is a stock, thinking is the sign, enthusiasm is the amplifier—and when the sign is negative the stronger amplifier only accelerates the collapse. In review, attribute failure across all three factors and watch for the hidden pattern 'ability fine, effort decent, mindset negative', which masquerades as bad luck while it is really a sign-bit error.

流程: 先校符号：自问'为谁而做'——凡以私利私欲为引擎者先归零，符号不正一切白搭 → 盘点能力：诚实列出天赋与短板，不以学历与头衔替代真实能力盘点 → 加大可控项：把主要精力投入唯一完全可控的变量——努力强度，以'不亚于任何人'为下限 → 乘法复盘：结果不理想时按三项逐一归因，警惕'能力不错、热情尚可、思维方式为负'的隐性崩塌

金句: 人生·工作的结果＝思维方式×热情×能力——能力平平可以用热情十倍弥补，思维方式一负，全盘皆负。

## M-INM-005 六项精进法 / The Six Endeavors

把人格与经营能力的修炼拆成六条人人今日即可执行的日课：一、付出不亚于任何人的努力；二、要谦虚，不要骄傲；三、要每天反省；四、活着，就要感谢；五、积善行、思利他；六、不要有感性的烦恼。要害有三：其一，'精进'是佛教语——修炼不是顿悟而是日复一日的重复，六条全是动词，没有一条需要天赋；其二，第三条'每天反省'配备具体仪式：每晚对着镜子逐日复盘言行，把傲慢、懈怠、私欲点名认领，属'神道歉就道歉'的低成本纠错回路；其三，第六条划清边界——反省针对可改进之事，感性的烦恼（已经发生且无法改变的情绪内耗）被明确判为浪费，'失业与失败已发生，反省之后立刻转向下一步行动'。方法论签名：人格不是悟出来的，是六件小事每天做出来的——凡状态低迷，先查六项哪项断了。

Decompose the training of character and capability into six daily verbs anyone can execute today: strive harder than anyone else; stay humble and shun pride; reflect every day; be grateful while alive; do good and think of others first; and waste nothing on emotional turmoil. 'Endeavor' is a Buddhist term—progress comes not from sudden enlightenment but from daily repetition, and every one of the six is a verb requiring no talent. Two disciplines give the list its force. First, daily reflection is ritualized, not vague: each night, face the mirror, audit the day's words and deeds, and claim each instance of pride, laziness, and self-interest by name, with tomorrow's countermeasure—a five-minute, low-cost error-correction loop that Inamori kept for decades, at times apologizing aloud to the mirror. Second, the sixth item draws a hard boundary: reflection serves what can still be changed, but 'sensitive worries'—emotional churn over what has already happened and cannot be altered—are judged pure waste; once the lesson is taken, the regret is emptied that same night and attention turns to the next actionable step. The operational discipline is to post the six items on the wall beside the work goals, so training and KPIs share one board; when a day brings no progress, the diagnosis is mechanical—which verb did you skip today? Gratitude practice resets the baseline daily: deliberately find someone or something to thank, switching attention from what is lacking to what is held, so morale does not depend on circumstance.

流程: 立项上墙：把六条写成可见的日课清单，与工作目标并列——修炼与KPI同表管理 → 夜间点名：每晚自省言行，对傲慢、懈怠、私欲逐条认领并定明日对策，五分钟完成低成本纠错 → 感谢重置：每日固定找出可感谢之人之事，把注意力从'缺什么'切换到'有什么'——心态基线由感谢托底 → 烦恼清仓：凡属无法改变的懊恼，限时（当晚）清空，转向'下一步能做什么'——情绪不跨夜

金句: 六项精进没有一条需要天赋——今天没有进步，就检查六项中哪一项今天断了。

## M-INM-006 敬天爱人经营法 / Revere Heaven, Love People (Altruistic Management)

以'敬天爱人'为经营的最高纲领：'天'是普遍正理与天道——顺应天道者不投机、不欺瞒；'人'是员工与众生——经营者先利员工，员工再利顾客，利润是利他链条的果而非因。此语取自西乡隆盛，稻盛将其制度化为'哲学共有'：把经营哲学逐条写成手册，与全体员工反复学习对话，直至成为共同判断基准。要害有三：其一，利他有次序与边界——'自利则生，利他则久'，先保证企业自身健全（不自曝其短的利他是伪善），再谈员工、客户与社会；其二，哲学共有不是洗脑而是对话：稻盛在日航以'为什么而工作'的频繁恳亲会谈（酒话会）与数千员工直接对话，哲学在问答中内化；其三，领导者是哲学的最大受审者——手册的第一条约束的是社长自己，员工看的是领导人做不做，不是墙上写什么。方法论签名：制度解决'不能做什么'，哲学解决'愿意做什么'——凡靠制度压不住的地方，靠共有哲学补位。

Take 'Revere Heaven, Love People'—a phrase Inamori adopted from Saigo Takamori—as the supreme charter of management. Heaven is the universal justice: those who follow it do not speculate and do not deceive. People are the employees and the wider public: the leader serves employees first, employees serve customers, and profit arrives as the fruit of that altruism chain, never as its premise. The motto is institutionalized as 'shared philosophy': the operating philosophy is written clause by clause into a manual with real company cases, then taught, questioned, and argued into a common judgment standard. Three disciplines keep it honest. First, altruism has an order and a boundary—'self-interest sustains, altruism endures': the firm must first be solvent, because altruism financed by concealed weakness is hypocrisy; only then employees, then customers and society, with every altruistic act passing a self-solvency check. Second, shared philosophy is dialogue, not indoctrination: at JAL Inamori held countless small evening gatherings, answering thousands of employees' questions face to face until the philosophy survived their cross-examination. Third, the leader is the philosophy's first defendant: clause one of the manual binds the president, and public self-penalty on breach—visible zero salary, visible apology—is what keeps the system alive; employees watch what the leader does, not what the wall says. Institutions settle what people cannot do; shared philosophy settles what they willingly do—where rules run out, philosophy fills in. A counterexample warns: organizations that invoke ethics only in crisis teach employees to read morality as theater—the sieve must run on small deals and small accounts in calm times, or trust collapses exactly when it is needed.

流程: 立宪成文：把'作为人何谓正确'的判断写成可讨论的哲学手册条目，每条附本企业的真实案例 → 恳亲内化：以小规模酒话会、晨会问答与全员对话反复讲解哲学——接受检验与质询，而非单向灌输 → 次序利他：先使企业健全，再厚待员工，再及顾客与社会；每项利他决策先过'自身不垮'的边界审查 → 领导受审：哲学手册第一条约束社长本人；领导者违背时的自罚与公开认错是体系存续的关键

金句: 敬天——顺应正理而不欺；爱人——先利员工而后利己：利润是利他的果实，不是利他的前提。

## M-INM-007 萧条再飞跃法 / Recession as Springboard

把萧条从'需要熬过的灾难'改写为'被迫完成的成长'：萧条是唯一让全员注意力集中、让一切冗余显形的时刻——'萧条是实现飞跃的舞台'。稻盛总结京瓷渡过石油危机、日元升值萧条与金融危机的五条对策：全员营销（连生产与研发人员一起上销售一线）、全力开发新产品、彻底削减成本、保持高生产率、构建良好的人际关系。要害有三：其一，心理转换先行——'雨后竹笋'：萧条把竞争者淋倒，幸存者获得无人争抢的阳光；其二，削减成本不是裁员而是'全员销售+共赴时艰'：京瓷在1973年石油危机中全员降薪而无人被裁，士气反升；其三，萧条期的高生产率要求'即使订单减半也不减人效'——把闲置人力转投研发与新市场，萧条结束时企业已换了一个发动机。方法论签名：萧条来时先问'它替我们暴露了什么、逼我们能做什么'——把灾难当免费的战略咨询。

Reframe recession from a disaster to endure into growth that is forced upon you: the downturn is the one moment when everyone's attention concentrates, all redundancy shows, and the company can accomplish what prosperity never motivated. Inamori distilled Kyocera's passages through the oil shock, the endaka recession, and the financial crisis into five responses: company-wide selling (R&D, production, and finance all rotate onto the sales front), full-speed development of new products, radical cost reduction, sustained high productivity with order volume halved but headcount efficiency kept, and good human relations—shared hardship instead of layoffs. Three disciplines carry the frame. First, the psychological switch precedes the tactics: leadership publicly defines the recession as an improvement window—like bamboo that grows tallest after the rain—and panic is banned from internal documents. Second, cost-cutting never means cutting people: in the 1973 oil shock, when orders fell ninety percent, Kyocera cut everyone's pay together and laid off no one, and morale rose; people are the last 'cost' to cut because the recovery bills you for the skills you discarded. Third, idle hours are pushed into new products and new markets, so when the downturn ends the company has already swapped engines—the 1973 squeeze produced sapphire recrystallization products that boomed on recovery. The audit at the end is mechanical: against the five-item checklist, verify one landed outcome each—new market, new product, new cost structure—so the 'forced growth' is real, not narrated.

流程: 定调转念：管理层首先公开把萧条定义为'集中改善与再开发的窗口'，禁止把恐慌写进内部文件 → 全员上前线：研发、生产、财务轮流编入销售队伍直接面对顾客——萧条把'部门墙'冲成'一线'，情报直达决策层 → 双重挤压：一边极限削减固定费用（不裁员而共赴时艰），一边把富余人力压进新产品开发——既止血又换引擎 → 跃迁验收：萧条结束时对照'五对策清单'验收——新市场、新产品、新成本结构必须各有一项落地成果

金句: 萧条是成长的机会——企业就像竹笋，雨停之后长得最高的，是那些把根扎深的。

## M-INM-008 意志式定价法 / Pricing as Management Will

把'定价'从成本加成的算术升格为经营者意志的直接表达：'定价即经营'——价格定错，一切努力白费；价格是经营者人格与洞察力的测试纸。稻盛主张定价有两条纪律：其一，定价独立于成本——客户愿付的价格由价值与市场决定，若按成本加成定价，成本越高价格越高，成本经营必然松弛；其二，定价之后，成本管理以'以既定价格反推'：既已定价，就把目标成本压到该价格下仍有合理利润——'定价定生死，成本定成败'。要害有三：其一，'销售最大化、费用最小化'两极同时用力，绝不接受'成本就这么高、只能卖贵'的宿命论；其二，降价必先问'降价换来的量，能否补偿单位利润的损失'——防止以份额幻觉自欺；其三，定价权来自持续的产品独特性：京瓷以技术壁垒守住'不被比价的定价权'，使意志式定价可持续。方法论签名：报价单上的每个数字都是经营者意志的签名——按成本加成报出的价格，签的是经营者的懒惰。

Elevate pricing from cost-plus arithmetic to a direct expression of managerial will: 'pricing is management'—a price set wrong wastes every effort downstream, and the price list is the test paper of the manager's insight and character. Two disciplines govern it. First, pricing is quarantined from cost: what the customer will pay is set by the value and uniqueness of the offering; price by cost-plus and rising costs simply justify rising prices, and cost discipline dies of comfort. Second, once the price is set, cost management works backward from it: target costs are pushed down to each process until the fixed price yields a sane margin—pricing decides life or death, cost execution decides success or failure, and surrendering to 'costs are just high' is the one forbidden sentence. Three further rules keep the will honest. Maximize revenue and minimize expense as simultaneous verbs, never as passive outcomes. Any discount proposal must first demonstrate that incremental volume will cover the lost unit margin—otherwise it is share vanity dressed as strategy. And pricing power must be earned continuously by product uniqueness: Kyocera kept customers arriving with specifications in hand rather than rival quotes, which is what makes value pricing sustainable instead of arrogant. When Hughes Aircraft demanded aerospace-grade ceramic quality in the 1960s, Inamori priced on the quality commitment rather than bidding low; the first successful delivery raised both price and trust together—a price, signed, is the manager's own signature on the company's fate.

流程: 价值定价：先答'客户因这产品的独特价值愿意付多少'，彻底隔离成本数字的干扰 → 反推成本：以定下价格为约束，倒排目标成本到每道工序，把'降本'变成工程课题而非财务口号 → 量利对账：任何降价提议必须测算'增量能否覆盖单位利润损失'，防止份额幻觉 → 守定价权：持续投资产品独特性，使公司长期站在'客户拿着规格来找你'而非'你拿着报价单求人'的位置

金句: 定价即经营——价格签下去的那一刻，公司的生死已被写出，剩下的成本管理只是把它誊清。

## M-INM-009 燃烧的斗魂法 / Burning Fighting Spirit within Love

经营需要'格斗者级的斗魂'，但斗魂必须装在'大爱'的容器里：所谓斗魂，是'如同母鸡护雏般的勇气'——不是攻击他人，而是为守护员工、顾客与正义事业敢于正面迎击任何困难的意志力。稻盛把它与'胆小怕事者不可经营'并置：创业与重建（日航）都需要领导者以燃烧的意志冲在最先，使员工'看见领导者的背影'而获得勇气。要害有三：其一，斗魂的方向是'守护'而非'征服'——母鸡的凶猛全部来自护雏之爱，无爱的斗魂退化为霸道；其二，斗魂要'燃烧而可视化'：日航重建中稻盛以78岁高龄每周驻场、当面训示、零薪担责，领导者燃烧的程度决定组织燃烧的温度；其三，斗魂与'深入terra incognita的勇气'配套——'需要提升自己的人格'：越是敢闯无人区，越要以哲学与自省保证火不烧偏。方法论签名：检验领导者的斗魂，不看他在会议室里多强硬，看困难来时他站在队伍的哪一端。

Management demands a fighter's soul, but the soul must be housed inside great love: the courage required is that of a hen protecting her chicks—not aggression against others, but the will to face any difficulty head-on in order to guard employees, customers, and a just cause. The timid cannot manage, yet toughness without a guarded object degenerates into tyranny; the direction of the fighting spirit is protection, not conquest. Three disciplines make it operational. First, aim the fire: name whom the courage protects—employees, customers, mission—and any hardness detached from them is self-audited before it is deployed. Second, meet the hardest problem first: the leader arrives first at the worst trouble and carries the first responsibility, because a back seen in the front line transmits courage better than any speech—at JAL, a seventy-eight-year-old unpaid chairman appeared weekly at Haneda, and the morale of a bankrupt company reversed within a year. Third, make the burning visible and bound it: on-site presence, zero salary, face-to-face reckoning conduct heat from the top downward, while philosophy and nightly reflection act as the firebreak that keeps the flame from crossing into arrogance after victories—Inamori paired the courage to enter terra incognita with the demand to keep improving one's own character, precisely because the bolder the explorer, the more the fire needs containment. The test is positional: do not ask how hard the leader argues in conference rooms; ask at which end of the line he stands when the difficulty arrives. A counterexample marks the boundary: fighting spirit turned on subordinates can sprint for a quarter and always burns the team out within the year—loveless aggression is a liability booked at interest.

流程: 定向守护：明确斗魂的守护对象（员工、顾客、使命）——凡脱离守护对象的强硬先自我审查 → 率先赴难：最大的困难由领导者第一个到场、第一个担责——背影比训话更能下达勇气 → 燃烧可见：把领导者的投入（驻场、零薪、当面沟通）变成全员可见的事实——温度自上而下传导 → 以爱控火：以哲学反省为灭火系统，防止斗魂在胜利后越界为傲慢与霸道

金句: 需要的斗魂，是母鸡护雏般的勇气——斗魂为爱而燃，爱为斗魂定界。

## M-INM-010 乐观构想悲观计划法 / Optimistic Conceive, Pessimistic Plan, Optimistic Execute

把一个项目的推进切成三段，为每一段配不同的心态与人格：构想阶段要以'无所不能的乐观者'放开想象——'要用乐观开朗的想法构思新事业，需要有无赖汉般的天真'；计划阶段则换上'彻底的悲观者'，把一切风险与失败模式穷举预案——'设想一切可能发生的困难，并为此做好万全准备'；执行阶段再换回乐观者，以必胜信念推进。要害有三：其一，三段切换要求不同的人格同台：稻盛自述自己天生乐观只能担当构想与执行，'悲观的计划'必须交给谨慎多虑的人——领导者要按人格配段，不要求全才；其二，悲观必须穷尽而彻底——'准备到以失败为前提也能活下来'，预案细到出现最坏情形时的资金与士气安排；其三，执行段的乐观不是回避风险，而是'预案已尽'之后的专注——知道所有坑在哪里的人，才能不看坑地奔跑。方法论签名：项目卡壳时先查心态配段——用乐观者做计划、用悲观者做执行，是最常见的双杀配置。

Split any new undertaking into three stages and assign each stage its own attitude and, if need be, its own person. In conception, be the boundless optimist—it takes the innocent audacity of an outlaw to conceive a new business, and using present constraints to shoot down ideas at birth is forbidden discipline. In planning, swap to the thorough pessimist: enumerate every failure mode, price every risk, and stockpile contingencies—money, people, morale—until the project could survive its own failure; Inamori's bar was 'prepared so completely that we live even if the worst arrives'. In execution, return to the optimist: with every pitfall already surveyed, run with eyes on the finish—the focus of the execution phase is not denial of risk but the concentration that only exhausted preparation makes possible. The signature rule is casting by personality: Inamori knew himself a born optimist fit for conception and execution, so pessimistic planning had to be handed to naturally cautious, worry-prone people—never demand one person wear all three faces well. Before founding DDI in 1984 he ran the sequence in full: months of nightly 'naive' conception fixed the rate-revolution goal, further months of pessimistic planning stocked war chests and retreat routes for a tariff war against a state-backed monopoly, and only then did the outsider enter. The diagnostic for stalled projects is mechanical: an optimist wrote the plan (risks missed) or a pessimist is running execution (feet frozen)—mis-cast attitudes kill more ventures than lack of talent ever does.

流程: 放开构想：立项期以'天真'为纪律——禁止用现实约束提前枪毙构想，让最大胆的方案先出场 → 悲观穷举：计划期切换人格，逐项列出'什么会让我们失败'并为最坏情形备妥资金、人才与士气的预案 → 配段用人：构想与执行交给乐观者，计划交给天生谨慎者——按人格特长分工，不苛求全才 → 乐观执行：执行期锁定预案、心无旁骛推进——所有坑已被踩点，奔跑者只看终点

金句: 乐观地构想，悲观地计划，乐观地执行——三副面孔，一个都不能装错位置。
