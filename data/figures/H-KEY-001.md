## 生平与历史定位

凯恩斯（1883-1946），生于剑桥一个经济学家之家（父约翰·内维尔·凯恩斯为逻辑学家与经济学家，母弗洛伦斯为剑桥首位女市长），伊顿公学至剑桥国王学院的精英轨道上以数学与古典学双优胜出，马歇尔与皮古的门下却在《概率论》里先同频率派分道——'合理信念度'的哲学立场为日后'根本不确定性'埋下地基。第一次世界大战把他从剑桥书斋拽进财政部：1919年巴黎和会上，他作为英国财政部首席代表目睹赔款条款的报复性算术，辞职并写下《和平的经济后果》，以一部畅销书预言了凡尔赛体系的二十年倒计时。1920年代他在货币理论中辗转（《货币改革论》抨击金本位、《货币论》尝试对储蓄-投资失衡建模），1929年大萧条击穿古典综合的最后防线，1936年《通论》以'有效需求''流动性偏好''动物精神'三件工具完成对萨伊定律的清算——宏观经济学自此作为独立学科诞生。1940年代他把理论兑现为制度：战时以《如何支付战争》设计通胀抑制型财政，1944年布雷顿森林会议上以bancor清算同盟方案与怀特方案角力，为战后货币秩序留下框架（虽最终版本偏向美国方案）。他同时是成功的投资者（国王学院B店基金在其掌舵下年化远超市场）、艺术赞助与皇家歌剧院/英格兰银行/IMF的多重角色扮演者，1946年复活节日在苏塞克斯蒂尔顿因心脏病去世——用他自己的话说，'长期来看我们都死了'。

## 核心思想

- 有效需求原理：产量与就业由总支出决定，经济可长期停留在低于充分就业的均衡——萨伊定律被整体推翻
- 流动性偏好：利率是放弃流动性的报酬，货币是对根本不确定性的保险单——利率存在'推绳子'的地板
- 动物精神：重大经济决策的核心原料是自发的乐观冲动而非计算——信心自实现，叙事即政策
- 乘数原理：边际消费倾向使一笔支出沿收入链放大——'给谁花钱'成为可计算的问题
- 根本不确定性：可量化的风险与不可量化的不确定必须分开——概率论先于通论
- 选美竞赛：金融定价是二阶预期的递归——市场短期是投票机
- 长期皆死：用长期均衡论瘫痪短期行动是逃避责任——每个'长期'由短期组成
- 国际清算同盟：顺差逆差对称归责、资本管制常态化——把和平写进清算规则
- 混合经济：国家不当厨师但当恒温器——社会化的投资函数而非生产资料国有化
- 好生活之问：经济问题解决之后，人如何值得地生活——布卢姆斯伯里的终极提问

## 名言

长期来看，我们都死了。In the long run we are all dead.

来源：《货币改革论》(A Tract on Monetary Reform, 1924) 第3章

## 影响

凯恩斯的影响以三波扩散：第一波在战后西方（1945-1970s），凯恩斯主义成为西方宏观政策的默认操作系统——充分就业被写入各国政策目标，IMF/世界银行/关贸总协定的布雷顿森林三角把他的制度设计变成世界秩序的底层代码，'我们都是凯恩斯主义者'成为时代口号；第二波在停滞-滞胀的回摆与再综合（1970s-2008），弗里德曼货币主义与卢卡斯理性预期发动反革命，但新凯恩斯主义以微观基础重建粘性工资与利率走廊，凯恩斯的核心命题以更精细的形式存活于教科书与央行模型；第三波在全球危机时代（2008-今），零利率与量化宽松使'流动性陷阱'从教科书脚注变成现实，2020年疫情期的财政-货币合流被讥为'凯恩斯的完全胜利'，而资本管制的回归（跨境资本流动宏观审慎）恰是他在布雷顿森林最孤独主张的迟来正名。

## 十大深度思维模式

### M-KEY-001 有效需求原理统摄法 / Effective Demand as Master Variable

- 领域：宏观经济学/总需求分析 / Macroeconomics / Aggregate Demand Analysis　级别：核心　优先级：1
- 来源：《就业、利息和货币通论》(The General Theory of Employment, Interest and Money, 1936) 第3章'有效需求原理'；第2章对萨伊定律的清算

**定义（中）**

把经济分析的起点从'供给创造需求'（萨伊定律）整体翻转：产量与就业水平不由供给能力单独决定，而由有效需求——社会愿意且能够支付的总支出——决定；有效需求不足时，经济可以长期停在'低于充分就业的均衡'而不自我修复。要害有三：其一，总量优先——个市场的出清逻辑不能加总为整体经济的出清逻辑，'节俭悖论'（人人储蓄反而减少总投资）即是加总谬误的实证；其二，均衡不等于理想——均衡只是'没有内生变动倾向'的状态，完全可以是一种浪费人力资本的高失业稳态，'均衡'一词不携带任何祝福；其三，政策含义直接涌出——既然缺口在需求侧，扩需求（政府支出、货币宽松）就是对症药，而非对供给的干扰。方法论签名：先问'钱从哪里花'再问'货从哪里来'——诊断经济先看支出的脉象，供给能力只决定天花板，有效需求才决定你此刻住在第几层。

**Definition (EN)**

Effective demand inverts the founding axiom of classical economics. Against Say's Law—supply creates its own demand—Keynes holds that output and employment are set not by productive capacity alone but by the total spending society is both willing and able to undertake; when effective demand falls short, the economy can settle into an equilibrium below full employment and stay there, because nothing in the price mechanism guarantees automatic repair. Three disciplines give the mode its force. First, the aggregate comes before the part: the clearing logic of a single market does not sum into a clearing logic for the economy as a whole—the paradox of thrift (everyone saving more shrinks total investment) is the canonical demonstration that composition changes the conclusion. Second, equilibrium is not endorsement: equilibrium merely means 'no endogenous tendency to change'—it can perfectly well be a high-unemployment steady state that squanders human capital, and the word carries no blessing. Third, the policy implication falls out directly: if the gap is on the demand side, expanding demand (public spending, monetary easing) is the specific remedy rather than an interference with supply. Signature: ask where the money is being spent before asking where the goods will come from—diagnose an economy by the pulse of expenditure; supply capacity only sets the ceiling, but effective demand decides which floor you are actually living on.

**关键概念**：有效需求、萨伊定律否定、低于充分就业均衡、节俭悖论、总量分析

**四步流程（中）**

1. 识别系统短板的位置：判断问题是'造不出来的约束'还是'卖不出去的约束'——前者在供给侧，后者在需求侧，药方完全相反
2. 计算有效需求的三大成分：消费、投资、政府购买各自的当前水位与变动趋势，重点盯投资（最易波动的分量）
3. 检验'均衡但恶化'状态：确认现状是否属于'可自我维持的坏均衡'——若是，等待自动修复等于永等
4. 对症注入需求：以可逆、可校准的支出或宽松填补缺口，同时设定退出条件，防止需求管理本身变成新的扭曲

**Process (EN)**

1. Locate where the constraint binds: judge whether the problem is 'cannot produce' (supply side) or 'cannot sell' (demand side)—the remedies are opposites
2. Tally the three components of effective demand—consumption, investment, government purchases—tracking current levels and trends, with special attention to investment, the most volatile component
3. Test for 'equilibrium but deteriorating': confirm whether the present state is a self-sustaining bad equilibrium—if so, waiting for automatic repair means waiting forever
4. Inject demand as the targeted remedy: use reversible, calibratable spending or easing to fill the gap, with exit conditions set in advance so demand management does not become a new distortion

**金句**：先看支出的脉象，再问货从哪里来——有效需求决定你住在第几层。 / Read the pulse of expenditure before asking where the goods come from—effective demand decides which floor you live on.

**代表案例（中）**

- 1929年大萧条：美国工业产出近乎腰斩、失业率25%持续多年而价格机制未能自动出清——萨伊定律预言的'自动恢复'九年不至，罗斯福新政与凯恩斯理论互为注脚
- 2008年金融危机后各国财政刺激：防止需求坍缩引发通缩螺旋
- 2009年中国四万亿投资：以基建支出填补外需断崖（亦为反面案例：副作用包括产能过剩与债务积累，说明需求注入必须校准剂量与退出）

**Cases (EN)**

- Great Depression, 1929: US industrial output nearly halved and unemployment stuck near 25% for years while the price mechanism failed to clear the market—the 'automatic recovery' promised by Say's Law did not arrive for nine years; the New Deal and Keynes's theory became footnotes to each other
- Post-2008 fiscal stimulus across economies: preventing a demand collapse from spiraling into deflation
- China's 2009 four-trillion-yuan program: infrastructure spending filling the export-demand cliff (also a cautionary case—side effects included overcapacity and debt accumulation, showing that demand injection must be dose-calibrated with a pre-set exit)

**现代应用（中）**

- 企业预算编制加入'需求侧仪表盘'：销售预测先看客户支出意愿指标，再看自身产能利用率
- 地方政府招商：先测算区域有效需求缺口，再决定补贴方向是引流（需求）还是扩产（供给）
- 个人职业选择：行业有效需求（雇主愿付的薪资总盘）比个人技能上限更决定短期收入水平

**Applications (EN)**

- Corporate budgeting adds a demand-side dashboard: forecast sales from customer willingness-to-spend indicators before capacity utilization
- Local investment attraction: measure the regional effective-demand gap first, then decide whether subsidies target demand (traffic) or supply (capacity)
- Career choice: an industry's effective demand (the total wage pool employers will pay) drives short-run income more than one's skill ceiling

### M-KEY-002 边际消费递减乘数放大法 / Diminishing Marginal Propensity & the Multiplier

- 领域：宏观经济学/需求传导 / Macroeconomics / Demand Transmission　级别：核心　优先级：2
- 来源：《通论》第10章'边际消费倾向'；理查德·卡恩1931年《国内投资与失业的关系》首提乘数概念，凯恩斯吸收并系统化

**定义（中）**

把'一笔支出的效果'从其票面金额解放出来：由于人们只把新增收入的一部分用于消费（边际消费倾向介于0与1之间且随收入升高而递减），一笔初始支出会沿'收入→消费→再收入'的链条逐轮传递、逐轮衰减，但其总效果是初始投入的一个放大倍数（乘数=1/(1-MPC)）。要害有三：其一，支出不是零和——甲的支出是乙的收入，乙再支出成为丙的收入，公共工程的每一元都在私人经济体内往复生息；其二，衰减率决定放大率——MPC越高，货币在体系内停留的轮次越多，乘数越大；穷人边际消费倾向高于富人，故同样一笔钱给低收入群体比给高收入群体的总需求效果更大，这把'给谁花钱'变成一个可以计算的问题；其三，漏损即衰减——储蓄、进口、税收都是乘数链条的漏水口，开放经济中小国乘数天然偏小，政策设计须先测漏损结构。方法论签名：每一笔支出都在为下一个人的收入预付定金——衡量一笔支出的价值，不看它落在谁手里，而看它此后被传递多少轮。

**Definition (EN)**

Marginal propensity analysis frees the effect of an expenditure from its face value. Because people spend only a fraction of any new income—the marginal propensity to consume lies between 0 and 1 and declines as income rises—an initial outlay travels down a chain of income-to-consumption-to-income rounds, attenuating each round yet summing to a multiple of the original injection (the multiplier = 1/(1-MPC)). Three disciplines give the mode its force. First, spending is not zero-sum: A's outlay is B's income, and B's further outlay becomes C's; every public dollar circulates through the private economy, earning interest in kind. Second, the decay rate sets the amplification: the higher the MPC, the more rounds money survives in the system and the larger the multiplier—since the poor spend a larger share of new income than the rich, the same dollar aimed at low-income groups generates more total demand than one aimed at the wealthy, which turns 'whom to spend on' into a calculable question. Third, leakage is attenuation: saving, imports, and taxes are all holes in the multiplier chain; a small open economy's multiplier is naturally small, so policy design must map the leakage structure first. Signature: every outlay is a down payment on someone else's income—judge a spending act not by whose hands it lands in but by how many rounds it travels afterward.

**关键概念**：边际消费倾向、乘数效应、递减规律、漏损、收入再循环

**四步流程（中）**

1. 测出链条的边际率：估算接收方把新增资源转投出去的比例，它是乘数大小的唯一决定量
2. 画出漏损地图：标出每一轮传递中流向储蓄、进口、税收、垄断加价的水量，判断链条能活几轮
3. 选择高边际接收方：把注入点设在最会把钱再花出去的主体上（低收入者、现金流紧张的供应商），而非储蓄率最高的主体
4. 按乘数定价支出：把每项候选支出的票面额乘以其估计乘数再比较，而非比较票面额本身

**Process (EN)**

1. Measure the chain's marginal rate: estimate what fraction of new resources the recipient re-spends—it is the sole determinant of multiplier size
2. Draw the leakage map: mark how much drains each round into saving, imports, taxes, and monopoly markups, and judge how many rounds the chain survives
3. Pick the high-propensity recipient: set the injection point at whoever re-spends most readily (low-income households, cash-strapped suppliers), not at the highest savers
4. Price spending by its multiplier: compare candidate outlays by face value times estimated multiplier, never by face value alone

**金句**：每一笔支出都在为下一个人的收入预付定金。 / Every outlay is a down payment on someone else's income.

**代表案例（中）**

- 1930年代美国公共事业振兴署（WPA）：政府直接雇用失业者发工资，工资再进入社区商业循环——每个公共职位在私人部门带动约2-3个间接就业
- 2008年后中国家电下乡：财政补贴农村购家电，农民边际消费倾向高，补贴撬动的总消费数倍于补贴额
- 航空业乘数：每1个航空业直接岗位带动旅游业等约6个间接岗位——各国产业政策用乘数逻辑为'支柱产业'定价（反面警示：2010年后部分新兴市场的基建刺激在产能已满载时注入，未产生乘数放大反而推高通胀与债务——乘数逻辑只在闲置产能存在时成立）

**Cases (EN)**

- US Works Progress Administration in the 1930s: direct federal hiring put wages into unemployed households, which re-spent them in local commerce—each public job induced roughly two to three indirect private jobs
- China's home-appliances-to-the-countryside (2008): rural households' high marginal propensity meant subsidies for purchases unlocked total consumption several times the subsidy
- Aerospace multipliers: each direct aerospace job is priced to induce roughly six indirect jobs in tourism and supply chains—industrial policy worldwide uses multiplier logic to rank 'pillar industries' (cautionary: post-2010 infrastructure stimulus in some emerging markets was injected when capacity was already full—no multiplier amplification, only higher inflation and debt, because multiplier logic holds only where slack capacity exists)

**现代应用（中）**

- 用户增长运营：把补贴投向边际消费/复购率最高的用户分层，而非普惠发放
- 企业选址：优先选供应商生态密集地，让每笔采购在本地链条多转几轮
- 政府转移支付设计：消费券定向低收入群体，比普涨个税起征点的总需求撬动更大

**Applications (EN)**

- Growth operations: aim subsidies at the user segments with the highest repurchase propensity, not at everyone equally
- Corporate siting: prefer dense supplier ecosystems so each purchase recirculates several more rounds locally
- Transfer design: targeted consumption vouchers for low-income groups leverage more total demand than raising the income-tax threshold across the board

### M-KEY-003 流动性偏好利率观法 / Liquidity Preference as the Price of Parting with Cash

- 领域：货币理论/利率决定 / Monetary Theory / Interest Determination　级别：核心　优先级：3
- 来源：《通论》第13章'流动性偏好的基本理论'、第15章'流动性偏好的动机'；第12章'长期预期状态'（选美竞赛喻）

**定义（中）**

把利率从'资本的报酬'重新定义为'放弃流动性的报酬'：货币不仅是交易媒介，更是最被渴望持有的资产，人们持币的三大动机——交易、谨慎、投机——共同决定在任一利率水平下愿意持有多少货币；利率因此不是储蓄与投资的均衡价格，而是公众愿意以多低的价格把手里的流动性让渡出去。要害有三：其一，未来本质不确定——人们持币不是因为'时间偏好'这种光滑的心理规律，而是因为未来不可计算，货币是对不确定性的保险单；其二，利率有地板——利率越低，持币的机会成本越小，公众吸收货币的意愿反而趋于无限，货币政策在深度衰退中可能'推绳子'，这就是后来所称的流动性陷阱；其三，预期主导资产价格——'最优雅的选美'：投资者不猜资产的真实价值，而猜'别人猜什么'，金融市场的定价本质是对他人预期的猜测，这把投机性不稳定内置进资本主义。方法论签名：现金是持有者向不确定性世界收取的保费——利率是你请别人放弃保单时必须支付的价钱。

**Definition (EN)**

Liquidity preference redefines the interest rate: not the reward for capital but the reward for parting with liquidity. Money is not merely a medium of exchange but the most desired asset to hold, and three motives for holding it—transactions, precaution, speculation—jointly determine how much money the public will hold at any interest rate; the rate is therefore not an equilibrium price between saving and investment but the price at which the public can be persuaded to surrender its liquidity. Three disciplines give the mode its force. First, the future is radically uncertain: people hold cash not out of some smooth psychological 'time preference' but because the future cannot be computed—money is an insurance policy against unknowability. Second, interest has a floor: the lower the rate, the smaller the opportunity cost of holding cash and the more willing the public is to absorb money, so monetary policy in deep recession can become 'pushing on a string'—the case later named the liquidity trap. Third, expectations drive asset prices: in Keynes's beauty contest, investors guess not true value but what others will guess—financial pricing is guesswork about other people's guesses, which builds speculative instability into capitalism itself. Signature: cash is the premium its holder charges an uncertain world—the interest rate is the price you must pay to ask someone to give up the policy.

**关键概念**：流动性偏好、三大持币动机、流动性陷阱、不确定性、选美竞赛预期

**四步流程（中）**

1. 区分三类持币需求：把体系内的货币存量按交易盘、保险盘、投机盘分解，判断当前主导动机是什么
2. 监测利率地板：当利率逼近零而公众仍囤积现金时，确认货币政策已触礁，及时切换工具
3. 审视让渡价格：任何要求他人交出灵活性（现金、期权、退路）的方案，都要按'对方放弃了什么保险'来定价补偿
4. 博弈二阶预期：在预期驱动的市场里，策略对象不是资产价值而是别人的猜测，故信息发布与预期管理重于基本面陈述

**Process (EN)**

1. Decompose money demand by motive: split the money stock into transactions, precautionary, and speculative balances, and identify which motive currently dominates
2. Monitor the rate floor: when rates near zero yet the public still hoards cash, acknowledge that monetary policy has run aground and switch instruments
3. Price the surrender of flexibility: any plan asking others to give up liquidity, options, or exit routes must be compensated by the value of the insurance they forgo
4. Play the second-order game: in expectation-driven markets, the object of strategy is others' guesses, not asset value—so message design and expectation management outrank fundamentals-claims

**金句**：现金是持有者向不确定性世界收取的保费。 / Cash is the premium its holder charges an uncertain world.

**代表案例（中）**

- 2008年后全球零利率与量化宽松：利率降至历史低位而民间仍囤积现金——教科书级流动性陷阱，央行被迫改用购债与前瞻指引直接管理预期
- 日本1990年代'失去的三十年'：零利率下企业仍偿还债务不投资——持币动机由交易转为谨慎，货币政策推绳子
- 银行挤兑与现金囤积：储户抢兑不是非理性，而是不确定性骤升时持币保险盘的正常扩张——存款保险制度本质是降低'持币保险'的需求
- 反例警示：挤兑式囤现是流动性偏好，不是所有囤钱都是——若家庭因收入预期悲观而长期储蓄，问题在需求侧收入而非持币偏好，降息与存款保险均无效，须直接补收入；把'保险需求'误诊为'利率问题'，宽松就变成推绳子

**Cases (EN)**

- Global zero rates and QE after 2008: rates at historic lows while the private sector still hoarded cash—a textbook liquidity trap forcing central banks into bond purchases and forward guidance to manage expectations directly
- Japan's lost decades from the 1990s: at zero rates firms kept repaying debt instead of investing—the dominant cash motive had shifted from transactions to precaution, and monetary policy pushed on a string
- Bank runs: depositors queuing to withdraw are not irrational but exercising the normal expansion of precautionary balances when uncertainty spikes—deposit insurance works precisely by shrinking the demand for that insurance

**现代应用（中）**

- 企业现金储备政策：把'持有多少现金'当作对宏观不确定性的保费报价，而非低效的财务拖累
- 个人理财：应急基金的本质是流动性偏好，规模应随职业不确定性（行业波动、合同制）上调
- 谈判策略：要求对方放弃退出权（锁仓、长约）时，必须为放弃的流动性支付溢价

**Applications (EN)**

- Corporate cash policy: treat 'how much cash to hold' as an insurance premium quoted against macro uncertainty, not financial inefficiency
- Personal finance: the emergency fund is liquidity preference in action—size it to career uncertainty (industry volatility, contract work)
- Negotiation: when asking a counterparty to surrender exit rights (lock-ups, long terms), pay a premium for the liquidity they give up

**关联模式**：M-KEY-001、M-KEY-008、M-KEY-010　**代表人物**：约翰·梅纳德·凯恩斯、约翰·希克斯、海曼·明斯基

### M-KEY-004 动物精神预期驱动法 / Animal Spirits & Expectation-Driven Action

- 领域：行为经济学/投资决策 / Behavioral Economics / Investment Decisions　级别：核心　优先级：4
- 来源：《通论》第12章'长期预期状态'：'a spontaneous urge to action rather than inaction...animal spirits'；凯恩斯对'我们对自己正在做的事情的知识其实非常模糊'的论证

**定义（中）**

承认重大经济决策的核心原料不是计算而是情绪：企业主的实业投资依赖'自发的行动冲动'（animal spirits）——一种想做而非不做的自发的乐观，因为未来根本不可计算，计算只能消灭行动而非启动行动；'如果人性不喜欢碰运气，则仅靠冷静计算不会有如此多的投资'。要害有三：其一，不可算处靠信心补位——概率可算的部分交给期望值，不可算的部分交给叙事与信心，信心的涨落就是投资的涨落；其二，信心的自我实现——乐观本身改善投资，投资改善收入，收入'证实'乐观，故经济繁荣与恐慌都部分地是集体心境的产物；其三，管理心境就是管理经济——既然波动的源头之一是叙事，那么领导者的表态、数据发布节奏、信心的公共仪式，都是真实的经济政策工具。方法论签名：人在算不清的时候不是停止行动，而是按信心行动——所以要看懂一个经济体的未来，别读它的报表，先听它的故事。

**Definition (EN)**

Animal spirits concede that the core input of great economic decisions is not calculation but sentiment: entrepreneurial investment runs on a spontaneous urge to act—self-generated optimism—because the future is radically incomputable, and computation can only veto action, never launch it: 'if human nature felt no temptation to take a chance, there would not be much investment merely as a result of cold calculation.' Three disciplines give the mode its force. First, confidence fills where calculation cannot: what probability can price goes to expected value; what it cannot goes to narrative and confidence, and the tides of confidence are the tides of investment. Second, confidence is self-fulfilling: optimism improves investment, investment improves income, and income 'confirms' the optimism—so booms and panics are partly products of collective mood. Third, managing mood is managing the economy: since narrative is one source of fluctuation, a leader's statements, the cadence of data releases, and public rituals of confidence are genuine policy instruments. Signature: when calculation fails, people do not stop acting—they act on confidence; so to read an economy's future, skip its spreadsheets and listen to its stories.

**关键概念**：动物精神、根本不确定性、信心自实现、叙事经济、冲动与计算互补

**四步流程（中）**

1. 把决策拆成两层：可计算部分交给期望值与模型，不可计算部分显式承认并转为'信心预算'——明确记录我们此刻靠什么信念在行动
2. 监测叙事温度：跟踪行业与组织的乐观/恐慌指标（签约速度、扩张请求数、办公室闲谈），把'心境'当作先行指标
3. 投资于信心本身：在系统性恐慌时刻，公开、超额、可被看见的投入（自己人先买、自己人先建）比追加算计更能扭转预期
4. 为乐观设保险：利用而非否认动物精神，但为'信心突然蒸发'预置流动性缓冲与止损条款

**Process (EN)**

1. Split the decision in two: hand the computable part to expected value and models; explicitly concede the incomputable part as a 'confidence budget'—record exactly which beliefs we are acting on right now
2. Track narrative temperature: monitor optimism/panic indicators (deal velocity, expansion requests, hallway talk) and treat mood as a leading indicator
3. Invest in confidence itself: in systemic panic, visible overcommitment by insiders (leaders buying first, building first) reverses expectations faster than additional analysis
4. Insure the optimism: ride animal spirits rather than deny them, but pre-position liquidity buffers and stop-loss clauses for the day confidence evaporates

**金句**：人在算不清的时候不是停止行动，而是按信心行动。 / When calculation fails, people do not stop acting—they act on confidence.

**代表案例（中）**

- 1929年华尔街崩盘前后：投资者按'股市永远涨'的叙事定价，信心反转后投资与消费同月坍缩——凯恩斯目睹自己几乎破产，以此提炼出animal spirits
- 2009年奥巴马'购买美国货'演讲与车企救市公开化：用领导人可见的投入托住消费者信心，避免恐慌自我实现
- 创业投资中的'信念先行'：VC投资备忘录承认'赌的是创始人的叙事能力'——根本不确定性下信心就是资产
- 反例警示：动物精神解释投资波动，不能为一切非理性背书——若把泡沫中的明知故犯（Ponzi层级）也归为'信心'，就抹掉了欺诈与误判的界限；1929与2008的差异正在于前者是心境摆动、后者叠加了系统性造假，治理工具也不同（预期管理 vs 刑事追责）

**Cases (EN)**

- Wall Street, 1929: investors priced by the narrative that stocks only rise; when confidence flipped, investment and consumption collapsed in the same month—Keynes, nearly ruined himself, distilled animal spirits from the wreckage
- 2009: Obama's auto-bailout publicity and 'Made in America' rhetoric used visible commitment to shore up consumer confidence and stop panic from fulfilling itself
- Venture capital's 'belief-first' practice: investment memos that openly say 'we are betting on the founder's narrative power'—under radical uncertainty, confidence is the asset

**现代应用（中）**

- 公司年报与业绩指引：把'信心叙事'当正式管理工具，指引本身就是投资
- 危机沟通：领导者先自购/自用（skin in the game）比追加数据披露更能止住恐慌
- 创业融资：融资节奏跟随'叙事温度计'——市场信心高位多融，低位靠盈利自养

**Applications (EN)**

- Earnings guidance as a confidence instrument: the narrative in guidance is itself an investment
- Crisis communication: a leader buying first (skin in the game) calms panic faster than extra data disclosure
- Fundraising cadence: follow a narrative thermometer—raise heavily at confidence highs, live on profits at lows

**关联模式**：M-KEY-003、M-KEY-005、M-KEY-009　**代表人物**：约翰·梅纳德·凯恩斯、乔治·阿克洛夫、罗伯特·席勒

### M-KEY-005 长期皆死短期优先法 / In the Long Run We Are All Dead (Short-Run Primacy)

- 领域：经济哲学/时间贴现 / Economic Philosophy / Temporal Priority　级别：核心　优先级：5
- 来源：1924年《货币改革论》(A Tract on Monetary Reform) 第3章：'In the long run we are all dead. Economists set themselves too easy...a task if in tempestuous seasons they can only tell us that when the storm is long past the ocean is flat again.'

**定义（中）**

对'长期自动均衡'论发起时间维度的攻击：'长期来看我们都死了'——经济学家如果只会说风暴过去海面会恢复平静，那他们在风暴中就太没用了；如果把'等长期自己解决'当政策，等于把当代人的失业、破产与饥饿记账到后世才能兑付的期票上。要害有三：其一，时间不对称——'长期'是由一系列'短期'组成的，每个短期里活生生的人的痛苦不可贴现为零；其二，自动调节论是逃避责任的话术——'市场会自己恢复'的真实含义是'我不想现在付代价'，方法论上必须把这句话翻译回利益结构再评估；其三，短期行动与长期纪律可以兼得——凯恩斯反对的不是长期视角而是用长期视角瘫痪短期行动，正确的姿势是'先救眼前，同时校准退场'。方法论签名：把'长期会好'翻译成'谁在短期为此付账'——一切以长期为名要求当代人忍耐的论点，必须先出示短期账单和付账人名单。

**Definition (EN)**

Short-run primacy attacks the doctrine of automatic long-run equilibrium on the axis of time: 'in the long run we are all dead'—an economist who can only say that the sea will calm after the storm is of no use during the storm; to treat 'wait for the long run' as policy is to book the unemployment, bankruptcies, and hunger of living people against a promissory note redeemable only by later generations. Three disciplines give the mode its force. First, time is asymmetric: the long run is composed entirely of short runs, and the pain of actual people in each of them cannot be discounted to zero. Second, automatic-adjustment talk is an alibi: 'the market will heal itself' really means 'I decline to pay now'—methodologically the sentence must be translated back into its interest structure before it is evaluated. Third, short-run action and long-run discipline can coexist: Keynes opposed not the long view but the use of the long view to paralyze short-run action—the right posture is 'rescue the present, calibrate the exit simultaneously.' Signature: translate 'it will be fine in the long run' into 'who pays in the short run'—every argument asking the present generation to endure for the sake of the long term must first exhibit the short-run bill and the list of payers.

**关键概念**：时间优先级、长期均衡批判、责任翻译、短期账单、退场校准

**四步流程（中）**

1. 审计'长期论'的付账人：听到'长期会自行解决'时，立即追问——在短期里谁失业、谁破产、谁承担，其利益立场是什么
2. 把长期目标拆为短期里程碑：防止'长期'成为拖延的同义词，每个短期都要有可交付的救急动作
3. 同步设计退场：救急措施出台之日即起草退出条件，把'短期优先'与'长期纪律'装进同一个决策包
4. 拒绝用折现率抹平人间：对当代人的损失设置不可贴现的权重底线，超过底线的痛苦不能以'长期收益'冲销

**Process (EN)**

1. Audit who pays during the long run: on hearing 'it will resolve itself,' immediately ask—who is unemployed, who bankrupt, who bears the cost in the short run, and what is their interest
2. Decompose long-term goals into short-run milestones so 'long term' never becomes a synonym for delay: every period must carry a deliverable relief action
3. Design the exit in parallel: draft withdrawal conditions the day rescue measures launch, packaging short-run primacy and long-run discipline in one decision
4. Refuse to discount away human beings: set a non-discountable floor of weight for present-generation losses beyond which suffering cannot be offset by 'long-run gains'

**金句**：把'长期会好'翻译成'谁在短期为此付账'。 / Translate 'it will be fine in the long run' into 'who pays in the short run.'

**代表案例（中）**

- 1930年代金本位教条：各国央行坚持'长期价格自动恢复'而拒绝降息，坐视失业率攀升十年——凯恩斯1925年《我是不是自由党员》对两党共同信奉的金本位长期论开火
- 2020年疫情封城辩论：'长期经济会恢复'不能作为'短期放任扩散'的理由——救命的短期行动与债务的长期纪律并行设计
- 企业转型中的'长期战略麻痹'：以'三年规划'为名拒绝止损当下亏损业务——短期账单（现金流断裂）先于长期愿景杀死公司（反面警示：1970年代以'菲利普斯曲线长期权衡'为名长期维持刺激，换来滞胀——短期优先不等于无限期刺激，退场纪律是本模式的内置约束）

**Cases (EN)**

- The interwar gold-standard orthodoxy: central banks refusing rate cuts on the promise that prices would 'restore themselves long-term' while unemployment climbed for a decade—Keynes fired at this long-runism in 'Am I a Liberal?' (1925)
- 2020 pandemic lockdown debates: 'the economy recovers long-term' could not license short-run inaction on contagion—life-saving immediate action and long-run debt discipline were designed in parallel
- Strategic paralysis in corporate turnarounds: using 'the three-year plan' to defer cutting loss-making businesses—the short-run bill (a cash crunch) kills the company before the long-term vision arrives (cautionary: the 1970s kept stimulus running long-term in the name of a 'Phillips-curve trade-off' and reaped stagflation—short-run primacy does not mean indefinite stimulus; exit discipline is a built-in constraint of this mode)

**现代应用（中）**

- 公司裁员决策审计：'长期效率'论点必须出示短期账单（失业补偿、知识流失、声誉）与付账人
- 公共基建评审：拒绝以'十年收益'掩盖当代拆迁与生态的即期成本
- 个人'再等一年'陷阱：把'以后再做'的成本按月量化，暴露长期论的短期账单

**Applications (EN)**

- Layoff audit: any 'long-run efficiency' case must exhibit the short-run bill (severance, knowledge loss, reputation) and who pays it
- Infrastructure review: refuse to let 'ten-year returns' mask the present costs of displacement and ecology
- The personal 'wait another year' trap: quantify the monthly cost of deferral to expose the short-run bill hidden in long-run talk

**关联模式**：M-KEY-001、M-KEY-004、M-KEY-006　**代表人物**：约翰·梅纳德·凯恩斯、约翰·希克斯、富兰克林·罗斯福

### M-KEY-006 国际货币制度工程法 / Bretton Woods Monetary Architecture

- 领域：国际金融/制度设计 / International Finance / Institutional Design　级别：核心　优先级：6
- 来源：1942年《国际清算同盟建议书》(Proposals for an International Clearing Union)；1944年布雷顿森林会议凯恩斯代表团方案与美国怀特方案的合并过程

**定义（中）**

把'防止战争重演'翻译为一张货币制度的工程图纸：凯恩斯清算同盟方案（1942-44）试图用国际清算货币 bancor、顺差国与逆差国对称的调整义务、以及资本管制的常态化，从制度上拆除1930年代'以邻为壑'的竞争性贬值与债务窒息链条。要害有三：其一，把失衡双向归责——只惩罚逆差国的制度必然重演魏玛式的紧缩灾难，凯恩斯坚持顺差国同样承担再膨胀义务（虽在布雷顿森林最终文本中被美国否掉一半）；其二，给资本流动上锁——他认为投机性短期资本的自由流动是稳定的敌人，常设资本管制被他视为文明的制度构件而非权宜之计；其三，制度是和平的代役——与其用条约约束主权，不如用利益结构使战争变得不划算，货币互赖即是最深的和平工程。方法论签名：不要在战后清算仇恨，要在战前设计让仇恨无利可图的账户结构——最深的和平协议写在清算规则里。

**Definition (EN)**

Bretton Woods translates 'never again' into an engineering drawing of the monetary system: Keynes's Clearing Union plan (1942-44) proposed an international settlement currency (bancor), symmetric adjustment obligations on surplus and deficit countries alike, and the normalization of capital controls—dismantling, by institutional design, the 1930s machinery of beggar-thy-neighbor devaluations and debt strangulation. Three disciplines give the mode its force. First, assign imbalance bilaterally: a regime that punishes only deficit countries must replay the Weimar austerity catastrophe—Keynes insisted surplus countries carry reflationary obligations too (half of which the US struck from the final text). Second, lock the doors on capital: he held that the free flow of speculative short-term capital is the enemy of stability, and standing capital controls were for him a fixture of civilized institutions, not a stopgap. Third, institutions as peace by proxy: rather than binding sovereignty with treaties, reshape the incentive structure until war is unprofitable—monetary interdependence is the deepest peace engineering. Signature: do not settle hatreds after the war; design, before it, an account structure that makes hatred unprofitable—the deepest peace treaty is written in clearing rules.

**关键概念**：bancor清算货币、对称调整、资本管制、以邻为壑防范、制度和平

**四步流程（中）**

1. 把灾难翻译成制度缺陷：每次系统性崩溃后，先追问'哪种账户结构使灾难成为每个人的理性选择'，再修补结构而非责备个体
2. 对称归责设计：任何平衡机制若只约束一方（欠债方、超支方、逆差方），必积累系统性怨恨，须为顺差方、盈余方写入对称义务
3. 给投机性流动设闸：区分服务实体的流动与服务投机的流动，为后者设计摩擦（税、审批、冻结窗口）
4. 以互赖锁和平：把对手方利益嵌进不可拆的清算结构，使'掀桌子'的成本高于'坐下来'

**Process (EN)**

1. Translate catastrophe into institutional defect: after each systemic collapse, first ask 'which account structure made disaster the rational choice for everyone,' then repair the structure rather than blame individuals
2. Design symmetric accountability: any balancing mechanism that binds only one side (debtors, overspenders, deficit countries) accumulates systemic resentment—write symmetric obligations for creditors and surplus sides
3. Gate speculative flows: separate flows serving the real economy from flows serving speculation, and build friction (taxes, approvals, freeze windows) for the latter
4. Lock peace with interdependence: embed the counterparty's interests in an unbreakable clearing structure so that 'flipping the table' costs more than 'staying seated'

**金句**：最深的和平协议写在清算规则里。 / The deepest peace treaty is written in clearing rules.

**代表案例（中）**

- 1944年布雷顿森林体系：固定汇率+IMF+世界银行——运行27年，为战后重建提供货币秩序；其解体（1971尼克松冲击）恰始于凯恩斯警告的投机资本流动失控
- 2010年欧元区危机：货币同盟只有逆差国调整义务（紧缩），顺差国（德国）无对称再膨胀义务——凯恩斯预判的单边调整导致南欧失业率长期25%
- 托宾税提议（1972）：对短期外汇交易征税增加投机摩擦——凯恩斯资本管制思想的现代转世（反面警示：1990年代亚洲金融危机中马来西亚资本管制短期奏效但长期抑制外资信心——上锁是防御工具，不是发展战略）

**Cases (EN)**

- Bretton Woods, 1944: fixed rates plus the IMF and World Bank ran for 27 years, supplying the monetary order for postwar reconstruction; its collapse (the 1971 Nixon shock) began exactly where Keynes warned—speculative capital flows out of control
- The eurozone crisis, 2010: a monetary union with adjustment duties only on deficit countries (austerity) and none on surplus countries—Keynes's predicted one-sided adjustment produced 25% unemployment across Southern Europe for years
- The Tobin tax proposal (1972): taxing short-term FX trades to add speculative friction—Keynes's capital-control thought reincarnated (cautionary: Malaysia's capital controls in the 1990s Asian crisis worked short-term but suppressed foreign-investor confidence long-term—a lock is a defensive tool, not a development strategy)

**现代应用（中）**

- 平台生态治理：佣金与结算规则让双方利益互嵌（商家成功才有平台成功），而非单方罚款条款
- 联盟谈判：把对称义务写进协议——只约束一方的平衡机制必然积累退出冲动
- 供应链金融：核心企业对中小供应商的账期设计，本质是体系内的'清算同盟'问题

**Applications (EN)**

- Platform governance: commission and settlement rules that interlock both sides' interests (merchants succeed only if the platform does) beat one-sided penalty clauses
- Coalition talks: write symmetric obligations into agreements—balancing mechanisms that bind one side only accumulate exit pressure
- Supply-chain finance: a core firm's payment terms for small suppliers are a clearing-union problem inside the system

**关联模式**：M-KEY-001、M-KEY-003、M-KEY-008　**代表人物**：约翰·梅纳德·凯恩斯、哈里·德克斯特·怀特、詹姆斯·托宾

### M-KEY-007 和约条款经济审计法 / Auditing the Economic Terms of Peace

- 领域：国际政治经济/条约审计 / International Political Economy / Treaty Auditing　级别：核心　优先级：7
- 来源：《和平的经济后果》(The Economic Consequences of the Peace, 1919) 第5-7章；凯恩斯1919年6月辞去英国财政部驻巴黎和会代表职务

**定义（中）**

1919年在巴黎和会现场以《和平的经济后果》单枪匹马拆穿'胜利者的算术'：要求战败国支付超出其支付能力的赔款，不是正义而是自杀——赔款链条的一端是德国的账本，另一端是协约国对美国的战争债务，整张网最终必然以违约、通胀或战争收场。要害有三：其一，任何'应然'必须通过'可然'审计——条款在道义上再正当，若违背对方的支付能力与政治承受力，条款本身就是下一场灾难的设计图；其二，报复是有复利的——把屈辱写进经济条款，屈辱不会随时间贴现为零，而会以民粹与复仇的形式按复利累积；其三，批评要从内部出示——凯恩斯不是从敌营喊话，而是以和会英国财政部首席代表的身份辞职并公开账本，内部人的批评才有账目的重量。方法论签名：审一份条约不看它惩罚谁，而看它让谁在十年后无路可走——每一份胜利文件里都埋着下一场战争的预算。

**Definition (EN)**

At Paris in 1919, The Economic Consequences of the Peace single-handedly demolished the victors' arithmetic: demanding reparations beyond a defeated nation's capacity to pay is not justice but suicide—one end of the reparation chain sits in Germany's ledger, the other in the Allies' war debts to America, and the whole web must end in default, inflation, or war. Three disciplines give the mode its force. First, every 'ought' must pass a 'can' audit: however morally justified a clause, if it violates the counterparty's capacity to pay and political tolerance, the clause itself is the blueprint of the next disaster. Second, revenge compounds: humiliations written into economic terms do not discount to zero over time—they accrue at compound interest in the currency of populism and revanchism. Third, criticize from inside the books: Keynes did not shout from the enemy camp—he resigned as the Treasury's chief representative at the conference and published the accounts; only an insider's criticism carries the weight of the ledgers. Signature: judge a treaty not by whom it punishes but by whom it leaves without a road in ten years—every victor's document carries the budget of the next war inside it.

**关键概念**：赔款能力审计、报复复利、内部人批评、胜利者陷阱、政治承受力

**四步流程（中）**

1. 审计对方的支付能力：把条约/合同/和解方案中的每一项义务折算成对方真实的现金流与政治承受力，超出承受力即判定条款不可执行
2. 给报复计复利：量化条款在对方社会激起的屈辱存量及其年化政治利率，评估十年后的复仇现值
3. 用内部身份说话：批评生效的前提是'我看过账本'——先取得内部人视角与数据，再公开退出与批评，位置先于修辞
4. 为胜利设计出口：起草任何惩罚性安排时同步起草给败方的可生存路径，没有出口的惩罚会变成双方的共同债务

**Process (EN)**

1. Audit the counterparty's capacity: convert every obligation in a treaty, contract, or settlement into the other side's real cash flow and political tolerance—beyond tolerance, rule the clause unenforceable
2. Compound the revenge: quantify the humiliation stock a clause builds in the other society and its annualized political interest, then price the present value of revanchism ten years out
3. Speak from inside the books: effective criticism requires 'I have seen the ledgers'—secure the insider's view and data first, then resign publicly; position precedes rhetoric
4. Design an exit for the defeated: when drafting any punitive arrangement, draft simultaneously a survivable path for the loser—punishment without exit becomes a joint debt

**金句**：审一份条约不看它惩罚谁，而看它让谁在十年后无路可走。 / Judge a treaty not by whom it punishes but by whom it leaves without a road in ten years.

**代表案例（中）**

- 《凡尔赛和约》第231条战争罪责与1320亿金马克赔款：1933年希特勒上台的历史债单——凯恩斯1919年的预言20年内全部应验
- 2008年后希腊救助方案：救助贷款以紧缩为条件，超出希腊政治承受力——'人道的紧缩'悖论重演赔款逻辑，极右翼由此坐大
- 劳动合同中的竞业禁止：把惩罚条款定到员工无法生存的强度，条款反而失效且招致报复性离职——胜方条款同样需要'可然'审计（反面警示：一战后协约国内部也有以'宽和条款'为名的妥协方案被民族主义舆论撕毁——可然审计通过不等于政治执行自动到位，条款还需要国内政治的担保人）

**Cases (EN)**

- Versailles Article 231 and the 132-billion-gold-mark reparations: the historical debt bill behind Hitler's rise in 1933—Keynes's 1919 forecast fully redeemed within twenty years
- Post-2008 Greek bailouts: loans conditioned on austerity beyond Greece's political tolerance—a humane-austerity paradox replaying the reparations logic, feeding the far right
- Non-compete clauses in labor contracts: penalties set beyond an employee's survivable level void the clause and invite vindictive departures—victor's terms need the same 'can' audit (cautionary: within the WWI victor camp itself, compromise plans billed as 'lenient terms' were torn up by nationalist opinion—passing the 'can' audit does not make political execution automatic; the terms still need domestic political guarantors)

**现代应用（中）**

- 并购整合条款：对被收购方团队的'惩罚性'对赌若超出其生存空间，必然招致人才出走与业绩报复
- 离婚/分手协议：把条款压到对方无法生存的强度，执行成本会以复利回流到起草方
- 国际制裁设计：制裁需预留给对方'可生存出口'，否则只生产坚决的敌人

**Applications (EN)**

- M&A earn-outs: punitive terms beyond the acquired team's survivable space guarantee talent flight and performance revenge
- Divorce settlements: terms pressed beyond the other side's survivable level return to the drafter as compounding enforcement costs
- Sanctions design: sanctions need a survivable exit for the target, or they manufacture committed enemies

**关联模式**：M-KEY-006、M-KEY-005　**代表人物**：约翰·梅纳德·凯恩斯、伍德罗·威尔逊、雅克·德洛尔

### M-KEY-008 选美竞赛投机定价法 / Beauty-Contest Speculative Pricing

- 领域：金融理论/市场心理 / Financial Theory / Market Psychology　级别：核心　优先级：8
- 来源：《通论》第12章选美竞赛段落；凯恩斯1920年代作为剑桥国王学院B店（Chest Fund）管理人与个人外汇/商品投机者的实战经验

**定义（中）**

把金融市场的定价机制从'价值发现'还原为'预期递归'：职业投资如同报纸选美竞赛——胜利者不是选出自己认为最美者，而是选出'猜测大多数人会认为最美'者，且要猜到第三阶、第四阶（'别人认为别人认为谁美'）；因此股价不是企业价值的称，而是对大众注意力的测量仪，投机者研究的是'市场在想什么'而非'公司值多少'。要害有三：其一，二阶博弈压倒一阶现实——只要'大家会追涨'的预期成立，基本面再差也能涨，此即泡沫的认知引擎；其二，击鼓传花有时序——凯恩斯同期操作的办公室人生哲学：'音乐还在响就得起身跳舞'，退出时点的竞争使市场天然趋向过度与突然反转；其三，反转是瞬间完成的价值回归——泡沫破裂不是缓慢修正而是信心悬崖，故风险管理必须按'一夜清零'设计而非按'逐步回归'设计。方法论签名：市场短期是投票机长期是称重机（格雷厄姆承其衣钵）——看懂投票机，先数清场内有多少人在猜别人的票。

**Definition (EN)**

Beauty-contest pricing demotes financial markets from 'value discovery' to 'expectation recursion': professional investment resembles a newspaper beauty contest in which the winner picks not the face he finds prettiest but the face 'most people will find prettiest'—and must guess to the third and fourth order ('what others think others think others think'). Share prices are therefore not a scale for enterprise value but a gauge of mass attention: speculators study what the market is thinking, not what the company is worth. Three disciplines give the mode its force. First, second-order play overrides first-order reality: so long as the expectation 'everyone will chase the rise' holds, assets can rally against any fundamentals—that is the cognitive engine of bubbles. Second, the game has a rhythm: 'the market can stay irrational longer than you can stay solvent' is the later aphorism—competition over exit timing drives markets toward excess and abrupt reversal. Third, reversal is instantaneous reversion to value: a crash is not a slow correction but a confidence cliff, so risk must be designed for overnight wipeout, not gradual mean-reversion. Signature: in the short run the market is a voting machine, in the long run a weighing machine—to read the voting machine, first count how many players in the room are guessing everyone else's votes.

**关键概念**：选美竞赛、二阶预期、投机时序、信心悬崖、投票机与称重机

**四步流程（中）**

1. 区分你在参加哪场竞赛：一阶博弈（研究价值）与二阶博弈（研究预期）的赢法相反，入场前明确自己玩的是哪一个、对手是谁
2. 监测'猜别人'的浓度：当市场参与者的讨论从基本面转向'别人会怎么想'（资金面、情绪面）时，泡沫进入后程
3. 按信心悬崖设计风控：仓位与杠杆按'一夜清零'情景校验，禁止以'均值回归需要时间'为杠杆续命的理由
4. 音乐节奏管理：明确自己的'退场音乐'触发条件并写成纪律，不与大众比谁更晚离场

**Process (EN)**

1. Know which contest you are in: first-order play (studying value) and second-order play (studying expectations) reward opposite skills—declare which you are playing and who your opponents are before entry
2. Measure the density of guessing-others: when market talk shifts from fundamentals to 'what will others think' (flows, sentiment), the bubble is in its late phase
3. Design risk for the confidence cliff: test positions and leverage against an overnight-wipeout scenario; never let 'mean reversion takes time' justify staying levered
4. Manage the music's tempo: write down the trigger conditions of your exit music as discipline, and refuse to compete with the crowd on who leaves last

**金句**：短期是投票机，长期是称重机——看懂投票机，先数清谁在猜谁的票。 / In the short run a voting machine, in the long run a weighing machine—to read the votes, first count who is guessing whose.

**代表案例（中）**

- 1929年美股与凯恩斯本人的投机生涯：1920年外汇投机几近破产、1929年美股崩盘损失惨重——两次亲历使其放弃'择时'转向'择价值'（国王学院B店后期长期持有策略年化远超市场）
- 1999年互联网泡沫：基金经理公开承认'我们知道估值荒谬，但音乐没停'——二阶博弈文本化；2000年3月信心悬崖一周内纳斯达克跌去25%
- 加密货币市场'博傻链'：白皮书价值讨论让位于'下一个人会接盘'的共识——凯恩斯选美结构在无基本面资产上的纯净样本
- 反例警示：选美定价只适用于'定价由共识决定'的资产——对现金流可计算的生产性资产，二阶猜测本身就是噪声；凯恩斯本人后期转向长期持有（B店年化跑赢市场）正是承认：能称重的资产不该用投票机读

**Cases (EN)**

- US equities in 1929 and Keynes's own speculation: near-ruin in currency speculation (1920) and heavy losses in the 1929 crash—two personal lessons that turned him from timing to value (the King's College Chest Fund's later long-hold strategy beat the market for decades)
- The dot-com bubble, 1999: fund managers admitting on record 'we know valuations are absurd, but the music is playing'—second-order play in text; the Nasdaq lost a quarter of its value in a week at the March 2000 confidence cliff
- Greater-fool chains in crypto markets: whitepaper analysis yields to 'the next buyer will show up' consensus—Keynes's beauty-contest structure in its purest form, on assets with no fundamentals

**现代应用（中）**

- 二级市场投资：持仓前写下'我赚的是价值回归的钱还是共识移动的钱'，并按此设退出纪律
- 产品定价：热门品类按'市场预期'而非成本定价——预期市里二阶博弈压倒一阶核算
- 舆情管理：监测'别人认为别人怎么想'的二阶情绪，而非仅监测一手好评率

**Applications (EN)**

- Investing: before taking a position, write down whether you are paid by value reversion or by consensus migration—and set exit rules accordingly
- Pricing hot categories by market expectation rather than cost: in expectation markets second-order play overrides first-order accounting
- Reputation management: monitor second-order sentiment (what people think others think), not just first-hand approval rates

**关联模式**：M-KEY-003、M-KEY-004、M-KEY-001　**代表人物**：约翰·梅纳德·凯恩斯、本杰明·格雷厄姆、乔治·索罗斯

### M-KEY-009 混合经济相机干预法 / Discretionary Statecraft in a Mixed Economy

- 领域：经济政策/国家与市场 / Economic Policy / State and Market　级别：核心　优先级：9
- 来源：《通论》第24章'通论可以引起的社会哲学'：'socialisation of investment...determining the aggregate amount of resources devoted to investment'；1939年《凯恩斯与自由党的信》系列政论

**定义（中）**

拒绝'计划经济或自由放任'的二选一，为'有意识管理的资本主义'提供第三张图纸：市场在微观配置上不可替代，但宏观总量会系统失灵，故国家不必接管面包房，只需执掌总投资水位的'社会化的投资函数'；干预是相机而行的——按诊断开药、按疗效调量、按条件退出，而非接管定价。要害有三：其一，干预的目标窄而深——不是替代市场而是稳定市场赖以存在的总需求地基，'重要的不是国家拥有生产工具，而是国家决定资源多少用于投资'；其二，干预是逆风的——政策方向与经济风向相反（衰退注入、过热收回），故政治上天然逆人性：扩支受欢迎、退出遭恨，干预的真正瓶颈在'收'而不在'放'；其三，专家治国但不专家独裁——凯恩斯相信受过训练的判断（而非机械规则）应当掌舵，同时警惕任何权力的无限期化，干预须自我设限。方法论签名：国家不当厨师，但当恒温器——市场的火该烧谁家由市场定，但全屋的温度由一个会看表的手掌管。

**Definition (EN)**

Discretionary statecraft refuses the binary of planning versus laissez-faire and drafts a third blueprint: consciously managed capitalism. Markets are irreplaceable in micro allocation, but macro aggregates fail systematically, so the state need not nationalize bakeries—only hold the socialized investment function that governs the level of total investment. Intervention is discretionary by design: prescribe by diagnosis, dose by response, exit by condition—never take over pricing. Three disciplines give the mode its force. First, the target is narrow but deep: not to replace the market but to stabilize the aggregate-demand ground the market stands on—'it is not the ownership of the instruments of production that matters; it is the amount of resources the state determines to devote to investment.' Second, intervention leans into the wind: policy runs opposite to the economic weather (inject in recession, withdraw in boom), which is politically unnatural—expansion is popular, contraction cursed—so the true bottleneck of intervention lies in the withdrawal, not the deployment. Third, expert hands but not expert rule: Keynes trusted trained judgment, not mechanical rules, at the wheel—while insisting every intervention carry its own limits, wary of any power made permanent. Signature: the state is not the cook but the thermostat—the market decides whose stove burns, but one watch-checking hand governs the temperature of the whole house.

**关键概念**：相机干预、投资社会化、混合经济、逆风操作、干预自我设限

**四步流程（中）**

1. 先诊断总量再动手：区分'某个行业失灵'（交给市场出清）与'总量塌方'（国家出手），防止干预泛化成计划
2. 把干预写成剂量与条件：出手时同时写明'加到多少算够、何时减、触发什么条件退'，把退出条款前置到授权文件里
3. 逆风的政治预算：为'收回'阶段提前储备政治资本（名分、联盟、沟通框架），因为收缩比扩张难十倍
4. 保留判断权避免规则化：用'有纪律的相机抉择'替代机械规则——目标写成区间与状态描述，不写成铁律

**Process (EN)**

1. Diagnose the aggregate first: separate 'one industry failing' (let the market clear it) from 'aggregate collapse' (state steps in)—prevent intervention from generalizing into planning
2. Write intervention as dose and condition: when deploying, simultaneously specify 'how much is enough, when to taper, what triggers exit'—front-load exit clauses into the authorization itself
3. Budget politics for the headwind: stockpile political capital (mandate, coalition, communication frames) for the withdrawal phase, since contraction is ten times harder than expansion
4. Keep judgment, avoid mechanization: disciplined discretion over mechanical rules—write targets as ranges and state descriptions, never as iron laws

**金句**：国家不当厨师，但当恒温器。 / The state is not the cook but the thermostat.

**代表案例（中）**

- 罗斯福新政（1933-38）：NRA、WPA、社保法案——国家注入总需求而不接管生产资料，'混合经济'的美国原型
- 战后英国福利国家（贝弗里奇报告1942→1945工党政府）：凯恩斯宏观管理+贝弗里奇社会保险=西欧混合经济标准配置；1976年IMF危机后撒切尔-里根回摆恰验证'退出比扩张难'的预言
- 2020年疫情各国'薪资保护计划'：政府替企业发工资保就业（保总需求不接管企业），并预设到期退出——教科书式相机干预与退出条款前置（反面警示：1970年代各国把'相机干预'固化为准自动的赤字扩张，退出机制形同虚设，最终酿成滞胀与反革命回摆——恒温器坏了比没有恒温器更糟）

**Cases (EN)**

- The New Deal (1933-38): NRA, WPA, Social Security—state demand injection without nationalizing the means of production, the American prototype of the mixed economy
- Britain's postwar welfare state (Beveridge 1942 to the 1945 Labour government): Keynesian macro management plus social insurance—the standard West European mixed-economy kit; the Thatcher-Reagan swing after the 1976 IMF crisis confirmed his prediction that withdrawal is harder than deployment
- Pandemic wage-support programs (2020): states paying workers' wages to preserve employment (sustaining aggregate demand without taking over firms), with sunset clauses pre-set—a textbook case of discretionary intervention with the exit designed in (cautionary: in the 1970s states hardened 'discretionary intervention' into quasi-automatic deficit expansion with a vestigial exit, breeding stagflation and the counter-revolutionary swing—a broken thermostat is worse than none)

**现代应用（中）**

- 平台治理：只在系统性风险处出手（流动性、垄断），不接管产品功能——监管做恒温器不做厨师
- 企业总部与业务单元：总部管资源配置总量与风险底线，具体经营留给市场信号
- 家庭财务：设定总储蓄率与投资总量（恒温器），具体消费选择交给成员自主

**Applications (EN)**

- Platform regulation: intervene only at systemic risks (liquidity, monopoly), never take over product features—be the thermostat, not the cook
- HQ vs business units: headquarters governs aggregate resource allocation and risk floors; operations follow market signals
- Household finance: set the aggregate saving rate and investment total; leave individual consumption choices to members

**关联模式**：M-KEY-001、M-KEY-002、M-KEY-005　**代表人物**：约翰·梅纳德·凯恩斯、富兰克林·罗斯福、威廉·贝弗里奇

### M-KEY-010 概率论跨界知识炼金法 / Probability, Polymathy & the Cambridge Method

- 领域：知识生产/跨学科方法 / Knowledge Production / Polymathic Method　级别：核心　优先级：10
- 来源：《概率论》(A Treatise on Probability, 1921) 第1章'合理信念度'；R. F. Harrod《凯恩斯传》；国王学院Chest Fund账目（1920s-1940s）；布卢姆斯伯里集团通信

**定义（中）**

把凯恩斯的知识生产方式本身提炼为模式：从《概率论》(1921)把'合理信念度'与频率派概率分开（为后来的根本不确定性埋下哲学地基），到通论把马歇尔经济学、货币实务、哲学与心理观察熔成一体——他的每个重大产出都来自跨域嫁接：哲学给经济学提供不确定性概念，投资实务给理论提供反例，艺术收藏与布卢姆斯伯里的论辩文化提供对'何为好生活'的最终关怀。要害有三：其一，理论与实战互为审计——凯恩斯管理国王学院基金与个人投机，两次爆仓逼出'流动性偏好'与'选美竞赛'，实务亏损是理论最强的同行评议；其二，从哲学地基建经济学——不满足于把'不确定性'当修辞，而是先写一本概率论专著厘清'我们能知道什么'，再让宏观理论长在这块地基上；其三，生活水准是理论的最终指标——'经济问题解决之后，人如何值得地生活'是布卢姆斯伯里圈对凯恩斯的持久提问，理论的终点不是增长而是'wisdom to live agreeably'。方法论签名：先造认识论的地基，再在上面盖理论的楼——一个学科的范式危机，往往要等隔壁学科的人来拆墙。

**Definition (EN)**

Polymathy as method: from the Treatise on Probability (1921), which split 'degrees of rational belief' from frequency-based probability and laid the philosophical groundwork for radical uncertainty, to the General Theory fusing Marshallian economics, monetary practice, philosophy, and psychological observation—each of Keynes's major outputs came from cross-domain grafting: philosophy supplied economics its concept of uncertainty, investment practice supplied theory its counterexamples, and the art-collecting, argumentative culture of Bloomsbury supplied the final concern with what a good life is. Three disciplines give the mode its force. First, theory and practice audit each other: Keynes ran the King's College Chest Fund and his own account, and two near-wipeouts forced out liquidity preference and the beauty contest—practice's losses are theory's strongest peer review. Second, build economics on philosophical foundations: unwilling to treat 'uncertainty' as rhetoric, he first wrote a treatise on probability to settle 'what can we know,' then grew macro theory on that foundation. Third, the standard of living is the theory's final metric: 'what it is worth living for once the economic problem is solved' was Bloomsbury's standing question to Keynes—theory's terminus is not growth but the wisdom to live agreeably. Signature: lay the epistemological foundation first, then raise the theoretical building on it—a discipline's paradigm crisis usually waits for someone from the next field to tear down its walls.

**关键概念**：概率论、根本不确定性哲学、理论与实战互审、跨界嫁接、好生活之问

**四步流程（中）**

1. 为领域危机找隔壁学科的地基：当本学科核心概念反复失灵时，去哲学/数学/心理学找该概念的认识论重述，而非在本学科内继续修补
2. 让实战亏损进入理论闭环：凡持有可检验立场者，强制记录自己的盈亏与预测误差，把'被打脸'制度化为理论的同行评议
3. 区分两种无知：可量化的风险（交给概率）与不可量化的根本不确定（交给判断与制度缓冲）——混用二者是大多数决策灾难的源头
4. 以好生活为终审：每项理论或政策的最终问题不是'它产出多少'而是'它让人值得怎样生活'——为增长设问时先设终极用途

**Process (EN)**

1. When a field's core concepts keep failing, borrow epistemology from the neighboring discipline: restate the concept philosophically rather than keep patching inside the field
2. Force practice's losses into theory's loop: whoever holds testable positions must record P&L and forecast errors—institutionalize 'being wrong' as theory's peer review
3. Distinguish two ignorances: quantifiable risk (hand to probability) and radical uncertainty (hand to judgment and institutional buffers)—conflating them is the source of most decision disasters
4. Make the good life the final auditor: the ultimate question for any theory or policy is not 'how much does it produce' but 'what life does it make worth living'—set the terminal use before setting the growth target

**金句**：先造认识论的地基，再在上面盖理论的楼。 / Lay the epistemological foundation first, then raise the theoretical building on it.

**代表案例（中）**

- 1920年凯恩斯外汇投机爆仓与1946年去世时1.5亿英镑当代身家：两次几乎归零（1920、1929）逼出'从择时转向择价值'——实战亏损改写了学术立场
- 《概率论》到《通论》的十五年间隔：先解决'我们能知道什么'再回答'经济如何运行'——认识论先行的方法论样板
- 布卢姆斯伯里圈的跨界生产：经济学家、画家、小说家、哲学家的定期论辩——'凯恩斯革命'的观念弹药多产自这间客厅而非财政部（反面警示：跨界嫁接若缺实战检验会滑向聪明人的自我确认——凯恩斯本人1920年外汇投机爆仓即是'理论自负'的学费，跨界必须配对审计）

**Cases (EN)**

- Keynes's 1920 currency wipeout to a £1.5M contemporary fortune at death (1946): twice nearly zero (1920, 1929) forced the turn from timing to value—practice's losses rewrote the academic position
- The fifteen years between the Treatise on Probability (1921) and the General Theory (1936): settle 'what can we know' first, then answer 'how does the economy run'—epistemology-first method as template
- Bloomsbury's cross-domain production: economists, painters, novelists, and philosophers in standing argument—the intellectual munitions of the 'Keynesian revolution' were forged in that drawing room, not the Treasury
- Counterexample: a bank-run stampede for cash is liquidity preference, but not every hoard is—if households save long-term because income expectations darken, the fault lies in demand-side income, not the preference to hold money; rate cuts and deposit insurance do nothing, and income must be replaced directly—misreading 'insurance demand' as 'an interest-rate problem' turns easing into pushing on a string
- Counterexample: animal spirits explain investment swings but cannot launder every irrationality—if knowing fraud (Ponzi layers) inside a bubble is also filed under 'confidence', the line between misjudgment and deception is erased; 1929 and 2008 differ precisely here—the former a swing of sentiment, the latter compounded by systemic fabrication, calling for different tools (expectation management vs criminal prosecution)
- Bloomsbury's cross-domain production: economists, painters, novelists, and philosophers in standing argument—the intellectual munitions of the 'Keynesian revolution' were forged in that drawing room, not the Treasury (cautionary: cross-domain grafting without field testing slides into the clever man's self-confirmation—Keynes's own 1920 FX-speculation blowup was the tuition paid for 'theoretical hubris'; crossing fields demands paired auditing)

**现代应用（中）**

- 企业战略部引入哲学/历史顾问：当核心KPI反复失灵时，去学科外找概念的地基重述
- 研究者的'亏损日志'：把预测错误制度化为理论迭代燃料，如同凯恩斯的两次爆仓
- 团队知识生产：定期跨领域论辩会（布卢姆斯伯里式），新范式多来自隔壁学科的拆墙人

**Applications (EN)**

- Strategy teams hiring philosophers/historians: when core KPIs keep failing, seek the concept's epistemological restatement outside the discipline
- The practitioner's loss journal: institutionalize forecast errors as fuel for theory revision, as Keynes's two wipeouts were
- Bloomsbury-style cross-domain salons: new paradigms usually come from the neighbor who tears down the wall

## Caveats

- 凯恩斯的'政府支出'常被简化为'花钱万能'——他本人在《通论》中强调乘数依赖闲置产能与开放度，充分就业后继续注入只产生通胀（他在1937年与1940年战时财政中反复警告）
- '长期我们都死'常被断章为短期主义辩护——原文语境是批判'以长期自动均衡为名拒绝当下行动'，他同时主张为长期纪律设计退场
- 布雷顿森林最终协定是凯恩斯方案与怀特方案的折中，bancor、对称调整义务与惩罚顺差国的条款均被删改——不能把IMF的运行等同于凯恩斯的设计
- 《和平的经济后果》对赔款额的估算（他认为最多可执行约百亿级）后被史料修正——但其'支付能力审计'的方法论本身未被推翻
- 凯恩斯个人投资生涯两次几乎破产（1920外汇、1929美股），其'选美竞赛'与'动物精神'理论既是观察也是自我教训——引用时应区分理论预言与个人投资绩效
